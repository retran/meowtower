# The world outside the Tower

## Summary

This record holds the town of «Туманный Брод» (Misty Ford), its places and
townsfolk, the forest beyond the mill where the Tower stands, and the rule
that decides who can see the Tower. The town keeps the tone rules of
CAN-0010: strangeness there is ordinary, and nobody remarks on it. The Tower
itself is set out in CAN-0030. Lotta's turn into an ally belongs to the
year's campaign in CAN-0080, and Irma and the mayor carry the mystery that
campaign resolves.

## Misty Ford

Misty Ford is a small town on canals. It has narrow houses with stepped
roofs, a bicycle at every door, humpbacked footbridges and a mill at the
edge. Fog here is a municipal service, not weather. It arrives on a timetable
posted on the town hall and runs late no more than the bus does. The
townsfolk are polite and unhurried, and they don't notice oddities at all,
because in Misty Ford oddity is just a Tuesday.

## The forest beyond the mill

The «Шерстяной лес» (Woollen Forest) starts beyond the mill. Moss like fluffy
yarn covers the trees, the paths wind, and mushrooms grow in neat rows as if
someone had counted them. Small harmless creatures live there: shaggy
ball-of-yarn hedgehogs, fireflies that blink in turn, and sceptic owls who
believe nothing. The sceptic owls aren't owls but very round birds, and they
ask you not to confuse the two. Deep in the forest, in a clearing that once
held only a tree stump, the Tower now stands.

## Why nobody sees the Tower

The adults of Misty Ford see the Tower but don't notice it. To them it is
"the new water tower", and they are a little annoyed nobody consulted them.
Asked directly, a townsperson shrugs: «Башня как башня. Всегда тут стояла.
Кажется» (A tower's a tower. It's always been here. I think).

Only people who count things for no reason see the real Tower: steps, clouds,
buttons, crows on a wire. The heroine always did this, so she saw it. Three
others also see the Tower for what it is:

- Madam Mayor Muff sees it because cats see everything. She watches the
  Tower every evening from the town hall windowsill and tells nobody.
- Granny Irma pretends she doesn't see it. But the scarf on her gate is always
  knitted in the same pattern as the Tower's newest floor.
- Lotta Weathervane starts seeing the Tower in the middle of the first
  season, when the heroine gives her a pair of glasses from the Tower. From
  then on Lotta is the ally outside and keeps a log of the town's oddities.
