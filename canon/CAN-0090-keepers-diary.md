# The Keeper's Diary

## Summary

This record holds «Дневник Смотрителя» (the Keeper's Diary): who wrote it,
where its pages hide, its voice, ten sample entries, and the six ciphers with
the Master's rules for them. The Diary's author is the answer to the central
mystery, so its pages follow the campaign's checkpoints and pace of the reveal
(CAN-0080). Its pages hide on the floors (CAN-0040), its creatures are Tangles
(CAN-0050), and the heroine carries it from her starting kit (CAN-0070).
Ciphers are voluntary riddles outside trials, and the Master's general rules
in CAN-0110 still apply to them.

## Who kept the Diary

Young Irma kept the Diary when she was the Keeper. She signs «С.» (K., for
«Смотрительница», Keeper), and on a few old pages she forgot to correct she
signs «И.» (I.). The Diary is a thick exercise book in a knitted cover, with
dog-eared pages, jam stains and drawings in the margins.

The pages are hidden all over the Tower: in the Archive's books, under the
boilers of the Works, in old ovens, in the canal locks, in «Барон»'s (the
Baron's) pockets. Many sit in hiding places that open after a clean
untangling. A page that isn't found today is re-hidden by the Tower and will
turn up later. The pages of the plot checkpoints arrive on the calendar,
whatever the outcomes.

## The Diary's voice

Lively, observant, a little dry. Short notes, drawings of creatures, arrows,
crossed-out words, notes in the margins. The Keeper writes like a researcher:
serious but with humour, sometimes complaining, sometimes admiring. She never
says outright who she is. Sometimes she addresses whoever finds the Diary:
«Если ты это читаешь, ты тоже считаешь ступеньки» (If you're reading this,
you count steps too).

## Ten sample entries

1. Creature: «Недоделитель» (the Underdivider). «Делит всё на части, но
   никогда не до конца. Из-за него в Башне нет ни одного целого пирога. Не
   кормить. Покормила. Зря.» (Divides everything into parts, but never all
   the way. Because of him there isn't a single whole pie in the Tower. Do not
   feed. Fed him. Shouldn't have.)
2. Creature: «Нулёк» (Zerolet). «Почти невидимый. Садится в конец чего
   угодно. Если что-то вдруг стало огромным, проверь, не сидит ли там Нулёк.
   Он обижается, если его не замечают. Я его заметила. Он так растерялся, что
   распутался сам.» (Almost invisible. Sits at the end of anything. If
   something has suddenly become huge, check whether Zerolet is sitting
   there. He's hurt if nobody notices him. I noticed him. He was so flustered
   he untangled himself.)
3. Map: the Works. A drawing of cogs with arrows. «Здесь главный котёл.
   Здесь стол. Здесь я спрятала то, что спрятала. Не ищи за котлом. (Ищи за
   котлом.)» (Here is the main boiler. Here is the table. Here I hid what I
   hid. Don't look behind the boiler. (Look behind the boiler.))
4. A note about a Guardian. «Шкаф Аркадий записал меня в раздел
   “Непредвиденное”. Я считаю это комплиментом. Он — нет.» («Шкаф Аркадий»,
   Arkady the Cabinet, filed me under "Unforeseen". I take it as a compliment.
   He doesn't.)
5. A rule. «Узел — это просто нитка, которая забыла, куда шла. Не ругай её.
   Напомни.» (A knot is just a thread that forgot where it was going. Don't
   scold it. Remind it.)
6. A riddle. «Меня распускают каждую ночь, а утром я снова целая. Меня
   связали, но не носят. Что я?» (I'm unravelled every night, and in the
   morning I'm whole again. I was knitted, but nobody wears me. What am I?)
   The answer is the Tower, and it opens a hidden room on the loop landing.
7. Creature: «Моль Вековая» (the Age-Old Moth). «Большая, мягкая, вежливая.
   Ест только то, что потеряно. Не бойся. Однажды она съела мою грусть.
   Грусть тоже была потеряна.» (Big, soft, polite. Eats only what is lost.
   Don't be afraid. Once she ate my sadness. The sadness was lost too.)
8. A note about a familiar. «М. опять спит на схеме. Когда я пытаюсь её
   убрать, она смотрит так, будто это я сплю на её схеме. Возможно, так и
   есть.» (M. is asleep on the pattern again. When I try to move her, she
   looks at me as if I'm the one asleep on her pattern. Perhaps I am.)
9. A cipher in mirror writing: a page to be read in a mirror. «Изнанка есть у
   всего. Даже у Башни. Даже у меня.» (Everything has an underside. Even the
   Tower. Even me.)
10. The last page, which opens in July. «Если ты это читаешь, значит, Башня
    выбрала тебя. Не я. Она. Это важно. Ты справишься лучше меня, потому что
    ты — это ты. Приходи на чай. Ты знаешь, где я живу. Ты всегда знала. —
    И.» (If you're reading this, the Tower chose you. Not me. It did. That
    matters. You'll do better than I did, because you are you. Come for tea.
    You know where I live. You always knew. — I.)

## Ciphers

Decoding is a voluntary riddle outside trials and outside diagnostics: it's
never scored and never recorded as mathematics. That holds for the cipher
«Числа вместо букв» (Numbers for Letters) too: it contains numbers, but it's
a game with the alphabet, not a trial. Each cipher opens a page of lore, a
piece of the map, a hiding place or an object for the heroine's room.
Difficulty grows season by season.

The first version of the game has no ciphers. A page that would be
enciphered arrives already developed: the Diary shows the whole page before
the heroine opens it, as it develops one letter for a stuck reader below. The
autumn pages still give their notes about the Guardians, hints to hatches and
names of creatures and hiding places. When ciphers arrive, pages found after
that come enciphered, as the table sets out (CAN-0080, "The campaign in the
first version").

| Cipher | How it works | When it appears | What it opens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| «Зеркальное письмо» (Mirror Writing) | The text is written backwards; read it in a mirror or right to left | Autumn | Notes about the Guardians, hints to hatches |
| «Числа вместо букв» (Numbers for Letters) | Each letter is replaced by its number in the alphabet (А is 1, Б is 2 and so on) | Autumn | Names of creatures, names of hiding places |
| «Шифр сдвига» (Shift Cipher) | Each letter is shifted along the alphabet by several steps. The key is hidden nearby: «шагов столько, сколько лап у кошки» (as many steps as a cat has paws) | Winter | Pages about the Great Tangle, Irma's notes about her familiar |
| «Петельное письмо» (Loop Writing) | The Keeper's invented alphabet: the letters sit in the cells of knitting grids, and each sign is the cell's shape plus loop dots. The key table is a separate page found at the end of autumn | Winter to spring | A map of secret passages, letters Irma never sent |
| «Через ступеньку» (Every Other Step) | Read every second (or third) letter, as if stepping over stairs | Spring | Hints to the Underside |
| «Мигание» (Blinking) | Messages from the lantern of «Мигальщик» (the Blinker): short and long flashes, with the key hidden on the Canal | Spring to summer | The way to the last key |

### Rules for the Master

- Ciphers are short: one or two phrases.
- The alphabet is Russian.
- The key can always be found in the world.
- Hints to ciphers come through familiars and the story, never through
  explanations.
- If the player is stuck, the Diary "develops" one letter by itself a session
  later.
