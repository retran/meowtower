# Items

## Summary

This record holds the Tower's items: the five categories, fifteen sample
items, the forge with its materials and recipes, the shop on the Fair, and the
rules for items the AI invents. Items change the heroine's looks, her room and
the story, and never touch a trial, because only the guiding thread (CAN-0030)
helps with a trial. The forge stands between two floors and the shop on the
Fair of Scales (CAN-0040); the Baron who keeps it is a Guardian from CAN-0040.
The heroine's starting kit is in CAN-0070, and the Master's wider rules for
invention are in CAN-0110.

## Categories

| Category | What it is | What it affects |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Accessories | Badges, hair clips, scarves, bags, glasses | Small story effects, a characteristic bonus in the story |
| Decor | Furniture, lamps, rugs, plants, trophies for the room | The heroine's room, scenes with familiars |

## The forge

On the landing between the Works and the Confectionery stands «Кузница Ниток»
(the Thread Forge): an anvil like a plump cushion and a hammer that is a ball
of yarn. Here the hunter forges, or rather knits, foci, outfits and
accessories from three materials:

- «звёздная пряжа» (star yarn): glowing threads left over from knots the
  Guardians untangled, which also gather by themselves from clean rows;
- «осколки звёздной стали» (star-steel shards): found on the floors and left
  behind by clean untangling;
- floor materials (see below).

Forging is a small ceremony: the anvil purrs, the hammer knocks, the familiars
bring threads, and the System announces the recipe. The player names each new
item.

The forge is open from the first days: the System gives the first recipe
after the first material is found. Big recipes, such as complex foci and path
outfits, open later as the story goes on.

### Recipes

A recipe is a System entry written like a knitting pattern: the item, its
materials and how much of each. The quantities are fields the code fills in.
Example: «Рецепт. Фонарь Терпеливого Света. Ряд 1: осколки звёздной стали.
Ряд 2: звёздная пряжа. Ряд 3: архивная пыльца. Торопиться не рекомендуется.»
(Recipe. Lantern of Patient Light. Row 1: star-steel shards. Row 2: star
yarn. Row 3: Archive pollen. Hurrying is not recommended.)

Recipes are visible in advance, and the result of forging is always known,
because forging has no chance in it. An accessory takes a small recipe, an
outfit a bigger one and a focus the biggest. Floors and Guardians give shards
and yarn to every hunter, and clean untanglings and clean rows add more, so a
recipe always comes within reach and a clean day only brings it closer.

## Chests

After every room the heroine finds a chest with three rewards laid out in
the open, and she takes one. The three always come from three different
kinds, drawn from four: cosmetics (an outfit, an accessory or a curiosity),
shards and yarn, buttons, and a page of the Diary (CAN-0090). Nothing in a
chest is hidden, and nothing in it is left to chance.

## The shop

On the Scales Fair there's «Лавка» (the shop). «Барон фон Наценка» (Baron
von Markup) stands behind the counter, but the System hangs the price tags,
and the Baron is deeply offended by this. So the prices in the shop are
honest and don't change.

- The only currency is buttons. The world has no real money.
- Everything on sale is visible at once: six things on the shelf, one
  focus, two outfits, two accessories and one curiosity. Two of them change
  every morning, so nothing stands there longer than three days, and the
  shelf never offers what the hunter already has. The shop has no mystery
  bags, no chances, no «последний экземпляр» (last one left) and no «скидка
  сгорит» (discount expires).
- The shop sells outfits, accessories, foci and curiosities. Guiding threads
  aren't for sale, because the Tower gives them.
- The Baron still tries to haggle, or to sell the sky or last Tuesday. Each
  time the System writes: «Товар не зарегистрирован. Цена не изменена.» (Item
  not registered. Price unchanged.)

## Rules for items the AI invents

These rules wait for AI-made items, which come after the first release
together with the live art that would draw them (RES-2800).

- Name: two to four words, a noun plus a property or a story, such as «Плащ
  Стража, Которого Угостили» or «Фонарь Терпеливого Света». No numbers, no
  other brands and no references to real games or cartoons.
- Description: one to three short sentences, dry and deadpan. Often an
  unexpected last line. No exclamation marks and no explaining the joke. It
  can mention a story from the heroine's adventure.
- Allowed effects: a visual effect on the blow (colour, sparks, sound), a
  story ability (it shines, opens small doors, scares Tangles off with
  cuteness), a characteristic bonus in the story, how characters react, decor
  for the room.
- Forbidden effects: anything that affects trials (hints, skipping, second
  attempts, showing the answer), because only the guiding thread (CAN-0030)
  can do that and items can't; weapons that wound; items tied to danger,
  fear, food used to shame, or looks used to shame; anything that copies
  other games.
- The Director sets limits and rarity. The Master hands out no items beyond
  the order.

## Open questions

- The forbidden effects say only the guiding thread can give "hints,
  skipping, second attempts, showing the answer". The specification has the
  thread buy a hint before an answer and a detailed explanation after it; the
  second attempt and the correct answer come free after every first attempt
  (RES-0400), and no mechanism skips a trial. Should the list read
  that nothing but the task window's own rules and the guiding thread touches
  a trial?
- Resolved 2026-09-26: the canon now carries the shelf of six things, two of
  which change each morning, and the first six recipes; prices and recipe
  quantities stay in the game's data. RES-2100 holds the amounts and the
  reason.
- The shop's list leaves out decor, while the specification's economy lists
  the shop as a later source of room decor. Does the shop sell decor once
  decor arrives?
- The forge is "open from the first days", and the first recipe comes after
  the first material (RES-2100); the specification's economy says the forge
  opens "from the first week" in the MVP. Which is it?
- Resolved on 2026-09-27: a chest offers three different kinds of reward
  drawn from four, cosmetics, shards and yarn, buttons and Diary pages.
  Research decided it on the owner's instruction, and RES-2100 holds the
  reason.
- Items invented by the AI come later than the MVP in the specification. Until
  then, where do new items come from beyond the fifteen samples and the
  forge's recipes?
