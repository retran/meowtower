# Elements and familiars

## Summary

This record holds the eight elements, the ring that sets which element is
showy against which, and the familiars: how they live, how they explain a
knot, the 24 starting familiars, the three legendary ones, the choice in
Session 0 and rare encounters. Each element belongs to a floor of the Tower
(CAN-0040) and to a thread of «Основа» (the Warp, CAN-0030). Familiars are born
from knots untangled with kindness, so they lean on the Tangles (CAN-0050).
The legendary familiars belong to the central mystery and arrive on the
campaign calendar (CAN-0080). The Master invents new familiars only on the
Director's order and by the rules in CAN-0110. Every familiar name here is a
placeholder: the player names her familiar when it hatches.

## Elements

Each familiar has one element. Elements are tied to the threads of «Основа»
and to the floors, but they're kinds of magic, like fire and water in fairy
tales, and they're never school subjects.

Each element has a symbol that shows beside its name. «Мера» is an
hourglass, drawn still: its sand never runs, and no hourglass appears in the
task window, on a rest stop, an eye exercise or a soft stop, because nothing
on her screen may look like a clock (CAN-0120).

## The ring of elements

The elements stand in a circle, and each one is showy against the next. In
the familiars' story battles the ring gives an advantage. In a fight with a
Tangle the blow just looks prettier: the familiar is proud and the Tangle is
impressed. The ring never affects a trial. The legendary familiars stand
outside the ring.

| Element | Showy against | Why | Weak before |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## How familiars work

A familiar is a small magical creature born from a knot that was untangled
with kindness. So in the world, and in every text the heroine sees, a
familiar is «узелок» (little knot; plural «узелки»), and never «фамильяр».
No text calls a knot «узелок», so the creature and the trial never blur. Familiars hatch from knitted eggs, grow through friendship and
evolve in three stages. They never fall seriously ill, never suffer, never
vanish for good and never die. If a familiar is "tired", it falls asleep in
the heroine's pocket and is lively again a scene later.

A familiar's abilities change the story: it lights the way, finds things,
distracts, and scares Tangles with its cuteness. The abilities never hint at
the answer to a trial on their own. A familiar gives a hint or an explanation
only along the guiding thread (see "How familiars explain").

After a clean untangling the familiar rejoices in its own way. After a
loosened knot it says something warm and neutral, such as «Узел стал мягче.
Пойдём вдоль стены» (The knot has gone softer. Let's walk along the wall), and
it's never sad "because of her". In dreamcore scenes the familiar is always
beside the heroine and is the first to light a lamp.

The names below are placeholders. The player names a familiar when it hatches
and can choose one or two character traits for it. The System offers the
placeholder first among its suggestions, and never suggests a move's name or
a name the heroine or another familiar already has. For example, a player
might call «Пуговка» «Искорка» (Little Spark) and the paper moth «Шуршик»
«Шелестун» (Rustlekin); from then on only her name lives in the game.

## How familiars explain

A familiar explains a knot only after the spell, and only when the heroine
has spent a guiding thread. In the world the familiar explains in its own
voice, and nobody else speaks. Outside the world, a separate role, the
Explainer, writes the familiar's lines from the steps the code computed; the
Explainer is not a character, and no text the player sees names it
(RES-0600). The Master, the storyteller, never sees the explanation, and it
never enters the story.

- Order: what the knot wanted, then each loop in turn (step, numbers, result),
  then where the thread turned aside if the knot cheated, then the result,
  then a short warm ending.
- Where the thread turned aside is always the knot's trick and never the
  heroine's fault: «Узел притворился, что знаменатели тоже складываются. Узлы
  так шутят.» (The knot pretended the denominators add up too. Knots joke like
  that.)
- The voice follows the familiar's character and the traits the heroine chose.
  «Пуговка» recounts under her breath and checks twice. «Винтик» hurries but
  goes strictly in order. «Безешка» explains dramatically, like a scene from a
  play. «Корица» explains gently and plainly. «Шуршик» sounds as if reading
  from a library card. «Бубенец» speaks in announcements.
- Length: at most eight sentences.
- Numbers appear in the explanation only as placeholders, and the code puts in
  the real numbers from the steps. The familiar adds no numbers, facts or
  other methods of its own.
- Forbidden: every word of the forbidden list in CAN-0010, in any form, such
  as «ошибка», «неправильно», «промах», «ты не поняла», «это же просто»
  (mistake, wrong, miss, you didn't understand, it's easy), the school words
  «задача», «пример», «урок», «школа», «оценка» (problem, sum, lesson,
  school, mark) and praise of intelligence such as «умница», «гений» (clever
  girl, genius). The familiar can rejoice in loops that fell into place and
  praise persistence.

Samples, with square brackets marking where the code inserts numbers:

- «Пуговка»: «Узел хотел знать, сколько осталось. Сначала я пересчитала
  покупки: [шаг 1]. Потом отняла от монет: [шаг 2]. Пересчитала ещё раз.
  Осталось [ответ]. Теперь я верю в этот узел.» (The knot wanted to know how
  much was left. First I recounted the purchases: [step 1]. Then I took them
  from the coins: [step 2]. I recounted again. [Answer] is left. Now I trust
  this knot.)
- «Винтик»: «Быстро. Сначала умножаем: [шаг 1]. Потом прибавляем: [шаг 2].
  Всё, готово — [ответ]. Узел хотел, чтобы мы прибавили раньше. Не выйдет,
  узел.» (Quick. First we multiply: [step 1]. Then we add: [step 2]. Done:
  [answer]. The knot wanted us to add first. Not happening, knot.)
- «Безешка»: «Занавес открывается. Узел делит пирог на части: [шаг 1]. Затем
  мы берём свои части: [шаг 2]. Финал. Ответ — [ответ]. Узел притворялся, что
  знаменатели складываются. Какая дерзость.» (The curtain rises. The knot
  cuts the pie into pieces: [step 1]. Then we take our pieces: [step 2].
  Finale. The answer is [answer]. The knot pretended the denominators add up.
  The nerve.)

## Evolution in the first version

Every familiar has three stages, as its evolution line below names them. The
first version of the game ships all three for the three starting familiars
and the first two for every other familiar; their third stages arrive later
with their pictures, together with the rest of the roster after the first
version. Evolution comes at friendship levels. Friendship grows
from shared adventures, rest stops and conversations, and never from right
answers. A familiar whose friendship already passed its third threshold when
the third stage arrives evolves at the next rest stop, so no friendship is
lost to the wait.

## The choice in Session 0

The heroine chooses one of three:

- «Пуговка» («Искра»), for a girl who loves order, mysteries and
  thoroughness.
- «Винтик» («Ход»), for a girl who loves speed, adventure and noise.
- «Безешка» («Крошка»), for a girl who loves drama, beauty and grand gestures.

The two she doesn't choose don't disappear: they turn up later on their own
floors and are glad to meet her.

## Rare encounters

Roster familiars arrive through friendship about once a week, and that
friendship always succeeds. Sometimes a success branch opens a rare
encounter: a floor's familiar shows itself before its time, sitting in a
hiding place or peeping out of an untangled knot. If the room took the other
path that day, the encounter isn't lost: the familiar shows itself another
time, in another scene. Legendary familiars come only through the story.
