# The heroine

## Summary

This record holds who the heroine is: a knot hunter, her six ranks, her five
characteristics, her five paths, her starting kit and her room in the Tower.
Ranks and characteristics shape the story only and never touch a trial. The
ranks unlock places and powers described in CAN-0040 (the floors' secrets),
CAN-0100 (the forge and the Fair) and CAN-0130 (the Underside). The System
that gives her tasks and changes her rank is set out in CAN-0030, the Diary in
CAN-0090 and the familiars in CAN-0060. The Director signals each rank on the
campaign calendar (CAN-0080).

## What kind of hunter she is

The heroine is a «охотница на узлы» (knot hunter): a girl who sees the threads
of «Основа» (the Warp) and can untangle them. The world has no school for
hunters and no teacher. She has the System, which gives her tasks and keeps
count; the Diary, where the previous Keeper left hints; and the familiars,
who believe in her more than she does. She is a catgirl, with soft ears and
a tail the colour of her hair. Her look comes from one of four character
sheets drawn from the reference picture keyart-heroine (CAN-0120): the
family picks the sheet with the player once, before her art is made, and it
holds in every frame after. The four sheets differ only in hair colour and
style, eye colour and the cardigan's colour; her cat ears and tail, the
cardigan and the dress's silhouette are the same on all four. In Session 0 the player chooses her name, the
colour of her cloak and her focus; the cloak colour tints the cardigan and
bows. The heroine is the protagonist: the world notices what
she does, the Guardians remember her, and the townsfolk feel the town has
become a little more even.

## Ranks

A rank is how deeply the hunter sees «Основа». The System changes the rank on
the Director's signal at campaign milestones, and the specification holds the
calendar. The Master makes each rank change its own solemn scene: the Tower
lights every window, the familiars line up, and the Guardians send
congratulations in their own style. A rank never goes down.

| Rank | Title | What the heroine sees | What opens in the story |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E | «Пробуждённая» (Awakened) | Knots and Tangles, but the threads are still blurred | Every floor, hidden hatches and portals between halls, the first familiar, the Diary, the first ciphers, short "slips" onto the sleepy edge of the Underside (CAN-0130) |
| D | «Петелька» (Little Loop) | The threads of one floor in full | The floors' secret places («Секреты», Secrets, in CAN-0040), trading at the Fair |
| C | «Кружевница» (Lacemaker) | How the threads of different floors connect | Choosing a path (class), the forge's big recipes, the Underside begins to call |
| B | «Спица» (Needle) | Future knots before they're tied | Talking with the Guardians as equals, entry into the Underside |
| A | «Веретено» (Spindle) | The whole Tower as one pattern | The Keeper's armchair lets her touch it; the town starts to notice the Tower |
| S | «Смотрительница» (Keeper) | «Основа» of all of «Туманный Брод» (Misty Ford) | The right to knit new rows of the Tower and to choose its future |

### Ranks in the first version

In the first version the heroine holds rank E from Session 0, and the rank
never changes. At rank E she gets every floor, hidden hatches and portals
between halls, the first familiar, the Diary and short slips onto the edge of
the Underside; the first ciphers wait until ciphers ship, and until then the
Diary develops an enciphered page before she opens it (CAN-0090). An opening
of a later rank that the first version has, such as the floors' secret
places, opens on the date that rank would have arrived, while the badge still
says E. An opening the first version doesn't have, such as a path or the
forge's big recipes, stays closed. A season finale that would raise the rank
gives its chapter title and main reward like any chapter finale, and the rank
stays E (CAN-0080). When ranks arrive, one solemn
scene gives the heroine the rank the calendar has reached.

The canon names no month for a rank. After the first version, the campaign's
season milestones set when ranks come (CAN-0080). Her rival «Мирра Шпилька»
(Mirra Hairpin) holds rank D, one above the heroine's starting E, and keeps
it all through the first version (CAN-0080).

## Characteristics

Characteristics affect only the story: how easily the player's bold idea
works. They never affect trials. The first version has no characteristics: a
level-up there is light and sound and gives no characteristic points.

| Characteristic | What it means in the world | Example in the story |
| --- | --- | --- |
| «Сила» (Strength) | Pulling tight threads, moving heavy things, holding on in the wind | Shifting a jammed door in the Works, holding on to a cloud that's flying away |
| «Ловкость» (Agility) | Sliding, jumping, being in time, hiding | Running along a swaying bookcase, dodging falling cakes |
| «Мудрость» (Wisdom) | Noticing the strange, reading signs, remembering the Diary | Noticing that a portrait has the wrong eyes, understanding the cipher of «Мигальщик» (the Blinker) |
| «Обаяние» (Charm) | Negotiating, making friends, persuading | Talking «Барон» (the Baron) into a fair deal, calming an offended Tangle |
| «Удача» (Luck) | Oddities fall out in her favour | A lost sock turns out to be a key; a staircase turns the right way by itself |

## Paths

At rank C the heroine chooses a path. A path changes her animations, sounds
and outfits, and which choices the story offers her more often. Later she can
master a second path.

| Path | Essence | Style in the story |
| --- | --- | --- |
| «Светоплётка» (Lightweaver) | Untangles threads with light and patterns | Showy flashes, shining patterns in the air |
| «Следопытка» (Tracker) | Finds secret passages, reads tracks, sees danger first | Hidden doors, maps, scouting |
| «Заводчица» (Breeder) | The strongest bond with familiars | Familiars act together, more scenes with them, easy hatching |
| «Мастерица» (Craftswoman) | Forges and knits items from the threads of «Основа» | The forge, mending, unexpected inventions |
| «Хранительница» (Protector) | Protects, reconciles, negotiates | Shields of thread, talks with the Guardians, friendship with Tangles |

## Starting kit

- A focus of her choice. The focus isn't a weapon for striking; it's the tool
  the hunter uses to direct threads. The four choices:
  - «спица-посох» (needle staff): long, with a glowing tip;
  - «фонарь-клубок» (yarn-ball lantern): glows when a thread spins inside it;
  - «блокнот-гримуар» (notebook grimoire): its pages draw patterns by
    themselves;
  - «крючок-жезл» (crochet-hook wand): curved, can hook threads from afar.
- «Плащ охотницы» (the hunter's cloak): a cream cable-knit cardigan with a
  hood that has its own knitted ears, and one patch, a heart on the sleeve.
  It is one garment, not a cloak over the cardigan. The player chooses its
  colour, which also tints her bows.
- «Рюкзак» (a backpack), with an endless side pocket where the familiar
  sleeps.
- «Дневник Смотрителя» (the Keeper's Diary), almost empty at first, because
  most of its pages are hidden in the Tower (CAN-0090).
- «Носок левый» (the Left Sock), described in CAN-0100. Nobody knows what it's
  for. It's needed.

## Her room in the Tower

Between the first and second floors there's a loop landing where the
staircase makes a circle. On the very first day a door appears there with a
nameplate that has no name yet: it's the heroine's room. The nightly
re-knitting never touches it.

- At first: a small round room with a window onto the forest, a hammock bed
  of yarn, a rug and one lamp.
- It grows with the story: after each floor something new appears. The list:
  a shelf of trophies from a Guardian, beds for the familiars, a wall for the
  map of the Tower and the Diary's pages, a fireplace with an unreal but warm
  fire, a balcony overlooking «Туманный Брод», a round window in the ceiling,
  and a secret little door into the library.
- The player chooses the decor, and everything she places stays for good. She
  can name the room.
- The familiars furnish their own corners, which gives reasons for small
  scenes.
