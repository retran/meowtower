# The Tangles

## Summary

This record holds what a Tangle is, how it looks, how the heroine untangles
it, why Tangles appear, the twenty main Tangles, the eight eerie-cute Tangles
of dreamcore, and the rules the Master follows to invent new ones. A Tangle
is a knot in the Warp come alive (CAN-0030), and it lives on the floor whose
thread it tangles (CAN-0040). It is never hurt and never lost: after any
outcome it goes back into the Tower's pattern. The eerie-cute Tangles appear
only within the creepiness level and dreamcore rules of CAN-0130, and the
spring Tangles from the Underside belong to the campaign in CAN-0080.

## What a Tangle is

A Tangle is a knot in the Warp that came alive. When a thread of Count,
Measure, Share or Facet twists somewhere, the knot starts to move, rustle,
talk and tangle everything around it: a staircase gets shorter, a pie splits
into unequal pieces, a clock ticks backwards. Tangles aren't evil. They are
just tangled, and it makes them uncomfortable, like a jumper worn inside out.

## How Tangles look

Tangles are round, soft and slightly "glitchy". Their edges tremble like a
picture with interference, their colours sometimes slip one pixel sideways,
and tangled threads show through their bodies. They have big eyes and an
expression of permanent mild bewilderment.

## How the heroine untangles them

There are no blows, wounds or pain. The heroine untangles the knot with the
spells of her trials. A correct spell is a bright flash of light that
returns the thread to its place. An imprecise spell is a flash that almost
unties the knot, with one little loop sticking out. A wrong spell or «не
знаю» (I don't know) is a soft glow that loosens the knot. When every spell
in the room has been cast, one of two things happens, and both are good.
Which one depends on how cleanly the room's spells went: if enough of them
untangled the knot cleanly, with an almost counting for half, the room ends
cleanly; otherwise the Tangle loosens and gives way. The game rules hold the
exact share, and the Director tells the Master which branch it is:

- Untangled cleanly: the Tangle unties into a ribbon, a fluffy ball of yarn
  or a cloud of sparks, sighs with relief and floats back into the Tower's
  wall, where it becomes part of the pattern. More often than not it leaves
  a gift: a button, a shard of star steel or a page of the Diary, and
  sometimes it opens a cache. Sometimes a Tangle that made friends becomes a
  familiar's egg.
- Loosened, it gives way: the Tangle grows calm and agreeable but doesn't
  untie yet. It shows a side path, curls up asleep, or bows politely and
  goes into the wall. The heroine takes the other way, where she meets
  something of its own. The Tangle returns to the pattern later, by itself,
  once it has rested.

Nobody loses, because the Tower always has more than one way.

## Why Tangles appear

The Tower knits itself every night, and every piece of knitting has
mistakes. The longer the Tower slept without a Keeper, the more knots built
up. In spring, Tangles come from the Underside: mirror Tangles with inverted
colours (CAN-0080).

the Underside (CAN-0130). Each has an eerie first impression and a kind

appears. The first release has three of them: «Шепотун», «Шкатулочница» and
«Портретница», all of level 1; the other five come later. «Площадка Сумерек» (the Twilight Playground) and «Бесконечный Мягкий
Коридор» (the Endless Soft Corridor) are dreamcore locations set out in

## Rules for the Master: inventing new Tangles

1. Each Tangle tangles one specific thing tied to its floor's thread. The
   name hints at what it does, and it reads first as a creature's nickname:
   a diminutive or a compound of an everyday word for the thing it tangles,
   such as «Нулёк» (from «ноль», zero) or «Скидкожор» (from «скидка»,
   discount). The name never rests on a school term the heroine may not know
   yet, such as «знаменатель» (denominator) or «делимое» (dividend), and it
   never makes a joke about the maths itself.
2. Look: round, soft, sweet, with one "glitchy" feature (an extra part, a
   slipped colour, a trembling outline, swapped parts). No teeth, claws,
   blood, empty eyes, long limbs or human faces.
3. Each has one dry character detail: embarrassment, pride, forgetfulness,
   stubbornness, resentment. The Tangle takes its own oddity seriously.
4. A Tangle hurts nobody and never truly frightens anyone. The worst it can
   do is mix up the way, hide a thing or make the world awkward. Eerie-cute
   Tangles seem strange at first, but their mystery always turns out kind
   and resolves in the same scene; they appear only at creepiness level 1
   and above (CAN-0130).
5. After untangling, a Tangle is always fine. It unties, sighs with relief
   and returns to the pattern. Tangles never vanish for ever, never die and
   never suffer.
6. A Tangle can become a recurring character: if the heroine made friends
   with it, the Master can bring it back later.
7. The first meeting with any Tangle, one from the tables above or a new
   one, gives the heroine the right to name it for the Diary. A name in the
   tables is a placeholder: the System offers it among its suggestions, and
   from then on the Master uses the name she chose.

## Open questions

- Resolved on 2026-09-26: the twenty names stay, and rule 1 now allows a
  name built on an everyday word for what the Tangle tangles, while it still
  forbids names resting on school terms and jokes about the maths. The draft
  rule forbade «математических терминов в виде каламбура» (mathematical terms
  as a pun) outright. RES-1500 holds the reason.
- Should rule 4 say «выше» (above)? It refers to the eerie-cute Tangles as
  coming «ниже» (below), but their table stands before the rules.
- Resolved on 2026-09-26: the canon says in words what decides a room's
  branch, how cleanly its spells went with an almost counting for half, and
  leaves the share itself, now a starting value of 0.6, to the game rules,
  so a calibrated value never has to change the canon. RES-1700 holds the
  reason.
