# Canon

The canon records hold the world of Tower Chronicles: its places, characters,
creatures, items, campaign and the rules the Master follows when it invents
more. They are a project-local kind, kept outside `project/` because
`meow-flow` (its command is `paw`) knows only its own kinds and rejects any other inside the
record.

Each record is one chapter of the world, in a file named
`CAN-NNNN-<slug>.md`, with this front matter:

```yaml
id: CAN-NNNN
artifact: canon
status: draft # draft, then approved; changed later by a new record
revised: YYYY-MM-DD
```

The canon is the source of truth for every name and fact of the world. Where
it contradicts a requirement or a decision on method, time, rewards or
safety, the requirement or decision wins and the canon is corrected, because
the research in `project/research/RES-0010-goal-scope-principles.md` records
that precedence.

Proper names and in-game lines stay in Russian in «», with an English gloss
the first time, because the game's text is Russian. The player renames the
familiars and some places in the game; the names here are placeholders until
she does.
