# The nine floors

## Summary

This record holds the Tower's nine floors, from the bottom up, each with its
thread and element, biome, mood, trials, inhabitants, NPCs, Guardian,
secrets and five adventure seeds. The floors keep a fixed order, but what is
inside them changes every night (CAN-0030). Eight floors carry one of the
four threads and one of the eight elements (CAN-0060); the Observatory stands
outside them and holds natural science. The Tangles that live on each floor
are listed in CAN-0050, the palettes' exact hex values are in CAN-0120, and
the Guardians' yearly arcs run through the campaign in CAN-0080.

## How floors and Guardians work

The floors run from the bottom up in a fixed order. Each floor is a world of
its own, with its own weather, inhabitants and Guardian. The Guardians aren't
villains but stubborn keepers of their floor, each with a weakness and a
running joke. A Guardian is never "defeated"; it agrees. Once the heroine
untangles the day's main knot, the Guardian lets her through, each time in
its own style. Real recognition comes slowly, along the arc of the year. A
Guardian doesn't appear on every floor every day. Over the year every
Guardian changes, and the heroine sees it happen. Each Guardian's arc runs
autumn, winter, spring, summer. The Guardians' names are fixed, not
placeholders: each introduces itself by name in its arc, so the player
never renames a Guardian.

The palettes are given in words here; CAN-0120 holds the exact hex values.

## Floor 1. The Rustling Archive

Thread Count, element Spark: numbers, place value, comparison, order.

- Biome and look: an endless library whose shelves rise like gorges. The
  books are alive: they fly in flocks, rustle their pages and settle on the
  shelves by height. Every bookcase has little houses, one storey per digit,
  and a digit that moves into the wrong house changes the whole bookcase.
  The library ladders ride about by themselves. Palette: cream, honey, warm
  amber, with accents of raspberry book spines.
- Mood: cosy and a little mysterious, like a library after closing. Silence
  with a rustle.
- Trials: put runaway books back in order; settle digits into the right
  houses so the bookcase stops swaying; find the thickest or thinnest book in
  a flock; open the catalogue safe by comparing numbers; round a catalogue
  entry a Tangle smudged.
- Inhabitants: book-sparrows, dust bunnies made of dust (they sneeze when
  stroked), bibliographer snails.
- NPCs:
  - «Тишина» (Hush), a small librarian mouse in huge slippers, who can hiss
    «тсс» (shh) so that even the Tangles go quiet.
  - «Сэр Переплёт» (Sir Binding), an old untitled knight-book looking for its
    cover, and very shy.
  - «Каталожные близнецы Дюжин и Дюжиня» (the catalogue twins Dozin and
    Dozina), two drawers who always argue about which of them comes first
    alphabetically.
- Guardian, «Шкаф Аркадий» (Arkady the Cabinet): a huge wooden card-index
  cabinet on four curved legs, with a hundred small drawers for a face; two
  round brass eyes blink in the top drawer. Pedantic and polite, he speaks
  slowly, as if taking each word out of a drawer.
  - Running joke: at every meeting he files the heroine under a new letter:
    «Охотница. Раздел: „Внезапно появляющиеся". Нет, „Люди с узелками".
    Нет…» (Hunter. Section: "Suddenly appearing". No, "People with
    familiars". No...)
  - Arc: in autumn he thinks the heroine is a catalogue error. In winter he
    gives her a drawer of her own. In spring he gives her the key to the
    drawer holding the Keeper's old cards. In summer he admits some things
    can't be sorted onto shelves and calls this the best discovery of his
    life.
- Secrets: behind the shelf of untitled books is the Keeper's reading room; a
  catalogue card carries the Keeper's handwriting and the signature «И.»
  (I.); one book always stands upside down, and turning it over makes the
  bookcases a door for a minute.
- Adventures:
  1. A flock of book-sparrows flew out of the Archive because a Tangle mixed
     up the order of their nests.
  2. Sir Binding is sure he has found his cover, but it belongs to a spell
     book that won't give it up.
  3. Hush has lost her «тсс», and the Archive has become terribly noisy.
  4. The catalogue twins quarrelled so badly that they split the Archive in
     two with a ribbon, and the heroine has to cross both "countries".
  5. A new bookcase that isn't in the catalogue has appeared in the Archive,
     and for the first time in his life Arkady doesn't know what to do.

## Floor 2. The Ticking Teapot Works

Thread Count, element Stride: addition, subtraction, multiplication,
division, order of operations.

- Biome and look: a copper workshop where everything moves: conveyor belts,
  cogs the size of a house, spring bridges, and pipes puffing steam that
  smells of tea. Every machine runs on Stride: if someone mixed up the order
  in a chain of actions, the teapot pours tea upwards. Palette: copper,
  peach, orange, mint pipes, cream steam.
- Mood: brisk, noisy, hard-working. Like a kitchen before a party.
- Trials: start a machine by setting the levers in the right sequence;
  gather the right number of parts on a conveyor; share bolts evenly among
  boxes; work out how much steam the handcar needs to reach the warehouse;
  mend a clock that counts backwards.
- Inhabitants: clockwork porter beetles, teapots on legs, little steam clouds
  that take offence if you blow on them.
- NPCs:
  - «Мастер Пружинка» (Master Springlet), a raccoon mechanic in goggles who
    does everything quickly and a bit crooked, but cheerfully.
  - «Кружка Номер Никто» (Mug Number Nobody), a teacup who dreams of becoming
    a teapot and practises puffing.
  - «Инспектор Болт» (Inspector Bolt), a strict spanner in a peaked cap who
    checks that every machine is boring.
- Guardian, «Бригадир Шпиндель» (Foreman Spindle): a big copper
  samovar-robot on little caterpillar tracks, with a steam moustache and a
  «Бригадир» (Foreman) plate on his chest. A good-natured workaholic, proud
  that the Works hasn't stopped for many years.
  - Running joke: he calls a tea break at the worst moments, including the
    middle of his own trial: «Стоп. Перерыв. Бой продолжится после печенья.»
    (Stop. Break. The fight resumes after biscuits.)
  - Arc: in autumn he thinks breaks are weakness but calls them by the rules.
    In winter he drinks tea himself for the first time and is surprised. In
    spring he teaches the heroine's familiars to take proper breaks. In
    summer he invents the «Праздник Остановки» (Stopping Day), when the whole
    Works rests for one day.
- Secrets: the boiler room holds a cog in a knitted cover (someone cared for
  it a great deal); the clock on the Works' main tower shows not the time
  but how many days the Tower has slept; behind the boiler stands the
  Keeper's old desk with copper knitting needles.
- Adventures:
  1. The conveyor carries nothing but clockwork beetles instead of parts, and
     they are very pleased.
  2. Inspector Bolt wants to ban the Mug from puffing because it is
     «нестандартно» (non-standard).
  3. The Works' main clock has started running backwards, and everything on
     the floor happens in reverse: first the sound of a breaking cup, then
     the cup.
  4. Master Springlet has built a handcar that goes only where it is asked
     politely.
  5. Foreman Spindle disappeared in the middle of the working day, and the
     Works stopped without him for the first time.

## Floor 3. The Cloud Share Confectionery

Thread Share, element Crumb: fractions, parts of a whole, comparing and
adding parts.

- Biome and look: a huge confectionery on meringue clouds. Pies the size of
  a carousel, rivers of syrup, wafer staircases, ovens like plump kind
  dragons (they are just ovens). Everything here divides: cakes, pies,
  clouds, even the sunbeams from the window are cut into slices. Palette:
  strawberry, vanilla, pistachio, lavender cream.
- Mood: sweet, cosy, a little hectic, like a kitchen before guests arrive.
- Trials: cut a pie so the guests get equal shares; work out which piece is
  bigger when a Tangle cut them differently; put a whole cake back together
  from scattered parts; measure out part of a recipe for a small oven;
  reconcile two cooks arguing over whose half is bigger.
- Inhabitants: cupcake hedgehogs with cream spines, sugar bees that make icing
  instead of honey, whipped cloudlets.
- NPCs:
  - «Шеф Ванилла» (Chef Vanilla), a tall crane cook in a chef's hat who only
    whispers, so the sponge doesn't fall.
  - «Поварята Пополам» (the Half-and-Half kitchen hands), two little raccoons
    who do everything exactly halfway: half wash the dishes, half say hello.
  - «Тётушка Изюм» (Auntie Raisin), a granny bun who hands out advice, all of
    it about raisins.
- Guardian, «Мадам Корж» (Madame Sponge): a huge plump she-bear like a
  three-tier cake, in layers of pink, cream and chocolate, with a cherry hat
  on her head. Noble, dreamy, a little dramatic.
  - Running joke: she can't cut any of her cakes because they are too
    beautiful. She has a queue of uncut cakes that have waited for years and
    become local landmarks.
  - Arc: in autumn she lets nobody near the cakes. In winter she lets the
    heroine cut one tiny piece and weeps at its beauty. In spring she learns
    to divide and opens the «Праздник Первого Куска» (First Slice Festival).
    In summer she bakes a cake for the whole Tower and cuts it without
    hesitating.
- Secrets: the oldest oven holds a baking tray with the Tower's pattern baked
  into it; one door opens only if you knock on it with a spoon; there is a
  cat's paw print in the flour on a shelf.
- Adventures:
  1. A Tangle stole all the knives and replaced them with ribbons, so pies
     don't divide any more; they tie into bows.
  2. The Half-and-Half kitchen hands are preparing a party but have done only
     half of it.
  3. A meringue cloud floated off the floor with guests still on it.
  4. Auntie Raisin has gone missing, and it turns out nobody can bake a
     single bun without her raisin advice.
  5. Madame Sponge has decided someone miscounted her queue of cakes and one
     cake is extra.

## Floor 4. The Canal of Drops

Thread Share, element Drop: decimals, the decimal comma, rounding.

- Biome and look: an evening town on water: canals, locks, humpbacked
  bridges, and lanterns where drops of light live. The water flows in steps,
  and each step is an exact fraction of a drop. If the comma in a street's
  name shifts, the street becomes much longer. Palette: turquoise, sky blue,
  silver, lemon lanterns.
- Mood: calm and cosy, like an evening walk by the water: splashing,
  lanterns, creaking boats.
- Trials: set the water in a lock to the right mark; pay for a crossing with
  small drop-coins; choose which boat carries its cargo more precisely; put
  the comma back on a sign that made the bakery enormous; round a route so
  the boatman understands where to go.
- Inhabitants: lantern fish, boats with character (some grumble, others
  sing), postman snails that carry letters over the water.
- NPCs:
  - «Лодочница Грета Весло» (Greta Oar the boatwoman), a beaver in a sailor's
    cap who knows every canal and never hurries.
  - «Мелочь» (Small Change), a tiny drop-boy who wants to be a whole puddle
    when he grows up.
  - «Смотритель Маяка Мигальщик» (Blinker, Keeper of the Lighthouse), an old
    lantern-stork on one leg who blinks messages nobody understands.
- Guardian, «Шлюзмейстер Кап» (Lockmaster Drip): a big important toad in a
  yellow raincoat, with a gold chain holding the keys to every lock and a
  monocle made of a drop. Precise, unhurried, very polite.
  - Running joke: he always wants a little more precision; he measures the
    water level so many times that a rain shower starts and finishes in the
    meantime.
  - Arc: in autumn he won't open a lock until everything is perfect. In
    winter, when the canal freezes, he first understands that the precision
    of ice is something else. In spring he teaches the heroine "precise
    enough". In summer he gives her the spare key with the words «Я доверяю
    вашей запятой» (I trust your comma).
- Secrets: a silver thimble lies at the bottom of the oldest lock; Blinker's
  lantern blinks in a cipher (CAN-0090 holds the ciphers); one of the boats
  remembers the Keeper and carries only those who know the Keeper.
- Adventures:
  1. Someone moved the comma on the Canal's sign, and now the whole floor
     thinks it is the sea.
  2. Small Change has decided to become a puddle right now and is gathering
     every drop on the floor.
  3. At night the locks opened by themselves, and the boats sailed away into
     an unknown canal.
  4. Blinker at last wants to be understood and asks for his messages to be
     deciphered.
  5. Lockmaster Drip has lost his monocle and refuses to measure anything
     «на глаз» (by eye).

## Floor 5. The Scales Fair

Thread Share, element Harmony: percentages, ratios, proportions, discounts,
exchange.

- Biome and look: a noisy fair under striped awnings, where everything is for
  sale and everything is exchanged: buttons for songs, clouds for ribbons,
  three chestnuts for one riddle. The Great Scales stand in the middle of the
  square, and if trading is unfair they tip with a crash and the whole market
  slides sideways. Palette: mint, lemon, coral, awning stripes.
- Mood: merry, bustling, a little crooked (but without malice).
- Trials: trade goods at a profit; work out which discount is really bigger;
  mix paint or a potion in the right ratio; level the Great Scales; split the
  takings fairly among the traders.
- Inhabitants: hedgehog traders with carts, barker pigeons, little sacks on
  legs that run away if nobody buys them.
- NPCs:
  - «Тётушка Лента» (Auntie Ribbon), a red fox haberdasher who sells ribbons
    of any length except the one you need.
  - «Счётчик Бобо» (Bobo the Counter), a beaver with a wooden abacus who
    counts everything, even what nobody asked for.
  - «Молчаливый Покупатель» (the Silent Customer), a figure in an enormous
    hat who buys nothing but comes to every fair. He turns out to be a kind
    elderly hippo who just likes to watch.
- Guardian, «Барон фон Наценка» (Baron von Markup): a plump hamster in a top
  hat taller than himself and a velvet waistcoat with a dozen pockets.
  Boastful, charming, sure he can sell anything.
  - Running joke: he offers "bargains" on things that aren't his: the sky,
    the heroine's boots, last Tuesday.
  - Arc: in autumn he tries to sell the heroine her own familiar. In winter
    he goes bust selling snow in winter. In spring he gives something away
    for free for the first time and can't get over it for three days. In
    summer he opens an honest shop with the sign «Всё по-честному. Это
    непривычно, но мы стараемся» (Everything honest. It's unusual, but we're
    trying).
- Secrets: under the Great Scales is a store of things the Keeper once
  traded; one weight on the Scales is knitted; the Silent Customer knew the
  Keeper personally.
- Adventures:
  1. The Great Scales broke and now tip towards whoever shouts loudest.
  2. Baron von Markup sold a cloud, and it refuses to leave with its new
     owner.
  3. A trader in oddities has come to the fair with goods that change while
     you look at them.
  4. All the sacks on legs ran away on the same day, and the fair has nothing
     to trade.
  5. Auntie Ribbon has at last sold a ribbon of the right length, and nobody
     can work out how she did it.

## Floor 6. The Measuring Dunes

Thread Measure, element Measure: length, mass, volume, time, units of
measurement.

- Biome and look: a warm desert of pink and gold sand. Huge hourglasses grow
  instead of cacti, rulers stand instead of fences, and measuring jugs serve
  as wells. The dunes crawl from place to place, so the distance between them
  always needs measuring again. The caravan travels by the stars and by tape
  measure. Palette: apricot, sand gold, pink sunset, turquoise oases.
- Mood: adventurous, spacious, a little fairy-tale.
- Trials: measure out water for an oasis; weigh the caravan's load so the
  camel isn't offended; find out whether the caravan reaches camp before
  sunset; convert the measures on the Keeper's old map into local ones; build
  a bridge of the right length over a crack.
- Inhabitants: cushion camels, tape-measure sand foxes, lizards that bask on
  a timetable.
- NPCs:
  - «Караванщица Самум» (Simoom the caravan-leader), an elderly tortoise in a
    turban who drives the caravan slowly but always on time.
  - «Мальчик-Бархан» (Dune Boy), a small sand spirit who follows travellers
    and pretends to be an ordinary heap of sand.
  - «Часовщица Песчинка» (Grain the hourglass-keeper), a snail-like keeper of
    the hourglasses who knows how much sand is in each but won't say.
- Guardian, «Великий Эталон» (the Great Standard): a huge silvery weight with
  a crown, a bow for a handle and little legs. Very serious and solemn, he
  speaks slowly, as if everything around him has to measure up to him.
  - Running joke: he thinks he is the standard of everything, including the
    right amount of politeness, the proper length of a greeting and the
    normal size of a hat.
  - Arc: in autumn he demands the heroine be compared with him (and doesn't
    know what to say). In winter he looks for something to compare himself
    with and finds a snowman. In spring he agrees everyone has their own
    measure. In summer the heroine gives him the medal «Эталон дружелюбия»
    (Standard of Friendliness), and for the first time he weeps sand.
- Secrets: the Keeper's old knitted tent stands in the oasis; one hourglass
  runs upwards; Dune Boy has a map he brought «оттуда, где всё наоборот»
  (from where everything is the other way round).
- Adventures:
  1. The dunes scattered, and Simoom's caravan got lost in the middle of the
     desert.
  2. Every hourglass stopped, and the day can't end.
  3. Dune Boy asks for help proving he is not just a heap of sand.
  4. The Great Standard found out he is «немного не эталон» (a bit off
     standard) and fell into gloom.
  5. A huge footprint turned up on the Dunes, from which you can work out how
     big the creature that passed was.

## Floor 7. The Garden of Facets

Thread Facet, element Facet: shapes, angles, symmetry, perimeter and area,
solids.

- Biome and look: a crystal garden where bushes are clipped into perfect
  shapes: spheres, cubes, spirals, stars. The paths are laid with tiles that
  fit together into patterns. Fountains reflect the garden symmetrically,
  and mirror ponds show it from the underside. Palette: lilac, lavender,
  glassy mint, rose quartz.
- Mood: graceful, quiet, a little mysterious, like a garden in a dream.
- Trials: complete a reflection in a mirror pond; lay path tiles with no
  gaps; fence a flowerbed with a fence of the right length; match each
  shadow to its shape; build a crystal from its net; straighten the angle of
  a gazebo.
- Inhabitants: crystal butterflies, cube rabbits, sphere hedgehogs, glass
  garden gnomes (not little people, just glass figures on legs).
- NPCs:
  - «Садовник Угол» (Angle the gardener), an old mantis in a straw hat who
    clips the bushes with his eyes shut.
  - «Близняшки Отражения» (the Reflection twins), two frogs, one real and one
    from the pond; nobody knows which is which, and they like it that way.
  - «Плиточник Мозаик» (Mosaic the tiler), a tortoise with a patterned shell
    who keeps rearranging it.
- Guardian, «Графиня Циркуль» (Countess Compass): a graceful long-legged
  heron with lilac feathers; one leg is thin as a needle and the other ends
  in a pencil lead. Aristocratic and dreamy, she speaks smoothly and
  ornately.
  - Running joke: she walks only in perfect circles, so she is late
    absolutely everywhere and arrives last at her own receptions.
  - Arc: in autumn she meets the heroine only a day after the appointed day.
    In winter she tries to walk in a straight line and falls into a
    snowdrift. In spring she learns to walk in a spiral, which is a little
    faster. In summer she arrives on time at the heroine's celebration and
    can't believe it herself.
- Secrets: one of the ponds is a door to the Underside (CAN-0080 and
  CAN-0130 set out the Underside); a bush shaped like a ball of yarn grows at
  the centre of the maze; the tiles of the main avenue form a map of the
  whole Tower.
- Adventures:
  1. A mirror pond has started reflecting things that aren't there: in the
     reflection the heroine wears a different cloak.
  2. The shaped bushes swapped places, and the garden turned into a maze.
  3. Countess Compass invited the heroine to a ball, but the ball starts
     only when the Countess gets there.
  4. Mosaic the tiler lost one tile, and the whole garden went askew because
     of it.
  5. The shadow of one bush went for a walk without the bush.

## Floor 8. The Whisper Sorting Office

Thread Count, element Echo: data, tables, charts, patterns, probability.

- Biome and look: a huge post office under the Tower's roof. Every whisper,
  rumour, opinion and question in the Tower flies in here as a paper bird.
  They are sorted into boxes, counted, filed into folders and stacked into
  column-towers that grow by themselves like charts. Garlands of chart
  pennants hang under the ceiling. Palette: sky blue, paper cream, postal
  red, soft turquoise.
- Mood: businesslike, rustling, a little gossipy and very friendly.
- Trials: sort whispers into boxes and work out what is whispered about most;
  read the columns and find where the Tangle hid; continue a strange sequence
  on the telegraph tape; predict the colour of the next bird out of the sack;
  piece together a torn table.
- Inhabitants: paper letter-birds, postal meerkats, rubber stamps on legs
  that stamp everything in sight.
- NPCs:
  - «Почтмейстерша Сургуч» (Sealing Wax the postmistress), a strict but kind
    dove in glasses who reads every letter only as far as the word «дорогой»
    (dear).
  - «Слух» (Rumour), a little paper boat who knows everything about
    everyone, but all of it slightly wrong.
  - «Архивариус Галочка» (Tick the archivist), a meerkat who ticks boxes in
    surveys even when nobody asked her.
- Guardian, «Опросник» (the Questionnaire): a big fluffy cloud with glasses
  and a pen, raining forms. Curious, thorough, sincerely wants to know
  everyone's opinion.
  - Running joke: his questions are always suspiciously specific: «Как часто
    вы чувствуете, что ваш левый ботинок думает о вас?» (How often do you
    feel that your left boot is thinking about you?)
  - Arc: in autumn he buries the heroine in questionnaires. In winter he
    surveys the Tangles and learns they miss order. In spring he fills in a
    questionnaire himself for the first time and is embarrassed. In summer
    he holds the «Большой Опрос Башни» (Great Tower Survey), which helps the
    heroine in the finale.
- Secrets: the farthest box holds the Keeper's letters, never sent; paper
  birds sometimes bring whispers from town (from Lotta or Irma, CAN-0020); a
  cipher slips through on the telegraph tape.
- Adventures:
  1. The whispers broke out of their boxes, and suddenly the whole Tower
     spoke in other people's voices.
  2. Rumour spread a rumour about himself and now can't sort out which part
     of it is true.
  3. The Questionnaire wants to find out which familiar is the Tower's
     favourite, and a scandal is brewing.
  4. A letter addressed to the heroine arrived at the Sorting Office, with a
     postmark from the past.
  5. The chart columns grew up to the ceiling and keep growing.

## Floor 9. The Observatory

Outside the threads and elements; natural science: forces and motion,
substances and weather, electricity and magnets, living things, the Earth
and the sky.

- Biome and look: a round dome at the top of the Tower with a huge
  telescope, a greenhouse, a weather station and shelves of jars where small
  weathers live: a little fog, a little downpour, a rainbow in a jar. All
  Misty Ford and the forest are visible from here. At night the dome opens,
  and the stars fly closer so they are easy to look at. Palette: deep plum
  evening, gold stars, mint greenhouse leaves, peach dawn.
- Mood: quiet, solemn, cosy. A place where you want to think and ask
  questions.
- Trials: find out why a greenhouse plant leans to one side; forecast the
  town's weather from the instruments; work out why the compass points at
  the cat; let the right weather out of a jar; work out which animal left
  tracks in the snow by the dome.
- Inhabitants: star moths, living greenhouse plants (polite but slow),
  weather creatures from the jars.
- NPCs:
  - «Профессор Барометр» (Professor Barometer), an old tortoise with a
    barometer for a shell who forecasts not the weather but moods.
  - «Росток» (Sprout), a curious flower in a pot on wheels who asks "why"
    questions and rolls after the heroine.
  - «Ночной Смотритель Лунник» (Moonling the Night Keeper), a quiet moon hare
    who lights the stars in the evening.
- Guardian: the Observatory has none. The Keeper's empty armchair guards it.
