# The Tower

## Summary

This record holds what the Tower is, the laws of the world's fabric, why the
floors change every night, who the System is and how it speaks, and how
guiding threads and the knot scheme work. It is the physics every other
chapter obeys: a spell returns a thread to its place, almost returns it or
loosens the knot, and none of the three is a failure. The floors the Tower rebuilds are set out in
CAN-0040, the Tangles its knots become in CAN-0050, and the eight elements
and the familiars who explain a knot in CAN-0060. Dreamcore variants and the
Underside follow CAN-0130, and the palettes follow CAN-0120.

## What the Tower is

The Tower is knitted, not built. Its walls are a dense fabric of glowing
threads, its floors are rows of loops, and its staircases are long plaits.
From outside it looks like a tall knitted candle the colour of cream caramel,
with small windows lit in many colours. Inside it is much bigger than
outside, and nobody inside minds.

## Laws of the world: the Warp

The whole world is woven from an invisible fabric called «Основа» (the Warp).
The Warp has four main threads:

- «Счёт» (Count) is how many of a thing there are. It holds quantity: how
  many steps a staircase has, how many apples a tree has, how many windows a
  house has.
- «Мера» (Measure) is how big, long, heavy and long-lasting everything is. It
  holds size and time.
- «Доля» (Share) is how a whole splits into parts and how the parts go back
  together. It holds dividing, exchange and balance.
- «Грань» (Facet) is what shape things are. It holds angles, sides, symmetry
  and how things stand in space.

There are four threads and eight elements of magic, because each element is
its own way of working one thread (CAN-0060). So every floor has a thread and
an element.

When the threads are in order, the world works: bridges reach the far bank,
pies divide evenly and clocks go forwards. When a thread tangles, a knot
forms, and the knot comes alive as a Tangle. Attention untangles a knot: the
hunter counts, measures, divides or builds whatever the knot mixed up.

A correct spell returns the thread to its place and untangles the knot
cleanly. There is a bright click, as if a knitting needle caught a loop, the
thread lies flat, and the world around grows brighter. Several clean spells
in a row make a clean row: the loops lie one against the next, and star yarn
gathers from them by itself.

An imprecise spell, one that is nearly right, almost untangles the knot. The
knot comes undone, but one little loop sticks out, and the loop is pleased
with itself. The thread is almost in place, and the way goes on as usual.

A wrong spell or an honest «не знаю» (I don't know) loosens the knot.
The thread glows softly and slackens, the Tangle calms down, and the Tower
shows another way: a side path, a small door or a staircase. This is neither
a defeat nor a mistake in the world's sense. The Tower always has more than
one way, and the other way also leads forward; it just meets different
things. Nothing breaks and nothing is lost: whatever didn't open today, the
Tower knits into another place, and it turns up later. So numbers in this
world are not a school subject but the magic that holds everything up. After
every spell there is a quiet sound, as if someone smoothed out a cloth.

The Tower is a huge spindle wound with the Warp of all Misty Ford and the
forest. While the Tower is in order, so is the town.

## Why the floors change every night

The Tower is alive and knits itself. Every night, while everyone sleeps, it
unravels several rows and knits them again. So:

- the rooms on each floor stand differently every day, and corridors lead to
  new places;
- where yesterday's hall stood there can be a garden, a lake or somebody's
  kitchen;
- new knots appear, and so new Tangles;
- the order of the floors themselves never changes: the Count floor is always
  first and the Observatory is always at the top. What changes is what is
  inside and how the plaited staircases join the floors, so the day's path
  can pass through the floors in any order. Every floor is open from the
  first day;
- sometimes one floor re-knits "in its sleep" and for one day becomes its
  dreamcore variant: pastel, quiet and a little strange (CAN-0130). The
  trials there are the same; the light, the sounds and whom you can meet
  change.

In the morning the System writes what changed overnight. Every session is a
new adventure, because the Tower has literally become different.

The re-knitting never touches the heroine's room, nor anything she forged,
received or named.

## The System

### Who it is

The System is the voice of the pattern the Tower was knitted from. Once it
was only instructions: «ряд первый, набрать петли, ряд второй, провязать»
(row one, cast on; row two, knit). Over the years the pattern learned to
speak but stayed an instruction. It reports, describes, hands out tasks and
keeps count. It never jokes on purpose, and that is why it is funny.

### How it looks

System windows are translucent panes like boiled-sweet slabs, with a soft
glow and rounded corners. A window appears in front of the heroine with a
quiet sound, like a knitting needle clicking.

The System announces a knot in its window, but the knot itself never opens
there. The knot opens on a plain, still page of solid colour: no glow, no
lace, nothing that moves and no story, only the knot, the place for the
spell, «Не знаю», the guiding thread and «Готово» (Done). While the hunter
counts, the world keeps quiet around her. When the spell is cast, the page
folds away and the System speaks again.

### How it speaks

- Briefly, formally, in the present tense.
- It never uses an exclamation mark.
- Sometimes it lists items like a knitting pattern: «Ряд 1. Ряд 2.» (Row 1.
  Row 2.) The code fills row numbers and every counter (experience, points,
  level) into separate fields of the window. The System itself writes no
  digits and no number words in its text.
- Sometimes it corrects itself after a pause.
- It treats the heroine with dry respect that slowly turns into quiet
  affection. It never grades or reproaches her.
- It reports what happened to the knot: untangled cleanly, almost, or
  loosened. Before a spell it never names the answer and never hints, except
  for the hint step paid for with a guiding thread. After a spell it
  announces the knot scheme, how the thread really lay; the code fills in the
  steps, numbers and answer (see the guiding threads section below).
- It never says a word from the forbidden list in CAN-0010, such as
  «неправильно», «ошибка», «промах», «мимо», «провал», «жаль» or «не
  получилось» (wrong, mistake, miss, off, failure, pity, didn't work),
  because the world changes, not the verdict on the heroine.
- It doesn't praise the heroine's mind with «умница» or «гений» (clever girl,
  genius). It notes events: the thread returned, the row came together, the
  path opened.

## Guiding threads and the knot scheme

### The guiding thread

A guiding thread is a thin silvery thread the Keepers once used to mark the
way through the Tower: tie it at the entrance to a knot, and it leads you
through loop by loop. The Tower winds guiding threads itself. Every night,
while it re-knits its rows, a few fresh threads wind into the side pocket of
the heroine's rucksack. In the morning the System reports this in one line,
and the code fills in how many. More threads turn up in caches and come from
clean rows. The System gives them for the rows of the daily task, and
familiars bring them when they hatch and grow.

### What a thread does

- Before a spell, the thread draws taut and shows the next loop: where to
  start, then the first step, then everything except the last. The spell
  becomes a spell "with a thread". The System notes this as a plain fact
  without judgement: a thread was used.
- After a spell, the familiar takes the thread and goes through the whole
  knot in its own voice. In the world only the familiar speaks here; the
  text is written outside the story, from the steps the code computed
  (CAN-0060 sets out how familiars explain).

### The knot scheme

After every spell the System shows how the thread should lie: the heroine's
answer beside the right one. What follows depends on the outcome:

- If the knot is untangled cleanly, the heroine can open the knot scheme,
  the steps and the answer, if she wants.
- If the knot is almost untangled or loosened, the System shows the knot
  scheme at once.

The scheme is free and never hides behind a thread. The code fills in the
scheme's steps, numbers and answer; the System only announces it.

### The twin knot

If a knot loosens, a similar knot ties itself nearby, and the heroine tries
again. An almost untangled knot gets no twin, because the scheme already
shows the one loop that stuck out. The twin has no effect on the story: the room still ends on its own
branch, and the outcome counts from the first spell. If she untangles the
twin, the thread is "secured". If not, the scheme is saved in the Diary, and
the Tower brings such a knot back another day.

### Rules of the world

- The Tower is generous: it winds more threads in a day than a day usually
  needs, so nobody needs to save them. If the heroine reaches for a thread
  and the pocket is empty, one thread turns up there by itself, once in each
  room. The guiding thread is always within reach: its place on the page
  never disappears, even when the pocket is empty until the next room.
  The pocket holds only so many threads, and the Tower quietly turns spare
  threads into buttons. The knot scheme needs no thread, so an empty pocket
  never leaves her without the way.
- Threads are not sold or bought, and they can't be won or lost.
- Using a thread is no shame, because every Keeper did it. The Diary says:
  «Нить — не костыль. Нить — дорога. Я ею пользовалась. Часто.» (A thread is
  not a crutch. A thread is a road. I used it. Often.)
- Nobody in the world speaks of a thread as a mistake, a hint "for the weak"
  or a lesson. A thread is as much a part of the Warp as star yarn is.
- The code fills in every number in hints, schemes and explanations. The
  Master doesn't see threads and doesn't narrate them.
