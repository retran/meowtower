# Meowtower

## What it is

Meowtower («Мяубашня») is a daily adventure game for one player, a
primary-school girl, and a long-running log of how she does maths. (from
RES-0010, high; RES-4200, high; the player from CLAUDE.md, high) Its goal is
to take her maths results apart into their components and find where her
independent thinking really stops, not to prove that she knows maths well.
(from RES-4200, high) The game is the way there: every adventure adds to the
log, and the parent reads what the log shows. (from RES-0010, high; RES-4200,
high)

The game measures and consolidates; the parent teaches new topics in lessons
outside the game, marks a topic after a lesson, and the game checks it again.
(from RES-0010, high; RES-1400, medium)

## The problem

School results blur four states the parent needs told apart: she doesn't know
it; she knows it but not fluently; she knows it with a little help; she
understands it beyond the usual school format. (from RES-4200, high) The
system tells these apart and says how sure it is. (from RES-4200, high)

She meets maths in Dutch at school, so a gap can lie in the maths or in the
access to the maths through language and format. (from RES-0800, high;
RES-4250, high) The records set out to show either one as plainly as the
other. (from RES-4200, high) The target is the VWO track, so the map covers
the Dutch levels 1F and 1S, a margin above them, and the Cito M7 and E7 tests
the parent sets dates for. (from RES-0010, high; RES-4080, high)

## Quality goals

1. Safety before story: real-life alarm signals take the Master out of role
   and call the parent. (from RES-0010, high)
2. A fair measurement: only her unassisted first attempts measure, every new
   form writes its own stream until a refit shows it helps, and time is never
   a reward. (from RES-0010, high; RES-4000, high)
3. Falsifiable reporting: the parent's report is never curated, every figure
   shows its count and an 80 % interval, a figure on too little data reads
   «мало данных» (too little data), and every reading is worded as something
   to check, not a diagnosis. (from RES-4200, high)
4. The player's joy and ownership of her story. (from RES-0010, high;
   RES-4120, high)
5. Everything checkable automatically, because an AI agent builds the game
   unattended, including a check that synthetic players with a maths gap and
   with a language gap are each shown as what they are. (from RES-0010, high;
   RES-4200, high; RES-4280, high)

## What it will not do

- Grade the player: no words «неправильно» ("wrong"), «ошибка» ("mistake")
  or «промах» ("miss"), no dead ends and nothing taken away. (from RES-0010,
  high)
- Show clocks: no timers, countdowns or times of day on her screens, and no
  screen opens or closes by the hour. (from RES-0010, high; RES-4000, high)
- Sum her up: the ability profile keeps its eight dimensions apart, adds them
  into no score, ranks nothing and is never shown to her. (from RES-4210,
  high)
- Teach new topics: the parent does that outside the game. (from RES-0010,
  high)
- Give a school assessment or drill for the test: the Cito preparation builds
  the tested skills and never copies test items. (from RES-0010, high;
  RES-4080, high)
- Let school data into the knowledge model: school and Cito results stay an
  outside check beside the home map. (from RES-4100, high; RES-4240, high)
- Sell anything or run lotteries. (from RES-0010, high)
- Let a language model compute numbers or answers. (from RES-0010, high)
- Send her answers or results out of the home: the server stays on the family
  Mac, and only masked, checked text leaves it for the few model calls the
  records allow. (from RES-0010, high; RES-2600, medium; RES-4000, high)

## Risks

- The iPad is the main technical risk, so a short spike on a real iPad comes
  first. (from RES-3000, high)
- Rewards can tempt her to guess, which corrupts measurement. (from RES-0010,
  high)
- One player gives little data: retention, transfer and the Dutch probe read
  «мало данных» for months, and some readings may never reach a firm result.
  (from RES-4210, high; RES-4220, high; RES-4230, high)
- The computed label on the parent's hypotheses misfires too often at every
  hold simulated so far, so it stays off until a later record finds a
  calibrated rule. (from RES-4280, high)
- Dutch text beyond the bridge's keywords needs the owner to widen the
  Russian-only rule for the player's text first. (from RES-4250, high;
  CLAUDE.md, high)

## Where it is going

The MVP holds the adventure of the day with its daily quests and rewards, the
hint ladder and the new answer forms of addendum 1, the Keeper's puzzles, the
Sources track, preparation toward Cito, silent play, the parent's sandbox, and
the parts of addendum 2 that shape the log from the first day: the parent's
written hypotheses, context tags on story frames and the hold on first
encounters. (from RES-4000, high; RES-4200, high) After the MVP come the
ability profile, the skill trajectory with confirmed retention, transfer,
the home-and-school quadrants, riddles for fractions, percentages and ratios,
and the Dutch diagnostic probe, one backlog item at a time, chosen by
watching her play. (from RES-3000, high; RES-4200, high) The game is built so
English and Dutch can be added and she can switch language. (from CLAUDE.md,
high)
