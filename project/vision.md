# Tower Chronicles

## What it is

Tower Chronicles («Хроники Башни») is a daily adventure game for one player,
a primary-school girl, that measures and consolidates her maths
and science while she plays. (from RES-0010, high; the player from CLAUDE.md, high)
Each adventure of the day refines a map of her knowledge, and the parent sees
the fresh map after every adventure: what she holds firmly, where the
frontier runs and what stops her going further. (from RES-0010, high)

The game measures and helps consolidate; the parent teaches new topics in
lessons outside the game. (from RES-0010, high) The parent marks a topic after
a lesson, and the game checks it again, so lessons are planned from the map
rather than blind. (from RES-0010, medium; RES-1400, medium) The records name
no existing product that was tried and found lacking; see the gaps in
`project/onboarding.md`.

## The problem

The game answers one question: what she already holds firmly, where the
frontier of her knowledge runs and what stops her from going further. (from
RES-0010, high) The target is the VWO track towards university, so the
diagnostics check full coverage of Dutch levels 1F and 1S, a margin above
them and how far above 1S she can already go. (from RES-0010, high) She meets
maths terms in Dutch at school, so a Russian term she doesn't know can look
like a gap in maths. (from RES-0800, high)

## Who it is for

| Audience | Wants | What they do today instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The player | An adventure she wants to return to every day, where correct answers pay off and mistakes cost nothing (from RES-0010, high) | Not stated in the records |
| The parent | A map of skills, gaps and limits after every adventure, to plan lessons from (from RES-0010, high) | Not stated in the records |

## Quality goals

1. Safety before story: real-life alarm signals take the Master out of role
   and call the parent. (from RES-0010, high)
2. Measurement accuracy and the player's joy. Every principle protects one of
   the two, and the records don't rank one above the other; see the gaps in
   `project/onboarding.md`. (from RES-0010, high)
3. Everything checkable automatically, because an AI agent builds the game
   unattended. (from RES-0010, high)

## What it will not do

- Grade the player: no words «неправильно» ("wrong"), «ошибка» ("mistake")
  or «промах» ("miss"), no dead ends and nothing taken away. (from RES-0010,
  high)
- Show clocks: no timers, countdowns, clocks or time bars on her screens.
  (from RES-0010, high)
- Teach new topics: the parent does that outside the game. (from RES-0010,
  high)
- Give a standardised school assessment: the VWO block is a home guide, not
  official school advice. (from RES-0010, high)
- Sell anything or run lotteries: chests offer one of three, and the shop
  takes buttons only. (from RES-0010, high)
- Let the AI compute numbers or answers: code computes every number. (from
  RES-0010, high)
- Send results out of the home: the server and its data stay on the family
  Mac. (from RES-0010, high; RES-2600, medium)

## Risks

- The iPad is the main technical risk, so a short spike on a real iPad comes
  first. (from RES-3000, high)
- Bonuses for correct answers give the player a motive to guess, which
  corrupts measurement. (from RES-0010, high)
- Russian maths terms she hasn't met can look like maths gaps. (from
  RES-0800, high)
- Hints before answering might become a habit that leaves few unassisted
  attempts. (from RES-3000, high)

## Where it is going

The game grows with how she plays: after the MVP, the family watches her play
and picks the next step by expected value. (from RES-3000, high) Candidates
are Ascents with comparable progress, the full familiar roster and battles,
free mode, Diary ciphers, AI-made items and a Dutch layer. (from RES-3000,
high) The owner has asked for the game to be built so English and Dutch can
be added and the player can switch language. (from CLAUDE.md, high)
