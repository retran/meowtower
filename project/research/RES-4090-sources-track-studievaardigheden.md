# The Sources track fits beside the 79-node graph as a second track with its own states, and it overlaps six graph nodes and changes seven approved decisions

## Summary

The owner's addendum of 2026-09-28 adds «Карта Смотрителя» (the Keeper's Map), a Sources track of five nodes, I1 to I5, that measures reading tables, charts, timetables, maps and two sources together. The track can live beside the skill graph without changing its 79 nodes. For that, the graph file gains a track part, as it already holds the science topics apart, and the knowledge model gives the track nodes states of their own. Cito's Studievaardigheden test does cover this skill. Two of its four parts, map reading and reading schemas, tables and graphs, are the track's content; the other two, information sources and study texts, depend on Dutch. That test, though, belongs to Cito's older LOVS system. Cito's current Leerling in beeld pages list no Studievaardigheden test, and the doorstroomtoets holds only Lezen, Rekenen and Taalverzorging. A current Cito test therefore reaches graph and table reading through Rekenen's domain Verbanden, if at all. The track overlaps six graph nodes, S1, S2, S5, M2, P5 and G7, whose content RES-0800 already maps to the same Dutch goals. It conflicts with seven approved decisions: ADR-0040, ADR-0050, ADR-0060, ADR-0070, ADR-0150, ADR-0180 and ADR-0190. It also conflicts with the approved requirements on slot sources or floor order, input classes, error classes, report screens and the MVP contents. The touch minimum of RES-3100 already meets the addendum's. I cover section 9 of the addendum and its acceptance test 15, and leave out the Dutch-word bridge, which puts Dutch words into about a fifth of the track's tasks, because section 8 of the addendum owns that bridge. The open questions are decided: a track task is a fixed part of its host floor, track attempts don't count toward the minimum of 28, source text is no smaller than the task style, the track asks only the addendum's four levels, and the report names no Studievaardigheden test.

## The question

How does the game add the Sources track without breaking the approved record? The track brings five nodes, four question levels, generated sources, four answer forms, readable sources on the iPad, seven reading error classes, about 2 tasks a day and a report section. The addendum sets the constraints: the track stays outside the 79 nodes, carries `citoBlock: null`, runs in Russian in the MVP, and takes at least one task in any 3 adventure days. It also demands that the answer come from the source and never from the question text.

The addendum assumes that reading a source is a skill apart from the maths nodes, one Cito tests on its own, so the game should measure it apart. Both halves of that need checking. Cito did test it on its own, in the LOVS Studievaardigheden test for groups 5 to 8 and in the old Eindtoets. Its current system tests reading graphs and tables inside Rekenen's domain Verbanden, which RES-0800 maps to the graph's S domain. The skill also isn't apart from the graph's content: S1 is «Чтение таблиц» (reading tables), M2 already holds timetables and G7 already holds legends, grid squares, compass points, routes and linear scales. What the track adds is a different task: a source with more data than the question needs, where the child has to find the right part before any maths starts. That difference, and no separate Cito test, is what justifies a separate measurement.

## Findings

### Cito's Studievaardigheden has four parts, and two of them depend on Dutch

Cito's justification of the Eindtoets Basisonderwijs 2010 lists the part «Studievaardigheden (totaal 40)» as «Hanteren van studieteksten (10)», «Hanteren van informatiebronnen (10)», «Lezen van schema's, tabellen en grafieken (10)» and «Kaartlezen (10)». Information sources means alphabetising and looking things up in a dictionary, an encyclopedia, a phone book or a website: «Alfabetiseren en de toepassing daarvan in allerlei contexten, zoals woordenboek, encyclopedie, telefoongids en internet». Study texts is «een aspect van het leesonderwijs» (an aspect of reading teaching), such as summarising. Both depend on Dutch, which is why the addendum defers dictionaries, indexes and tables of contents to the Dutch layer. Map reading and reading schemas, tables and graphs are the track's content. Source: Cito, Wetenschappelijke verantwoording van de Eindtoets Basisonderwijs 2010, sections 3.1 and 3.2.3, read 2026-09-28.

### Cito splits each reading part into select, identify, analyse and interpret

The same document names four sub-skills for maps and for schemas, tables and graphs: «selecteren, identificeren, analyseren en interpreteren». Selecting is choosing the right source for a question. Identifying is finding data «door gebruik te maken van de titel, symboolverklaringen, celgegevens, rij-opschriften, as-opschriften, schaalverdelingen» (by using the title, legends, cells, row labels, axis labels and scales). Analysing is finding a pattern «in de gegevens van één of meer tabellen en grafieken» (in the data of one or more tables and graphs). Interpreting explains the pattern from general knowledge of the world. Cito also names kinds the addendum doesn't: «structuurschema's, stroomdiagrammen» (structure schemas and flow charts). The addendum's levels map onto these only in part. Level 1 "find" is identifying, and level 2 "compare or select" and level 4 "combine" are analysing. Level 3 "calculate from the source" has no Cito sub-skill, because it is arithmetic. Selecting a source and interpreting from world knowledge have no level. Source: the same document, section 3.2.3, read 2026-09-28.

### The Studievaardigheden test belongs to the LOVS, and Cito's current pages don't list it

Cito's LOVS manual says «Sinds september 2011 zijn alle toetsen voor groep 5 tot en met groep 8 van Studievaardigheden beschikbaar, zowel op papier als digitaal» (since September 2011 every Studievaardigheden test for groups 5 to 8 has been available on paper and digitally). Source: Cito, Computerprogramma LOVS handleiding, version 4.14, February 2022, section 9.4.13, read 2026-09-28. Cito's Leerling in beeld page names eleven tests, from Rekenen-Wiskunde to Engels, and no Studievaardigheden. Source: cito.nl, Leerling in beeld - leerlingvolgsysteem, read 2026-09-28. The doorstroomtoets FAQ says «De doorstroomtoets bestaat uit de onderdelen Lezen, Rekenen en Taalverzorging», with Rekenen in «Getallen, Verhoudingen, Meten en meetkunde, Verbanden». Source: cito.nl, Leerling in beeld - doorstroomtoets, veelgestelde vragen, read 2026-09-28. Whether the player's school still gives a Studievaardigheden test in 2026-2027 is unconfirmed. A web search snippet from wijzeroverdebasisschool.nl says graph reading now falls under Rekenen and looking things up under Lezen; the page itself answered 403, so that claim stays unconfirmed too.

### Six graph nodes already hold the track's content

RES-0800's node tables give S1 «Чтение таблиц» (reading tables), S2 bar and line charts, S5 global graphs, M2 «Время: часы, промежутки, календарь, расписание» (time: clocks, intervals, calendar, timetable), P5 map and drawing scale, and G7 «План и карта: легенда, клетки, стороны света, маршрут, линейный масштаб» (plan and map: legend, grid squares, compass points, route, linear scale). RES-0800 maps the SLO domain Verbanden, «tables (timetables, prices); simple global graphs and bar charts», to S1 to S6 and G4. The RES-1200 catalogue already has S1 tasks on «a value in a 4x4 table; the difference of two cells; a timetable» with the trap «neighbouring row or column». Its M2 tasks ask for «an interval across the hour», with the trap «an hour = 100 minutes». So I1 to I4 cover the same content as six graph nodes, and two of the seven reading error classes, `misread_cell` and `time_across_hour`, match two traps the catalogue already has. Source: RES-0800 and RES-1200, read 2026-09-28.

### The graph file has no place for a track, and its validator counts nodes and domains

ADR-0050 fixes `content/graph.yaml` in three parts, `nodes`, `topics` and `sloGoals`. Its validator checks "79 maths nodes, nine domains", and each node carries `level` as 1F, 1S, 1F/1S or stretch and a `domain` from N, A, F, D, P, M, G, S or T. A track node has no SLO level and no domain among the nine. So a track node put under `nodes` fails the count and the schema. The science topics E1 to E5 are the precedent: they sit in `topics`, a part with no prerequisite field, outside the count. Source: ADR-0050, read 2026-09-28.

### The knowledge model has no rows for track nodes, and the I5 gate needs states

The addendum opens I5 once at least two of I1 to I4 reach «понимает» (understands). ADR-0060 keeps "a row for every node of the graph up to the end of group 8, level 1S, and for every stretch node" and computes states only for them. The science topics have no state at all, and ADR-0180's science screen shows "no score, state or percentage". So the gate asks for states nothing computes. The addendum's general rule says each new form writes its own stream and stays out of the «сама» (on her own) estimate of the graph until an offline refit shows it improves prediction. Track attempts therefore update track nodes only. Source: ADR-0060 and ADR-0180; addendum, general rules; read 2026-09-28.

### The Director has three slot sources, a fixed floor order and no window for a track

ADR-0070 fills every room slot from the frontier, review or a parent topic. The approved floor order runs entry scene, warm-up, 2 mental arithmetic tasks, 1 or 2 rooms, sometimes a Guardian, then the chest. A track task needs a place in one of the two, a fourth slot source or a new part of the floor, and either choice amends an approved requirement. The three-day window counts the 8 domains with a floor, and ADR-0070 already carries a second window of the same kind: the Observatory gets at least one visit in any 3 adventure days. A track window follows that pattern. If the track's host floors include S, the domain window alone brings a host floor within 3 adventure days. ADR-0070 promises that only "as long as each day completes 3 floors", so a short day breaks it and the Director needs its own check. Source: ADR-0070 and RES-1000, read 2026-09-28.

### About 2 track tasks a day take time from the 60-minute adventure

ADR-0190 asks a simulated 60-minute adventure for at least 28 scored first attempts at the fluency threshold, a minimum the owner imposed on 2026-09-26. Two track tasks, each with a source to read, take time that graph tasks had. The record doesn't say whether track attempts count toward the 28, and ADR-0190's simulation doesn't include them. Source: ADR-0190, read 2026-09-28.

### The answer forms need a choice outside the six input classes and a new tap answer

ADR-0040 lets a template ask for `choice` only in the classes `shape`, `net`, `symmetry`, `explain_why`, `science` and `model`, because a typed answer can't be guessed. The addendum allows a choice of at least 4 options in every track task. ADR-0040's answer kinds hold `point` (a node of the coordinate grid) and `grid` (an exact set of cells), and neither is one tap on a cell, a bar or a map square of a drawn source. Number and time answers need nothing new: `integer`, `decimal` and the two `time` kinds exist, and a duration answers as minutes with the unit label ADR-0150 draws beside a field. Source: ADR-0040 and ADR-0150, read 2026-09-28.

### The engine already draws pictures from parameters, and a source is one more picture

ADR-0040 has `src/render` draw every task picture as SVG from the task's parameters, and the log rebuilds any task from template and seed. A generated source fits that path: the template draws the whole data set into its parameters, and the renderer draws the table, chart, timetable or map. The approved record forbids task parameters that depend on the display language, so the source's labels, such as stop names, fish kinds and compass letters, must be keys into the string file of ADR-0160. That is also what CLAUDE.md asks of every player-facing string. Source: ADR-0040, ADR-0160 and CLAUDE.md, read 2026-09-28.

### The seven reading error classes don't fit the four error classes of the limits

The error-type limit places every mistake in one of four classes, conceptual, procedural, computational or unclassified, and ADR-0180 reports their shares. ADR-0040 names each trap by a misconception identifier shared across nodes, and RES-1200 gives each trap a kind, `conceptual`, `procedural` or `fact`, which ADR-0180 maps to the four classes. The addendum's seven classes, `misread_cell`, `axis_scale`, `legend_misread`, `scale_conversion`, `time_across_hour`, `wrong_source_part` and `ignored_condition`, work as trap identifiers but not as a fifth class. Source: ADR-0040, ADR-0180 and RES-1200, read 2026-09-28.

### Report v1 has exactly eight screens

ADR-0180 fixes report v1 at eight screens: summary, VWO readiness, graph map, node card, misconceptions, limits, science and the story book. The addendum adds a section «Работа с источниками» (working with sources). ADR-0180's VWO ladder counts graph nodes, and the approved record keeps graph layers apart in the report unless the parent mixes them, so the track has to stay out of coverage. Source: ADR-0180, read 2026-09-28.

### The approved touch minimum is 56 px on the tablet, stricter than the addendum's 44 pt

RES-3100 concludes that "on the tablet every control must have a touch zone of at least `touch-min`, 56px", and that 44 px serves only a pointer. RES-3900 resolved that a 64 px key meets it. The addendum asks for at least 44 by 44 pt. On iPad Safari one CSS pixel is one point, so a 56 px zone meets both, and the two disagree only if 44 is read as permission to go smaller. Source: RES-3100 conclusions 15 and 26, RES-3900, read 2026-09-28.

### The text-size checks don't reach SVG text, and zoom meets the still task window

ADR-0150's Playwright checks find text "at least 13 px everywhere, story 20 px and task 24 px", and its stylelint rule rejects any CSS `font-size` that isn't a type-scale token. Text inside an SVG drawn by `src/render` sets its size as an attribute, which neither check reads. The addendum asks for source text no smaller than the main text; in the task window that is the `task` style, 24/34, or 28/40 with large text (RES-3100). ADR-0150 also requires no `getAnimations()` result inside the open task window, and a pinch zoom with an eased transition would produce one. No approved record addresses page zoom on the iPad. Source: ADR-0150, RES-3100, read 2026-09-28.

### The browser matrix already covers acceptance test 15

ADR-0190 runs end-to-end tests "on the MVP matrix: WebKit emulating an iPad in landscape and Chromium at 1280x720", and ADR-0150 adds WebKit at 1180 by 820. Test 15's snapshots in WebKit and Chromium fit that matrix, and ADR-0190 keeps an adult's real-iPad checklist, because Playwright's WebKit isn't Safari on an iPad. Source: ADR-0190, ADR-0150, read 2026-09-28.

### The MVP contents list doesn't hold the track

The approved MVP contents list names what the first version must include, and ADR-0190 says "the MVP holds exactly the approved scope". The Sources track isn't on the list, and the addendum makes it MVP. Source: ADR-0190, read 2026-09-28.

### Four options answer the question

| Option | What its advocate says it is better at | Against it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Do nothing: keep S1, S2, S5, M2, P5 and G7 as they are | no new node, state, window or screen; the graph already covers the SLO goals for tables, charts, timetables and maps, and a current Cito test reaches them through Rekenen's Verbanden | the graph's tasks show small, clean sources, so a child who can't find the right row among distractors looks fluent; the seven reading errors are never counted; two sources together have no home; the owner has asked for the track |
| A `source` format on the six graph nodes, like `bare` and `context` in section 8 | reuses the graph, the model and the window; the report splits accuracy by format, as it will for bare and context tasks | I5 has no node to live on; the reading classes scatter across six nodes; the frequency of 2 a day can't be guaranteed without a track rule; a reading error would lower a maths estimate |
| A tenth domain I inside the graph | the model, the Director, the graph map and the report take it with no new path | breaks the 79-node count, the nine-domain schema and the RES-0800 fixture; the VWO ladder would count nodes with no SLO level; a ninth floor domain needs 9 floors in 3 days, which a short day misses; the owner ruled it out |
| A track I1 to I5 beside the graph, as the addendum sets it | measures the reading step apart from the maths, so "finds but doesn't calculate" and "calculates but misreads" become visible; keeps the 79 nodes, the fixture and the ladder intact; the science topics set the precedent | a second estimate for content six graph nodes already estimate; a new part of the graph file, model rows, a slot source or floor part, a window rule, an answer kind, a component and a report section |

I lead with the track beside the graph, because the owner instructs it and because it is the only option that keeps a reading error out of a maths estimate while still counting it. The case against it is real. Cito's current pages list no test that reports this skill on its own, so the track measures something the next Cito test may not report. The six overlapping nodes also mean two estimates can disagree about the same timetable. The report has to show that disagreement as a finding, «считает, но ошибается в чтении» (calculates but misreads), or the parent reads two figures for one skill. The format option is better at reuse. It is the fallback if the track's states prove too thin at 2 tasks a day, since 5 nodes at about 14 tasks a week give each node about 3 attempts a week; I would call them too thin if a track node is still «не проверено» (not checked) after 6 weeks of play. The conclusions below settle each default, with the rejected alternative named beside it.

## Conclusions

1. `content/graph.yaml` must hold the Sources track as a part of its own beside `nodes`, `topics` and `sloGoals`, with the five nodes I1 to I5, each with `citoBlock: null`. ADR-0050 must be amended so the validator still counts 79 maths nodes in nine domains and checks the track part apart.
2. The knowledge model must keep an estimate and a state for each track node, computed from unassisted first attempts on track tasks alone. ADR-0060 must be amended to add those rows, because the I5 gate needs «понимает» on two of I1 to I4. A track attempt must never update a graph node, and a graph attempt must never update a track node. Track attempts must write a stream of their own that stays out of «сама» until ADR-0060's activation rule admits it, and none joins during the MVP.
3. The Director must place about 2 track tasks on each adventure day that reaches a host floor, and at least one in any 3 consecutive adventure days, counting completed floors only. It must offer I5 only after two of I1 to I4 reach «понимает». ADR-0070 must be amended with the track window beside the Observatory's. The host floors must be S, M, G and P, because the track's content sits in those domains; the alternative, every floor, spreads the track evenly but puts a timetable on a fractions floor where no story explains it. A track task must come as a fixed part of the host floor, like mental arithmetic, and never take a room slot, so the requirement on floor order is superseded and the one on slot sources stays.
4. The day-plan simulation must show the minimum of scored first attempts in a 60-minute adventure with track tasks included, counting graph and track attempts separately. Only graph attempts must count toward the minimum of 28, so the day plan must still reach 28 scored graph first attempts with the track tasks in it.
5. Every track task must be contextual and generated by a template from a seed, with the source's full data set in its parameters and drawn as SVG by `src/render`. Every label must be a key into the string file, so the log rebuilds the source and a second language renders it from the same parameters.
6. Every generated source must hold data the solution doesn't use, and at least one unused value must sit next to each value the solution reads, so a `misread_cell` answer differs from the correct one and the trap stays distinguishable.
7. The track's property test must run on 10,000 seeds per template, the bar ADR-0040 sets for every other template. For each task it must find exactly one correct answer or region, no correct answer or intermediate value in the question text, and no answer the template's solver can reach with the source removed.
8. ADR-0040 must be amended with a `source` input class that allows `choice` of at least 4 options. It must also gain an answer kind for one tap on a region of the source, such as a cell, a bar or a map square, credited 1 for the correct region and 0 for any other, as `point` and `grid` give no partial credit either, and a neighbouring cell is the `misread_cell` trap. Number and time answers must use the existing kinds, and a duration must be answered as a number with its unit label.
9. The seven reading error classes must be trap identifiers under ADR-0040's rule of one identifier per misconception, and each must map to exactly one of the four error classes the limits report. `time_across_hour` and `misread_cell` must reuse the identifiers of the matching M2 and S1 traps in the catalogue, so the misconceptions screen shows one row for each misconception. Each must carry a trap `kind`, so ADR-0180's mapping places it. The kind must follow this test: a reading error is `conceptual` when the child misunderstands how the source encodes a quantity, and `procedural` when she understands the encoding but takes the wrong part. By that test `axis_scale`, `scale_conversion` and `time_across_hour` are conceptual, and `misread_cell`, `legend_misread`, `wrong_source_part` and `ignored_condition` are procedural. The rejected alternative, one kind `procedural` for all seven, is simpler but hides from the parent whether a scale or a row is the trouble.
10. The report must show «Работа с источниками» apart from the graph. The section holds I1 to I5 with their states, first-attempt accuracy by question level, the reading error classes, and the two gaps «находит, но не считает» (finds but doesn't calculate) and «считает, но ошибается в чтении» (calculates but misreads). ADR-0180 and the requirement fixing eight screens must be amended to add it. No track node may enter the VWO coverage, the ladder or the gap list.
11. Every tappable part of a source must have a touch zone of at least 56 px on the tablet, which meets the addendum's 44 pt, and ADR-0150 and RES-3100 need no change for it.
12. ADR-0150's text-size test must be amended to measure text inside SVG sources as well. Source text must be no smaller than the `task` style, 24 px, or 28 px with large text, because the task text is the main text of the task window where a source is read.
13. Pinch zoom must work inside the source component, follow the fingers with no eased transition, and leave the rest of the task window still, so the task window stays free of animations. Zoom must live inside the component rather than rely on page zoom, because the component keeps zoom inside the task window, where the no-animation check already looks. My expectation that page zoom on the iPad also shifts the task window's fixed layout is untested.
14. Acceptance test 15 must run its snapshots in WebKit at the iPad viewport and in Chromium, and the adult's real-iPad checklist must gain a source task with a tap answer, a pinch zoom inside the source and a page pinch outside it, to see what page zoom does to the task window.
15. The MVP contents list must include the Sources track, and ADR-0190's scope must be amended with it.
16. The report and the Parent Room must not name a Studievaardigheden test and must not claim that the player's school gives one, because Cito's current pages list no such test and the track's value doesn't depend on it.
17. The track must ask only the addendum's four question levels on I1 to I5. Choosing a suitable source and reading a schema or flow chart, Cito's two sub-skills the addendum leaves out, must stay out of the MVP.

### Decided on 2026-09-28

The report names no Studievaardigheden test, and nothing asks the parent whether the school gives one. Cito's current pages list no such test, the track measures the reading step whatever the school tests, and naming the school's tests would add a school detail the report doesn't need. Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to decide the open questions; the owner approves it with this record.

A track task comes as a fixed part of its host floor, like mental arithmetic, not as a fourth slot source. A fixed part guarantees about 2 track tasks a day without competing with frontier and review for room slots, and it supersedes one requirement, the floor order, as the other choice would. Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to decide the open questions; the owner approves it with this record.

Track attempts don't count toward the 28 scored first attempts of a 60-minute adventure; the simulation counts them apart. The owner imposed that minimum on maths fluency, and keeping it on graph attempts leaves ADR-0190's rule unchanged. Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to decide the open questions; the owner approves it with this record.

"No smaller than the main text" means the task text, 24 px, or 28 px with large text, as conclusion 12 leads with. The task style is the main text of the task window where a source is read, and pinch zoom inside the source carries the maps that fit fewer labels. Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to decide the open questions; the owner approves it with this record.

The track doesn't ask Cito's two missing sub-skills, choosing the suitable source and reading a schema or flow chart. The addendum sets five nodes and four levels, and adding sub-skills would change more approved records for a skill Cito's current tests don't report. Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to decide the open questions; the owner approves it with this record.
