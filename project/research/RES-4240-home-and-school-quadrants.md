# The four home-and-school quadrants fit the report after the MVP only if each side is read on its own measure: the school's status with a target guard, the home side from tested accuracy, and Cito through its own relative domain analysis, with every reading shown as a check to run

## Summary

The owner's addendum 2 of 2026-09-28, item 5, asks the "home and school" screen of ADR-0310 to put each school goal, and each Cito domain mapped to nodes, into one of four quadrants: high or low at home, high or low at school. Each quadrant carries what the pair might mean and what to check. School data still stays out of the knowledge model. The idea fits the record: ADR-0310 already keeps school values apart from the model and the Director, and a quadrant needs only projections that already exist. Three premises don't hold as written. The school's goal status is relative to a target the vendor sets at or just above her current percentile and can move monthly, so "target reached" isn't "high" (RES-4100). Cito's per-domain analysis isn't a level: its manual compares each category with the expected score of pupils of the same overall ability and makes no profile for the top and bottom 10 %, so "Cito level per domain" doesn't exist. The home state of one node rests on a block of 5 answers, and a pupil who answers 80 % right still lands below 4 of 5 about one block in four, so a quadrant built on one block flips often. The item conflicts with ADR-0310's rule that the screen marks two cases only, with the approved requirement that a difference reads as two measures and never as a fault, and with the Cito form's fields in ADR-0290. I decided the eight open questions on 2026-09-28. The quadrants replace ADR-0310's two marks. The home side reads accuracy from tested states. The school side reads the status, guarded by the target level: `reached` counts as high only on a target at level 3 or above, and `needs_help` as low only on a target at level 3 or below. Cito enters through the teacher's category analysis, compared with her own home profile. The Cito form gains one optional field in the MVP. Every other part of the item waits for the post-MVP report. This record covers item 5 only; the profile, retention, transfer, the Dutch probe and the hypotheses page are items 1 to 4, 6 and 8 and their own records.

## The question

How should the "home and school" screen sort each pair of a home reading and a school reading into four quadrants, and tell the parent what to check, without letting school data reach the knowledge model and without saying more than the two measures support? The constraints are ADR-0310's fence around school data, ADR-0210's rule that new streams stay out of "on her own", the no-clock rule, and item 1 of the same addendum, which makes every interpretation a hypothesis and hides nothing weak.

The item assumes that each side has a "high" and a "low" that mean the same kind of thing, so that a pair lands in one quadrant and the quadrant says something about her. The findings test that assumption on all three sources and find it holds for none of them as written. The home state measures her own counted answers against fixed rules (ADR-0060). The school's status measures her against a personal target near her own level, and its level measures her against pupils nationally (RES-4100). Cito's domain analysis measures one domain against her own overall Cito score. A quadrant is therefore a cross of two different questions, and it's useful only if the screen says which question each side answers.

The item also assumes that a disagreement points at her: at format, language or anxiety on one side, at badly calibrated home tasks on the other. A disagreement can also come from chance in small samples, and the finding on block size tests how often.

## Method

On 2026-09-28 I read the owner's addendum 2 in full, with item 5 closely and items 1, 3 and 6 where item 5 points to them. I ran `paw find` for "Cito result", "home and school", "quadrant" and "measures differ"; the last two match nothing. I read RES-4100 and ADR-0310 in full, SPC-0310's section on the "home and school" screen, the approved requirements on the screen, on conversion between measures and on wording a difference, RES-4080's findings on Cito's reports and the form, ADR-0290's Cito form, school goals and home skill scale, ADR-0210's MVP scope, bridge and owner table, ADR-0060's state rules and block definition, ADR-0180's state labels and lesson labels, ADR-0300's Sources track, ADR-0340's opening, and RES-0800's table of the four Dutch domains against the graph's nodes.

On the web on 2026-09-28 I read Cito's Rekenen-Wiskunde page for Leerling in beeld and the Cito LOVS computer program manual, version 4.14 of February 2022, section 5.3.13 on the category analysis, as text extracted from the PDF with `pdftotext`. I read the abstract and opening pages of Abedi and Lord (2001) from the PDF, the abstract of Hickendorff (2013) through the ERIC database, the abstract of Barroso and others (2021) through Europe PMC, and the publisher's abstract of Livingston and Lewis (1995). I computed block outcomes under a binomial model with `python3`.

I couldn't obtain a Leerling in beeld category report for any pupil, so whether the new system keeps the LOVS form of the analysis is unconfirmed. The publisher of Hickendorff (2013) refused the fetch with 403, so I read only the ERIC abstract. PubMed returned only a cookie notice for Barroso and others. I found no study of how a school learning system's goal status agrees with a mastery measure, and no source on whether the vendor's tasks are timed.

## Findings

### The addendum fixes the quadrants, their thresholds and the fence

Item 5 sets four rows. High at home and at school: "навык устойчив" (the skill is stable), check nothing, confirm by retention. Low on both: "реальный пробел" (a real gap), a lesson on the node, then its trajectory. High at home, low at school: "формат, язык, тревога, условия выполнения" (format, language, anxiety, test conditions), check with the Dutch probe on that node, with Cito-format sources and by asking the teacher about conditions. Low at home, high at school: home tasks harder or badly calibrated, or the school tests the goal in a narrow format, check in the sandbox, compare the goal's wording and recalibrate. High at home means a node state of «бегло» (fluent) or above, and low means «понимает» (understands) or below. High and low at school come from the status, reached or needs help, or from the Cito level per domain. School data stays out of the knowledge model, "иначе получилась бы круговая аргументация" (otherwise the argument would be circular).

### ADR-0310 marks only two cases, and the quadrants widen them to four

ADR-0310 and SPC-0310 mark a goal «измерения расходятся» (the measures differ) "in two cases only": status `reached` while every linked node is below «Понимает», and `needs_help` while every linked node is «Бегло» or «Устойчиво». ADR-0310 gives the reason that "any finer rule needs an exchange rate between a percentile band and a mastery state, which nobody measured". The quadrants add the two agreeing cells and move the home threshold for the first mark from below «Понимает» to «Понимает» or below. The two agreeing cells need no exchange rate either, since each side is cut on its own scale. The moved threshold does widen what counts as a difference, and ADR-0310's rule must be amended for it.

### The item's "what it may mean" column conflicts with the approved wording rule

The approved requirement built from RES-4100 says a difference must read "as a difference between two measures, not as a fault in the player or in either measure", and ADR-0310's line under each mark names no cause. The addendum's third row names causes in her, anxiety among them, and its fourth names a fault in the home measure. Item 1 of the same addendum words every interpretation as a hypothesis to check, «возможна языковая причина — проверить зондом» (a language cause is possible, check with the probe), and the addendum ranks itself above addendum 1. The wording rule must therefore be amended to allow a named possible cause when it is worded as something to check, lists causes on both sides, and never stands alone.

### The school's status is relative to a target near her own level

RES-4100 read the vendor's help article on 2026-09-28: the target can update "elke maand automatisch ... op basis van de huidige vaardigheid van de leerling", set "op of net boven (maximaal 5%) de huidige vaardigheid". A pupil in the second level band, with a target in the same band, reaches it. So `reached` says she met a target placed at her own level, and on a low target it says nothing about mastery. `needs_help` says she fell below a target placed near her own level, so on a high target it can leave her well above the national middle. The level bands are RES-4100's finding: levels 1 to 5 in bands of 20 %, so level 3 is the national 40th to 59th percentile. The third status, `developing`, is neither high nor low, and the addendum leaves it out.

### Cito's domain analysis compares each category with her own overall score, not with a level

The Cito LOVS manual, version 4.14, read 2026-09-28, section 5.3.13: «De gewogen score van een leerling wordt per categorie (bijv. Getallen bij Rekenen-Wiskunde) vergeleken met de verwachte score van leerlingen met een vergelijkbaar vaardigheidsniveau.» A significant deviation gets the signal «Opvallend» or «Zeer opvallend», and one that isn't significant «Niet opvallend». The worked example reads "presteert Halil 17% boven verwachting". The manual adds that "meestal voor de 10% zwakst presterende (niveau V- of E) leerlingen en de 10% best presterende (niveau I+ of A+) leerlingen geen profiel kan worden aangemaakt". For Rekenen-Wiskunde 3.0 the standard categories are «getallen, optellen & aftrekken, vermenigvuldigen & delen en meten, tijd en geld», with a second analysis on «context en kaal» for addition and subtraction. So a Cito domain has no level of its own: its reading is a relative strength or weakness within her profile, and a pupil at I+ has no reading at all.

### Leerling in beeld names four domains and keeps the domain analysis on the teacher's side

Cito's Rekenen-Wiskunde page, read 2026-09-28, names the domains «Getallen, Verhoudingen, Meten en meetkunde en Verbanden» and promises «detailanalyses op de domeinen en het onderscheid formeel/functioneel», addressed to the teacher. RES-4080 found that the parents' guide shows only the overall functioning level or the I to V or A to E level. Whether Leerling in beeld's domain analysis takes the LOVS form above is unconfirmed on 2026-09-28, and the parent gets it only by asking the teacher.

### The Cito form has no field for a domain or a category

The approved Cito form of ADR-0290 holds the moment, the test and its level, the vaardigheidsscore, the functioneringsniveau, the referentieniveau, the level on I to V or A to E, the subject, an optional bare-versus-context split, an optional expected advice and a note. Nothing holds a category and its signal. The form is part of the MVP (ADR-0210), so the M7 result of January 2027 is entered before the post-MVP report exists. `external_test_recorded` names the result it replaces, so a later structured entry is possible, but only while the parent still has the teacher's printout.

### RES-0800 already maps the four Dutch domains to graph nodes

RES-0800's table maps Getallen to N1 to N7, A1 to A11, A6a, A13, F1 to F8 and D1 to D6; Verhoudingen to P1 to P6, M8 and A14; Meten en meetkunde to M1 to M10, M12, M13, G1 and G3 to G7; Verbanden to S1 to S6 and G4; and the word problems T1 to T4 to none. The LOVS categories «optellen & aftrekken», «vermenigvuldigen & delen» and «meten, tijd en geld» have no mapping in the record.

### A block of 5 answers puts an 80 % pupil below "4 of 5" about one time in four

ADR-0060 gives a node a state from its last full block of 5 graded observations: 4 or more right for «понимает, нужна скорость» or «бегло», 3 or 3,5 for «понимает». Under a binomial model, which I computed on 2026-09-28 and which ignores the half marks, a pupil who answers each task right with probability 0.8 scores 4 or more in 74 % of blocks and 3 or fewer in 26 %. At 0.6 she scores 4 or more in 34 %. Livingston and Lewis (1995) define classification consistency as agreement between two observed classifications and accuracy as agreement with the true one, and both fall where true scores sit near the cut. Every quadrant is two such cuts at once, so a pair near either threshold changes quadrant between snapshots with no change in her.

### Speed separates «понимает, нужна скорость» from «бегло», and the school's tests don't time her

ADR-0060's rules `block-slow` and `block-fast` differ only in the median time of right answers. RES-4080 confirmed that the Cito tests have no time limit. So a node at «понимает, нужна скорость» has the accuracy of «бегло», and placing it "low" against an untimed school measure would read a speed gap as a knowledge gap. The addendum's thresholds, «бегло» or above and «понимает» or below, leave this state in neither class. Whether the vendor's goal tasks are timed is unconfirmed on 2026-09-28.

### Language load lowers context items more than bare ones

Hickendorff (2013), read through ERIC on 2026-09-28, analysed over 2,000 Dutch pupils in the first three grades and found that computational skill and applied problem solving are distinct abilities with a latent correlation "from 0.81 in grade 1 to 0.87 in grade 3", and that pupils' language level had larger effects on applied problem solving. Abedi and Lord (2001), read 2026-09-28, gave 1,174 US grade 8 pupils original and linguistically simplified word problems: English language learners scored lower, the simplified items scored "slightly higher", and English language learners, pupils in low and average maths classes and pupils of low socio-economic status gained most. Both support language as a hypothesis for "high at home, low at school" on context goals, and neither measures it for one child, which is what item 6's probe is for.

### Anxiety and achievement correlate weakly to moderately, and the game can't see anxiety at school

Barroso and others (2021), read through Europe PMC on 2026-09-28, pooled 747 effect sizes from studies of 1992 to 2018 and found "a small-to-moderate, negative, and statistically significant correlation (r = -.28)", present from childhood on. A correlation of that size makes anxiety a fair hypothesis and a poor explanation of one pupil's result, and nothing the game logs observes her at school. The only check within reach is asking the teacher, which the addendum lists.

### The quadrant needs nothing new in the log before the MVP, except the Cito categories

ADR-0060's `node_snapshots` keeps each node's state per game day with play, rebuilt by the full recompute, so the home state at a snapshot's document date can be read back later. School snapshots come after the MVP (ADR-0210). The Cito form's result is entered in the MVP, and its category analysis exists only on the teacher's printout. That one value is the only reading a later report can't reconstruct from what the log holds.

### The suggested checks lean on items and records that don't all exist yet

The retention check is item 3 and the Dutch probe is item 6 of the same addendum, both still in research. The sandbox is ADR-0340's. The Sources track is ADR-0300's; RES-4080 found that copying Cito's item formats crosses Cito's line against practising for the test, so "Cito-format sources" can mean only the track's own tables and diagrams. A lesson on a node is ADR-0180's lesson mark. Asking the teacher fits the Dutch memo of ADR-0290, which is parent-facing Dutch in `ru.json` with a Russian gloss and so needs no change to the Russian-only rule in `CLAUDE.md`. The probe's player-facing Dutch does conflict with that rule, and item 6's record settles it; this item only links to the probe.

### Five options, compared

| Option | Better at | Worse at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Do nothing: keep ADR-0310's two marks | no new rule; no named cause anywhere; the two marks hold whatever exchange rate one assumes | the two agreeing cells, which the parent reads most, get no line; no check is suggested; the addendum asks for four quadrants |
| The addendum as written | the owner's wording; one table the parent reads at a glance | a low target reached counts as high; "Cito level per domain" doesn't exist; a speed gap reads as a knowledge gap; causes in her are named as meanings |
| Quadrants read on each side's own measure, with checks (the lead) | each side cut where its own measure supports a cut; Cito compared relative to relative; every cell worded as a check | three rules the parent must learn; many rows land in no quadrant; Cito rows need the teacher's analysis |
| Snappet goals only; Cito stays on the timeline | no change to the Cito form; no teacher document to chase | loses the one school measure that separates context from bare items, which the language hypothesis needs |
| A scatter of school level against home share, with no cuts | no cut-point flips; shows how far a pair sits from agreement | puts a percentile and a mastery share on two axes of one chart, which reads as a conversion the approved requirement forbids; harder to read |

The case against the lead is its cost in clarity. The parent must read three different "highs", and with the guards many goals fall in no quadrant: every `developing` status, every goal whose linked nodes fall on both sides, every goal whose nodes weren't checked within 30 days, and every Cito row for a pupil at I+ or with no teacher's printout. A strong pupil may get almost no Cito rows at all, and then the fourth option gives the same screen for less work. The addendum's version would fill more cells, but some of them would be wrong in a way the parent can't see. I lead with the third option because a wrong cell costs more than an empty one: item 1 of the same addendum puts falsifiability first, and a cell built on a target the vendor set at her own level can't be falsified.

## Conclusions

1. The "home and school" screen after the MVP must sort each goal with a confirmed snapshot link, and each Cito category the parent entered, into one of four quadrants or into none, and must replace ADR-0310's two marks with that sorting, which amends ADR-0310 and SPC-0310. No approved requirement fixes the two marks, so none changes for this conclusion.
2. The home side of a goal must read only tested states from the "on her own" stream (ADR-0060, ADR-0210), never an inferred state, a probe-only state or a new stream. A node must count as high at a block score of 4 or more (`block-slow`, `block-fast`, `stable`), and as low at 3,5 or less (`block-low`, `block-mid`). A goal must be high only when every linked node is high, and low only when every linked node is low.
3. The home side must use the node state at the snapshot's document date from `node_snapshots`, and must show the current state beside it. A node whose last unassisted first attempt is more than 30 days before that date must place its goal in no quadrant, under «давно не проверялось дома» (not checked at home for a long time).
4. The school side of a snapshot goal must count `reached` as high only when the goal's target level is 3 or above, and `needs_help` as low only when the target level is 3 or below. `developing`, a status whose target level is `null`, a goal level of 0 (the vendor's «niet gestart of niet genoeg opgaven gemaakt»), and a status the parser couldn't place must place the goal in no quadrant, and a status from a `target_moved` pair must carry that mark on the screen.
5. The Cito side must compare like with like: a category the teacher's analysis marks «Opvallend» or «Zeer opvallend» below expectation must count as a relative weakness, one marked above expectation as a relative strength, and «Niet opvallend» must place it in no quadrant. The home side of a Cito row must be relative too, read from `node_snapshots` at the Cito form's test moment with the current share shown beside it: the category counts as a relative strength when its nodes' share at a block score of 4 or more exceeds her share over the tested nodes outside the category by at least the row's margin, and a relative weakness when it falls short by at least that margin. The margin is 100 times the square root of q(1 - q)(1/n_in + 1/n_out), with q her share over all tested nodes and n_in and n_out the tested nodes inside and outside the category, so it grows as the counts shrink. Only a node whose last unassisted first attempt is within 30 days before the test moment counts as tested, and a category with fewer than 10 tested nodes must place its row in no quadrant.
6. The screen must show, for every row in no quadrant, why, and must show under the Cito rows that Cito builds no profile for the top and bottom 10 % of pupils.
7. Each quadrant must be shown as a check to run, with its possible causes on both sides as the addendum lists them, and under every row the line of ADR-0310 that the two sides measure different things. This amends the approved requirement that a difference must read as a difference between two measures and never as a fault in the player or in either measure, to allow a named possible cause worded as a check.
8. Each check must be a link or a sentence and must change nothing in play by itself: the lesson mark stays the parent's act (ADR-0180), the Dutch probe on a node is reachable only through item 6's decision, the sandbox check opens ADR-0340's sandbox, and no quadrant feeds the Director or the knowledge model, as ADR-0310 already rules for snapshot links.
9. A row must say, for each linked node, whether its state rests on its last full block alone, as every state but `stable` does under ADR-0060, or on `stable`'s two checks, and must mark the row «одна проверка» (one check) where any linked node rests on one block, because a state from one block of 5 changes class about one time in four with no change in her (the finding on block size). The mark will be common, since every row with no `stable` node carries it. Item 1's interval rule must apply to the row once its decision exists.
10. The Cito form of ADR-0290 must gain, in the MVP, one optional field `categories`: a list of entries, each with the category, the signal and the deviation in per cent where the printout shows it. The category must be chosen from a list of the four Leerling in beeld domains and the four LOVS standard categories, or typed as the printout writes it. The signal must be one of `below_notable`, `below_very_notable`, `not_notable`, `above_notable`, `above_very_notable` or `other` with the printout's words, because whether Leerling in beeld keeps the LOVS form is unconfirmed and a fixed list would reject the one copy of the value. A typed category or an `other` signal must place its row in no quadrant until the parent maps it. This amends ADR-0290, `external_test_recorded`'s payload, and the approved requirement that lists the Cito form's fields, from the moment and the test taken to the note.
11. The four domains must map to nodes by RES-0800's table, and the four LOVS categories through a small offline mapping in `content/`, drafted by the building agent and confirmed by the parent, as ADR-0310's goal catalogue is.
12. The Dutch memo of ADR-0290 must add the category analysis and the conditions of the test, such as reading aloud or extra time, to what the parent can ask the school for.
13. School data must stay out of the knowledge model and every model call, under ADR-0310's four static checks, and a quadrant must be computed on the Mac as a projection, never logged, so a recompute gives the same screen.

### Decided on 2026-09-28

1. The four quadrants replace ADR-0310's two marks, and the screen words each quadrant as a check with causes on both sides, because the owner's addendum 2 ranks above addendum 1 and its item 1 already makes each interpretation a hypothesis. Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to process addendum 2 through to the specifications.
2. The home side is cut by accuracy, block score 4 or more against 3,5 or less, so «понимает, нужна скорость» counts as high, because the Cito tests are untimed (RES-4080) and a speed gap read as a knowledge gap would send the parent to the wrong check. For snapshot goals I assume the vendor's goal tasks are untimed too, which is unconfirmed; if the vendor turns out to time them, goal rows go back to the addendum's cut, «бегло» or above, and «понимает, нужна скорость» places a goal in no quadrant. Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to process addendum 2 through to the specifications.
3. `reached` counts as high only on a target at level 3 or above, `needs_help` counts as low only on a target at level 3 or below, and `developing` counts as neither, because the vendor places the target at or just above her current percentile, so a status alone says where she stands against herself. I chose level 3 because it is the national middle band of 40 to 59 % (RES-4100), and the guard keeps a reached low target from reading as high and a missed high target from reading as low. Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to process addendum 2 through to the specifications.
4. Cito rows compare her relative strengths at school with her relative strengths at home, the category's nodes against the nodes outside it, with a margin of one standard deviation of that difference by chance and at least 10 tested nodes in the category, because Cito's category analysis is relative to her own overall score and has no level to cut. At 10 nodes inside, 40 outside and a share of 0.7, the margin is about 16 points; at 5 inside it is about 22 points, more than one answer in a block of 5, so a small category could show a difference only when it is extreme, which is why 10 is the floor. The owner revisits the one-deviation rule once a year of real home data shows how far category shares move between months with no lesson. Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to process addendum 2 through to the specifications.
5. The Cito form gains its optional `categories` field in the MVP, because the teacher's printout is the only copy of that value and the M7 result arrives before the post-MVP report. Every other part of item 5 comes after the MVP with ADR-0310. Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to process addendum 2 through to the specifications.
6. A goal whose linked nodes fall on both sides, or whose home check is older than 30 days, goes in no quadrant and says why, because "every linked node" is ADR-0310's own rule and 30 days is ADR-0180's "not checked for a long time". Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to process addendum 2 through to the specifications.
7. No quadrant triggers anything in play: each check is a link the parent follows, because ADR-0310 keeps snapshot data out of the Director and a quadrant is built from it. Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to process addendum 2 through to the specifications.
8. The screen reads the home state at the snapshot's document date and shows the current state beside it, because a state from after the snapshot would compare two different moments. Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to process addendum 2 through to the specifications.
