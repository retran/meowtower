# Domain P's percent and scale goals start at Dutch group 7, a group later than the graph assumes

## Summary

The graph places P1 and P2 (percent) at group 6, P3 (share, value-added tax) and P6 (comparing offers) at group 7, P5 (scale) at group 6 and P7 at group 8 (`content/graph.yaml` lines 555 to 663). The Dutch goals in the tussendoelen put the first percentages at the end of group 7, percentages of a number and percentages above 100% at the end of group 8, and scale at the end of group 7 with a 1:50 000 example at group 8. The graph should be checked against these before any template is written for P1 to P3, P5 and P6.

The Russian federal programme places percent and proportion in grade 6 (`frp25-ooo` lines 446 and 583 to 587), which is Dutch group 8 under the mapping that a Russian grade n is group n + 2. It therefore reaches percent at the same age as the Dutch group 8 goals. Solving a proportion by the basic property of a proportion is Russian-only in the sources read, so it is an extension. Multiplicative comparison ("times as many") appears in Russian grades 3 and 4 and in the Moro grade 2 text, and it is the precursor of P4.

The catalogue rows in RES-1200 (lines 259 to 265) give traps and forms for every P node. Most of those forms are confirmed by the sources below. The "+10% then -10%" and successive-percentage traps are only partly confirmed: the Dutch text confirms the "same total" rule, and no source for compounding was found.

## The question

Which Dutch goal sets the group and range for each P node, what do the Russian programme and the extracted olympiad and Kangaroo papers add, and which task forms can fill ten templates per subtype and answer kind? The owner's rule is that the Dutch programme is the base and the Russian programme supplies marked extensions.

## Method

A research agent drafted this record from the extracted source texts; the main session checked a sample of the quotes of the sibling drafts against those texts, and the quotes below were not each rechecked. Column assignments in the tussendoelen are by position in the text layout, so they should be checked against the PDF before the column boundaries are relied on.

Read, in full or in the cited ranges: the tussendoelen Verhoudingen section (lines 1437 to 1674) and the table of contents (lines 55 to 80); the concretisation Domein Verhoudingen parts A and B (lines 2602 to 2914 and 3040 to 3114); the federal programme grades 1 to 4 (lines 403, 443, 579, 2563, located by keyword) and grades 5 to 9 (lines 440 to 447, 563 to 613, 720 to 733, 1144 to 1173); the Moro texts, located by keyword and read at the cited lines; the Vilenkin 2024 percent chapter (lines 1497 to 1615), ratio chapter (lines 10707 to 10810 and 10880 to 10945), proportion chapter (lines 11262 to 11385) and scale chapter (lines 12185 to 12256); and the olympiad papers at the cited lines (MCCME 2021 lines 226, 256 to 270, 694 and 857 to 864; MCCME 2023 lines 966 to 977; Kangoeroe 396 lines 872 to 887, 1068 to 1119, 1176 to 1181, 1249 to 1254; Kangoeroe 397 lines 626 to 638 and 733 to 743).

Searched by keyword only: the concretisation for 1F and 1S percent items beyond the lines above, the Russian grade 4 text beyond line 579, the Vilenkin percent and scale tasks beyond those cited, and the Tartu papers. The OCR of the Moro texts is noisy, and a minus or multiplication sign is often read as "%", so only explicit words were used.

Not read: concretisation part C (lines 3201 onward), the rest of the Russian grade 4 text, the Dutch Kangoeroe items for groups 7 and 8, and the Russian "сложные проценты" passage at `frp25-ooo` lines 2287 to 2289, which was seen in a keyword search and not placed by grade.

## Findings

### P1 and P2: the Dutch goals set percent at the end of group 7, not group 6

Percentages first appear in the end-of-group-7 column: "kan in alledaagse situaties notaties met percentages tot 100% herkennen, uitspreken en interpreteren" (tussendoelen lines 1466 to 1468). The end-of-group-6 column has no percent item (lines 1440 to 1477). Percent of a number, "25% van 60 → 0,25 x 60", is in the end-of-group-8 column (lines 1555 to 1558). The 1F concretisation includes "'1 op de 4' is 25% of 'een kwart van'" (concretisering line 2692).

### P3 and P6: the Dutch goals set them at the end of group 8

"notaties met percentages, ook boven 100%, herkennen ... Bij rente, btw, winst, verlies, groei, toename, afname" (tussendoelen lines 1453 to 1455) is in the end-of-group-8 column. Offers appear as "Aanbieding schrijfblokken bij H&D 4 halen 3 betalen en bij VEMA 50% korting. Als de schrijfblokken even duur zijn, waar krijg je dan de meeste korting?" (lines 1651 to 1656), also end of group 8. The graph has P3 and P6 at group 7 (`graph.yaml` lines 585 to 597 and 632 to 648).

### P4: the Dutch goals set ratio and price per unit at the end of group 6

"kan eenvoudige verhoudingen herkennen in alledaagse situaties (zoals in recepten, kettingen)" (tussendoelen lines 1453 to 1454) and "kan verhoudingsproblemen met geld, tijd en maten oplossen (bv.: Het berekenen van de prijs per stuk, per kilogram of per liter, snelheid...)" (lines 1534 to 1538) are in the end-of-group-6 column. "kan de relatie tussen eenvoudige verhoudingen (zoals 4 : 5) en breuken (zoals 4/5) herkennen" (lines 1604 to 1606) is also group 6. This matches the graph (`graph.yaml` lines 600 to 616).

### P5: the Dutch goals set scale at the end of group 7, with 1:50 000 at group 8

"kan schaalnotaties uitspreken, herkennen als een verhouding en er betekenis aan geven (bv.: 1 : 100 betekent dat 1 cm op de kaart in werkelijkheid 100 cm is)" (tussendoelen lines 1462 to 1465) is end of group 7. "De kaart heeft een schaal van 1:50.000. Hoeveel km fietst Mehmed?" (lines 1503 to 1507) is end of group 8. At 1S, "1 : 100 ... 'een staat tot honderd'" and "Op een autokaart staat 1:500 000" appear (concretisering lines 2755 to 2760). The graph has P5 at group 6 (`graph.yaml` lines 616 to 631).

### P7: successive percentages are a rule in the Dutch goals, not compounding

"begrijpt en kan uitleggen dat je percentages alleen bij elkaar mag optellen of aftrekken, als ze betrekking hebben op hetzelfde totaal" (tussendoelen lines 1586 to 1588) is the end-of-group-8 rule. No Dutch goal or item was found for +20% then -20%, or for finding the original after a discount. That is the stretch node P7, and it stays unverified against the Dutch text.

### The Russian programme places percent and proportion in grade 6

"В начале 6 класса происходит знакомство с понятием процента." (`frp25-ooo` line 446). The grade 6 content: "Отношение. Деление в данном отношении. Масштаб, пропорция. Применение пропорций при решении задач." (lines 583 to 584) and "Понятие процента. Вычисление процента от величины и величины по ее проценту... Выражение отношения величин в процентах." (lines 585 to 587). The grade 6 planned results: "Пользоваться масштабом, составлять пропорции и отношения." (line 728). The grade 5 text has no percent or proportion in the lines searched. The Russian text therefore places the topics at grade 6, which is Dutch group 8 and the same age as the Dutch percent-of-number goal. The proportion topic and the solving of proportions by the basic property are extensions, because no Dutch goal names them.

### The Russian grades 1 to 4 have multiplicative comparison and parts only

"Увеличение или уменьшение числа в несколько раз. Кратное" (`frp25-noo` line 403, grade 3) and "Задачи на нахождение доли величины" (line 579, grade 4). These are extensions relative to the Dutch goals for domain P, which name no multiplicative comparison before group 6; the Dutch text names "kan breuken en verhoudingen met elkaar vergelijken" only at the end of group 6 (tussendoelen lines 1608 to 1610).

### The Soviet-era Moro texts give multiplicative comparison in grade 2, not percent

Grade 1 (1990): no percent, proportion, scale or "times" item in the lines searched; the sharing task «Возьми 6 кружков и разложи их поровну» (around line 4543) is the nearest precursor. Grade 2 (1977): «Кружков в 4 раза больше, чем квадратов, а квадратов в 4 раза меньше, чем кружков.» (lines 4682 to 4683) and the rule «Чтобы узнать, во сколько раз одно из данных чисел больше или меньше, чем другое, надо большее число разделить на меньшее.» (lines 5389 to 5394). No percent item was found in either text.

### Vilenkin 2024 gives the widest set of forms, but it is a modern text

It defines percent: «Чтобы найти 1 % величины, надо её значение разделить на 100.» (vilenkin6-2024 lines 1513 to 1514), with tasks such as «Из них 45 % составляют новинки» (lines 1539 to 1541) and «Какой процент елей в лесу?» (lines 1579 to 1580). It defines ratio: «Отношение ... показывает, во сколько раз число а больше числа b» (lines 10748 to 10751); proportion: «Равенство двух отношений называют пропорцией» (line 11279); and scale: «Масштабом карты называют отношение длины отрезка на карте к длине соответствующего отрезка на местности.» (lines 12202 to 12203), with the task «Длина отрезка на карте 4 см ... масштаб карты 1:10 000 000» (lines 12214 to 12231).

### Olympiad and Kangaroo items that fit domain P

- MCCME 2021, grade 5, task 5.3 (lines 256 to 270): «он заплатил бы в 4 раза больше ... А если бы все ручки были шариковыми, то покупка обошлась бы в 2 раза дешевле ... Во сколько раз гелевая ручка дороже, чем шариковая?» Answer 8, by chaining 4 and 2.
- MCCME 2021, grade 7, task 7.8 (lines 857 to 863): «Суммарный вес всех банок варенья Карлсона в 13 раз больше ... после чего ... в 8 раз больше». Answer 23. A ratio reasoning problem, not a percent one.
- MCCME 2023, grade 8 solutions (lines 966 to 977): «скорости ... различаются в 4,5 раза, то АР : РВ = 1 : 4,5 = 2 : 9.» A ratio used as a scale on a route.
- Kangoeroe 396, level heading "havo/vwo klas 3, 4 & 5" at line 1071: «De taartpunt is 15% van de hele taart. Hoeveel graden is de hoek» (lines 1074 to 1077, answer 54); «Een olievat ... 30% leeg ... 30 liter komt overeen met 40%» (lines 1114 to 1118, answer 75); «De verhouding springtijd heen:springtijd terug is 4:5» (lines 1179 to 1181, answer 2 km). The item at lines 878 to 887 («ongeladen vrachtwagen ... 80% ... een kwart») is in a section whose heading was not confirmed.
- Kangoeroe 397: «85% spreekt Engels, 75% spreekt Frans. Hoeveel procent spreekt Frans én Engels?» (lines 634 to 638, answer 60%); «Van alle vrouwtjeskangoeroes ... 50% van 25% + 20% van 75%» (lines 736 to 743, answer 360). The level heading for these items was not located.
- Tartu Kangaroo 2020, grades 3 and 4 (oly-tartu-ecolier line 38): «На каком рисунке в тёмный цвет закрашена наибольшая часть квадрата?» A part-whole comparison, nearer the fractions domain. The Tartu texts contain no percent, ratio or proportion item in the lines searched.

The extracted Kangoeroe reports of 2002 and 2003 show vmbo, havo and vwo sections in the lines read, so the claim in RES-4281 that these items are for groups 7 and 8 is not supported for these files.

### The 1F and 1S concretisation gives the percent and scale tasks

At 1F: «Hoeveel procent is dat?» with «1 op de 4 fietsers» (concretisering lines 3061 to 3065), and «Welke beschrijvingen geven hetzelfde weer? 1 ; 20%; 1 op de 20; 5%, 1/5, 2/100» (lines 2706 to 2707). At 1S: «Op een autokaart staat 1:500 000» (lines 2755 to 2760) and «Op een kaart (schaal 1:400 000) liggen Rotterdam en Utrecht ongeveer 15 cm» (lines 2877 to 2878). Relative comparison: «In klas A zitten 24 kinderen: 10 meisjes en 14 jongens. In klas B zitten 20 kinderen: 10 meisjes en 10 jongens. In welke klas zitten naar verhouding meer meisjes?» (lines 2905 to 2910).

### Task forms the sources use for domain P

Each form is drawn from a cited passage, and a form counts once however its numbers vary.

1. Read and write a ratio as "1 op de 4", "1/4" and "25%" (tussendoelen lines 1459 to 1463).
2. Fill a gap in a ratio table, then read the ratio (Dutch "verhoudingstabel", lines 1447 to 1449; concretisering lines 3061 to 3077).
3. Convert a count out of a total to a percent: 16 of 40 is what percent (concretisering lines 3064 to 3065).
4. Percent of a number: 25% of 60 (tussendoelen lines 1555 to 1558); 45% of 1500 (Vilenkin lines 1539 to 1551).
5. Find the whole from a percent: 20% is 80, so the whole is 400 (Vilenkin lines 1557 to 1574).
6. Percent one number is of another: 528 of 1600 is 33% (Vilenkin lines 1579 to 1589).
7. Percent increase or decrease («На сколько процентов увеличилось число 90, если его увеличили на 45?», Vilenkin line 2513).
8. New price after a discount: an ice machine at €80, 25% off (tussendoelen lines 1553 to 1557).
9. Original price from discount and new price: a train ticket at €30 after 50% off (lines 1579 to 1582).
10. Profit, loss and value-added tax: 100% and 150% profit on a car bought for €1200 (lines 1586 to 1591).
11. Percent above 100%: «110% winst» (concretisering lines 2726 to 2728).
12. Percent of the rest: 85% cotton, so the rest is 15% nylon (lines 2720 to 2725).
13. Fraction to percent and back: 3/4 is 75% (lines 2716 to 2719 and 3045 to 3056).
14. Map distance from a scale: 8 cm on 1:50 000 (tussendoelen lines 1503 to 1507); in reverse, 4 cm on 1:10 000 000 (Vilenkin lines 12214 to 12231).
15. Read a scale bar or a scale ratio: «schaal 10:1» for a flea (concretisering lines 2893 to 2898).
16. Relative comparison of two groups: 10 of 24 against 10 of 20 (lines 2905 to 2910).
17. Which offer is better: 4 for 3 against 50% off (tussendoelen lines 1651 to 1656).
18. Share a quantity in a ratio: 2 to 3, or divide the dough 890 g and 980 g (Vilenkin lines 10911 to 10914).
19. Times as many: «в 4 раза больше» (Moro 1977 lines 4682 to 4683); «во сколько раз весь каток больше» (Vilenkin lines 10787 to 10792).
20. Ratio of two quantities in different units: 99 g to 4.4 kg (Vilenkin lines 10808 to 10813).
21. Ratio of a part to a whole from a remainder: «Jorien en Dolf zetten steeds de helft van hun zakgeld op de bank» (concretisering lines 2911 to 2913).
22. Chained multiplicative ratios: MCCME 2021 task 5.3, answer 8.
23. Percent of a percent: Kangoeroe 397 lines 736 to 743, answer 360.
24. Overlap from two percents: Kangoeroe 397 lines 634 to 638, answer 60%.
25. Percent of a circle: 15% of 360 degrees (Kangoeroe 396 lines 1074 to 1077).
26. Speed ratio as a split of a route: MCCME 2023 lines 970 to 973.
27. Reverse from a fraction of a quantity: 9 of 27 children is 1/3 (tussendoelen lines 1513 to 1514).

### The answer contract

Percent answers fit the decimal and integer kinds. A ratio written as two numbers («2 : 9», «1 : 100») fits the approved pair kind, and a set kind is needed where several statements are correct («in which class is the proportion larger»), which is also approved.

## Conclusions

1. The graph's typical groups for P1, P2, P3, P5 and P6 need a decision against the Dutch goals (percent to 100% at group 7, percent of a number and above 100% at group 8, scale at group 7, offers at group 8) before the templates are written.
2. Each P template states the group whose goal it meets, and a template that uses percent before group 7 is an extension and says so.
3. Templates that use the Russian grade 6 proportion topic, the solving of a proportion by the basic property, or a percent in a Russian-only form are extensions and name the Russian grade and passage (`frp25-ooo` lines 583 to 587).
4. Multiplicative-comparison templates ("times as many") are extensions at group 3 and 4, backed by the Russian grade 3 and 4 text (`frp25-noo` line 403) and by Moro grade 2 (lines 4682 to 4683).
5. Each subtype has at least ten templates from different forms above. P1 and P2 can reach ten from forms 3 to 7. P4 can reach twenty from forms 1, 2, 18, 19, 20 and 21.
6. Olympiad-style templates for P3, P4, P5 and P6 draw on MCCME 2021 tasks 5.3 and 7.8, MCCME 2023 grade 8, and the Kangoeroe items at report 396 lines 1114 to 1118 and 1179 to 1181 and report 397 lines 634 to 638. Each names its paper and location.
7. P7 (successive percentages) has no source in the Dutch goals or the extracted Russian text beyond the same-total rule (tussendoelen lines 1586 to 1588). Its templates stay at stretch and are flagged as unverified against a source until one is read.
8. A ratio written as two numbers uses the approved pair kind, and a "which of these hold" template uses the approved set kind.
