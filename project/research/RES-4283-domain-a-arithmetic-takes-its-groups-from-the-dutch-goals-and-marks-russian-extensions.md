# Domain A's templates take their group from the Dutch goals, mark Russian grades 3 to 6 as extensions, and need one answer kind the contract lacks

## Summary

Each domain A node takes its group from the end-of-group Dutch goal that first contains its operation. Eight nodes sit one or two groups later in those goals than in the graph's `typicalGroup`, so the graph must change or explain the gap before templates are written. The Russian programme reaches several operations at the same age or a year earlier; those topics are marked as extensions or as ahead of the Dutch goal. The sources give about 23 distinct task forms. Ten templates per subtype are reachable for every node, but twenty are supported only for the broader nodes: A3 and A4 have fewer forms, and A15 and A16 have too few for ten from these sources. The contract has the set, pair and line answer kinds, and still lacks a kind that places several named digits into cells.

## The question

Which Dutch group sets each node A1 to A16? What does the Russian programme add, and which parts are extensions? Which task forms can the templates use, and which answer kinds do those forms need? The owner's rules apply: the Dutch programme is the base, and a Russian grade n is Dutch group n + 2.

## Method

A research agent drafted this record from the extracted source texts. The main session checked a sample of the quotes of the other domain drafts against those texts and found them accurate; the quotes below were not each rechecked, and each cited line should be confirmed when a template relies on it.

Read in full: the Getalbegrip and Bewerkingen tables of the tussendoelen (lines 468 to 1195) and the Russian grade sections (grades 1 to 4 lines 168 to 173, 256 to 275, 399 to 430, 543 to 564; grades 5 to 9 lines 491 to 512, 563 to 611). Read at the lines cited: the 1F and 1S concretisation (lines 1041 to 1054, 1275 to 1278, 1451 to 1456, 1576 to 1639, 1686 to 1719, 1899 to 1929, 2251 to 2300, 2413 to 2442) and `src/shared/answer.ts`. The two Moro texts and the olympiad papers were searched by keyword and only the quoted lines read: the Tartu Kangaroo papers for grades 3 and 4 and younger, the MCCME 2021 paper, and the Dutch Kangoeroe reports 396 and 397. Only the headings of the MCCME 2023 paper were read.

The columns of the tussendoelen were read from the text layout (headers at lines 470, 728 and 979). An error of one column moves a goal by one group, so each column reading should be checked against the scan.

Not obtained: the group label of the Kangoeroe papers, a Soviet-era text for grades 3 to 6, any Dutch equation goal, and the Vilenkin 2024 text.

### The Russian programme by grade, against the Dutch base

Grade 1 (group 3) sets numbers to 20 and "Увеличение (уменьшение) числа на несколько единиц" (grades 1 to 4 lines 169 to 173), the same as the Dutch group 3 goal. Grade 2 (group 4) sets numbers to 100 and "Табличное умножение в пределах 50" (lines 256 to 258, 273 to 274), the same age as the Dutch group 4 goal.

Grade 3 (group 5) sets numbers to 1000, "Увеличение или уменьшение числа в несколько раз. Кратное сравнение чисел" (lines 401 to 404), and written work to 1000 (lines 424 to 427). Comparison by how many times is not in the Dutch goals read and is an extension.

Grade 4 (group 6) sets numbers to a million with "поразрядное сравнение" (lines 545 to 547). Dutch group 6 covers ±100,000 (tussendoelen lines 563 to 566), and the Dutch million is at group 7 (line 573), so Russian is ahead by a year: it is marked ahead and is not used above the Dutch group 6 range. Grade 4 also sets "Умножение и деление на 10, 100, 1000" (lines 563 to 564) and division of up to six-digit numbers by a two-digit number (lines 562 to 563), where the Dutch group for multiplying by 10 is group 7 (tussendoelen lines 1123 to 1125). Both are ahead.

Grade 5 (group 7) sets "Римская нумерация как пример непозиционной системы счисления" (grades 5 to 9 lines 495 to 496), not in the Dutch goals read, so an extension. Grade 5 also sets "Признаки делимости на 2, 5, 10, 3, 9. Деление с остатком" (lines 507 to 508), a year ahead of Dutch group 8 (tussendoelen lines 1126 to 1127), and rounding (line 498), which matches Dutch group 6 (lines 575 to 576) and is not new.

Grade 6 (group 8) sets "Делители и кратные числа, наибольший общий делитель и наименьшее общее кратное" (lines 570 to 571); the least common multiple is not in the Dutch goals read, so it is an extension. It also sets "Положительные и отрицательные числа ... Модуль числа ... Арифметические действия с положительными и отрицательными числами" (lines 588 to 592) and "Прямоугольная система координат на плоскости" (line 593), both extensions. The Dutch base has temperature below zero: "kan temperatuur onder 0 graden aflezen en interpreteren (bv.: -2 graden...)" (tussendoelen lines 2318 to 2320) and "-5 is kouder dan -2" (lines 4984 to 4985), which read and compare temperatures without arithmetic. Grade 6 also sets solving "перебором возможных вариантов" (line 460 to 461), a reasoning form that the Dutch goals ask for as well (tussendoelen lines 531 to 534).

### Soviet-era evidence in the two Moro texts

The Moro texts are OCR and cover grades 1 and 2 only. Their forms match the Dutch base, with these additions:

- Place-value explanation: «Объясни, что обозначает каждая цифра в записи числа: 15? 13? 18? 11? 10? 20?» (grade 1, lines 1292 to 1293).
- Sum of place-value terms: «Замени каждое число суммой его разрядных слагаемых» (grade 2, lines 220 to 222), which matches the Dutch decomposition goal.
- Make and order numbers from given digits: four two-digit numbers from the digits 3 and 5, in increasing order (grade 2, lines 232 to 236).
- Insert the operator: «Вставь вместо звёздочек знак действия так, чтобы записи были верными» (grade 2, lines 768 to 774). The Dutch goals name no missing-sign form.
- Missing subtrahend: «Найди вычитаемое» (line 854). A hidden number from a clue: «16 меньше задуманного числа на 7» (line 1076).
- Compare and state the difference: «Сравни числа и скажи, на сколько единиц первое из них больше или меньше» (lines 860 to 861); the OCR of the third pair is damaged.

The OCR damages digits, for example «Зи 6, 2и7» for «3 + 6, 2 + 7» (line 934), so each quote is checked against a scan before a template cites it.

### Distinct task forms in the sources

Each form counts once, however its numbers vary.

- F1: compute the expression as written («Reken uit.», concretisering line 1593).
- F2: compute, then say how (tussendoelen lines 779 to 780).
- F3: estimate first, then compute and compare (concretisering lines 1587 to 1590).
- F4: say whether the exact result is larger or smaller than the estimate (tussendoelen lines 781 to 785).
- F5: check an answer and find the error («Ze vergeet de komma in het antwoord», tussendoelen lines 1091 to 1094; Mehmed's «79 is ongeveer 80», concretisering lines 2354 to 2357).
- F6: a one-step word problem in a context (concretisering line 1605).
- F7: a two-step word problem combining operations (tussendoelen lines 956 to 961).
- F8: a missing component (`x + 15 = 42`, graph A11; «Найди вычитаемое», Moro grade 2 line 854).
- F9: insert the missing sign so the record is true (Moro grade 2 lines 768 to 774).
- F10: fill in one repeated digit in a sum (Kangoeroe 396 lines 740 to 742).
- F11: put digits into named cells so that the given sums hold (MCCME 2021 lines 272 to 274, digits 1 to 6 on seven lines each summing to 15; lines 151 to 152, letters A to E and digits 1 to 5).
- F12: find a hidden number from clues (MCCME 2021 lines 134 to 137, answer 729 at line 137).
- F13: choose the true statement among options («24 : 3 is niet gelijk aan 3 : 24», concretisering lines 1103 to 1107; Kangoeroe 396 lines 502 to 505).
- F14: the largest or smallest value under a condition (Kangoeroe 397 lines 518 to 522 and 524 to 528; Kangoeroe 396 lines 850 to 852; Tartu ecolier lines 179 to 184).
- F15: count the numbers that fit a rule («Hoeveel getallen zijn er tot 1000 met één 9 erin?», tussendoelen lines 532 to 534; Kangoeroe 397 lines 531 to 534).
- F16: a chain of fixed steps (Tartu ecolier lines 52 to 55, the grasshopper that jumps to a cell 3 higher).
- F17: erase digits from a written sequence and re-read it (Tartu preecolier lines 116 to 119).
- F18: say what the remainder means in a context (tussendoelen lines 1175 to 1181; concretisering lines 2251 to 2272).
- F19: read the calculator result and say what it means (concretisering lines 2434 to 2439).
- F20: add all numbers made from given digits (Kangoeroe 397 lines 763 to 766, answer 66660).
- F21: make and order numbers from digits (Moro grade 2 lines 232 to 236).
- F22: split a number into place-value terms (Moro grade 2 lines 220 to 222).
- F23: compare two numbers by difference or by how many times (Moro grade 2 lines 860 to 861; federal programme grades 1 to 4 lines 402 to 404).

Forms per node: A1 and A2 reach twenty from F1, F2, F5 to F9, F13, F16, F21 and F23. A3 and A4 have about nine (F1, F2, F5, F6, F7, F9, F13, F15, F16), so ten is reachable and twenty is not supported. A11 has F8, F9 and F13. A12 has F13, F14 and F15. A15 and A16 have only F8 and F9 from these sources, which is not enough for ten, so their records must mark the gap.

### Answer kinds the forms need

`src/shared/answer.ts` now has the set, pair and line kinds that ADR-0520 approved: `set` selects every correct option, `pair` takes two numbers in order, and `lineAnswer` and the `within` distance on a point accept a number placed "ongeveer". One kind is still missing: several named digits in one answer. F11 needs it, because the MCCME tasks place six digits in cells A to F and five in another task, and separate integers do not check that each digit is used once. Operator insertion (F9) is a choice, a remainder (F18) is the quotient-remainder kind, and counting (F15) is an integer.

### Silences and conflicts

- No equation goal appears in the tussendoelen or the concretisation lines read, so A15 and A16 have no Dutch goal.
- No least-common-multiple goal appears in the lines read, and Russian grade 6 has one.
- Roman numerals, negative arithmetic and the coordinate plane appear only in the Russian text.
- Dutch multiplication by 10 is at group 7, and Russian grade 4 has it.
- The concretisation lines carry no group labels in the text read, so its mapping to groups is inferred from the tussendoelen.
- The Kangoeroe papers do not state their group in the text read.

## Conclusions

1. The graph's `typicalGroup` for A2, A3, A4, A6a, A7, A9, A10 and A12 must agree with the Dutch end-of-group goal in the table above, or the node must record why it does not. Each template takes its group from that goal.
2. A Russian topic absent from the Dutch goals is an extension: Roman numerals (grade 5), the least common multiple (grade 6), negative numbers and absolute value (grade 6), the coordinate plane (grade 6) and comparison by how many times (grade 3). Each such template names its grade and passage.
3. A Russian topic that the Dutch goals reach a year later (numbers to a million at grade 4, multiplication by 10 at grade 4, divisibility at grade 5) appears only as a template marked ahead, at the Dutch group where the Russian grade sits.
4. Each subtype has at least ten templates, each from a distinct form above. A3, A4, A15 and A16 cannot reach twenty from these sources, and A15 and A16 cannot reach ten, so their records state that and name the source still to read.
5. Where the subtype allows it, at least two templates per subtype are reasoning tasks, drawn from F13 to F16, and each cites the Dutch reasoning example or the olympiad item it follows.
6. Placing several named digits into cells needs a new answer kind, which is a decision for a later record, before the F11 templates are written.
7. A remainder template states the context that the remainder answers (F18), because the 1S goals name that interpretation as the goal.
8. A calculator template states whether the calculator is allowed, because the 1S text separates tasks without a calculator from calculator tasks.
9. Each quotation from the Moro texts or the olympiad papers is checked against a scan before a template cites it, because the OCR damages digits.
