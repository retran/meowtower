# Domain S follows the Dutch goals from group 3, and the Russian mean and median come only in grade 7, after group 8

## Summary

The Dutch goals are the base for domain S. They start with picture and bar charts in group 3, read tables and bar charts in groups 4 and 5, read pie charts and line graphs in group 6, and reach choosing a representation in group 8. The mean appears in group 6, and the median and the mode appear in no Dutch goal read. The Russian federal programme matches the Dutch order for tables and bar charts in grades 1 to 6. It puts the mean, median, probability and frequency in grade 7, which is Dutch group 9 and outside the range of groups 1 to 8, so these are extensions beyond the range. The graph's typical groups for S1, S2, S4, S6 and S7 do not match the sources. The sources ask for answers in writing, for a drawn chart and for a chosen set, and the contract has no kind for the first two.

## The question

Which Dutch goal sets the range and group of each domain S node (S1 to S7), what do the Russian programme and the Soviet-era textbooks add, which task forms do the sources use for each subtype, and which answer kinds do those forms need?

## Method

A research agent drafted this record from the extracted source texts; the main session checked a sample of the quotes of the sibling drafts against those texts, and the quotes below were not each rechecked.

Read: the Dutch tussendoelen Verbanden table (lines 2651 to 2763) and the per-group Verbanden goals (lines 2976 to 5077); the Dutch concretisation Domein Verbanden parts A, B and C (lines 4944 to 5466); the Russian federal programme grades 1 to 4 (targeted reads), grades 5 and 6 (lines 456 to 748) and grade 7 (lines 3050 to 3131 and 3266 to 3281). Searched and not read in full: the two Moro textbooks (OCR, keyword search only), the MCCME 2021 and 2023 papers, the Tartu papers, the Kangoeroe papers 396 and 397, and Vilenkin grade 6 (2024, a modern textbook, not Soviet); each was searched for table, diagram, graph, mean, probability, pattern and related terms, and the matching passages were read.

Not obtained or not checked: the Kangoeroe and Tartu papers in full, the MCCME 2023 domain S items beyond the mean item, the Moro scans beyond the matched lines, and the Dutch tussendoelen for group 2 separately. The Dutch texts returned no median or mode.

## Findings

### S1 tables: Dutch group 4 for reading and group 5 for unordered data

The group 4 block reads: "kan gegevens uit een eenvoudige tabel, beeld- en staafdiagram aflezen, interpreteren en er bewerkingen mee uitvoeren" (tussendoelen lines 3505 to 3506). Group 5 adds "kan ongeordende gegevens in een eenvoudige tabel verwerken" (line 3831). The concretisation at 1F: "Weten dat je gegevens uit een tabel zoals een dienstregeling van bus of trein, of een lesrooster kunt aflezen" (lines 4949 to 4951); at 1S: "Kunnen aflezen van gegevens uit complexere tabellen, ook waarin meer gegevens gecombineerd worden" (lines 4952 to 4953). The graph sets S1 at `typicalGroup` 5 (`graph.yaml` line 994), and the Dutch text sets reading at group 4.

### S2 bar and line charts: Dutch group 3 for picture and bar charts, group 6 for line graphs

Group 3: "kan een eenvoudig beelddiagram met plaatjes en staafdiagram met hokjes aflezen" (tussendoelen line 3214). Group 6: "kan gegevens in eenvoudige cirkeldiagrammen en lijngrafieken aflezen en interpreteren" (line 4210), and "kan beelddiagrammen waarin de beeldfiguren waarden hebben die groter zijn dan 1 aflezen" (lines 4208 to 4209). The graph sets S2 at `typicalGroup` 5 (`graph.yaml` line 1004), while the Dutch bar-chart reading goal sits at group 3.

### S3 mean: Dutch group 6 with simple numbers

"begrijpt wat 'gemiddelde' betekent en kan het gemiddelde berekenen in eenvoudige situaties met eenvoudige getallen" (tussendoelen lines 1293 to 1294, the group 6 column of the operations block). Group 7: "kent de betekenis van het begrip 'gemiddelde', kan een gemiddelde uitrekenen en uitleggen hoe hij rekent" (lines 1303 to 1304). The graph's group 6 for S3 matches (`graph.yaml` line 1017). The concretisation lists "Berekenen van het gemiddelde" as example tasks (lines 1850 and 1855), and the section heading at that line was not checked.

### S4 pie chart: Dutch group 6 for reading and group 7 for percentages

Group 6: "kan gegevens in eenvoudige cirkeldiagrammen ... aflezen" (tussendoelen line 4210). Group 7: "kan gegevens uit een beschrijving of tabel verwerken in een voorgestructureerde cirkeldiagram (bv. Percentages inkleuren in een cirkel die in tien gelijke punten is verdeeld)" (lines 4653 to 4654). The choice of chart is group 8: "kan beargumenteren welke grafische voorstelling: beelddiagram, staafdiagram, lijngrafiek, cirkeldiagram, het beste past bij verzamelde gegevens" (lines 5074 to 5075). The graph's S4 at group 7 (`graph.yaml` line 1033) is later than the Dutch reading goal at group 6.

### S5 global graphs: Dutch group 6 for drawing from a text and reading, group 8 for trends and forecast

Group 6: "kan een lijngrafiek globaal tekenen op basis van een beschrijving in woorden en omgekeerd" (tussendoelen lines 4211 to 4213). Group 8: "kan trends herkennen, conclusies trekken of voorspellingen doen" (lines 5064 to 5065). Concretisation 1S: "Op basis van een grafiek of diagram voorspellingen doen over een toekomstige situatie" (lines 5456 to 5457).

### S6 patterns: Dutch group 3 for ordered figures, group 5 for number rows

Group 3: "ziet het verband of patroon in eenvoudig geordende figuren en kan dit patroon voortzetten (zoals bij een ketting met een patroon op kleur of vorm)" (tussendoelen lines 3216 to 3217). Group 5: "kan eenvoudige verbanden en patronen in rijen getallen en figuren herkennen en op basis hiervan de rijen voortzetten (bv.: 1 - 3 - 5 - 7 - …; 100 - 93 - 86 - 79 - …;)" (lines 3833 to 3834). Group 8: "Dit betreft getalsmatige patronen (rijen voortzetten) ... volgens eenvoudige rekenregels" (lines 5070 to 5073). The graph sets S6 at `typicalGroup` 5 (`graph.yaml` line 1061), but the first pattern goal is at group 3.

### S7 median and mode: no Dutch goal read

The median and the mode appear in none of the Dutch texts searched: the tussendoelen, the concretisation, and a search across the scratchpad found no mediaan or modus. The graph sets S7 at `typicalGroup` 8 (`graph.yaml` line 1077), so no source supports that group.

### The Russian programme matches the Dutch order for tables and bar charts

Grade 1: "Чтение таблицы, содержащей не более 4 данных. Извлечение данного из строки или столбца" (grades 1 to 4 lines 203 to 204), and for patterns "Закономерность в ряду заданных объектов: её обнаружение, продолжение ряда" (lines 195 to 196). Grade 2: "Работа с таблицами: извлечение и использование для ответа на вопрос информации, представленной в таблице (например, таблицы сложения, умножения, графика дежурств)" (lines 313 to 315). Grade 3: "Столбчатая диаграмма: чтение, использование данных для решения учебных и практических задач" (lines 471 to 472). Grade 4: "Запись информации в предложенной таблице, на столбчатой диаграмме" (lines 597 to 604). Russian grade 1 is level with Dutch group 3 for tables, grade 2 with group 4, and grade 3 for bar charts is behind Dutch groups 3 and 4 and level with group 5; this is a mapping and not a stated equivalence.

Grade 5: "Представление данных в виде таблиц, столбчатых диаграмм" (grades 5 to 9 line 542), and tasks solved «перебором возможных вариантов» (line 460). Grade 6: "Представление данных с помощью таблиц и диаграмм. Столбчатые диаграммы: чтение и построение. Чтение круговых диаграмм" (lines 613 to 614), and the planned result "Извлекать информацию, представленную в таблицах, на линейной, столбчатой или круговой диаграммах" (lines 742 to 743). Dutch pie-chart reading is at group 6 and the Russian one at grade 6 (group 8), so the Russian programme is behind here.

### Russian-only topics beyond group 8 are extensions

Grade 7 (Dutch group 9): "Описательная статистика: среднее арифметическое, медиана, размах, наибольшее и наименьшее значения набора числовых данных" (grades 5 to 9 lines 3127 to 3128), and "Случайный эксперимент (опыт) и случайное событие. Вероятность и частота" (line 3130). These are extensions beyond the range of groups 1 to 8 and support S7 only as extensions. The grade 5 and 6 text contains no mean, median or probability (searched in lines 491 to 748).

### The Soviet-era textbooks give only tables and patterns

Moro grade 1 (1990): «Обозначь в таблице числа: 19; 11; 10; 20; 12.» (moro1-1990 lines 1317 to 1318); «4. Какие числа пропущены в этом ряду?» (line 1328); «Продолжи узор» (line 2974). The OCR is poor, and the row at line 1331 is garbled.

Moro grade 2 (1977): «236. Заполни таблицу. Назови значения первого слагаемого, второго слагаемого, суммы.» (moro2-1977 lines 2903 to 2907); «237. Рассмотри таблицу и заполни её. Как можно записать короче?» (lines 2931 to 2933); «317. 1) Заполни таблицу ... 2) Используя таблицу, укажи те значения буквы ... при которых верно неравенство 17+6<25» (lines 3619 to 3626). A search for диаграмм, график, вероятн and среднее found no match in the grade 2 text. This is the textbook evidence available; it does not cover domain S statistics.

### Vilenkin grade 6 (2024, modern, not Soviet)

It has the mean: «Ответ задачи можно получить, если найти среднее арифметическое скоростей» (vilenkin6-2024 lines 1111 to 1112) and thermometer readings for a mean (lines 1164 to 1165); a pie chart: «На рисунке 1.5 изображена круговая диаграмма» (line 2192); bar and pie charts from a table: «Постройте столбчатую и круговую диаграммы длин крупных рек России по таблице» (lines 2572 to 2573); and a frequency table: «Составьте частотную таблицу роста учеников» (line 3643). It is neither Soviet nor the federal programme; it shows what a 2024 grade 6 text asks.

### Olympiad and Kangoeroe items that fit domain S

- MCCME 2023, grade 8, task 8.1: «Клетки пирамиды заполнили по следующему правилу: над каждыми двумя соседними числами записали их среднее арифметическое. Некоторые числа стёрли» (oly-mccme-2023 lines 861 to 863), answer 6 (line 869). A mean-based olympiad task.
- A Kangoeroe probability item: «Je gooit met twee dobbelstenen en berekent de som van de ogen ... Welke van de volgende uitkomsten heeft de grootste kans?» with the sum table «In de tabel zie je alle mogelijke combinaties en hun som» (report 396 or 397, lines 1097 to 1102; the search output did not separate the two files, so the file is unconfirmed). A graph-reading item: «In de grafiek kun je de waterhoogte h cm na t minuten aflezen» (around lines 554 to 556 in the same search output).
- Tables as grids in the Tartu and MCCME papers are not data tables: Tartu «3. В каждой клетке таблицы записано одно действие» (oly-tartu-ecolier lines 31 to 33) and MCCME 2021 «Числа от 1 до 9 расставили в клетки таблицы 3 × 3» (line 908) are number grids that fit domain N or logic. The Tartu item about erasing digits from 1 to 18 (oly-tartu-preecolier lines 116 to 119) is closer to counting.

### The concretisation asks for explanations and drawing

«Welke grafiek vind je het handigst hier? Leg uit waarom» (concretisering lines 5328 to 5330); «Teken eens een lijn in de grafiek die bij dit verhaaltje hoort» (line 5199); «Maak een staafdiagram bij de gegevens. Maak (kleur) onderstaande cirkeldiagrammen» (lines 5349 to 5351). These need an answer kind for a written reason and for a drawn chart.

### The sources conflict on group for five nodes

S1 (graph 5, Dutch 4), S2 (graph 5, Dutch 3 and 4), S4 (graph 7, Dutch 6), S6 (graph 5, Dutch 3) and S7 (graph 8, no Dutch goal, Russian grade 7 = group 9). S3 and S5 match the Dutch goals. The Dutch texts contain no probability or median goal, and probability appears in the Russian programme only in grade 7. Neither Moro text contains диаграмм, график, среднее or вероятн in its matches.

## Conclusions

1. Templates for S1 to S6 follow the Dutch goals: tables and bar charts from group 3 to 5, pie charts and line graphs in group 6, the mean in group 6, and the choice of a representation in group 8. Each template states its group range.
2. Median, mode and probability are Russian grade 7 extensions, beyond the range of groups 1 to 8, and their templates are marked as extensions with the grade and the passage (`frp25-ooo` lines 3127 to 3130).
3. S7 at `typicalGroup` 8 has no source. Its templates either move to group 9 as extensions or the graph's typical group is corrected; the decision belongs to the design step.
4. The graph's typical groups for S1, S2, S4 and S6 conflict with the Dutch goals and need a decision before the templates are assigned to groups.
5. Each subtype needs at least ten templates. The sources support about ten distinct forms for S1 (read a value, the difference of two cells, a timetable, a missing cell, fill a table, make a table from a text, explain a table, find the mistake, choose a value, work out a total), so the target of twenty is not yet met for any subtype.
6. The contract needs a kind for a written reason, since the sources ask «leg uit waarom» and «heeft hij gelijk?», and a kind for a drawn chart or line, for example «teken een lijn» and «maak een staafdiagram». The set kind, already approved, covers selecting every correct option.
7. Mean templates use simple numbers and state the procedure, because the group 6 goal asks for simple numbers and the group 7 goal asks the child to explain the method.
8. Reasoning templates for each subtype draw on the Dutch examples (for example «Welke grafiek ... Leg uit waarom») and on the Kangoeroe and MCCME items above where they fit.
