# The draft proposes rewards for effort, with accuracy bonuses on top and no lottery

## Summary

The draft proposes that the main rewards come from play and effort: experience, buttons, a chest after every room, floors, quests and ranks. Accuracy adds bonuses on top: star-steel shards, star yarn, better chest rewards and success-branch secrets. A mistake or «Не знаю» ("I don't know") takes nothing away, and speed earns nothing. The chest shows three rewards and the player picks one, and the Director fills it deterministically, with no chances or hidden odds. The shop sells for buttons only, at fixed prices, and never for money. The draft lists the currencies and items, how each is earned and what each does, and the commercial game patterns it refuses. The draft left the amounts blank, and research proposes them: a token button per answer with most buttons from quests and chests, shards and yarn from a base plus an accuracy bonus, and shop prices and forge recipes that buy about two notable items a week. This record covers rewards, goal horizons, reference games, the economy, the chest, the shop and the refused patterns; experience and levels are in RES-2000, familiars in RES-1900, and guiding threads and the guards on measurement in other records.

## The question

How does the draft propose to reward the player so that she keeps playing without being pushed to guess, rush or pay? The draft assumes accuracy bonuses can sit on top of effort rewards without distorting measurement, provided quick guesses earn no bonus. That assumption carries risk: any reward that depends on being right gives a reason to avoid «Не знаю», so the guards against guessing carry the whole weight.

## Findings

### Main rewards come from play and effort, and accuracy adds bonuses on top

Main rewards: experience, buttons, a chest after each room, floors, quests and ranks; in the MVP, without ranks (see the resolved finding below). Accuracy bonuses on top: star-steel shards for clean untanglings, star yarn for clean rows, higher chest quality and success-branch secrets. A mistake and «Не знаю» take nothing away. Speed earns no reward.

### Guards on measurement stop bonuses from pushing the player to guess

Quick guesses earn no bonuses, and «Не знаю» earns the same base experience. The guards are set out in another section of the draft, «Защита измерения» (Guarding the measurement).

### An Ascent gives rewards for completion, not accuracy

An Ascent gives no accuracy bonuses: rewards come for finishing. Deferred until after the MVP by the draft.

### Guiding threads are a separate resource earned by play

Guiding threads are earned by play and spent on hints and explanations. The draft sets them out in its section «Путеводные нити» (Guiding threads).

### The draft chooses a visible, deterministic chest over a lottery

Three rewards are visible at once and the player picks one. The Director fills the chest deterministically by rules: one item from each of three different categories, weighted towards what she has little of. It uses no chances, hidden odds or "guaranteed after N chests". The draft gives no reason in this paragraph, but it lists lottery chests, chances and pity among the commercial patterns it refuses.

| Situation | Chest quality |
| --- | --- |
| After an `alt` branch | three ordinary or good rewards |
| After a `success` branch | one of the three is sparkling |
| Guardian chest on a "triumph" | a sparkling reward plus the floor's special reward |

The quality levels are ordinary, good and sparkling. Series add shards and yarn, not chances; a series is the clean-row streak inside one adventure (see the resolved finding on clean rows). Rare items come at story milestones and in sparkling slots, by the rules of the economy.

### The collection is paced for a school year of daily play

Each week gives one or two notable items and a new roster familiar. Every two weeks gives a new chapter.

### The free mode gives decor, lore and battles but not main growth

The free mode, «Свободная прогулка» ("Free Walk"), gives decor, lore pages and familiar battles, but not main growth, so the main rewards stay in the adventure of the day. Deferred until after the MVP by the draft.

### The draft chooses a fixed-price shop over lotteries and money

Fixed prices are visible at once. The stock holds 6 items, and 2 of them change each morning by the seed of the day. The shop takes buttons only: no money, no chances and no "the discount expires". The draft gave no prices and no rule for which 2 change; the resolved finding on amounts below proposes both.

### The draft refuses eight patterns from commercial games

- purchases with money and any "premium" currency;
- lottery chests, chances and pity;
- penalties for a missed day and for losing a streak;
- "offer expires" timers and countdowns;
- notifications and "come back" reminders;
- leaderboards and rewards for speed;
- penalties, losses and "damage" for mistakes or «Не знаю»; resetting what was earned;
- guilt and attachment lines from characters.

### Resolved: ranks and stats are not rewards in the MVP, and its main rewards are experience, buttons, chests, floors and quests

Proposed by research on 2026-09-26; the owner approves it with this record.

The rewards section lists «ранги» ("ranks") among the main rewards, the goal horizons list ranks by season, and the pattern table names stats among visible growth. The MVP section keeps the heroine at rank E after Session 0: «в MVP есть только ранг E после Сессии 0» ("the MVP has only rank E after Session 0"), and defers stats. RES-2000 weighs putting both in the MVP, ranks alone, or neither, and holds the reason for neither: the first rank change falls about ten weeks after a September start, past the two weeks of acceptance play, and stats need a screen, a scene-order field and story tuning the player wouldn't see pay off in that time. In the MVP the main rewards are experience, buttons, a chest after each room, floors and quests; ranks and stats join the list when they ship. A season finale in the MVP awards its chapter title and main reward and no rank (RES-1600).

Decided on 2026-09-27 by research, on the owner's instruction to answer the open questions; the owner approves it with this record. A level-up in the MVP carries no grant: the ceremony alone. The amounts in the next finding are balanced without a level-up grant, and growth already shows through experience, familiars and quests (RES-2000).

### Resolved: buttons come mostly from quests and chests, shards and yarn have a base from floors and Guardians plus an accuracy bonus, and prices buy about two notable items a week

Proposed by research on 2026-09-26; the owner approves it with this record.

The draft left every amount blank. I sized them against the figures other records already give: 25-35 scored first attempts an adventure plus about 3 unscored warm-ups (RES-0100), 3 maths floors a day with 1-2 rooms each, so about 4-5 room chests and 3 floor chests, about one Guardian an adventure, a room clean share near 0.6-0.7 (RES-1700), 8-10 guiding threads a day with a cap of 30 (RES-0500), and this record's own pace of one or two notable items a week. All amounts are starting values in versioned content files (`content/shop.ru.json`, `content/recipes.ru.json`, RES-2500), to be checked against the two weeks of play in stage 0.3.

Two ways to pay buttons were weighed:

- Most buttons per answer. The player sees income after every answer. But the Director plans the adventure's volume from her pace (RES-0100), so a faster player gets more tasks in the hour, and per-answer income then pays for speed, which conclusion 2 forbids.
- A token button per answer, and most buttons from daily quests and chest picks. Quests and chests come per day and per room whatever her pace, so speed earns almost nothing, and she still sees a button land after each answer.

The second way wins. Two ways to pay shards and yarn were weighed:

- Accuracy only. Clean work pays fastest, but on a weak day the forge stalls, which reads as a penalty, and a player at the frontier on purpose (RES-1000) would forge least.
- A base from floors and Guardians plus an accuracy bonus, the split the draft's economy table already gives. Every profile forges; clean work speeds it by about 40 %.

The second way wins, because it keeps the draft's split and the rule that nothing reads as a loss. The amounts:

By my calculation a typical day (about 31 first attempts, 28 of them scored, clean share 0.65, one Guardian, two chest picks of buttons) gives about 100 buttons, 24 shards and 7 to 8 yarn. A day at clean share 0.4 gives the same buttons, about 17 shards and about 4 yarn. The clean-row counts come from the run lengths of a 28-task day: about 3 clean rows and 1.4 big ones at share 0.7, and about 1 clean row at 0.4.

Shop prices and the shelf:

| Category | Price in buttons |
| --- | --- |
| Curiosity | 80 |
| Accessory | 200 |
| Outfit | 450 |

A week of about 700 buttons buys an outfit and an accessory, or most of a focus. The shelf has six slots: 1 focus, 2 outfits, 2 accessories and 1 curiosity. Each morning the seed of the day replaces 2 slots with items of the same category: first any slot she emptied by buying, then the slot that has stood longest. So an item stays at most three days, a bought slot refills the next morning, and the shelf never offers something she owns. An item that left unsold can come back after 7 days. Decor gets no slot until decor ships. The MVP shop catalogue needs at least 20 items outside the forge recipes, because 6 on the shelf plus 2 new ones each morning use up 20 before the first unsold item may return.

### Resolved: a chest offers three different categories drawn from cosmetics, shards and yarn, buttons and Diary pages, and the Observatory is the ninth floor

Decided on 2026-09-27 by research, on the owner's instruction to answer the open questions; the owner approves it with this record.

The draft says the Director fills a chest with "one item from each of three different categories", and its economy table names four: cosmetics, shards, buttons and pages. The draft never said which three. A chest now offers three different categories drawn from these four:

- cosmetics: an outfit, an accessory or a curiosity;
- shards and yarn: star-steel shards or star yarn, at the amounts in the finding on amounts above;
- buttons: 15, 25 or 40 by quality;
- Diary pages: a page of the Keeper's Diary (CAN-0090), which this record elsewhere calls a Journal page.

Two readings were weighed. A fixed three, such as always cosmetics, shards and buttons, is simpler to build, but pages would then never come from a chest, and the draft's table lists them. Three drawn from four keeps all four sources and lets the Director's rule "weighted towards what she has little of" choose the category left out. The second reading wins. How the Director measures "what she has little of" stays open.

The draft's pattern table says «9 этажей-миров» ("9 floor-worlds"), and the familiars section names a ring of 8 elements with 8 floors. The two agree: the ninth floor is the Observatory, which holds science and sits outside the ring of eight elements (CAN-0040). The draft's open point about the ninth floor is therefore closed.

### Resolved: the Director weights a chest towards the category where she is furthest from her next goal

Decided on 2026-09-27 by research, on the owner's instruction to answer the open questions; the owner approves it with this record.

For each of the four categories the Director computes how far her stock falls short of the next thing that stock buys, as a share from 0 to 1:

| Category | Stock | Next goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cosmetics | items she owns | the number of shop and forge items open to her |
| shards and yarn | her shards and yarn | the next forge recipe she can't yet make |
| buttons | her buttons | the cheapest shelf item she doesn't own |
| Diary pages | pages she holds | pages the campaign has released so far |

The chest takes the three categories with the largest shortfall. A tie breaks by the day's seed, so the same state gives the same chest and the choice never depends on her answers. Two options lost: counting raw stock, which compares buttons with pages on no common scale, and a fixed rotation, which ignores what she lacks and so drops the draft's rule.

### Resolved: a series is the clean-row streak inside one adventure

Proposed by research on 2026-09-26; the owner approves it with this record.

The draft rejects day streaks, so «Серии» ("series") here can only mean the streak of clean outcomes that RES-1700 counts within an adventure. A clean row is the streak reaching 3; a big clean row is the streak reaching 5, 10, 15 and so on. Only clean scored first attempts that are not rapid guesses grow it. RES-1700 holds the full rule and the reason.

## Conclusions

1. A mistake or «Не знаю» must never take away anything the player has earned.
2. No reward must depend on answering speed.
3. Quick guesses must earn no accuracy bonus.
4. Every chest must show its three rewards before the player picks, and the Director must fill it deterministically by visible rules, with no random draw, hidden odds or pity counter.
5. After a `success` branch one of the three chest rewards must be sparkling, and after an `alt` branch all three must be ordinary or good.
6. The shop must sell for buttons only, at fixed visible prices, with 6 items of which 2 change each morning by the seed of the day.
7. Guiding threads must never be sold, and threads above 30 must turn into buttons.
8. Tricks must change only animation and sound, never power.
9. The game must offer no purchase with money, no premium currency, no countdown timer and no leaderboard.
10. No character must speak a line of guilt or attachment to bring the player back.
11. A success-branch secret the player missed must return later.
12. Game texts and prompts must not name the commercial reference games.
13. The shop must be open from day one of the MVP and the Thread Forge from the first week.
14. In the MVP ranks and stats must not appear as rewards, and a level-up must grant no stat points.
15. Every first attempt, whatever its outcome, must give 1 button, and a second attempt none; each daily quest must give 10 buttons; a chest's button reward must be 15, 25 or 40 by quality; each thread above the cap must turn into 2 buttons.
16. Each clean scored first attempt that is not a rapid guess must give 1 star-steel shard, and each finished floor 2 shards in any state; a chest's shard reward must be 5, 8 or 12 by quality.
17. Each finished Guardian problem must give 3 star yarn in any ending, each clean row and each big clean row 1 yarn, and an optional pattern row 2; a chest's yarn reward must be 2, 3 or 4 by quality.
18. Each room chest must give 1 of its floor's material and each floor chest 2, beside the pick; each success-branch find 1 more, and each Observatory visit 1 moon mote.
19. Shop prices must start at 80 buttons for a curiosity, 200 for an accessory, 450 for an outfit and 800 for a focus, read from a versioned content file.
20. The shelf must hold 1 focus, 2 outfits, 2 accessories and 1 curiosity; each morning the seed of the day must replace 2 slots with items of the same category, sold slots first and then the longest-standing; the shelf must never offer an owned item, and an unsold item must not return for 7 days.
21. Every forge recipe must have three rows, shards, yarn and one floor material, at 5/2/2 for the first recipe, 25/6/3 for an accessory, 80/20/5 for an outfit and 160/40/6 for a focus, read from a versioned content file.
22. The MVP must ship the six recipes in the resolved finding on amounts and a shop catalogue of at least 20 items outside them.
23. In the MVP a level-up must carry no grant, only the ceremony, as research decided on 2026-09-27 on the owner's instruction, because the amounts in this record are balanced without one.
24. Every chest must offer three rewards from three different categories drawn from four: cosmetics, shards and yarn, buttons and Diary pages, as research decided on 2026-09-27 on the owner's instruction.
25. The Tower must have nine floor-worlds, counting the Observatory as the ninth floor outside the ring of eight elements.
26. The Director must fill a chest from the three categories where her stock falls furthest short of its next goal, breaking a tie by the day's seed and never by her answers.
