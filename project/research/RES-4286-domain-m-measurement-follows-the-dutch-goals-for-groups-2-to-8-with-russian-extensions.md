# Domain M follows the Dutch goals for groups 2 to 8, with Russian grades 1 to 6 as extensions, across 14 nodes and 26 subtypes

## Summary

The Dutch goals set each measurement node's group, and the templates keep their numbers and units inside that range. The Russian federal programme teaches the same units in the same order, one to two years earlier for length, time and the area formula, and adds a few topics the Dutch goals do not name: centners, work problems, speed in metres per minute and multiplicative comparison of masses. Those are marked as extensions. The reasoning tasks that the Dutch goals list, and the olympiad items below, supply the reasoning templates. The answer contract needs a tolerance for estimates, which the approved number-line distance supplies, and a way to give a date.

## The question

What must the templates for domain M ask, and at which group, so that they follow the Dutch goals (the base), add Russian grade extensions marked as such, and reach at least ten templates per subtype with distinct task forms, including olympiad-style reasoning? The sources must show which task forms exist and which answer kinds the forms need.

## Method

A research agent drafted this record from the extracted source texts; the main session checked a sample of the quotes of the sibling drafts against those texts, and the quotes below were not each rechecked. Read: the graph entries for M1 to M14 (`content/graph.yaml` lines 664 to 902), the catalogue rows in RES-1200 (lines 271 to 284) and the node meanings in RES-0800 (lines 177 to 192); the Dutch tussendoelen Meten section for groups 2 to 8 (lines 1683 to 2480) and the 1F and 1S concretisation for Meten (lines 3466 to 3805); the federal programme for grades 1 to 4 (lines 167 to 592) and for grades 5 and 6 at base level (lines 535 to 642); the two Moro textbooks (the 1990 grade 1 and the 1977 grade 2, both Soviet-era, read in part and searched by keyword); and the olympiad items cited, read at the lines named. Every other olympiad line below was found by keyword search and is marked as such.

Not read in full: the Vilenkin grade 6 (2024) text, the Dutch Meetkunde subdomain (no circle or triangle goal was located), the MCCME 2021 and 2023 papers beyond the items cited, and the Kangoeroe reports.

## Findings

### The Dutch goals set the range of each node, from groups 3 to 8

- M1 (length and mass, conversion): group 5 names the metric units, "kent naast de meter en de centimeter ook de standaardmaten millimeter, decimeter, kilometer ... 1 km = 1000 m" (tussendoelen lines 1697 to 1700). Group 6 has decimal lengths, "4,5 km is 4 kilometer en 500 meter" (lines 1972 to 1973). Group 7 has decimal mass, "0,358 kg = 358 g" (lines 2252 to 2254). Group 8 converts with decimals in both directions (lines 1992 to 1994, the 1S column).
- M2 (time): group 4 reads clocks to the half hour and quarter hour, "Het is half 2 uur. Hoe laat is het over 3 uur?" (lines 1882 to 1883). Group 5 reads clocks to the minute and intervals, "tijdsduur berekenen tussen twee tijdstippen in uren en minuten" (lines 1876 to 1877 and 1885 to 1886). Group 7 counts days between dates, "het aantal dagen berekenen tussen twee data in hetzelfde jaar" (lines 2350 to 2352).
- M3 (money): group 2 pays with coins up to 10 euro (lines 1930 to 1931). Group 5 works out change, "hoeveel geld een klant terugkrijgt ... Het kost € 28,95" (lines 1931 to 1933). Group 7 rounds to 5 cents (lines 2409 to 2410).
- M4 (perimeter): group 5 measures the perimeter of squares and rectangles on squared paper (lines 1743 to 1746). Group 6 uses the formula, "2 x (l + b) of 2 x l + 2 x b" (lines 1983 to 1984).
- M5 (area): group 5 counts squares, "de oppervlakte van een vierkant of rechthoek op roosterpapier globaal en precies bepalen" (lines 1757 to 1759). Group 6 gives the formula "l x b" (lines 2070 to 2074).
- M6 (triangle): group 7, "de oppervlakte van driehoeken en rechthoekige veelhoeken berekenen" (lines 2078 to 2079).
- M7 (volume): group 7 names cubic units and uses "lengte x breedte x hoogte" (lines 2156 to 2157 and 2172 to 2175).
- M8 (speed): group 6, "Een wandelaar legt ongeveer 5 kilometer af in een uur" (lines 2467 to 2468). Group 7 computes average speed, "(gemiddelde) snelheden berekenen en vergelijken" (lines 2466 to 2469).
- M9 (area units): group 7 names km2, hm2, dam2, hectare and are, and "1 km2 = 100 ha = 1.000.000 m2" (lines 2056 to 2058).
- M10 (capacity): group 6, "1 dl = 10 cl; 1 l = 10 dl; 1 l = 100 cl" (lines 2156 to 2158). The 1S column gives "1 dm3 = 1 liter = 1000 ml en 1 m3 = 1000 liter" (lines 2163 to 2164).
- M11 (circle): no Dutch goal was located in the sections read, because the Meetkunde subdomain was not read.
- M12 (benchmarks): group 7, "1 hectare is ongeveer twee voetbalvelden groot" (lines 2072 to 2073). The 1S column gives "Een hele grote stap is ongeveer 1 meter" (concretisering line 3771).
- M13 (instruments): the 1F concretisation names the instruments, "liniaal, maatbeker, weegschaal, thermometer" (concretisering lines 3684 to 3688). Group 6 reads a thermometer to tenths (tussendoelen lines 2318 to 2319). Group 7 reads below zero, "-2 graden betekent 'twee graden onder nul'" (lines 2319 to 2320).
- M14 (average speed and work): group 7 covers average speed (lines 2466 to 2469). Work problems were not found in the Dutch goals read. M14 is stretch in the graph, so it stays a stretch subtype.

### The 1F and 1S concretisation gives the reference contexts

The concretisation asks for notation and reading, not only computation. 1F: "Hoe lang is het velletje A4-papier? Meet het met je liniaal" (concretisering lines 3693 to 3694). 1S: "Welke tijdmaat gebruik je?" (line 3756) and "Hoe duur is een brood ongeveer: 20 eurocent, 2 euro of 20 euro?" (lines 3801 to 3802), and "Een auto rijdt op de snelweg vaak 120 km per uur" (line 3799).

### The Russian programme gives the same units by grade

Grade n is Dutch group n + 2.

- Grade 1 (group 3): "Длина и её измерение. Единицы длины ... сантиметр, дециметр" (grades 1 to 4 lines 174 to 175).
- Grade 2 (group 4): mass in kilograms, time in hours and minutes, and length to millimetres, "Соотношение между единицами величины (в пределах 100)" (lines 260 to 263).
- Grade 3 (group 5): gram, rouble and kopeck, second, and the square metre (lines 405 to 416). The text reads "Периметр многоугольника: измерение, вычисление" (line 451) and "Вычисление площади прямоугольника (квадрата)" (lines 452 to 455), so the area formula is in Russian grade 3, one year ahead of the Dutch formula in group 6.
- Grade 4 (group 6): tonne and centner (lines 552 to 553), days to century (line 554), litre and km/h, m/min and m/s (lines 555 to 557), perimeter and area of figures made of two or three rectangles (lines 591 to 592).
- Grade 5, base level (group 7): "Решение задач ... скорость, время, расстояние" (grades 5 to 9 lines 537 to 540), area of rectangles and polygons and their units (lines 554 to 556), and the volume of a cuboid (line 561).
- Grade 6, base level (group 8): speed and work (lines 605 to 608), "Оценка и прикидка, округление результата" (line 611), and "Приближенное измерение длины окружности, площади круга" (lines 632 to 634).

### Extensions: topics the Dutch goals do not name

Each is marked as an extension with its Russian grade and line: the centner (grades 1 to 4 line 553, grade 4); work problems with productivity and joint work (lines 575 to 577, grade 4), which connect to M14; speed in metres per minute (line 557, grade 4); multiplicative comparison of masses, "тяжелее-легче в..." (lines 405 to 406, grade 3); and the circumference and area of a circle at base level (grades 5 to 9 line 634, grade 6), since the Dutch circle goal was not located.

### Where the sources differ

The Russian programme teaches the area formula in grade 3 and the Dutch do so in group 6. Russian time units reach the century in grade 4, and the Dutch century goal is group 8 (tussendoelen lines 2355 to 2357). The Dutch goals count money up to 5,000 euro from group 2 onwards in the 1F and 1S context. The Russian text is silent on money above 1,000 roubles in grades 1 to 4 and states no range.

### The Moro textbooks give Soviet grade 1 and 2 tasks for mass, capacity, time and perimeter

Grade 1 (1990): «Масса первой гири 1 килограмм, масса второй гири 2 килограмма» (moro1-1990 lines 1046 to 1047); «В ведро входит 10 л воды. Сколько литров воды можно добавить в ведро, если в нём 6 л? 4 л? 7 л воды?» (lines 1083 to 1085). The mass and capacity tasks take the form of finding a missing quantity.

Grade 2 (1977): the contents list "Периметр" (line 146, OCR), "Меры времени ... Час, минута" (lines 178 to 185) and "Килограмм, грамм" (line 199). The tasks include «Измерь стороны и найди периметр каждого треугольника» (lines 13633 to 13634); the time tasks include «Урок начался в 11 часов 45 минут. Покажи на модели часов» (lines 10571 to 10572) and «Сколько минут составляет 2 часа?» (line 10581); and a money task reads «Мама дала Васе 1 рубль ... за ... копеек» (lines 2956 to 2958), with a blank to fill in. The grade 2 contents are OCR and some headings are garbled. These Soviet textbooks show the forms; they are not a curriculum level.

### Task forms the sources use for domain M

Each form is distinct, and a subtype can draw on several.

1. Convert between units, both directions, whole numbers (M1, tussendoelen lines 1697 to 1700 and 2254 to 2255).
2. Convert a decimal length or mass (M1, lines 1972 to 1973 and 2252 to 2254).
3. Read a scale: ruler, measuring jug, thermometer, scale (M13, concretisering lines 3684 to 3700; Moro 1990 lines 1070 to 1085).
4. Choose the best unit for a context (M1 and M12, tussendoelen lines 1708 to 1710, 2008 to 2014, 2214 to 2216).
5. Estimate an object's size against a reference, "ongeveer" (M12, concretisering lines 3771 to 3777; tussendoelen lines 2008 to 2014).
6. Read a clock to the minute, or write the time in words (M2, tussendoelen lines 1876 to 1877; concretisering lines 3748 to 3750).
7. Find the end time from a start time and a duration (M2, tussendoelen lines 1889 to 1892; Moro 1977 lines 10575 to 10577).
8. Count days between two dates (M2, lines 2350 to 2352).
9. Pay an exact amount with coins or notes (M3, lines 1930 to 1931).
10. Find change from a note (M3, lines 1931 to 1933; Moro 1977 lines 2956 to 2958).
11. Estimate a price from a list (M3, concretisering lines 3792 to 3802).
12. Round a money amount to 5 cents (M3, tussendoelen lines 2409 to 2410).
13. Find the perimeter of a drawn shape (M4, lines 1743 to 1746; Moro 1977 line 13633).
14. Find the side from a perimeter, with several answers (M4, lines 1751 to 1753, «Hoe lang kunnen de lengte en breedte dan zijn?»).
15. Count squares on a grid to get an area (M5, lines 1757 to 1759).
16. Compute an area by formula (M5, lines 2070 to 2074).
17. Find which rectangles have the same area but different perimeters (M4 and M5, lines 2090 to 2094).
18. Find the area of a triangle or of a composite shape (M6, lines 2078 to 2084).
19. Count cubes or apply length times width times height (M7, lines 2171 to 2175).
20. Compute speed, time or distance from two given values (M8, lines 2466 to 2470; federal programme grades 5 to 9 lines 537 to 540).
21. Convert km/h to m/s (M8 and M14, lines 2466 to 2469; federal programme grades 1 to 4 lines 555 to 557).
22. Compare two masses or two lengths and say which is heavier or longer, or by how much (M1 and M3, tussendoelen lines 1843 to 1846; federal programme grades 1 to 4 lines 405 to 406).
23. Reason about a measure («De lengte van de gang meet je niet met een potlood», M1, lines 1725 to 1728).
24. Decide which of two containers holds more, though the tall one is smaller (M10, lines 1810 to 1815).
25. Write a measure in decimal notation («2,37 m is 2 meter en 37 centimeter», M1 and M10, lines 1971 to 1973).

### Olympiad and Kangaroo items that fit domain M

Each is a source for a reasoning template, and no numbers are copied.

- MCCME 2023, grade 4 (oly-mccme-2023 lines 41 to 43, variants at 1736 to 1764): «Круглые гири весят 200 граммов, квадратные — 300 граммов, а треугольные — 150 граммов. ... Какая чаша тяжелее и на сколько граммов?» A mass-balance task.
- MCCME 2023 (lines 607 to 608, keyword search only): «амурский тигр увеличил свою скорость на 10 км/ч», a speed and circuit task whose heading was not read.
- MCCME 2021, grade 4 (lines 80 to 82, keyword search only): «У Пети есть 25 монет, каждая из которых имеет номинал 1, 2, 5 или 10 рублей.» A coin-counting task.
- MCCME 2021, grade 5 (lines 347 to 349, keyword search only): «Сколькими способами Денис сможет заплатить без сдачи за пирожок стоимостью 16 рублей?» A payment-combination task.
- MCCME 2021, grade 7 (lines 795 to 801): «Какое наименьшее значение может принимать периметр прямоугольника?», a rectangle cut into nine squares, answer 52. A perimeter reasoning task.
- MCCME 2021, grade 7 (lines 821 to 822, keyword search only): distance in whole kilometres marked on road posts.
- MCCME 2021, grade 8 (lines 888 to 889): «Квадрат разрезали на четыре равных прямоугольника, а из них сложили большую букву П ... периметр которой равен 56.» A perimeter from a rearrangement.
- MCCME 2021, grade 10 (lines 1399 to 1405): «Сколько секунд потребуется Васе, чтобы сбегать вверх и вниз по эскалатору...?», answer 324. A time-unit conversion with rates.
- Kangaroo ecolier (oly-tartu-ecolier lines 92 to 97, 101 to 104, 124 to 128): a dog on an 11 m chain by a house wall with sides 5 m and 7 m; boards of 1 m make a fence of 4 m, and how many for 10 m; matches of 1 cm and 3 cm make a square. Length-reasoning items.
- Kangaroo preecolier (oly-tartu-preecolier lines 121 to 124): «Каждая стрелка направляется от более тяжёлой вещи к более лёгкой.» An order-by-weight task.
- Dutch Kangoeroe 2003 report (report 396 lines 537 to 540, 551 to 552, 558 to 564, 587 to 597, 644 to 649, 662 to 669, 708 to 713, 726 to 737, all by keyword search): banknote totals in euro, clock digits, distances in metres, a square's side from two areas, a glass half full at 10 cm, a date after 2003 minutes, sticks that form a triangle, and a zigzag of 100 cm and 1 cm segments.
- Dutch Kangoeroe 2002 report (report 397 lines 524, 542, 554 to 558, 698 to 702, 753 to 758, all by keyword search): fruit crates in euro, a duration of 16 h 32 min, a water level in cm over minutes, girls' weights in kg, and a jump of 1 m doubling to 37 km.

The reports are the Dutch Kangoeroe reports for 2002 and 2003. Their year and grade labels cover groups 7 and 8 and secondary classes, so they do not show that every item is for groups 7 and 8 only.

### Answer kinds the forms need

The current kinds cover most forms: integer, decimal, time, choice, grid, steps and order. Three needs remain. A tolerance for an estimate is needed because forms 5 and 11 accept a range; the approved number-line distance covers a number placed on a line, and an estimate of a price or a size needs the same kind of acceptance. A date is needed because forms 8 and 9 give a day and month; a pair may serve, and this was not decided. A combined answer, "which pan and by how many grams", is a choice plus an integer, which the steps kind may cover.

### Silences and conflicts

The graph prerequisite for M12 names "M9 subtype: ha" (`content/graph.yaml` lines 871 to 874), but M9 has only the subtype `area-units` (lines 815 to 826), so the prerequisite points to a subtype that does not exist. The M4 subtype names (rect-squares, rect-sides and so on) do not match the catalogue's names (perimeter, polygon). The Dutch circle goal and the Russian base circle goal (federal programme grades 5 to 9 line 634) are not checked against each other. The triangle area in Russian was not located beyond the general grade 6 area line (lines 632 to 634).

## Conclusions

1. Each M subtype takes its group range from the Dutch goal lines cited above, and its numbers, units and contexts stay inside that range.
2. A topic found only in the Russian programme is an extension: centners, work problems, speed in metres per minute, multiplicative comparison of masses, and the Russian circle at base level, each with its grade and line.
3. Each subtype has at least ten templates with distinct task forms drawn from the forms above; the list supports about 25 across the nodes, so twenty is reachable for M1, M2, M4 and M5.
4. Each subtype with a reasoning goal has at least two reasoning templates, using the Dutch reasoning lines (for example tussendoelen lines 1725 to 1728, 1751 to 1753, 2090 to 2094) or the olympiad items above.
5. Estimation templates use an acceptance distance before they are written.
6. Unit-conversion templates test the factor traps named in RES-1200: 1000 for millimetres and metres, 10 for decilitres and litres, and 1000 for millilitres and litres.
7. A date answer needs a decision: a pair, or a new kind.
8. Olympiad-derived templates name the paper and line they follow and copy no numbers.
9. The M11 circle and the M6 triangle stay open until the Dutch Meetkunde goals and the Vilenkin grade 6 text are read.
10. The M12 prerequisite is corrected in the graph before M12 is templated.
