# The research records, one per topic of the draft design

## Summary

This record indexes the research moved from the owner's draft specification of Tower Chronicles, one record per topic, so each topic can be approved on its own. The world itself is in the canon records under `canon/`.

## Method

Each record was moved from one range of the draft specification on 2026-09-26. The index below is generated by `meow-method index research --write`.
