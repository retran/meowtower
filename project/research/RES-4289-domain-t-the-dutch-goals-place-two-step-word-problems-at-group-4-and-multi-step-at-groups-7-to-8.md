# Domain T's Dutch goals place two-step word problems at group 4 and multi-step problems at groups 7 to 8, not at the graph's groups 3 and 6

## Summary

The graph's T1 (group 3) and T3 (group 5) match the Dutch goals. T2 (group 3) and T4 (group 6) do not. The first combined-operation context goal in the Dutch tussendoelen is at the end of group 4, and the calculator sequence goal is at the end of group 8. The Russian programme puts two-step problems at grade 2 (group 4) and "2–3 действия" at grade 4 (group 6), so no source supports a four-step problem at group 6. Four-step templates are an extension at best.

## The question

Which group and which task forms should each word-problem subtype (T1 one-step, T2 two-step, T3 three-step, T4 four-step) use, under the owner's rule that the Dutch programme for groups 1 to 8 is the base and the Russian programme, with a Russian grade n read as group n + 2, gives extensions that must be marked as such?

## Method

A research agent drafted this record from the extracted source texts; the main session checked a sample of the quotes of the sibling drafts against those texts, and the quotes below were not each rechecked.

Read: `content/graph.yaml` lines 1088 to 1131; RES-0800 lines 218 to 225 and 363 to 371; RES-0700 lines 108 to 130 (RES-0700 is the answer input and checking research, not the word-problem structure research the brief named); RES-1200 line 314; SPC-0040 lines 37 to 66, 142 to 180 and 221 to 230. The Dutch tussendoelen (a two-column PDF; columns assigned by position and checked against the per-year section at lines 3326 to 4853) and the concretisation lines 376 to 415. The Russian programme: grades 1 to 4 lines 180 to 183, 283 to 294, 355, 437 to 446, 490, 572 to 581, 625; grades 5 to 9 lines 456 to 462, 533 to 541, 602 to 607. The two Moro texts by search only. The olympiad papers: the MCCME 2021 paper, the MCCME 2023 paper (sampled at items 4.1, 4.3, 4.7 and answer lines), the Tartu papers and the Kangoeroe reports 396 (2003) and 397 (2002); the group levels of the Kangoeroe papers are not confirmed from their headers.

Not read: Vilenkin 2024; six surplus-data search hits in the MCCME 2023 paper, the two federal programmes and Vilenkin 2024, left unopened; and the Dutch Verhoudingen percentage goals beyond tussendoelen lines 1396 to 1431, which are not mapped to groups. The SLO identifiers `slo-getallen-1f-08` and `slo-getallen-1s-07` do not occur in any extracted SLO text.

## Findings

### T1 at group 3 matches the Dutch goals at the end of groups 2 and 3

Tussendoelen lines 738 to 740 (end of group 2): "kan eenvoudige optel- en aftrekproblemen in een context met hoeveelheden tot ten minste 12 (handelend) oplossen." Lines 741 to 742 (end of group 3): "kan bij een optel- of aftreksituatie tot ten minste 20 een formele bewerking geven en omgekeerd." Multiplication contexts under 20 are at the end of group 3 (lines 839 to 846).

### T2 at group 3 is earlier than the Dutch combined-operation goal, at the end of group 4

Tussendoelen lines 3336 to 3338 (per-year block, end of group 4; block header at line 3328): "kan contextproblemen oplossen onder ten minste 100 waarbinnen een combinatie van bewerkingen wordt gevraagd (bv.: In onze klas zitten 14 jongens en 18 meisjes…)." The same goal is in the per-domain table at lines 954 to 956. Russian grade 2 (group 4) has "План решения задачи в два действия" (grades 1 to 4 lines 283 to 285). Russian grade 1 (group 3) has only "Решение задач в одно действие." (lines 180 to 183).

### T3 at group 5 matches the end-of-group-5 goal

Tussendoelen lines 3657 to 3659 (end of group 5): "kan contextproblemen oplossen onder ten minste 1000, waarbij een combinatie van bewerkingen wordt gevraagd (bv.: Jip bestelt twee bekers chocolademelk van elk 3 euro en twee stukken appeltaart van elk 2 euro en 50 cent. Hoeveel moet hij betalen?)." The end of group 7 adds "kan bij contextsituaties uitleggen waarom bepaalde bewerkingen samengenomen mogen worden (bv.: 2 kopjes van 2 euro en 2 borden van 6 euro, dan mag je 2 x (2 + 6) uitrekenen.)" (lines 4396 to 4397).

### T4 at group 6 has no Dutch goal at group 6 and no Russian support beyond "2–3 действия"

The end-of-group-6 column has only "begrijpt wat 'gemiddelde' betekent…" (tussendoelen lines 1293 to 1294). The first multi-operation context goal is at the end of group 7 (lines 4394 to 4395). The calculator sequence goal is at the end of group 8: "kan contextproblemen waarin een opeenvolging van bewerkingen uitgevoerd moet worden, uitrekenen met de rekenmachine (bv.: Je koopt 3 pakken koffie van € 2,68 en 4 pakken limonade van € 1,17…)" (lines 4847 to 4848; the same goal at lines 1366 to 1368). The 1S level is the end-of-primary target: "het streefniveau 1S te halen" (concretisering lines 376 to 377). Russian grade 4 says "Работа с текстовой задачей, решение которой содержит 2–3 действия" (grades 1 to 4 lines 572 to 573). No source supports four-step problems at group 6.

### The 1F and 1S concretisation names the same operations at two levels

1F: "vertalen van eenvoudige situatie naar berekening", "globaal beredeneren van uitkomsten" (concretisering lines 398 and 402). 1S: "vertalen van complexe situatie naar berekening", "afronden binnen gegeven situatie: 77,6 dozen berekend dus 78 dozen kopen" (lines 398 and 402 to 403). The 1S column includes 1F (line 415).

### The Russian programme adds extensions: enumeration, logic, work, percentages and proportions

Marked as extensions:

- Enumeration: "устанавливать логику перебора вариантов для решения простейших" (grades 1 to 4 line 355, grade 2); "Решение задач перебором всех возможных вариантов" (grades 5 to 9 line 535, grade 5; line 604, grade 6).
- Logic problems: "Решение логических задач" (grades 5 to 9 line 534, grade 5).
- Work: "работы (производительность, время, объём работы)" (grades 1 to 4 line 576, grade 4).
- Percentages and proportions: "на проценты, на отношения и пропорции" (grades 5 to 9 line 459, the grade 5 and 6 note).

The Dutch Verhoudingen goals contain proportion ideas (tussendoelen lines 1408 to 1431, "verhoudingstabel"); their groups were not mapped here. Domain P's record (RES-4287) maps them.

### The answer contract

The current kinds (SPC-0040 line 64) are integer, decimal, fraction, mixed, quotientRemainder, time, point, compare, choice, grid, order, steps, equation and region, with the approved additions set, pair and a number-line distance. The set kind covers enumeration (form 18), and the pair kind covers reverse chains and decompositions (form 17). Composition (form 16) needs a stated path, since SPC-0040 lines 65 to 66 describe `judgeCompose` only for a composed riddle.

### Where the sources are silent or conflict

- No source read gives a word problem with surplus data. SPC-0040 line 148 and RES-0700 line 114 define T4 as having one extra number. The six surplus-data search hits are unread, so this is neither confirmed nor refuted.
- `content/graph.yaml` lists one subtype per T node. SPC-0040 line 171 names `T1.insufficient` to `T4.insufficient` and `T1.surplus` to `T3.surplus`, which the graph does not hold, and SPC-0040 line 148 says the graph holds no `T4.surplus`.
- RES-1200 line 314 gives the T answer kinds as integer, decimal and steps, while SPC-0040 line 64 gives the full list.
- The graph's SLO identifiers do not occur in the extracted text.
- The Dutch goals and the Russian grades give no percentage or ratio problems per group in the extracts read.

## Conclusions

1. The graph's T2 `typicalGroup` needs a decision: it should read 4, or a group 3 two-step template must be a one-action form. No source gives a two-step problem at group 3, and both the Dutch goal (tussendoelen lines 3336 to 3338) and Russian grade 2 (grades 1 to 4 lines 283 to 285) place it at group 4.
2. The graph's T4 `typicalGroup` of 6 has no source. The earliest Dutch multi-operation goal is at the end of group 7 (tussendoelen lines 4394 to 4395), and the calculator sequence is at the end of group 8 (lines 4847 to 4848). Russian grade 4 caps steps at two to three (grades 1 to 4 line 573), so a four-step template at group 6 is an extension and does not cite the Russian text for four steps.
3. T3 at group 5 holds only if its numbers stay under 1,000 (tussendoelen line 3657).
4. Templates at each subtype draw forms from the list above, Dutch base first; enumeration, logic, work, percentages and proportions are extensions, each naming its Russian grade and passage.
5. Composition needs a stated answer path before forms 15 and 16 are templated.
6. Each subtype needs at least ten templates from distinct forms. The list gives more than twenty distinct forms, so ten per subtype is achievable on forms alone, but T4 has no base-level form at group 6.
7. Before templates are written, the six surplus-data hits and the unread Vilenkin and MCCME 2023 texts are read.
