# «Сплети загадку» can take fractions, decimals, percentages, ratios and multi-step expressions if the engine expands each new operation into the four it already judges, the masker treats a decimal, a fraction and a fraction word as numbers, and each new family passes its own parse test

## Summary

The owner's addendum 2 of 2026-09-28, item 7, extends «Сплети загадку» (Weave a riddle) from word-problem types to seven constructions: equal groups, the two meanings of division, a fraction of a number, decimals, percentages, ratios and multi-step expressions. It asks the parser and the engine to support fractions, decimals, percentages and ratios as graph operations, and it keeps the Director's gate at "understands". The approved record, ADR-0230 as amended by ADR-0360 and ADR-0370, judges riddles with four operations over exact rationals. So the new constructions need no new verdict rules: a fraction of a number, a percentage and a ratio each expand into `+`, `−`, `·` and `:` with fixed expansions, and the approved verdict table stays as it is. What blocks the item is the masker and the gate. The approved rule on masking names digit runs and a closed set of number words, so «2,5» becomes two tokens, and «три четверти» (three quarters) or «вдвое» (twice) reaches the parser with a word the graph can't use. The Director's gate reads a problem type's tier node, and a construction has no problem type. The published studies I read support the item's premise: decimal operators and the two division models change which operation pupils in grades 5, 7 and 9 choose, and adults apply whole-number arithmetic to percentages. No study I read measures one child by composing, so the item stays in the composing stream and outside every estimate. I lead with expanding the new operations, and compare it with doing nothing, cards only and native operations. The whole item goes to the post-MVP backlog, and no field joins the MVP, because the log's template and version already name each riddle's construction. I decided the nine questions the owner would otherwise be asked. This record covers item 7 alone.

## The question

What must be true of the game and the approved record so that «Сплети загадку» can offer the seven constructions of item 7, judge them fairly, keep her numbers on the Mac and stay out of every estimate? The addendum fixes the constructions with example inputs: `7 · 8` for equal groups, `120 : 6` for the two meanings of division, `3/4 от 20` (3/4 of 20), `2,5 · 4`, `25 % от 80` and `80 − 20 % от 80`, `3 : 2` and «разделить 30 в отношении 3 : 2» (divide 30 in the ratio 3 : 2), and `(120 − 30) : 3` and `48 : 6 + 5`. It asks the parser and the engine to support fractions, decimals, percentages and ratios as graph operations. It lets the Director choose a construction only when its node is at "understands" or above.

The question assumes that the engine needs new graph operations. The verdict needs none. ADR-0040 already computes in `Q`, an exact rational type on `bigint`, so 2,5 · 4 and 3/4 of 20 are exact values. A fraction of a number is a division and a multiplication, a percentage of a number is a division by 100 and a multiplication, and a share of a ratio is a division by the sum of the parts and a multiplication. The parser needs names for these operations, because a child writes «три четверти от двадцати» and not «20 : 4 · 3». The judge can then expand each name into the four operations it already compares. So the real changes sit before the parse, in what counts as a number, and in the Director's gate.

The question also assumes that composing a problem for `3/4 от 20` shows "fraction as an operator" better than solving it. The studies I read show that the number types in a problem change which operation people choose, which supports composing as a window on meaning. None of them measures one child's composing against her solving. So the assumption holds for the report's counts and for no estimate, as ADR-0210 and ADR-0230 already keep it.

## Findings

### The addendum adds seven constructions, new graph operations and the same gate

Item 7 of the owner's addendum 2 extends item 2 of addendum 1 with the seven constructions and example inputs listed under the question. It says composing checks understanding hardest where a school algorithm is easy to learn without meaning. It gives the parser and the engine's comparison fractions, decimals, percentages and ratios as graph operations. It keeps the rule that the Director chooses a construction only when the node is at "understands" or above, as in addendum 1. It notes that the estimate example from the external review is covered by «На глаз» (By eye) and feeds item 2's dimension of conceptual understanding. Item 2 lists «Сплети загадку» among the sources of that dimension; the profile belongs to item 2's record.

### The engine already computes in exact rationals, so decimals and fractions need no new arithmetic

ADR-0040 computes every answer in `Q`, a rational type on `bigint`, and treats a decimal as a fraction over a power of 10, so 0,1 + 0,2 is exactly 0,3. A unit test asserts `3/10`. ADR-0230 puts `judgeCompose` in `src/shared/compose.ts`, which imports only `src/shared/`. So `judgeCompose` can compute 2,5 · 4 = 10 and 20 : 4 · 3 = 15 exactly, and a riddle's value comparison has no rounding to decide.

### Each new operation expands into the four the verdict already compares

The approved match rule compares operations and numbers, with `+` and `·` commutative and associative and `−` and `:` in fixed order, and the other verdicts compare values. A fraction of a number, a/b of N, is N : b · a. A percentage of a number, p % of N, is N : 100 · p. A share of a total T in the ratio a : b is T : (a + b) · a for the first part. A part from a known part K of a ratio a : b is K : a · b. After the expansion, a story that says «разделили 20 на 4 части и взяли 3» (divided 20 into 4 parts and took 3) and one that says «три четверти от 20» give the same graph and `match`. A story «четверть от 80» (a quarter of 80) for the target `25 % от 80` gives other operations with the same value, which is `match_other_structure`. I found no case among the addendum's examples that the expansion judges against the meaning the addendum states.

### The constant 100 and the sum of the ratio's parts come from the expansion, not from the parse

The approved rule on the parse rejects any graph that names a number other than a token of the masked text. The expansion supplies 100 for a percentage and a + b for a ratio from the operation's definition in code, so the parser names no number. That reading keeps the rule's reason, that no model supplies a number, but the rule's text speaks only of numbers the graph names, so the requirements step confirms it.

### The approved rule on masking splits a decimal and leaves fraction words unmasked

The approved requirement on masking replaces digit runs and a closed set of Russian number words: the cardinals, the collective numerals from «двое» to «десятеро», «полтора», «десяток», «дюжина» and «сотня». A digit run makes «2,5» two tokens and «3/4» two tokens, and the parser can't join them into one number because it may not name one. ADR-0210 already says "a number is a digit run, a decimal or a fraction", so the decision and the requirement disagree on this point. ADR-0230's list of what it doesn't settle names «вдвое», «втрое», «половина», «пополам», «пара» and ordinals as words outside the set: a graph that needs one names no token, and the riddle ends as `unparsed`. Fraction stories need exactly those words. «Три четверти от двадцати» masks to «n1 четверти от n2», and «четверти» carries the denominator the graph needs. So without a wider set, most fraction and many ratio riddles would end as `unparsed`, which is neutral for her and useless for the report.

### A ratio written with a colon and a division share one sign

Code writes `:` for division (ADR-0040), and the addendum writes a ratio as `3 : 2`. In her text «3 : 2» masks to «n1 : n2», which the parser can read as a division or a ratio. As a target, `3 : 2` alone has no total and no known part, so no single value exists to compare. A target of a ratio that names a total or a known part has one.

### The Director's gate reads a problem type's tier node, and a construction has no problem type

The approved gate lets the Director offer a riddle of a type only when the node of that type's tier is at "understands" or above, until a record defines "understands" for a type. ADR-0230 draws the target from a word-problem template of the floor. The constructions map to nodes and not to types: equal groups to A3, the two meanings of division to A4, a fraction of a number to F2, decimals to D4, a percentage of a number to P2 and a discount to P3, a ratio to P4, and a multi-step expression to A11 (RES-0800). SPC-0060 names "understands", "fluent" and "stable" as the tested states at or above "understands", and gives inferred states apart from tested ones. The approved rule on the riddle's floor, one riddle only on a floor that holds word problems, doesn't depend on the target's source.

### The composing stream and the report line are keyed by tier and problem type

ADR-0230 keeps the composing stream per tier, problem type and form, and shows «Может составить задачу» (Can compose a problem) beside the matrix of type by steps. A construction riddle has no tier and no type, so the stream has no key for it and the report line no row. `compose_shown` records the template and its version, from which a construction can be read back, so no report needs a new field to count by construction later.

### The approved error classes cover order and the two division meanings, and miss the errors the studies name

ADR-0230 gives `compose_on_vs_times`, `compose_order` and `compose_partition_vs_quotition`, all in the conceptual class of the error-type limit. `compose_partition_vs_quotition` compares a division's kind with the kind the target's template declares and leaves the verdict at `match`, so a template for `120 : 6` must declare a kind for the class to mean anything. None of the three names a swap of `·` and `:`, a percentage used as a plain number, or an additive ratio, the errors the next three findings describe.

### Decimal operators and the two division models change which operation pupils choose

Fischbein, Deri, Nello and Marino (1985) asked 623 pupils in grades 5, 7 and 9 at 13 Italian schools to choose the operation for 26 multiplication and division word problems. Numbers that broke the constraints of the tacit models, such as a decimal operator, were "particular sources of difficulty at all three grade levels". The findings fit repeated addition as the model of multiplication and partitive division as the model of division, and the quotative model influenced choices only in grade 9. Greer (1987) calls it "firmly established" that the types of numbers in a single-operation problem affect the choice of operation. So a decimal or a quotitive division riddle is where a wrong operation is most likely, and the reference set needs such texts.

### Adults apply whole-number arithmetic to percentages

Jacobs Danan and Gelman (2018) asked 1,629 undergraduates about a price changed by percentages. On a price of $100 changed by 50 % twice, 25 % answered wrongly, and on an unspecified price changed by 30 % down and up, 62 % did. The most frequent error treated percentages additively, and about 33 % of participants used natural-number arithmetic. The sample is adults, so the figures forecast nothing for the player. They show that a percentage used as a plain number is a named, common error, and `80 − 20` for `80 − 20 % от 80` is that error in a riddle.

### The additive strategy is the most reported error in ratio tasks

Misailidou and Williams (2003) gave 24 missing-value ratio items to 303 children aged 10 to 14 in four schools in north-west England. They call the additive strategy, which applies the difference between two terms of one ratio to the other, "the most commonly reported erroneous strategy" in the literature. They also found that it "accounts for only a small proportion of children's problems with ratio", and that pictures made additive errors less frequent. So an additive ratio riddle is worth a class of its own, and it can't carry the whole ratio report.

### Card riddles with three roles already cover one-step multiplication and division

Yamamoto et al. (2014) describe Monsakun Touch 3, in which a child poses a one-step multiplication or division problem from three sentence cards. The model gives each quantity a role, base quantity, proportion and compared quantity, and the unknown's role decides whether the story is a multiplication or one of the two divisions. Thirty-nine pupils in grade 3 used it in eleven lessons over five weeks. The number of problems they posed rose from pretest to posttest (p = .005), and the scores on problems with extraneous information differed between those who had used earlier Monsakun versions and those who hadn't (p = .04). The teacher needed three lessons for cards saying "cut", which the pupils linked to division even in a multiplication story. So the card form of ADR-0230 can carry equal groups and both division meanings with three roles, and card words can pull a child towards an operation, as ADR-0230's cost section already warns.

### One acceptance test over the whole set hides a family that fails

Acceptance test 3 passes the parser at 95 % agreement over 200 texts, and a pass at exactly 190 of 200 bounds the true agreement from below at about 91.0 % by my arithmetic on the Wilson interval. A family of 50 texts inside a larger set could fail badly while the whole passes. At 48 of 50 the Wilson interval runs from about 86.5 % to 98.9 %, and at 95 of 100 from about 88.8 % to 97.8 %. ADR-0230 turns text riddles on by one flag, `COMPOSE_FREE`, and one record per parse model in `verify/parser-eval.json`.

### Units and mixed quantities have no operation in the graph

A decimal story often uses two units, such as «2 кг 500 г» (2 kg 500 g). A graph that joins them needs 1000, which is no token, so the approved rule rejects it and the riddle ends as `unparsed`. A table of unit conversions in code could supply 1000 as the expansion supplies 100, but it would add a source of numbers into the verdict that neither the addendum nor RES-4020 asked for.

### Four options answer the question, and each is better at something

| Option | What its advocate would say it is better at | Case against it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Do nothing: riddles stay on the word-problem types of T1 to T4 | no change to the masker, the parse, the gate or the test set; nothing new to label | drops the owner's instruction, and leaves fractions, percentages and ratios, where the studies place the errors of meaning, without a composing measure |
| Cards only for the new constructions, never parsed | no masker change and no parse test, a verdict in milliseconds, and Monsakun already covers multiplication and division with three roles | card words hint at the operation, as the "cut" cards did; card sets for fractions, percentages and ratios are content nobody has tested; she never writes her own story, which ADR-0230 keeps as the aim |
| Named operations in the parse, expanded into `+`, `−`, `·` and `:` before `judgeCompose` | the approved verdict table and match rule stay word for word; one normaliser; `Q` gives exact values; the parse's operation names can still be counted | hides whether she used fraction language or divided and multiplied, unless the log keeps the names; the masker and the gate still change |
| Native operations with their own equivalence rules in `judgeCompose` | the verdict itself can tell "3/4 of 20" from "20 : 4 · 3", closer to the addendum's "graph operations" | new match rules per operation, each a requirement change; equivalences such as 25 % and a quarter need rules nobody has written; a second verdict path to test |

I lead with the named operations expanded into the four. It is the only option that keeps every approved verdict rule while giving the parser words for what a child writes. The case against it is that expansion merges "fraction as an operator" with "divide, then multiply", which is the distinction the addendum wants to see. The log answers that: `compose_parsed` keeps the graph as the parser named it, so a later report can count operator stories apart from the expanded verdict. Native operations would put that distinction into the verdict, at the cost of rules I found no evidence to set. Cards only is the fallback for any family that fails its parse test, as ADR-0230 already makes cards the fallback for the whole form.

## Conclusions

1. A riddle's target must be able to come from one of seven constructions, equal groups, the two meanings of division, a fraction of a number, decimals, percentages, ratios and multi-step expressions, besides the word-problem types of T1 to T4.
2. The parse reply must be able to name a fraction as a number (a/b, with a = 1 when no count stands before the fraction word), a mixed number (a whole plus a/b), a fraction of a number, a percentage of a number, a share of a ratio from its total and a part of a ratio from a known part, each over tokens only, because a child writes «два с половиной» and «две целых пять десятых» as quantities and the parse may name no number to build them.
3. Before comparing, `judgeCompose` must expand each named operation into `+`, `−`, `·` and `:` over `Q` by fixed rules: a/b of N as N : b · a, p % of N as N : 100 · p, a share of T in a : b as T : (a + b) · a or · b, and a part from K as K : a · b. A fraction written as one token, a/b, and a count followed by a fraction word must expand alike, through the numerator a and the denominator b. A fraction word with no count, such as «четверть от 80», takes a = 1 from the operation's definition, as a percentage takes 100. A mixed number, in digits or in words such as «два с половиной», expands as its whole part plus a/b. The verdict rules of ADR-0230 must then apply unchanged.
4. The constants an expansion supplies must come from the operation's definition in code, and the parse must name no number other than a token.
5. The masker must turn a decimal written with a comma or a point, a fraction written with a slash and a mixed number each into one token, and the token's value in `Q` must stay on the Mac.
6. The masker must also mask «половина», «треть», «четверть», every ordinal from «второй» to «тысячный» in every gender and case, the multiplicative words from «вдвое» to «вдесятеро», «пополам» and «пара», as the owner's number words are masked. A cardinal run followed by an ordinal, such as «двадцать пятых» or «сто первый», must mask as one `d` token whose value is the whole denominator, so «три двадцать пятых» reads as 3/25. «Целых» stays a word, because it marks a whole part and carries no value. This must hold so no graph a fraction or ratio story needs is refused for an unmasked word.
7. The egress guard's `mask_incomplete` refusal must cover every word conclusion 6 adds, and the masking test must generate their case forms from a dictionary the numerals file wasn't written from, as ADR-0230's test does for the approved words.
8. A ratio target must name a total or a known part, so every ratio riddle has one value to compare. In her text, a colon between two tokens must parse as a division unless the words «в отношении» (in the ratio) or «на … приходится» (for … there are) mark a ratio, so the prompt's author has one rule.
9. A construction riddle must be offered only when every node the construction needs is at "understands" or above: A3, A4, F2, D4, P2 (P3 for a discount), P4, or A11 and the node of each operation in a multi-step expression.
10. A division target must declare its meaning, sharing into parts or grouping by a size, so `compose_partition_vs_quotition` can compare against it.
11. `judgeCompose` must give three more error classes in the conceptual class of the error-type limit: `compose_times_vs_divide` when `·` stands where the target has `:` on the same numbers, or the reverse; `compose_percent_as_number` when the percentage's token p stands as an operand outside a percentage expansion where the target's expansion holds it, as in 80 − 20 for 80 − 20 % от 80; and `compose_ratio_additive` when the target is a part K : a · b from a known part of a : b and the graph adds the difference instead, K + b − a. Each class sits beside the verdict the value comparison gives, which for these patterns is `wrong_structure` unless the two values happen to be equal. The share-of-total ratio target gets no additive class, because I found no additive pattern described for it.
12. The composing stream must count construction riddles per construction and form, and for the two meanings of division also per requested meaning and parsed kind, and the report line «Может составить задачу» must show those counts beside the word-problem matrix, as counts and no state.
13. A construction riddle must stay out of every estimate, state, probe, block, success share, holding-steps count and step-input share, as every riddle does under ADR-0230.
14. Acceptance test 3's reference set must gain labelled texts for each family that writes a new operation, fractions, decimals, percentages and ratios, including decimal operators below 1, percentages used as numbers, additive ratios and stories with two units. Each family's text form must play only after its own subset passes. The approved set of 200 must hold, within its 200, at least 20 texts for each of equal groups, quotitive and partitive division and multi-step expressions with brackets, and those three constructions play as text on the whole set's pass.
15. Paraphrase templates and card frames for every construction must be per-language strings in the Russian content files, written offline and checked like every frame file, as ADR-0230's consequences set for the card frames and paraphrase templates it adds.
16. Nothing player-facing in this item may be in Dutch, because the item adds no Dutch and `CLAUDE.md` keeps player text Russian only.
17. These approved records must be amended, each by the change named:
    - The requirement on masking: a decimal, a fraction and a mixed number are one number each, and the word set gains conclusion 6's words.
    - The requirement that the parse names only tokens: tokens of the new classes are allowed, and expansion constants aren't numbers the graph names.
    - The requirement on the Director's gate: construction riddles read the nodes of conclusion 9.
    - The requirement on the error-type limit's conceptual class: the three classes of conclusion 11.
    - The requirements on acceptance test 3's reference set, its pass rule and the composing flag: per-family subsets and passes.
    - ADR-0230: the target's source, the stream's key, the report line, the error classes, the expansion in `judgeCompose`, the masker's word list and the per-family record in `verify/parser-eval.json`.
    - ADR-0210: the masked number includes a mixed number and conclusion 6's words.

### Decided on 2026-09-28

1. The engine expands the named operations into the four it already judges, and `judgeCompose`'s verdict table stays as ADR-0230 sets it, because this keeps every approved verdict rule and `compose_parsed` still records the operations as the parser named them. Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to process addendum 2 through to the specifications.
2. Fraction words and ordinals are masked as tokens of their own class, `d1` to `dk`, beside `n1` to `nm`, because a parser that sees «n1 n2 от n3» can't tell a denominator from a count, and the class tells it the word's role without its value. Ordinals used as positions are masked too, and the graph leaves them unused like irrelevant data, because telling the two uses apart needs the parse the masker comes before. Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to process addendum 2 through to the specifications.
3. The multiplicative words, such as «вдвое», are masked as `n` tokens with the preposition kept, «вдвое» becoming «в n1 раза», because the parser then reads the same form as «в 2 раза» and the span still maps back to her word. «Пара» (a pair) is an `n` token of value 2, and «пополам» (in half) becomes «на n1 части» with n1 = 2, for the same reason. Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to process addendum 2 through to the specifications.
4. The graph gets no unit conversion, and a story that needs one ends as `unparsed`, because a conversion table would be a new source of numbers into the verdict that nothing in the record asks for. The reference set holds such stories labelled `unparsed`, reported apart and left out of each family's 48 of 50, so test 3 shows how the parser handles them without charging the decimal family a miss the parser can't avoid; how often she writes them shows only in the log. Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to process addendum 2 through to the specifications.
5. A construction's gate reads the tested state, or an inferred "fluent", of each node the construction needs, because ADR-0060's inference exists so a fluent descendant spares a prerequisite from a new test. Construction riddles keep the approved floor rule and draw their target from any node that passes the gate, because a riddle is a story scene outside the room slots and needs no link to the floor's nodes. Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to process addendum 2 through to the specifications.
6. For the two meanings of division, the Director asks for the meaning with fewer riddles that got `match` with no `compose_partition_vs_quotition`, counted per requested meaning, because a story of the other meaning also gets `match` and would hide a meaning she never writes. The target is a note that names the meaning in words from the content file. The approved rule then applies: a story of the other meaning keeps `match` with `compose_partition_vs_quotition` beside it. Fischbein et al. found the quotative model the later one, so asking for the rarer meaning is how the stream learns whether she has both. Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to process addendum 2 through to the specifications.
7. Each of the four families that writes a new operation gets 50 labelled texts in `tests/reference/compose.ru.json`, and its text form plays only when the approved 200 pass by ADR-0230's own rule and the family's subset, held apart from them, agrees on at least 48 of 50. I chose 50 over 100 because the parent labels 200 more texts, not 400, at ADR-0230's rate of about 20 in 15 minutes, which is about 2.5 hours. Cards cover a family meanwhile, and ADR-0230's reversal conditions watch the logged labels. A live run then costs up to $8 on the offline key: 400 worst-case parses at about $0.017 make about $6.80, and the rest covers the judge checks. Equal groups, the two meanings of division and multi-step expressions write no new operation, so they need no subset and ride on the approved 200, which must hold at least 20 texts of each within its 200, and at least 10 of each meaning of division, a count I chose so each construction is present without changing the set's size. Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to process addendum 2 through to the specifications.
8. The three new error classes are `compose_times_vs_divide`, `compose_percent_as_number` and `compose_ratio_additive`, each in the conceptual class, because each is an error the studies above name, and a class for an inverted fraction is left out because no study I read names it. Decided on 2026-09-28 by research, on the owner's instruction to process addendum 2 through to the specifications.
9. The whole item goes to the post-MVP backlog as one item, and the MVP gains no field, because `compose_shown` already records the template and version from which a construction is read back. It stays one item because every family depends on the same masker, expansion and gate, which must be built and tested once before any family plays, and decision 7 already lets families go live one at a time by their tests.
