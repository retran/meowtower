# The Dutch goals start fractions at group 5, the Russian programme starts shares in grade 3, and the graph starts F1 a year early

## Summary

The Dutch programme is the base. Its end-of-group fraction goals start at group 5 with the meaning of half, quarter and whole. Ordinary-fraction arithmetic is set at groups 6 to 8 and at the 1S concretisation. The Russian federal programme starts shares in grade 3, which is Dutch group 5 under the owner's rule that a Russian grade n is Dutch group n + 2, so it agrees with the Dutch base there. It starts formal ordinary fractions in grade 5 (group 7), and its grade 5 list includes all four operations, so it is ahead of the Dutch group 6 and 7 goals and level with group 8. F1 (`share`) is at group 4 in the graph, and both programmes place it at group 5, so the graph is a year early. The Soviet-era evidence is one OCR text (Moro, grade 2, 1977) with a section on shares. The olympiad evidence is sparse: three items with fraction reasoning, two of them readable.

## The question

What must the templates of domain F ask, at which group, so that they follow the Dutch base, mark Russian extensions, and cover task forms widely enough for ten or more templates per subtype?

## Method

A research agent drafted this record from the extracted source texts, and the main session checked a sample of its sibling drafts' quotes against those texts (they matched); the quotes below were not each rechecked. Read: the Dutch tussendoelen (Getalbegrip and Bewerkingen fraction rows), the Dutch concretisation (1F and 1S fraction rows), the Russian federal programme for grades 1 to 4 and 5 to 9, the Moro grade 2 (1977) text, and parts of the Dutch Kangoeroe reports and the MCCME 2021 paper. The column assignments in the tussendoelen come from the layout of the extracted text, where the group headers are at fixed columns; they were not checked against the PDF, so the group given to a Bewerkingen row is provisional.

Not read: Vilenkin grade 6 (2024), the full Tartu papers (searched by keyword, no fraction hit), the full MCCME 2021 and Kangoeroe 396 papers (searched only), the Moro grade 1 text (searched, no fraction content found), and the grade 4 federal planned results. The fraction-percent rows of the Verhoudingen domain belong to domain P and are not drafted here.

## Findings

### F1 share: the Dutch goal sets it at the end of group 5, not group 4

The Dutch end-of-group-5 goal reads: "kent de betekenis van de begrippen: heel, half, kwart, de helft, halveren, verdubbelen, deel en geheel." (tussendoelen lines 546 to 549, the Getalbegrip fractions block under the group 5 column, header at line 512). The 1F notation goals are "Schrijf 'drie vierden' als getal" and "Hoe schrijf je 'een derde' in een breuk?" (concretisering lines 734 to 745). The graph's F1 `typicalGroup` is 4.

The Russian programme places shares at grade 3, which is Dutch group 5: "Доля величины: половина, треть, четверть, пятая, десятая часть в практической ситуации. Сравнение долей одной величины. Задачи на нахождение доли величины." (grades 1 to 4 lines 445 to 447). The grade 3 planned result is "называть, находить долю величины (половина, четверть); сравнивать величины, выраженные долями" (lines 912 to 920). Grade 4 adds "Доля величины времени, массы, длины." (line 559). The Russian and Dutch sources agree, and both sit one year after the graph.

### F2 fraction of a number: 1F and 1S differ by the size of the numbers

The 1F text: "Een deel van een hoeveelheid kunnen berekenen, met elementaire breuken en eenvoudige ronde gehele getallen (of eenvoudig af te ronden getallen) in betekenisvolle contextsituaties." Its examples are "deel van 150 euro" and "deel van de 24 kinderen in onze groep heeft een beugel. Hoeveel kinderen zijn dat?" (concretisering lines 1789 to 1796). The 1S text: "Een deel van een hoeveelheid berekenen, in contexten en met kale getallen, ook met moeilijker breuken." (lines 1789 to 1790, right column). The tussendoelen group 6 row, provisional: "kan rekenen met de breuk als operator in informele contextsituaties (bv.: ... 3/4 deel van 120 liter)" (lines 1318 to 1320).

The Russian programme includes "Нахождение части целого и целого по его части" in grade 5 (grades 5 to 9 line 523), which is Dutch group 7. The graph's F2 `typicalGroup` is 5, a year earlier than the Russian grade 5 and earlier than the Dutch 1F reference for this goal.

### F3 comparing fractions and the number line

The Dutch group 6 column, provisional: "kan veel voorkomende benoemde breuken vergelijken en ordenen" and "kan benoemde breuken plaatsen op een getallenlijn tussen hele getallen (zoals bij een maatbeker)" (tussendoelen lines 711 to 717). The 1F concretisation: "Waar ligt 1 ... op de getallenlijn van 1 tot 2 (liter)?" and "Welke breuk hoort erbij?" (concretisering lines 1725 to 1739). The 1S text: "Vergelijken ook via standaardprocedures en met moeilijker breuken" (lines 2001 to 2005). The 1S comparison task asks "Wat is de grootste breuk?", "Zet de breuken in volgorde van klein naar groot." and Lars's shortcut "wat overblijft" with "Leg uit wat Lars zegt. Kun je zo altijd redeneren?" (lines 2011 to 2017). The Russian grade 5 text: "Изображение дробей точками на числовой прямой" and "Сравнение дробей" (grades 5 to 9 lines 519 to 521).

### F4 equivalent fractions and simplifying

The Dutch group 7 column, provisional: "kan breuken vereenvoudigen (waaronder ook 'helen eruit halen') en kan aangeven of een breuk de meest vereenvoudigde breuk is (bv.: 17/3 = 5 2/3; 9/12 = ¾; 4/10 kun je vereenvoudigen naar 2/5)" (tussendoelen lines 706 to 707), and "kan gelijkwaardige breuken bedenken (compliceren)" (lines 709 to 710). The 1S row: "Vereenvoudigen en compliceren van breuken en breuken als gemengd getal schrijven", with "Haal de helen eruit", "Vereenvoudig de breuk", "Maak er honderdsten van" (concretisering lines 2111 to 2123); the level column is not visible in the extraction for this row, and the row is placed at 1S on the group 7 goal. The Russian grade 5 text: "Основное свойство дроби. Сокращение дробей. Приведение дроби к новому знаменателю." (grades 5 to 9 line 520).

### F5 and F6 addition and subtraction

The 1F concretisation does not split same and unlike denominators; it puts both in one goal: "Optellen en aftrekken van veel voorkomende gelijknamige en ongelijknamige breuken binnen een betekenisvolle situatie", with "liter melk en liter melk toevoegen, hoeveel melk is dat samen?" and "Twee flessen cola van 1 liter, hoeveel liter cola is dat samen?" (concretisering lines 1760 to 1772). The 1S text: "optellen en aftrekken, ook via standaardprocedures" (lines 1763 to 1772) and "Optellen en aftrekken met moeilijker breuken en gemengde getallen, ook via standaardprocedures en in kale opgaven" (lines 2062 to 2067).

The tussendoelen, provisional: group 7, "kan gelijknamige breuken optellen en aftrekken in contextsituaties en in formele rekentaal"; group 8, "kan ongelijknamige breuken optellen en aftrekken, inclusief helen eruit halen ... ook via standaardprocedure 'gelijknamig maken'" (lines 1314 to 1315). The graph has F5 (same denominator) at group 5 and F6 (unlike) at group 6, so it places both one to three years earlier than the Dutch group 7 and 8 goals. The Russian grade 5 list includes "Сложение и вычитание дробей" (grades 5 to 9 line 522).

### F7 fraction times whole number and times fraction

1F: "In een betekenisvolle situatie een breuk vermenigvuldigen met een geheel getal" (concretisering lines 1794 to 1796). 1S: "Ook een geheel getal vermenigvuldigen met een breuk of omgekeerd ... ook met moeilijker breuken" (lines 2089 to 2095), and "Een breuk met een breuk vermenigvuldigen of een deel van een deel nemen, met name in contextsituaties" (lines 2147 to 2151). Tussendoelen group 8, provisional: "kan een geheel getal vermenigvuldigen met een breuk en omgekeerd (bv.: 6x3/5; 3/4x12)" (line 4825). The Russian grade 5 text includes "Умножение и деление дробей, взаимно-обратные дроби" (line 522).

### F8 division by a fraction

1S only. Whole number divided by a fraction: "Een geheel getal delen door een breuk of door een gemengd getal ... met name in contextsituaties", with "Hoeveel glazen kun je vullen?" (concretisering lines 2172 to 2174). Fraction divided by a fraction: "Een breuk of gemengd getal delen door een breuk, vooral binnen een contextsituatie", with "Hoeveel pakjes van ... liter moet je kopen als je 1 liter slagroom nodig hebt?" and "Hoeveel lessen van ... uur zitten er in een schooldag van 4 uur?" (lines 2190 to 2201). Tussendoelen group 8 and 1S, provisional: "kan een heel getal delen door een breuk ... (bv.: 10 : 2 1/2)" (lines 4828 to 4830). The graph's F8 is `level: "1S"`, which agrees. The SLO goal attached to F8, `slo-getallen-1f-26`, has a text at 1F in the graph's own `sloGoals` list, so the subtype's level and its goal's level disagree.

### Task forms the sources use

Each is a form, not a template.

1. Name the shaded share of a figure («Назови каждую из незакрашенных долей прямоугольника», Moro 1977 task 814, lines 9766 to 9776; «Wat betekent dat?» for 3/4 liter, concretisering lines 771 to 772).
2. Count how many equal parts make a whole («Сколько половин круга в одном круге?», Moro 1977 lines 9720 to 9722; «Сколько таких долей в одном квадрате?», lines 9730 to 9732).
3. Write a fraction from words («tweevijfde» with a slash, concretisering lines 758 and 775; «drie vierden» as a number, line 737).
4. Say a fraction aloud («Hoe spreek je deze breuk uit? (drievierde; driekwart)», line 794).
5. Name numerator and denominator («Wat is de teller in de breuk? Wat is de noemer?», lines 760 to 761; tussendoelen lines 697 to 699, provisional).
6. Place a fraction between two whole numbers («Tussen welke gehele getallen ligt 4 1/2 op de getallenlijn?», line 839; «Waar ligt ... op de getallenlijn van 1 tot 2», line 1737).
7. Which of two fractions is larger, and why (Moro 1977 line 9753, OCR garbled; concretisering line 1370, numbers lost in the extraction).
8. Judge a wrong claim («Brit zegt dat ... groter is dan ... omdat 10 groter is dan 5. Leg eens uit waarom het niet klopt», lines 1372 to 1374).
9. Order three fractions and explain a shortcut (lines 2011 to 2017).
10. A fraction of an amount in context («deel van 150 euro», lines 1789 to 1796).
11. Add like fractions in context (line 1765).
12. How many packs make one whole («Hoeveel pakken van liter melk is evenveel als liter?», lines 1773 to 1778).
13. Complete to one («2/7 + … = 1», tussendoelen lines 1314 to 1317, provisional).
14. Convert a fraction to a decimal, with and without a calculator (concretisering lines 1745 to 1751 and 2026 to 2032).
15. Simplify or write as a mixed number (lines 2111 to 2123).
16. What remains after parts are taken: a disc eaten by halves, thirds, quarters and fifths (Kangoeroe 397 item 27, lines 1021 to 1029; the grade is not stated in the lines read).
17. A share of a number of objects in a reasoning problem (MCCME 2021 task 8.5, «лишь четверть дам не были приглашены на танец, и 2/7 от общего количества джентльменов никого не пригласили», answer 41, lines 964 to 968; the grade is not stated in the lines read).
18. Freezing changes volume by 1/11, and the question is the part by which melting reduces it (Kangoeroe 397 lines 1193 to 1194).
19. Share of a route as a decimal (MCCME 2021 lines 1113 and 1128 to 1129).
20. There is no smallest fraction («Leg eens uit waarom er geen kleinste breuk bestaat», tussendoelen lines 1347 to 1348, provisional).

### Soviet-era evidence

Moro grade 2 (1977, OCR) has a section "Доли. Получение и сравнение долей." (lines 9714 to 9717) with tasks 812 to 816 and 821. Tasks 815 and 821 are garbled by OCR, and tasks 812 and 813 are readable. This is the only Soviet-era fraction evidence read. The Moro grade 1 text (1990) has no fraction section in its readable text; a search for дроб, доля, половин and четверт found nothing.

### Where the sources are silent or conflict

- The Dutch tussendoelen put addition and subtraction at group 7 and 8 and multiplication and division at group 8 and 1S, while the graph puts F5 at 5, F6 at 6, F7 at 6 and F8 at 7. This is a conflict for the design record.
- The 1F concretisation groups like and unlike addition in one goal, so the split of F5 and F6 is the graph's, not the Dutch.
- Fraction numbers are lost in some concretisation rows (lines 1370, 2097 and 2151), so some task examples cannot be quoted in full.
- `slo-getallen-1f-25` is missing from the `sloGoals` list in the graph.
- No fraction task appears in the Russian federal programme before grade 3.

## Conclusions

1. F1 `share` does not start before Dutch group 5 or Russian grade 3; the graph's `typicalGroup` of 4 is a year early and needs a decision in the design record.
2. F2 `fraction-of` templates at group 5 use only elementary fractions with round numbers (halves, quarters, tenths) in a context, and the 1S templates carry the harder fractions.
3. F3 placement and comparison at group 6 use fractions the child can place between whole numbers; comparison by a standard procedure is marked 1S.
4. F4 `simplify` templates reduce to lowest terms and check whether a fraction is fully reduced (Dutch group 7), and they do not use mixed numbers before the 1S goal.
5. F5 and F6 templates separate like and unlike denominators because the graph does, but the Dutch 1F goal does not, so each template states its level.
6. F7 and F8 templates at 1S use fractions in context, with the whole-by-fraction and fraction-by-fraction forms from concretisation lines 2089 to 2201.
7. The forms above support at least ten templates per subtype for F1 to F4, and fewer for F7 and F8 without repeating one form with different numbers; F7 and F8 need more readable source items to reach twenty.
8. A pair kind covers the numerator and denominator of form 5, and a set kind covers «select every fraction greater than 1/2»; both are approved.
9. Russian grade 5 content (Dutch group 7) is an extension for F4 to F8 wherever the Dutch group is earlier, and each such template names grade 5 and the passage.
10. Before F7 and F8 templates are written, Vilenkin grade 6 and the Tartu papers, which were not reached, are read.
