# The draft proposes one adventure a day through three or four Tower floors, with tasks in a separate window, battles that never harm the heroine, a Session 0 and a ritual with the parent

## Summary

The draft proposes that each day the player goes through one adventure across 3 or 4 floors of the Tower. Each floor has an entry scene, an unscored warm-up, mental arithmetic, rooms of trials, sometimes a Guardian and a chest. Tasks open in a plain task window, a panel separate from System windows, which shows a review and a second attempt after the first attempt and uses no words of right and wrong. Each answer is a spell against a Tangle; the room's length is fixed before it starts, so the number of spells and chests depends on time played, not on correctness. A first session, Session 0, runs 20-25 minutes without diagnostics and teaches the controls. After play the parent and the player may spend 5 minutes together, and the parent reads results later. This record covers the modes, the adventure's structure, the task window, the battle, Session 0 and the ritual with the parent. It leaves resume, clocks and breaks, the soft stop, task selection and rewards to other records.

## The question

What does one day of play look like, from entry to the end of the row, and what does the child see at each step? The draft assumes that a fixed shape (floors, rooms, a finale) can fit any pace by planning volume from her real tempo. A challenge to that: the draft's own time figures disagree, so the shape was sized for 30-45 minutes and now must fill the 60 minutes the owner decided.

## Method

Read the owner's draft «Хроники Башни — спецификация», sections «Приключение дня» (Adventure of the day) inside «Текущий объём (MVP)», «Игровой цикл, режимы, время и Сессия 0» (Game loop, modes, time and Session 0) with «Режимы» (Modes) and «Структура ежедневной сессии» (Structure of the daily session), «Окно задания» (Task window), «Бой с Путаницами» (Battle with the Tangles), «Сессия 0: „Пробуждение“» (Session 0: Awakening) and «Ритуал с родителем» (Ritual with the parent), on 2026-09-26.

The draft leaves open how the Director forecasts that time remains for a fourth floor, and what counts as a slow pace that allows a floor with no rooms.

## Findings

### The draft gives two lengths for the adventure, and the owner has settled on one hour

The MVP section says one adventure a day, aiming at about 60 minutes of active time. The game loop section says «около 30–45 минут» (about 30-45 minutes), and so does the modes table. The owner settled on 2026-09-26 that the adventure targets 60 minutes of active time. The MVP section outranks the rest of the draft, and it agrees with the owner.

### Resolved: The adventure lasts 60 minutes, the soft stop comes at 60 minutes of active time, and an extension adds 20 minutes

The owner decided on 2026-09-26: the adventure lasts 60 minutes, and the soft stop comes at 60 minutes of active time. The modes table below now gives 60 minutes in place of the game loop section's 30-45. Research on the same day kept the MVP section's 20 minutes for «Ещё один ряд» and set the default daily maximum at 100 minutes, so that two extensions fit after the soft stop, or time in the Tower without tasks after an early finale; RES-0300 holds the comparison. Proposed by research on 2026-09-26; the owner approves it with this record. The owner's decision of 2026-09-27 replaced the daily-maximum part: the game has no daily maximum, and the soft stop returns after each extension (RES-0300, «Resolved: the game has no daily maximum»).

### The Director plans the adventure's volume from her real pace

The 60-minute length is a soft target. The Director plans the adventure's volume from her actual pace, the median time over the last 5 days, not from the clock. No timers appear on screen.

### The MVP adventure has a fixed order of parts

«В прошлый раз…» (Last time…) and the daily quests, then a route of 3 maths floors (4 if the forecast leaves time), then on each floor: an entry scene, an unscored warm-up, mental arithmetic of 2 tasks, 1-2 rooms of 3-5 tasks each, sometimes a Guardian, the floor chest, then a finale with a cliffhanger. The Observatory gets a short visit (2-3 questions) once every three adventure days. The guide figure is 25-35 first attempts per adventure, plus second attempts after reviews.

### The MVP section repeats the eye exercise and the soft stop

An eye exercise comes every 20 minutes of active time. If the adventure isn't finished and the day's active time reaches 60 minutes, the story offers to save at the nearest boundary (after an answered task with its review, or after a scene): «Сегодняшний ряд связан наполовину. Башня подождёт.» (Today's row is half knitted. The Tower will wait.) The player can choose «Ещё один ряд» (One more row), which adds 20 minutes.

### The game has five modes, two of them deferred

| Mode | In the game | What it does | How often |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Daily adventure | «Приключение дня» (Adventure of the day) | 3-4 floors, adaptive trials with a review and a second attempt, story with the Master, report update | Every day, about 60 minutes (the draft's table said 30-45); an unfinished one continues tomorrow |
| Control run (deferred until after the MVP by the draft) | «Восхождение» (Ascent) | Chapter finale: anchor forms, the Guardian ladder, endurance; a comparable point for trends | Later: once every two weeks, one or two days |
| Free mode (deferred until after the MVP by the draft) | «Свободная прогулка» (Free Walk) | Story with the Master without tasks, Diary ciphers, collection, room, familiar battles | Later: by choice, up to 30 minutes a day |
| Tower without tasks | the heroine's room | Room, Diary, forge, shop, familiars | After the adventure's finale, until the day ends at 04:00 |
| Parent Room | hidden, behind a PIN | Report, map, trends, lesson marks, settings, choice of graphics | When needed |

### The daily session runs in five steps

1. Entry (1-2 minutes): «В прошлый раз…», or «Продолжаем с того же места» (We continue from the same place) if the adventure is unfinished; a System window with the daily quests and the morning stock of guiding threads.
2. Floors along the day's route: 3 maths floors (4 if the forecast leaves time) by the three-day window rule; the Observatory gets a short visit once every three days. The route alternates heavy and light domains and changes from day to day; in the story, the stairs retied themselves overnight.
3. On each floor: an entry scene (up to a minute), then a first task without scoring (the warm-up), then mental arithmetic of 2 tasks, then 1-2 rooms chosen by the Director (0 only at a slow pace), then sometimes a Guardian (a ladder of steps), then a chest. The Observatory has only a scene and 2-3 science questions. After each first attempt comes the correct answer; after a wrong answer, a short solution and a second attempt.
4. Rest stops, eye exercises and the offer to finish come through the story.
5. The end of the row (2-3 minutes, `camp` in code): the heroine returns to her room; a cliffhanger; a summary of growth (experience, level, familiars, quests, star-steel shards and star yarn collected) and the story states of the floors in words. No scores, no percentages and no comparison with past days.

The Director gives more rooms to floors with higher information value. Each domain gets its floor at least once in any three adventure days.

### The MVP section and the session structure disagree on floors without rooms

The MVP section says each floor has «1–2 комнаты по 3–5 заданий» (1-2 rooms of 3-5 tasks). The session structure allows «0 — только при медленном темпе» (0, only at a slow pace). The MVP section outranks the rest, so by the draft's rule a floor has at least 1 room.

### The task window is plain and shows only the task and its controls

A task opens in its own task window. The window holds no sprite animations, flickering effects or story text: only the task, the keyboard, the «Не знаю» (I don't know) button and the guiding thread button (a hint for a thread, which makes the attempt assisted). The draft said the task opens «в отдельном окне Системы» (in a separate System window); the resolved finding below replaces that.

### Resolved: a task opens in the task window, a flat panel separate from System windows, and the System only announces the knot

Proposed by research on 2026-09-26; the owner approves it with this record.

The draft opens a task in a System window. The owner's design (RES-3200, RES-3500) makes the task window its own component, `TaskWindow`: a flat `surface-200` panel with no gradient, lace, glow or motion, and `SystemWindow/README.md` says the System window is never used for a task. Two options were weighed:

- The draft's System window. It ties the task to the System's voice in the world, because the System hands out knots. But the System window is a translucent gradient at 84 % opacity with a glow, a lace hem and a pop entrance, and it is a polite live region, so a screen reader announces it again on every change.
- The design's separate task window. Its ground is solid, so long task text follows the design's rule that long text sits only on solid `surface-200` (RES-3100). It never moves, which keeps the draft's own ban on effects in the task window, and every task appears in one identical frame, so answer times from different tasks stay comparable.

The design wins, because it keeps everything the draft asks of the window and removes the one part, the System window's look, that works against reading and timing. The System keeps its role in the world: it announces the knot in a System window before the task («Обнаружен узел», Knot detected) and speaks the outcome line after it, and the task itself opens in the task window between the two. CAN-0030 states the same.

### After the first attempt the window stays open for the review

After the first attempt the window doesn't close at once. It shows a dry outcome line, her answer and the correct answer. After a correct answer it offers «Как легла нить» (How the thread lay), a free short solution, and a button for a detailed explanation that costs a thread. After a wrong answer or «Не знаю», the short solution shows at once, then a second attempt on a parallel task. Then the window closes, and the first attempt's outcome appears as a spell in the scene. The window uses none of the words «верно/неверно» (correct/incorrect) or «ошибка» (mistake) and none of the marks ✓/✗; the review is called «Схема узла» (The knot's scheme).

### The first task after an entry scene or any pause is an unscored warm-up

The unscored warm-up is the first task after a floor's entry scene and after any pause: eyes, rest stop or return from the background.

### Easy unscored tasks follow three alt outcomes and also appear at random

After 3 `alt` outcomes in a row (wrong or «Не знаю»), one easy unscored task follows, from a node in the fluent state or below the frontier. It is almost sure to untangle cleanly and gives back a feeling of strength. To keep it from signalling «I got it wrong», the same easy unscored tasks also appear at random places: at each boundary between tasks with probability 1/12 by the session seed, at most one in 5 tasks. The random insertion by seed doesn't depend on answers, so a session has 5-6 random insertions on average and usually fewer caused by errors; one easy task can't reveal its cause. An easy task looks the same as any other.

An easy task takes a room slot and isn't added to it: the room's length (3-5) doesn't change. The limit of one in 5 tasks applies only between random insertions by seed, so their places don't depend on answers. If an easy task caused by errors falls on a slot that already has a random insertion, one easy task is shown.

### «Не знаю» is always available and earns the same base experience as an answer

The «Не знаю» button is always there and gives the same base experience as an answer.

### Every answer is a spell, and the heroine is never harmed

The battle is the story's wrapping for trials. Each answer, «Не знаю» included, is a spell whose strength depends on the outcome:

- correct: a bright strike, the thread in place, a star-steel shard and a step towards a clean row;
- partly correct: a strike with a loop sticking out;
- wrong or «Не знаю»: the thread weakens gently, and the Tangle grows calmer and more agreeable.

The heroine never takes damage. The Tangle never attacks her and never laughs at her.

### Resolved: a spell has three outcomes, clean, almost and loosened

Proposed by research on 2026-09-26; the owner approves it with this record.

The world bible (CAN-0030) named two spell outcomes, clean and loosened, while this section, the System's lines and the rest of the specification name three. RES-1700 weighs the two options and holds the reason: the partial verdict already feeds the knowledge model, the success share and the room share, so a two-outcome world would disagree with the code. The three strikes above stand as the canon's three outcomes: a correct answer untangles cleanly, a partly correct answer gives «почти» (almost), with one loop sticking out, and a wrong answer or «Не знаю» loosens the knot. The room still ends on one of two branches.

### The draft fixes the room's length before it starts, so chests depend on time played and not on correctness

The Director assigns the number of tasks in a room (3-5, from the room seed) when the room opens and never changes it. Nodes and subtypes inside the room can adapt to answers, but their number can't. Escalation (topping up to a block, stepping down to a prerequisite) carries on in the next rooms. So the number of spells in a room, the moment of the chest and the number of chests per session depend only on time played. Correctness decides the strength of strikes, the bonuses, the quality of chest rewards and the branch the room ends on. If she saves at the soft stop mid-room, the room is saved and continues tomorrow from the same slot; the draft's hard end at the daily maximum is gone, because the owner removed the daily maximum on 2026-09-27.

### Battle lines follow the first attempt's outcome and avoid words of missing

Battle lines are assembled from the outcome of the first attempt. A stop list forbids lines with «промах» (miss), «мимо» (wide) and the like; a Tangle dodges only as a scripted pause between spells. The correct answer and the review show in the task window after the attempt, not in the scene.

### A room ends on a success branch or an alt branch, and both lead on

The `success` branch: the Tangle is untangled cleanly and floats off into the pattern, often leaving a gift. The `alt` branch: the Tangle is weakened and gives way in its own manner, and the heroine takes another path. Both lead equally to the next room or to the Guardian.

### Transitions never reveal where the Director moved in the graph

A change of node or floor always shows as one of the story transitions: a hidden hatch, a portal, a braided staircase, a door. They follow a shuffled cycle that doesn't depend on whether the Director moves to a prerequisite, a neighbouring node or upwards. Room branches show the outcome, never the Director's direction in the graph: a step down to a prerequisite and a step up look the same. The test «Исходы и ветки» (Outcomes and branches) checks that the sequence of transitions doesn't depend on the chosen nodes.

### Session 0 lasts 20-25 minutes, has no diagnostics and runs in eight steps

1. A transparency talk (1-2 minutes, in the System's text with a parent's voice alongside). The game has maths tasks. Tasks adapt to learn what she already knows, so they are sometimes hard, and that's normal. A correct solution makes a spell stronger and opens secrets. If it doesn't work out, or she pressed «Не знаю», the story takes another, equally interesting road; experience is still credited and nothing is taken away, and the System shows how the thread lay and gives a similar knot again. Guessing doesn't pay; an honest «Не знаю» is better. She can spend guiding threads on hints and on the familiar's explanations, and she gets plenty of them. Mum and dad see the results and can read the story. The storyteller is a computer program.
2. Creating the heroine: name, cloak colour, focus. The draft also listed appearance here; the heroine's look now comes from a character sheet the family picks before art is made (RES-3400).
3. The starting familiar: «Пуговка» (Button), «Винтик» (Little Screw) or «Безешка» (Little Meringue); name and traits.
4. The first chest: a choice of 1 of 3 rewards.
5. Training on trivial numbers: the keyboard, a fraction, «Не знаю», the scratchpad, choosing an option, the knot's scheme and the second attempt, a hint and an explanation for a guiding thread (the System gives the first threads).
6. Motor calibration: 10 tasks of plain input (type a shown number, a fraction, a time). The result is a correction for input speed on this device.
7. A vocabulary probe (about 5 minutes): the player matches Russian maths terms with pictures, such as «знаменатель» (denominator), «периметр» (perimeter) and «делимое» (dividend). The result marks risky terms.
8. The campaign's first scene (checkpoint 1) and the end of the row.

### After play the parent and the player may spend 5 minutes together

- After play, 5 minutes together if the player wants: she shows new familiars and reads a piece of the story.
- The parent looks at results later, without her.
- An Ascent (deferred until after the MVP by the draft) is announced the day before through the story, without a countdown: «Башня зажигает окна по вечерам. Готовится Восхождение.» (The Tower lights its windows in the evenings. An Ascent is being prepared.)
- The Parent Room holds a memo: don't discuss node estimates with the child; share her joy at triumphs in the story, but praise effort and courage, not «ум» (cleverness); don't question her about «хитрые обходы» (clever detours) or turn them into reproach; don't use the game as a reward or a punishment.

### Resolved: Session 0 sets the heroine's name, cloak colour and focus, and her look comes from a character sheet the family picks beforehand

Proposed by research on 2026-09-26; the owner approves it with this record.

The draft's Session 0 has the player choose her heroine's appearance. The owner's design fixes the look by keyart-heroine, and its hero screen notes that the family picks the heroine's sheet from four variants. RES-3400 compares a fixed look, a choice of four sheets in Session 0 and a choice of four sheets made once before art is made, and holds the reason for the last: it keeps the player's say in her heroine and one set of heroine art. So step 2 of Session 0 keeps the name, the cloak colour, which tints the cardigan and bows, and the focus.

## Conclusions

1. The adventure of the day must target 60 minutes of active time, and the Director must plan its volume from the median time over her last 5 days.
2. The adventure must run in the order: last time and daily quests, 3 maths floors (4 if the forecast leaves time), then a finale with a cliffhanger.
3. Each floor must run in the order: entry scene, unscored warm-up, 2 mental arithmetic tasks, rooms of 3-5 tasks, sometimes a Guardian, then a chest.
4. The Observatory must get a short visit of 2-3 science questions once every three adventure days.
5. The end of the row must show growth and floor states in words, and never scores, percentages or comparisons with past days.
6. The task window must be a flat panel separate from System windows, and must hold only the task, the answer field or options, the keypad, «Не знаю», the guiding thread button and «Готово», without sprites, effects or story text.
7. After a correct first attempt, the window must offer a free short solution and a detailed explanation for one thread; after a wrong answer or «Не знаю», it must show the short solution at once and then a second attempt on a parallel task.
8. The task window must never show «верно», «неверно», «ошибка», ✓ or ✗, and must call the review «Схема узла».
9. The first task after a floor's entry scene or any pause must be an unscored warm-up.
10. After 3 `alt` outcomes in a row, one easy unscored task must follow, and easy unscored tasks must also appear by the session seed with probability 1/12 per boundary, at most one in 5 tasks, looking like any other task.
11. An easy task must take a room slot, and the room's length must stay unchanged.
12. The room's length must be fixed at 3-5 from the room seed when the room opens.
13. The heroine must never take damage, and a Tangle must never attack or mock her.
14. Battle lines must never use «промах», «мимо» or similar words.
15. Both room branches must lead on equally to the next room or the Guardian.
16. Transitions between nodes or floors must follow a shuffled cycle independent of the chosen nodes, with a test that checks it.
17. Session 0 must last 20-25 minutes, contain no diagnostics, and run the eight steps in order, including the transparency talk, motor calibration of 10 tasks and the vocabulary probe.
18. The Parent Room must hold the memo on how to talk with the child about the game.
19. The soft stop must come at 60 minutes of active time on an unfinished adventure, the owner's decision, and each «Ещё один ряд» must add 20 minutes, after which the soft stop returns; the game has no daily maximum, as the owner decided on 2026-09-27.
20. Every first attempt must show as a spell with one of three outcomes, clean, almost or loosened, as RES-1700 sets out.
21. The System must announce a knot in a System window before the task and speak the outcome line after it, and must never hold the task itself.
22. Session 0 must let the player choose the heroine's name, cloak colour and focus, and must offer no choice of look.
