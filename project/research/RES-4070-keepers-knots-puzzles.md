# The owner's optional puzzle branch in the Keeper's Diary fits the approved record only after fourteen of its rules change, and it is named «Петельки Смотрителя», because «узелок» already names a familiar in every text the player sees

## Summary

The owner's addendum 1 of 2026-09-28 adds «Узелки Смотрителя» (the Keeper's knots) to the MVP: a voluntary branch of maths circle puzzles on the Keeper's Diary pages, outside the adventure's slots, the knowledge model and the day's budget, fed by a curated bank of about 50 puzzles over 11 themes with five interactive widgets. The branch can be built on the approved record, but it contradicts it in fourteen places, and each needs an amendment. The largest conflict is the name: every text the player sees already calls a familiar «узелок» (RES-3300), so «Узелки Смотрителя» reads as "the Keeper's familiars", and the branch is renamed «Петельки Смотрителя». The addendum also contradicts itself twice. Its rules open the puzzles at a rest stop, while its acceptance test 8 forbids them before the finale. Its hint table calls the rungs hand-written, while its preparation step has `PLANNER_MODEL` write them. Its preparation step also runs `PLANNER_MODEL` offline, and ADR-0100 refuses a play role on the offline key. After the finale no soft stop fires, because ADR-0090 brings it only on an unfinished adventure, so the soft stop must be extended to puzzle time. The six questions the record left open were decided on 2026-09-28: the name, puzzles at a rest stop, the bound after the finale, the puzzle of the week, its star yarn and the offline budget. The sources the addendum names exist and are published works under copyright; retelling a puzzle's idea in new words is lawful in Russia, the Netherlands and the United States, because each protects expression and excludes ideas and mathematical concepts. The record covers section 7 of the addendum and its acceptance test 8, and leaves the hint ladder's new price (section 1) and the parent's sandbox (section 13) to their own records.

## The question

What must now be true of the design so that the owner's puzzle branch works inside the approved record, and which approved records does it change? The constraints are the owner's: the branch is voluntary, never takes an adventure slot, never enters the knowledge model, shows at most one new puzzle a day and three open at once, and draws only on a bank the parent approved.

The question assumes the branch is separate from everything the approved record governs, so that adding it only adds. It isn't. The branch lives in the Diary, whose voice and signatures the canon fixes around the central mystery (CAN-0090, CAN-0080). It spends guiding threads and pays star yarn from the economy RES-2100 balanced. It runs after the finale, when ADR-0090 opens only screens without tasks, and at the rest stop, whose time the eye count excludes. It adds a report section to a report fixed at eight screens, and a model pipeline to a gateway that ties every role to a key. Each of those touches is a place where "outside the adventure" still has to follow a rule written for the adventure.

The question also assumes the player wants puzzles. The approved backlog made that the signal for shipping Diary ciphers after the MVP: "Diary ciphers: if she likes puzzles" (RES-3000). The addendum reverses the order: the branch ships in the MVP and grows "по интересу игрока" (by the player's interest). The record therefore has to measure that interest from the first day, or the growth rule has nothing to read.

## Findings

### The owner puts a voluntary puzzle branch with fixed limits into the MVP

The addendum's section 7 makes «Узелки Смотрителя» an optional branch of circle-tradition puzzles: logic, parity, the pigeonhole principle, weighings, pourings, working backwards, enumeration, grid cuttings, number rebuses and patterns. Its stated aim is to train the reasoning that "в VWO важнее скорости счёта" (matters more in VWO than speed of calculation) and to give a ceiling without pressure. A puzzle appears on a Diary page after the day's finale, as a drawing and a short riddle in the Keeper's hand, and a bigger "puzzle of the week" stays open for several days. Puzzles never take an adventure slot and never enter the flow limit, the domain coverage rule or the day's budget. At most one new puzzle comes a day and at most three stay open; a puzzle can be put away into a "box" and then stops counting as open. Nothing is lost for an unsolved puzzle. The addendum's table puts the item in the MVP and says it changes "новый раздел, Дневник, отчёт, «Что входит в MVP»" (a new section, the Diary, the report and the MVP contents list). Source: the owner's addendum 1, section 7 and the table of additions, read 2026-09-28.

### The owner fixes the bank at about 50 puzzles over 11 themes, prepared offline and approved by the parent

The bank lives in `content/puzzles/*.json`. Each puzzle carries a statement wrapped in the canon's story, an answer type, a check in code, 3 hint rungs, a full solution, idea tags, a difficulty from 1 to 5 and the source of the idea. The answer formats are a number, a set of numbers, an arrangement (a magic square or a rebus), grid cells (cuttings) and a sequence of moves in a widget. Five widgets cover weighings («Весы Базара»), pourings («Кувшины»), knights and liars («Таблица рыцарей и лжецов»), grids («Клетчатое поле») and matchsticks («Спички»). The MVP holds every widget and every theme, at least 4 puzzles a theme at difficulty 1 to 4, and about 50 in all; difficulty 5 waits for the player's reaction. Preparation runs offline. `PLANNER_MODEL` retells the idea in the setting and writes the rungs, `CHECK_MODEL` solves it blind and must match the code check, and `SAFETY_MODEL` checks it. The parent then approves it in the review queue, which the addendum estimates at about an hour and a half for 50 puzzles. The Director of puzzles doesn't repeat a theme until the last 7 days held at least 4 others, and the first puzzles come from the second or third adventure, not from «Пробуждение» (the Awakening). If fewer than 20 are approved or a theme is empty, the branch starts with what exists and an empty theme never comes up; the rest of the MVP doesn't depend on it. The owner rules out live generation, because "хорошую нестандартную задачу модель составляет плохо, и ошибка в условии здесь особенно разрушительна" (a model composes a good non-standard problem badly, and an error in the statement is especially destructive here). Source: the owner's addendum 1, section 7, read 2026-09-28.

### The addendum contradicts itself on whether a puzzle opens before the finale

The rules say puzzles are available "в любое время, кроме открытого окна задания: во время приключения — на привале, после финала — сколько угодно" (at any time except while a task window is open: during the adventure at a rest stop, after the finale as much as she likes). Acceptance test 8 says "Узелки: не до финала приключения" (puzzles: not before the adventure's finale). The two can both hold only if test 8 means that a new puzzle is offered after the finale while an open one can be worked at a rest stop, and the addendum doesn't say so. Source: the owner's addendum 1, section 7 "Правила" and "Приёмочные тесты" item 8, read 2026-09-28.

### The addendum contradicts itself on who writes the hint rungs

Section 1's table gives the puzzles' rungs as "написанные вручную" (hand-written). Section 7's preparation step says "`PLANNER_MODEL` пересказывает идею в сеттинге и пишет ступени" (retells the idea in the setting and writes the rungs). Both can hold if "hand-written" means fixed text a person approved, with no generation at play time, which is what the parent's approval gives. RES-0500 conclusion 9 has the engine "build the three hint rungs from the template's computation graph with no LLM", and a puzzle has no template, so neither reading fits that rule as written. Source: the owner's addendum 1, sections 1 and 7, read 2026-09-28.

### «Узелок» already names a familiar in every text the player sees, so «Узелки Смотрителя» reads as "the Keeper's familiars"

RES-3300, RES-1900 (conclusion 13) and RES-2000 (conclusion 13) make «узелок» the familiar's name in every player-facing text, and the approved record lets a knot be called only «узел» (knot) or «петелька» (little loop) there. CAN-0080 renamed Mister Knot «Бантик» (Little Bow) "because every text the heroine sees now calls a familiar «узелок», and his name would read as any familiar", approved by the owner on 2026-09-27, and the canon's System lines already say «Узелок разжёг костёр из искр» (the familiar lit a fire of sparks) (CAN-0030). So the branch's name says "the Keeper's familiars" to the player. The canon gives the Keeper exactly one familiar, the cat Muff, and keeps "The Keeper had a cat called Muff" for checkpoint 7 in May (CAN-0080). The addendum chose «Узелки Смотрителя» over «Загадки Ирмы» so the name wouldn't give the mystery away; the chosen name keeps Irma out but points at the Keeper's familiar, which the mystery also withholds until May. Sources: RES-3300, RES-1900, RES-2000, CAN-0030, CAN-0080, the owner's addendum 1, section 7 "Название", read 2026-09-28.

### The canon signs the Diary «С.» and paces the «И.» clue at once every several sessions

CAN-0090 says young Irma "signs «С.» (K., for «Смотрительница», Keeper), and on a few old pages she forgot to correct she signs «И.»". CAN-0080 lists the signatures «И.» and «С.» among the running clues, which "the Master weaves in one at a time, no more often than once every several sessions", and holds the answer until checkpoint 9 in July. The addendum lets a puzzle carry "мелкие зацепки канона (подпись «И.», вязаные мотивы)" (small canon hooks: the signature «И.», knitted motifs) but never reveal the mystery. A puzzle page can come every day, so a puzzle signed «И.» each time would run the clue daily, faster than the canon allows. Sources: CAN-0090, CAN-0080, the owner's addendum 1, section 7, read 2026-09-28.

### The approved record closes tasks after the finale until 04:00, and the addendum removes the clock window

ADR-0090 says "After the day's finale, and until 04:00, the server sends no task and opens only screens without tasks: the room, the Diary, the forge, the shop and the familiars", and RES-0300 ends the game day at 04:00. The addendum's general rules say the lines about 04:00 "больше не действуют" (no longer apply) and name «Узелки Смотрителя» among the places "доступны всегда" (always available). A puzzle is a maths problem with a check and a verdict, so whether "screens without tasks" admits it is unclear from the text; the rule's reason, keeping play to one new adventure a day, isn't touched by a puzzle, which is no adventure. Sources: ADR-0090, RES-0300, the owner's addendum 1, general rules, read 2026-09-28.

### After the finale no soft stop fires, so the owner's rule that it covers puzzle time has no mechanism

ADR-0090 brings the soft stop "When the day's active time reaches the soft-stop point on an unfinished adventure", and says "The game has no daily maximum", by the owner's decision of 2026-09-27: "no rule ends play or withholds a task because of the day's total". The addendum says "Мягкая остановка действует как везде: время на узелки входит в общее время игры" (the soft stop works as everywhere: puzzle time counts in the day's play time). After the finale the adventure is finished, so the soft stop never plays, and a puzzle session after the finale has no bound but the eye exercise, which ADR-0090 plays at once on the screens after the finale. Sources: ADR-0090, the owner's addendum 1, section 7, read 2026-09-28.

### The eye count excludes rest-stop time, and the addendum puts puzzles at the rest stop

RES-0300 says "The 20-minute counter measures active time since the last exercise, without the time of the exercise itself or of rest stops", and describes a rest stop as a campfire scene with the familiars of 2 to 5 minutes. A puzzle worked at a rest stop is close work on a grid or a table, and under that rule its minutes would not count towards the eye exercise. Sources: RES-0300, the owner's addendum 1, section 7, read 2026-09-28.

### The approved MVP scope has no puzzle branch and defers the Diary's only riddles until the player shows she likes puzzles

ADR-0190 lists the MVP contents for stage 0.3 and the deferred items, "Diary ciphers" among them, as RES-0010 records them. RES-3000 sets the signal for shipping ciphers: "Diary ciphers: if she likes puzzles". ADR-0190 also runs a scope guard that fails the build on named traces of deferred items. The puzzle branch appears in none of the lists. Sources: ADR-0190, RES-0010, RES-3000, read 2026-09-28.

### The approved record forbids lessons that teach a new maths topic, and a puzzle's solution page teaches an idea

ADR-0190 says "The game offers no lesson that teaches a new topic: every task comes from a template tied to a node", so the game measures and consolidates and the parent teaches new topics outside it. A solved puzzle gives "страница Дневника с решением почерком Смотрителя" (a Diary page with the solution in the Keeper's hand), and the themes include the pigeonhole principle and parity, which the skill graph doesn't teach. A solution page that explains the pigeonhole principle teaches a new idea, though not a school topic of the graph. Sources: ADR-0190, the owner's addendum 1, section 7 "Награды", read 2026-09-28.

### The approved record keeps non-maths Diary play out of the knowledge model by event type

RES-1500 concludes that "Cipher puzzles in the Keeper's Diary must never be scored or logged as maths", and CAN-0090 calls decoding "a voluntary riddle outside trials and outside diagnostics". ADR-0110 gives the mechanism: "a cipher answer is a `diary_cipher` event, which the input filter of the knowledge model (ADR-0060) excludes". ADR-0060 takes as an observation only "a graded, unassisted first attempt". The addendum keeps puzzles out of the knowledge model and the list of gaps, because "их результаты шумные" (their results are noisy), and adds the events `puzzle_offered`, `puzzle_opened`, `puzzle_move`, `puzzle_attempt`, `puzzle_hint`, `puzzle_solved` and `puzzle_shelved`. A puzzle is maths, unlike a cipher, so the cipher rule's wording doesn't cover it. Sources: RES-1500, CAN-0090, ADR-0060, ADR-0110, the owner's addendum 1, section 7 and the events list, read 2026-09-28.

### ADR-0100 refuses a play role on the offline key, and the addendum runs `PLANNER_MODEL` offline

ADR-0100 lists `PLANNER_MODEL` in the player tier and says "The gateway refuses an offline role on the play key, and a play role on the offline key outside verify mode", so an offline run can't spend the month's play limit. The offline frame pipeline uses `GEN_MODEL` and `CHECK_MODEL` on the content tier (ADR-0130, RES-0720). ADR-0100's budget table has buckets for the adventure, explanations, the month, the art run, the bake-off and live art, and none for a puzzle-bank run. The addendum's model table has `PLANNER_MODEL` retell puzzles and write rungs "офлайн", and `CHECK_MODEL` solve them blind offline. Sources: ADR-0100, ADR-0130, RES-0720, the owner's addendum 1, "Модели и бюджет", read 2026-09-28.

### The approved blind solve compares one answer with the engine's, and most puzzle formats have more than one right answer

ADR-0130's blind solve fills a frame with three sets of numbers, lets the checking model solve each "stating an answer in free form", and passes the frame "only if all three match" the engine's answer. "The solver sees the problem text and nothing else: no answer and no list of candidate answers". A puzzle has one fixed set of numbers, and its formats include grid cuttings, arrangements and move sequences, where several different answers can all be right. The addendum's "его ответ должен совпасть с проверкой в коде" (its answer must match the code check) works for such formats only if the code check validates any answer against the puzzle's rules and doesn't compare with one stored answer. Sources: ADR-0130, the owner's addendum 1, section 7, read 2026-09-28.

### A language model may not compute a task's numbers, and the addendum has one retell the puzzle

RES-1200 finds that "the language model never computes numbers or answers" in a task. ADR-0130 lets model text into a task only as "a placeholder frame" whose numbers code fills. The addendum's `PLANNER_MODEL` "пересказывает идею в сеттинге" (retells the idea in the setting), and in a pouring or a weighing puzzle the numbers are the idea: jugs of 3 and 5 litres measure 4, while jugs of 2 and 4 can measure no odd amount. Sources: RES-1200, ADR-0130, the owner's addendum 1, section 7, read 2026-09-28.

### The parent's approval is the guard ADR-0130 already found weakest, and the puzzle review asks more of it per item

ADR-0130 records each approval as an event holding the item's hash and estimates about a minute a frame, "about two and a half hours in all" for frames. Its strongest objection is that "a queue that long tends to end approved unread". The addendum's estimate of about an hour and a half for 50 puzzles gives about 1.8 minutes a puzzle, for a statement, 3 rungs, a full solution and a widget setup each. Sources: ADR-0130, the owner's addendum 1, section 7 "Готовность к первому дню", read 2026-09-28.

### One forbidden-word list binds every text the player sees, the puzzles included

RES-3300 resolves that one forbidden list binds "every text the heroine sees, in every word form", and ADR-0160 checks it on every string, naming "a Diary page" as a place a shame word can reach her. The list holds verdict and shame words such as «неправильно», «ошибка», «промах» and «мимо», and «задача», «пример», «урок» and «школа». Circle puzzles are traditionally phrased as «задача», and the addendum's reply after two wrong answers has to be written without the verdict words. Sources: RES-3300, ADR-0160, read 2026-09-28.

### Task parameters must not depend on the display language, and a letter rebus does

RES-2550 concludes that "Task parameters must not depend on the language", and ADR-0160 puts every player-facing string in a per-language file, as the repository's CLAUDE.md asks, so English and Dutch can be added later. The addendum names the bank `content/puzzles/*.json` and lists "числовые ребусы" (number rebuses) and "расстановка (магический квадрат, ребус)". A cryptarithm spelled in Russian words has no Dutch or English version with the same letters, while a magic square or a rebus in digits and shapes carries over unchanged. Sources: RES-2550, ADR-0160, the owner's addendum 1, section 7, read 2026-09-28.

### Puzzle rewards add a star yarn source to an economy balanced without one, and give no adventure advantage

RES-2100's amounts table lists every source of star yarn: 3 for a finished Guardian problem, 1 for each clean row and big clean row, 2 for an optional pattern row, and 2 to 4 from a chest. The addendum gives "звёздная пряжа" (star yarn) for a solved puzzle without an amount, a collection mark by theme, and a title for each series of 5 puzzles of one theme. Star yarn buys forge recipes, whose tricks change "the spell's animation and sound, not its power" (RES-2100), and ADR-0140 fails the build on "a trick field read by any rule". So puzzle yarn speeds cosmetics only, which fits the addendum's rule of no bonuses in the adventure. RES-2100 already lists "odd achievements" among the sources of titles. Sources: RES-2100, ADR-0140, the owner's addendum 1, section 7 "Награды", read 2026-09-28.

### The addendum's free thread and repeated answers fall outside the thread and attempt rules written for adventure tasks

RES-0500 prices each hint rung at one thread on adventure tasks, tops the stock up from the backpack pocket only at zero, and expects about 8 to 10 threads a day. RES-0400 gives one parallel second attempt and no third. The addendum lets the player answer a puzzle as often as she likes, and after two wrong answers the familiar offers one free thread, once a puzzle. None of the approved rules covers a puzzle, because each is written for adventure tasks. Sources: RES-0400, RES-0500, the owner's addendum 1, section 7, read 2026-09-28.

### Report v1 has eight fixed screens, and the new section fits one of them

ADR-0180 fixes report v1 at "the eight screens of report v1", which RES-2300 lists: the summary, VWO readiness, the graph map, the node card, misconceptions, limits, science and the story book. RES-2300 puts the ceiling in the VWO readiness ladder («1S с запасом и потолком выше», 1S with a margin and a ceiling above) and on the graph map as the «потолок» zone. The addendum's section «Нестандартное мышление» (non-standard thinking) shows puzzles solved by theme, the mean hint rung, puzzles she came back to by herself and puzzles solved without a hint on the second or third day, and it "стоит рядом с «потолком выше 1S» и читается как запас, а не как оценка" (stands beside "the ceiling above 1S" and reads as a reserve, not as an assessment). Sources: ADR-0180, RES-2300, the owner's addendum 1, section 7 "Измерение", read 2026-09-28.

### Russian, international and US law protect a puzzle's wording and leave its idea free

Article 1259 point 5 of the Russian Civil Code says "Авторские права не распространяются на идеи, концепции, принципы, методы, процессы, системы, способы, решения технических, организационных или иных задач, открытия, факты" (copyright doesn't extend to ideas, concepts, principles, methods, processes, systems, ways, solutions of technical, organisational or other problems, discoveries, facts). Point 3 protects works "выраженные в какой-либо объективной форме" (expressed in some objective form). Article 2 of the WIPO Copyright Treaty and article 9(2) of the TRIPS Agreement both say protection extends "to expressions and not to ideas, procedures, methods of operation or mathematical concepts as such". The Dutch Auteurswet has no clause stating the rule itself, and I didn't read the treaties' lists of parties, so the Dutch position rests on the treaty text. 17 U.S.C. §102(b) excludes "any idea, procedure, process, system, method of operation, concept, principle, or discovery". So the idea that jugs of 3 and 5 litres measure 4 is free to use, while an author's statement, its story, its figure and its written solution are protected. Sources: the Russian Civil Code on consultant.ru, the WIPO Copyright Treaty, the TRIPS Agreement, the Auteurswet on wetten.overheid.nl and 17 U.S.C. §102 on law.cornell.edu, read 2026-09-28.

### Russian law allows quotation only with attribution and in a volume the purpose justifies

Article 1274 point 1 of the Russian Civil Code allows use without consent "с обязательным указанием имени автора, произведение которого используется, и источника заимствования" (with the author's name and the source always stated), for quotation "в научных, полемических, критических, информационных, учебных целях … в объеме, оправданном целью цитирования" (for scientific, polemical, critical, informational and educational purposes, in a volume the purpose justifies). A bank of statements copied whole from a book would be neither a quotation nor a justified volume, and the game shows the player no author's name. The addendum's rule that texts are "пересказываются заново в сеттинге Башни, дословно не копируются" (retold anew in the Tower's setting, never copied word for word) keeps the bank on the side of ideas. Sources: the Russian Civil Code, article 1274, on consultant.ru; the owner's addendum 1, section 7, read 2026-09-28.

### Four ways to give her non-standard problems were compared, and the owner decided for the full curated bank

| Option | What it's better at | The case against it |

## Conclusions

1. The MVP must hold an optional puzzle branch in the Keeper's Diary, with at most one new puzzle a day and at most three open at once, a shelved puzzle counting as not open. A puzzle must take no slot in the adventure and no place in the flow limit, the domain coverage rule or the day's plan, and a puzzle left unsolved must cost her nothing. ADR-0190's stage 0.3 list and RES-0010's MVP contents must be amended to name it, and Diary ciphers stay deferred, because a cipher is a game with the alphabet and a puzzle isn't one.
2. Every text the player sees must name the branch «Петельки Смотрителя» and one puzzle «головоломка», never «узелок», Irma or the Keeper's familiar, because «узелок» already names a familiar (RES-3300) and the Keeper's familiar is withheld until checkpoint 7. Section 12's schedule and section 13's sandbox must use the same two words. Internal identifiers such as `puzzle_*` stay as they are, since the player never sees them.
3. A puzzle page must be signed «С.», and a puzzle may carry «И.» or another running clue only as one of CAN-0080's running clues, counted in that list's pace of one clue every several sessions. CAN-0090 must gain a section on puzzle pages that states this.
4. A new puzzle must be offered only after the day's finale, and the first one no earlier than the second adventure, as the addendum sets. An open puzzle must also be workable at a rest stop, which is how test 8 is read: it forbids a new puzzle before the finale, not work on an open one. Opening a puzzle at a rest stop must end the campfire scene, and closing it must return her to the adventure.
5. The game must open the puzzles with the room, the Diary, the forge, the shop, the familiars and «Свободное перо» at any moment the task window isn't open, during an adventure included, and at any hour after the finale. ADR-0090 and RES-0300 must be amended: the game day's boundary stays at 04:00 in the device's time zone as an unseen service value that no screen, line or number names, and it keeps its jobs, among them one new adventure a day, the daily quests and the daily rewards; "screens without tasks" gains the puzzle branch by name, because its reason, one new adventure a day, is untouched by a puzzle.
6. Time spent on a puzzle must count in the day's active time and towards the 20-minute eye count wherever the puzzle is open, the rest stop included, because it is close work on the screen. RES-0300 and ADR-0090 must be amended to exclude only the rest stop's campfire scene, and not a puzzle opened from it.
7. After the finale, the soft stop must fire on puzzle time when the day's active time reaches the soft-stop point, offering to put the puzzle away with «Ещё один ряд» as usual, because the addendum says the soft stop works as everywhere and ADR-0090 otherwise brings it only on an unfinished adventure. It must end nothing by force, so the game still has no daily maximum. ADR-0090 and RES-0300 must be amended to match.
8. The knowledge model must never read a puzzle event. Every puzzle action must be written as its own event type (`puzzle_offered`, `puzzle_opened`, `puzzle_move`, `puzzle_attempt`, `puzzle_hint`, `puzzle_solved`, `puzzle_shelved`), never as a graded attempt, and the model's input filter must exclude them as it excludes `diary_cipher`. RES-1500's rule for ciphers must be widened to every Diary puzzle, and ADR-0020 and ADR-0110 must list the new events.
9. ADR-0190's rule against lessons that teach a new topic must be amended to allow a puzzle's solution page, because the addendum wants the branch to teach circle ideas such as parity and the pigeonhole principle, while the ban on teaching new school topics keeps its reason for the skill graph's nodes.
10. Each puzzle must keep every number, every rule of the puzzle and its check in data and code written or approved by a person, with the statement's numbers filled from placeholders, because RES-1200 keeps a model from computing a task's numbers and a changed number can make a puzzle unsolvable. A model may retell only the words around them.
11. The code check must accept any answer that satisfies the puzzle's rules, not only a stored answer, because cuttings, arrangements and move sequences often have several right answers. For every widget puzzle the code must prove the puzzle solvable before it enters the review queue, and a stored reference solution must pass the code check.
12. The blind solve must show `CHECK_MODEL` the statement only, with no answer and no candidates, as ADR-0130 does for frames, and a puzzle passes when the code check accepts the model's free answer.
13. The offline preparation must run on the offline key with offline roles, because ADR-0100 refuses a play role there: the retelling and the rungs through `GEN_MODEL` or a new offline role, the blind solve through `CHECK_MODEL`, and the safety step as ADR-0130's frame pipeline runs it. ADR-0100 must be amended with the role and a budget bucket of its own for a puzzle run on the offline key, capped by the decision step that amends ADR-0100, and never spent from the play key.
14. The game must serve only puzzles whose current content hash the parent approved, recorded as an approval event as ADR-0130 records frames, and any edit must void the approval. The review screen must show the statement, the three rungs, the full solution, the code check's result on the reference solution and the source of the idea, so the parent can judge each puzzle in the time the addendum budgets.
15. Every puzzle rung must be fixed text approved with the puzzle, with no model call when she plays, so no model runs before her answer, and the addendum's "hand-written" and "written by `PLANNER_MODEL`" both hold. RES-0500 conclusion 9, which builds every rung from a template's computation graph with no model, must be amended to name the approved puzzle bank as the other source of rungs, because a puzzle has no template graph.
16. Every puzzle string, including the familiar's lines after a wrong answer, must pass the one forbidden-word list of RES-3300 in every word form, so no puzzle calls itself «задача» or answers with «неправильно».
17. The bank must split into language-free puzzle data and per-language strings, as RES-2550 and ADR-0160 require for tasks, and a rebus must use digits or shapes, because a rebus spelled in Russian words has no Dutch or English version with the same parameters.
18. Each bank entry must name the source of its idea, and its statement, story, figure and solution must be written anew, never copied or scanned from the source, because the sources are under copyright and the free MCCME PDFs allow only reading and printing, while the law leaves ideas free.
19. A solved puzzle must give a Diary solution page, star yarn, a mark in the collection by theme and, for each series of 5 in one theme, a cosmetic title, with the same reward after a hint and no advantage in the adventure. A solved puzzle must give 2 star yarn and the puzzle of the week 3, and RES-2100's amounts table, ADR-0140's content and the balance simulation must include them.
20. A puzzle must allow repeated answers, and after the second wrong answer the familiar must offer one free thread, once a puzzle. RES-0400's rule of no third attempt and RES-0500's price of a rung must be amended to say they bind adventure tasks, and RES-0500's thread balance must count the puzzle ladder's spend.
21. The puzzle of the week must count as one of the three open puzzles and must come as that day's one new puzzle, so both of the owner's limits hold as written.
22. The bank's size must not gate the build, because the addendum starts the branch with whatever is approved and lets an empty theme never come up; the verify command must check each puzzle, and not a count per theme as it does for science.
23. The Director of puzzles must not repeat a theme until at least 4 other themes have come in the last 7 days, and must skip a theme with no approved puzzle.
24. Report v1 must show «Нестандартное мышление» on the VWO readiness screen beside the ceiling, labelled as a reserve and not an assessment. The section must show puzzles solved by theme, the mean hint rung, puzzles she returned to by herself, puzzles solved without a hint on the second or third day, and puzzles offered, opened and shelved. The last three counts are the data the owner's rule reads when it grows the branch by her interest. Report v1 keeps its eight screens, and ADR-0180 must add the section.
