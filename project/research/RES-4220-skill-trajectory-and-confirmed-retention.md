# A planned retention check measures a stable node fairly only if the Director holds the node back from every path that shows it, and the log already records every fact the weekly breakdown, the trajectory and the retention status read except the task a short solution or a hint rung belongs to

## Summary

The owner's addendum 2 of 2026-09-28, item 3, asks for three things. The first is a weekly breakdown of each node's first attempts by depth of help. The second is a trajectory of each node over time. The third is a confirmed-retention status: after a node first reaches «устойчиво» (stable), the Director plans a retention check 28 to 35 days later and keeps the node out of the frontier and review until then. Two correct observations out of 2, or 3 out of 4, confirm retention. The evidence supports a check after a gap. In a field study of 672 pupils in the ASSISTments tutor, pupils who had shown mastery by three right answers in a row still failed about a fifth of retention tests: 76.0 % were right after 14 days, against 80.4 % after 1 day. How fast a pupil reached mastery predicted who failed. The approved record conflicts with the item in seven places. The first four are Director rules. A held node breaks three of them: the spaced-review ladder, the priority of a node unseen for more than 30 days and the rule that review takes the node unchecked longest. It narrows the fourth, mental arithmetic, which may show a stable node once a week, to never, which that ceiling allows. The fifth is the dynamics rule, which admits only unassisted first attempts from blocks, probes and review. The sixth is the name «на пороге» (on the threshold), which already names a node mark. The seventh is report v1, which holds no dynamics screen. The item also leaves open when a series fails, which paths the hold covers and what happens after a failure. Research decided every open question on 2026-09-28, below. The report views and the Director's check join the post-MVP backlog. The MVP gains one thing: `solution_shown` and `hint_shown` name the task they belong to, because a later report can't find the node of a short solution without it. I cover addendum 2 item 3, its events and its acceptance test 1, and leave the profile, transfer, the four quadrants, the Dutch probe, the riddles and the hypotheses to the records on its other items.

## The question

What must the game, its Director, its log and its report do so that the weekly breakdown, the trajectory and the retention status measure what the owner wants, and what must change in the approved record for that? The owner's addendum 2 is the instruction, dated 2026-09-28, and it outranks addendum 1 and the approved record where they disagree.

The item assumes that a stable node naturally goes unseen for weeks, so a check "if it doesn't come up naturally" is rare, and that holding the node back only from the frontier and review is enough to keep the gap clean. Both parts fail on the approved record. A stable node doesn't go unseen: mental arithmetic may show it once in any 7 days, review takes the eligible node unchecked longest, and a lesson mark starts two recheck blocks. Other paths show a node's task too: the Guardian's word problems, the control facts at every adventure's start and end, riddles and the fact Volley. So a gap of 21 days forms only where the Director blocks every one of these paths, and the question becomes which paths the hold covers and which nodes can't be held at all. The item also assumes that "2 of 2 or 3 of 4" is a complete rule. It says when retention is confirmed, not when it fails, and not what happens between the checks.

## Findings

### The addendum fixes the breakdown, the trajectory, the retention observation, the check, the status and three fields

The owner's addendum 2, item 3, read 2026-09-28, sets these rules. For each node and subtype the report splits each week's first attempts into «сама» (on her own), «на пороге (хватило ступени 1)» (on the threshold, rung 1 was enough), «с опорой (ступени 2–3)» (with support, rungs 2 to 3) and «требует обучения (разбор)» (needs teaching, a walkthrough). It shows "5 сама, 1 на пороге, 2 требует обучения" in place of "5 of 8". The trajectory is a line over dates with the share «сама», the mean depth of help from addendum 1 item 1, and marks for walkthroughs, the parent's lessons and review intervals. Its example ends with a row "through a month without preparation", which the item calls the point of the whole line.

A retention observation is an unassisted first attempt on a node when at least 21 days have passed since the last meeting with the node, in any subtype. In that time the node must have no lesson mark and no walkthrough on the prerequisites "that hint at the answer". When a node first reaches «устойчиво», the Director plans a retention check for it 28 to 35 days later, unless the node comes up naturally before. In that time the node is chosen neither by the frontier nor by review, only by the check itself, and to her the check is an ordinary trial. «Удержание подтверждено» (retention confirmed) means 2 of 2 or 3 of 4 retention observations are right. The item calls it the strongest sign of mastery in the report. A failure is a signal to return the node to review and to mark it on the trajectory. The report lists «удержание подтверждено» and «удержание не подтвердилось» (retention not confirmed) with dates, and item 3's report line adds "planned". The shown-task event gets `purpose: "retention_check"` and `daysSinceLastExposure`, and the event list adds `retention_check_planned`. Acceptance test 1 reads: the check is planned 28 to 35 days after the first «устойчиво», the node isn't chosen by the frontier or review in that time, and an observation with a lesson mark inside the interval doesn't count.

Item 1 asks every figure of the profile and the trajectory to show its number of observations and an 80 % interval, and to be marked «мало данных» (too little data) and left out of conclusions on small data.

### Stable nodes meet her every week on the approved Director, so a 21-day gap forms only when the Director blocks every path

ADR-0070 lets a node in «устойчиво» enter mental arithmetic no more than once in any 7 consecutive days, which an approved requirement fixes (read 2026-09-28). The rule is a ceiling, so a stable mental node can appear four times in 28 days. A review task comes from a node tested «бегло» (fluent) or «устойчиво» whose expected chance of success is at least 0.85, and among eligible nodes the Director takes the one unchecked longest. So the longest-unseen stable node is exactly the one review picks next. ADR-0060's review ladder puts a node's next review 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days after the 1st to 5th unassisted success in a row, then every 30 days. Stable needs "fluent" in two checks at least 14 days apart, so a node reaches stable around the 4th or 5th success, when its next review is 14 or 30 days away. A node whose last unassisted first attempt is more than 30 days old gets priority in task selection, which another approved requirement fixes.

Five more paths show a node's task apart from the frontier and review. The Guardian brings word problems on about one floor in three, choosing steps from the T nodes' states, not a node by value. Each adventure holds 2 control facts at its start and 2 at its end, and a control fact updates its node's estimate. A lesson mark opens recheck blocks on days 1 to 3 and 12 to 16 (ADR-0180). Riddles name their node in `compose_shown` (SPC-0230), and the Volley shows fact rows as `item_shown` with a `factId` (SPC-0290). Diary puzzles carry a theme and no node (SPC-0280), so they are no meeting with a node. ADR-0070 gives a node's room tasks only on its domain's floor, and its three-day window gives every domain a completed floor in any 3 consecutive adventure days when each day completes 3 floors. Every path that shows a task of a node therefore writes an event that names the node, and the gap since the last meeting can be computed from the log.

### A held node breaks three approved Director rules and narrows a fourth

Holding a stable node for 28 to 35 days conflicts with the review ladder, whose 14-day or 30-day review falls inside the hold. It conflicts with the stale-node priority, because from day 31 to day 35 the node is more than 30 days old and must get priority. It conflicts with the review order, which would pick the held node as the one unchecked longest. It changes mental arithmetic from "at most once in 7 days" to "never" for the held node, which the ceiling allows. The honesty rule of ADR-0070 isn't touched: a check chosen by its due date never depends on the success share. Nor is the rule that no response to her carries `purpose`: a check with `purpose: "retention_check"` looks like any other task.

The retention check itself is the observation the stale-node rule and the review ladder ask for, so the conflict is one of timing. It is real for a node that reaches stable at its 4th success: ADR-0060 would review it on day 14, and the hold moves that to day 28 at the earliest.

### The control-fact nodes and the word-problem nodes can't be held without breaking fixed rules

The control facts come at fixed points of every adventure and update their node, so a node that supplies control facts never goes 21 days unseen while she plays. Holding it would take the control facts away, and ADR-0070's fatigue signal reads their times. The Guardian's first problem of a day has k + 1 steps, where k is the largest number for which T_k is fluent or stable. After a clean outcome the next has k + 2, and after any other outcome it has k steps, which approved requirements fix (ADR-0070). A held T_k would forbid the k-step problem that the ladder must give after a non-clean outcome. The basic facts have their own states and the Volley (ADR-0290), which measure automaticity by showing facts often. Retention in the item's sense can't be measured for these nodes without breaking one of these rules.

### The ASSISTments retention tests show that mastery in a row doesn't predict retention, and that the gap matters less than how fast mastery came

Xiong and Beck describe ARRS in ASSISTments, which "assumes that if a student masters a skill with three correct responses in a row, such mastery is not necessarily an indication of long-term retention". It tests the skill again 7 days after mastery, then 14, 28 and 56 days after the previous test, and gives a relearning set after a failure (read 2026-09-28). In their experiment 672 middle and high school pupils from 34 classes were randomly assigned a first test 1, 4, 7 or 14 days after mastery. Retention fell from 80.4 % after 1 day to 76.0 % after 14 days. How fast the pupil reached mastery mattered more. Pupils who mastered in 3 or 4 problems answered 84.4 % of 1-day and 81.2 % of 14-day tests right. Pupils who needed more than 7 answered 67.5 % and 54.8 %. Before the experiment, on ARRS's fixed schedule outside it, they had already observed 82 % against 62 % right on the first retention test for the same two groups. Their conclusion is that retention intervals "should probably not be fixed, but should vary based on the student's knowledge of the skill".

So three right answers in a row leave about a fifth of pupils failing a retention test, and a retention check measures something the run doesn't, which supports the item. Performance during practice still carries information: speed to mastery predicted who failed. A node reached quickly and one reached slowly both enter "stable" through the same rule, and their retention differs more than any interval between 1 and 14 days explains. The report should show each node's retention observations with the node's history, not a pass mark alone.

### Spacing research puts the best review gap at about a twentieth to a fifth of the time the skill must last

Cepeda and colleagues taught 1354 adults 32 obscure facts, gave a second session after a gap of up to 3.5 months and tested at a further delay of 7, 35, 70 or 350 days (Psychological Science, 2008, read 2026-09-28 in its ERIC copy). "The optimum gap value was about 20% of the test delay for delays of a few weeks, falling to about 5% when delay was one year." For test delays of 35 and 70 days the best gaps among those tried were 11 and 21 days for recall. The study used adults and trivia, not children and procedures, so it can't set her interval. A 21-day gap was the best among those tried for a 70-day delay, and at one year the best was 21 days too, while 35 days is past the best gap for every delay tried. Holding a node for 28 to 35 days therefore costs some of the spacing benefit the review ladder exists for, more at 35 days than at 28, and the check itself is the review that follows the gap.

### In children's multiplication the spacing benefit at 1 week had gone at 5 weeks

Barzagar Nazari and Ebersbach report their earlier study in the introduction of a 2018 paper (Frontiers in Psychology, read 2026-09-28 on PubMed Central). Third graders practised semi-formal multiplication in three sessions on three consecutive days or in one massed session. Distributed practice helped strongly 1 week after the last session; "five weeks later, however, this effect disappeared". Seventh graders on probability showed the effect at 1 and 6 weeks. I didn't read the original, so these are the authors' own later summary. A result at 1 week can therefore differ from one at 5 weeks in children's arithmetic, which is the case for a check at 28 days or more over one soon after mastery.

### Performance during practice is an unreliable index of learning

Soderstrom and Bjork review motor and verbal learning and conclude that performance "measured during or immediately after the acquisition process" is "often an unreliable index of whether the relatively long-term changes that constitute learning have taken place". They define learning by "long-term retention or transfer" (Perspectives on Psychological Science, 2015, read 2026-09-28). This is the owner's point in the example trajectory's last row, and it is why a retention observation must follow a gap with no teaching in it: a right answer the day after a walkthrough measures performance.

### The rule 2 of 2 or 3 of 4 confirms a child with a 70 % chance about 70 % of the time

With early stopping, the item's rule confirms after two right answers, fails after two wrong ones, and otherwise goes on. It then fails after 1 of 3, because 3 of 4 is out of reach, and after 2 of 4. If each observation is right with probability p, independently, the chance of confirmation is p^2 + 2p^3(1 - p). That is 0.99 at p = 0.95, 0.96 at 0.9, 0.85 at 0.8, 0.70 at 0.7, 0.53 at 0.6 and 0.38 at 0.5 (my arithmetic, 2026-09-28). The expected number of observations is 2 + 2p(1 - p)(1 + p), about 2.3 at p = 0.9. The rule separates a child at 90 % from one at 50 % well, and a child at 90 % from one at 70 % poorly. ASSISTments' 14-day retention of 76 % sits in that poorly separated band. «Удержание подтверждено» is therefore weaker evidence than "the strongest sign of mastery" suggests. Item 1's falsifiability rule asks the report to show the counts beside the label, so the parent reads "2 of 2" as two observations.

Each observation needs its own gap of at least 21 days, so a series that needs all four observations takes at least three gaps after the first check, about 3 to 4 months at 28 to 35 days each.

Pelánek and Řihák, analysing mastery criteria on simulated and real data, found that "the choice of data sources used for mastery decision and the setting of thresholds are more important than the choice of a learner modeling technique" (New Review of Hypermedia and Multimedia, 2018, read 2026-09-28 in the authors' copy). This supports spending the care on which attempts count as retention observations, which the decisions below do.

### The item leaves open when a series fails, what happens between checks, and which walkthroughs spoil an observation

The item defines confirmation only. It doesn't say whether 0 of 2 fails at once or waits for 4, how the next check is planned after 1 of 2, or what "return the node to review" does. On the approved record, ADR-0060 already puts the next review 1 day after an unassisted failure. A review the next day ends the gap, so after a wrong retention answer the next observation can come only after a new 21-day gap. The item says the node returns to review, not whether a later check follows. It limits the spoiling walkthroughs to prerequisites "that hint at the answer", which no program can judge per task. It names lesson marks on the node, while ADR-0180's improvement labels read a mark on the node or any of its prerequisites.

The log's walkthroughs are three events: the short solution, `solution_shown`, which opens by itself after `partial` or `alt` (ADR-0080); the detailed explanation, `explanation_shown`, which names its `explanation_bought` and so its task (SPC-0120, SPC-0030); and each hint rung, `hint_shown`. ADR-0080 already reads all three as feedback on the node, marking every later task of the node that game day `postFeedback`. SPC-0020 lists the facts these events record but no payload field naming the task for `solution_shown` or `hint_shown` (read 2026-09-28). The `postFeedback` mark needs the task's node, so the server must know it. A later report that finds which node a short solution belonged to has only the event order to go on, and that order is fragile across resumes and devices.

### The owner's four bins overlap an approved node mark and need a rule for wrong answers and one-rung ladders

ADR-0220 defines «на пороге» as a mark on a node: its state is «Пока не освоено» or «Уточняется», it has at least 3 assisted attempts in 14 days, and at least 60 % of them were first attempts answered right with rung 1 as the deepest rung. An approved requirement fixes this, and SPC-0180 draws it on the Summary and the node card (read 2026-09-28). The item's bin «на пороге» counts one attempt. A parent who sees «на пороге» on a node card and "1 на пороге" in its week reads one word for two things.

The owner's example splits 8 first attempts into three bins, so every first attempt falls in exactly one bin. A wrong first attempt has to land somewhere, and the item's «требует обучения (разбор)» fits it. ADR-0080 shows the short solution at once after `partial` or `alt`, «Не знаю» included, so the walkthrough in the bin's name is the one the flow shows after every first attempt short of `clean`. ADR-0220 records that a ladder of one rung, on a basic fact or a one-step template, gives the whole method at rung 1. A reversal condition there warns that «на пороге» on basic facts would tell the parent a nudge was enough where the full hint was needed. `attempt_submitted` carries `hintLevel` and `hintMaxLevel`, so the bin can read both.

### The trajectory is a dynamics view, and the approved dynamics rule admits neither assisted attempts nor retention checks

An approved requirement, carried by ADR-0180 and SPC-0180, says every dynamics view uses only unassisted first attempts from blocks, probes and review (RES-1400). The item's breakdown counts assisted first attempts by depth, and its trajectory shows the mean depth of help beside the share «сама». A retention check is none of block, probe or review. RES-1400 set the rule so that a trend compares checked attempts. The share «сама» can keep that rule with retention checks added, and the depth columns can't keep it at all, since they count assisted attempts. Report v1 has nine screens and no dynamics screen, and the dynamics screen waits until after the MVP, as two approved requirements fix (ADR-0180, ADR-0360, SPC-0180). ADR-0220's mean depth averages the last 30 days, with 0 for no hint, the deepest rung 1 to 3 and 4 for a second attempt, and «нет данных» (no data) when there is none.

### At her pace a node's week holds a few first attempts, so a weekly share needs a floor and an interval

ADR-0070 plans about 30 graded first attempts an adventure, 9 to 14 of them on the frontier, across 69 mandatory nodes and their subtypes (RES-1000, read 2026-09-28). A node under active measurement gets a block of 5 in 7 days and then reviews spaced by the ladder, so most nodes see 0 to 5 first attempts a week and most subtypes 0 to 2; this is my estimate from the Director's rules. A count such as "3 сама, 1 требует обучения" is exact at any size. A share of 3 of 4 needs its interval: an 80 % Wilson interval for 3 of 4 runs from about 0.43 to 0.92. SPC-0180 already shows «мало данных» when an estimate-matrix cell holds fewer than 5 answers, and the limits when fewer than 3 sessions hold data.

### A plan written as an event survives a recompute, and a plan derived from the states doesn't

The stable state comes from rules under four versions, and a new threshold, rules or model version recomputes every past state (ADR-0060). A node stable on 12 October under one version can become stable on 19 October under the next, while the Director held it from 12 October. If the retention series were anchored at "the first «устойчиво»" computed afresh, a recompute would move the anchor, and an observation already counted would fall outside the series or a new one would enter. `retention_check_planned`, written when the Director acts, fixes the anchor in the log. The retention observation itself reads only facts no version changes: the tasks shown, the attempts' `assisted` flag and outcome, the walkthrough events, the lesson marks, and the graph's prerequisites under the graph version.

### The retention check stays inside "on her own", because it is a purpose and not a form

ADR-0210 keeps new forms out of "on her own" through the `forms` list of `item_shown` and the model's `admittedForms`, and ADR-0060 counts a review task as an ordinary observation (read 2026-09-28). A retention check shows an ordinary task of an existing template with a new `purpose`, like a review, and writes no new stream. The rule that new observation streams stay out of "on her own" therefore doesn't apply to it. The trajectory's share and the retention status read the same unassisted first attempts the estimate reads.

### Four ways to measure retention, and each wins at something

| Option | Better at | Case against |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Do nothing: keep ADR-0060's forgetting and the review ladder, and show no retention status | changes no rule; the review at 30 days already tests the node after a gap | weekly mental arithmetic, lesson rechecks and review keep the gap short, nothing marks which review came after a real gap, and the report can't say whether a skill lasted |
| Derive retention observations from the log alone, with no hold and no planned check | changes no Director rule and breaks none of the four conflicts; a projection over facts the log already has | the Director shows stable nodes often, so natural gaps of 21 days come mostly on nodes it neglects, and the status would rest on the nodes least looked after |
| The owner's check: plan one at 28 to 35 days and hold the node from every path that can be held | gives every holdable stable node one clean observation, and lets the report state retention for each | withholds a stable node for up to 5 weeks, moves reviews ADR-0060 would give at 14 or 30 days, takes up to one slot per check, and can't cover control-fact or word-problem nodes |
| An adaptive gap: a check sooner for a node that took many attempts to reach «устойчиво» and later for one reached fast, as Xiong and Beck suggest |  follows the one predictor of retention their data showed, and spends checks where a failure is likeliest | their data show the predictor, not a rule for the gap; one child gives too few nodes to fit one; and the owner fixed 28 to 35 days, so an adaptive gap would override the owner on evidence that doesn't set a number |
| ARRS's schedule: checks at 7, 14, 28 and 56 days after stable | tested on hundreds of pupils in a maths tutor, and relearns each failure at once | a 7-day check barely differs from a 1-day one in their data, the owner fixed 21 days, and four checks per node would take more slots than the frontier can spare |

Research chose the owner's check, with the log-only definition of a retention observation beside it, so that a natural gap also counts. It is the only option that measures retention on every holdable node with a gap the owner set. The case against it: the hold costs review a node would otherwise get, a gap of 35 days is past the best review gap Cepeda and colleagues found for every delay they tried, the rule of 2 of 2 separates 90 % from 70 % poorly, and the status never reaches the control-fact or word-problem nodes. The report must say so beside the list. The evidence gives no reason to abandon the idea, because ASSISTments shows a fifth of mastered skills failing a later test, and the learning and performance review shows that performance during practice is an unreliable index of learning.

## Conclusions

1. The report must split each node's first attempts of a week into four bins by what the first answer was and how much help came before it. «Сама» is a `clean` first attempt with no hint. «Хватило первой ступени» (rung 1 was enough) is a `clean` first attempt whose deepest rung is 1 on a ladder of two or more rungs. «С опорой» is a `clean` first attempt after rung 2 or 3, or after rung 1 on a ladder of one rung. «Требует обучения» is every first attempt that ends `partial` or `alt`, «Не знаю» included, at any depth. The bin «на пороге» must not be used, because that name belongs to the approved node mark.
2. The breakdown and the trajectory must count the graded first attempts shown for a block, a probe, review, a lesson recheck or a retention check that ADR-0060 doesn't drop. They leave out rapid guesses, excluded tasks, second attempts, mental arithmetic, control facts, the Volley and every task whose `forms` holds a new form. A week must be Monday to Sunday by ADR-0090's game day, and each bin must show a count, per node, with the split by subtype on the node card.
3. The trajectory must have one point per node and week, and the share «сама» at each point must pool that week with as many earlier weeks, up to 4, as it takes to reach 5 first attempts, and name the weeks it pooled. At 0 to 5 first attempts a week a single week rarely reaches the floor, and a line of blanks shows the parent nothing. The share carries its count and an 80 % Wilson interval, and shows «мало данных» in place of the share below 5 first attempts. The mean depth of help follows ADR-0220's definition over that week. The line must carry marks at their dates for each short solution or detailed explanation shown on the node, each lesson mark and «тренировали факты» mark, each review task and each retention observation with its result.
4. The dynamics rule must admit retention checks beside blocks, probes and review for the share «сама», and must admit assisted first attempts in the depth bins and the mean depth, labelled as help. The share «сама» must keep reading unassisted first attempts only.
5. A retention observation must be a graded, unassisted first attempt on a node that ADR-0060 doesn't drop, on a task shown for a block, a probe, review, a lesson recheck or a retention check, at least 21 game days after the node's latest meeting. A meeting is any `item_shown` or `compose_shown` of the node, in any subtype, purpose or form, second attempts included. It must not count when, since that meeting, the node had a lesson mark or a «тренировали факты» mark, or a direct prerequisite of the node, by the graph version active at the attempt, had a `solution_shown`, `explanation_shown` or `hint_shown`. Only a `clean` outcome counts as right. A mental arithmetic task, a control fact or a Volley row still counts as a meeting, and never as a retention observation, because it measures a fact's automaticity against a time threshold (ADR-0290), and the breakdown and the trajectory leave it out for the same reason.
6. When a holdable node reaches «устойчиво» for the first time, or a new series starts under conclusion 11, the Director must write `retention_check_planned` with the node, a `seriesId`, the check's number in its series from 1 to 4, the anchor date and a due window of 28 to 35 game days after the node's latest meeting, and must hold the node until its check. The hold covers the frontier, review, stale-node priority, island checks, mental arithmetic, riddles and the Volley.
7. The nodes that supply control facts, the basic-fact nodes of ADR-0290 and T1 to T4 must be exempt from the hold and the planned check. The report must show «удержание не измеряется» (retention not measured) for them with the reason, and must still list any natural retention observation they get.
