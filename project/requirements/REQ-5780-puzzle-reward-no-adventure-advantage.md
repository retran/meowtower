# REQ-5780

A puzzle MUST reward the player only with its Diary solution page, its mark in the collection, star yarn, a title and the free guiding thread of REQ-5786.

The owner rules out any bonus in the adventure from puzzles. Star yarn buys forge recipes whose tricks change how a spell looks and sounds and never its power, and the free thread serves only the puzzle's own hint ladder.
