# REQ-5250

Between 40% and 60% of the compound word problems (tiers T2 to T4) the player gets to solve in any 30 days MUST take step-by-step input, and the rest the final answer only, with riddles she composes counted in neither part.

The band and the 30-day window make the half RES-0700 asks for checkable. A riddle takes her text, not an answer, so it has no input to share out.
