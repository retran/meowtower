# REQ-6208

Every scene MUST offer three starters and a «Дальше» (Next) button that moves the story on while the text field is empty.

A starter is the start of a sentence the player can build on, so the words she sends are hers, and «Дальше» still means she never has to type. The requirements step kept the count of three from the approved scene design, since the addendum names no count.
