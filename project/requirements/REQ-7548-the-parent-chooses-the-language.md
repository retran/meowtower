# REQ-7548

The parent MUST choose the game's language in the parent settings, from Russian, English and Dutch, with Russian as the default, and the interface, the tasks and their pictures MUST follow that choice, because the parent decides what the player reads.

A player who sees an English task with a Russian hint, or who can change the language herself, has a mix nobody chose.
