# REQ-7968

A node's stage MUST fall only after three observations in a row with the estimate at least 0.1 below that stage's line.

On the game's parameters this cut changes of stage for a steady player from 17 to 0.5 in 180 days and still found a true drop in 12 days at a task every two days.
