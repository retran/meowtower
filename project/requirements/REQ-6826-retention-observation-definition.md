# REQ-6826

Apart from the hinted retention check that counts as wrong, a retention observation MUST be a graded, unassisted first attempt on a node, not dropped by the knowledge model, on a task that shows no new form and was shown for a block, a probe, review, a lesson recheck or a retention check, at least 21 game days after the node's latest meeting before that task's own show.

The owner's item sets the 21-day gap; the plan waits 28 to 35 days so that a check still clears 21 days when a meeting it didn't foresee slips in. Mental arithmetic, control facts, Volley rows and new forms never supply an observation, because the breakdown and the trajectory leave them out too, so the status reads the same attempts as the line it sits on.
