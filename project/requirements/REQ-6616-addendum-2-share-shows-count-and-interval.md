# REQ-6616

Every share in the report parts addendum 2 adds MUST show, unless it shows «мало данных» (too little data) under its floor, its right answers and attempts and its 80 % Wilson interval over the raw, unweighted counts of the window it names beside it.

A named window and raw counts let the parent recompute the figure from the attempts the node card lists, and the Wilson interval gives the same numbers on every recompute. The parts are the profile, the trajectories, retention, transfer, the home-and-school quadrants, the probe and the hypotheses. ADR-0060's entropy uncertainty stays for the Director.
