# REQ-0724

«Не знаю» (I don't know) MUST sit in the task window's action row, away from the digit keys, and never on the keypad.

A slip of the thumb onto «Не знаю» ends a first attempt that can't be taken back, scores 0 and counts towards the avoidance signal.
