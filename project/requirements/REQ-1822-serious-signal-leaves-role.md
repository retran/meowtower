# REQ-1822

When the player's text carries a serious real-life signal, such as danger, someone hurting her, a request to keep a secret from her parents, thoughts of self-harm or an unknown adult online, the Master MUST leave its role with the fixed line «Это звучит серьёзно. Об этом лучше рассказать маме или папе — они помогут» (This sounds serious. It's better to tell Mum or Dad about it; they'll help).
