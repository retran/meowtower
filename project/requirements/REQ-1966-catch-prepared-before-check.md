# REQ-1966

Before a catch scene's check, the game MUST show the bestiary's clue for the creature and let the player choose what she brings: an item, a pet of her team or an approach the clue suggests.

Her preparation is part of the hunt, and a choice that matches the clue earns a story perk the content file names.
