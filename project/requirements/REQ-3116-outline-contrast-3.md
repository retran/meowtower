# REQ-3116

Every outline that marks the edge of a control or panel MUST reach a contrast of at least 3:1 against each surface it sits on, in every theme, preset palette and custom palette.

Research measured the design's outline below 3:1 on the sunken surface in three light palettes, so a control drawn there loses its edge.
