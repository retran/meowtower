# REQ-6886

The report MUST state beside the retention list that 2 right of 2 can't reliably tell a child who is right 90 % of the time from one who is right 70 % of the time.

The rule confirms a child at 70 % about 70 % of the time, so the parent needs to read «удержание подтверждено» as weaker evidence than its name suggests. The parent judges whether the wording says that plainly.
