# REQ-8212

Withdrawn by ADR-3270: the registry holds no expression task, and a task shows whole, so its time runs from the moment it shows. The player sees no timer, and the time serves statistics only.

On a task the Director times against a threshold from an expression, the clock MUST start when the numbers appear, after the story.

So reading stays out of timed fluency, and the existing thresholds keep their meaning.
