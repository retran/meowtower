# REQ-7002

The home side of a quadrant MUST rest only on tested node states from the stream of what the player did on her own, never on an inferred state, a state from a probe alone or another stream.

A quadrant compares what she showed at home with what the school measured, and an inferred state is the model's guess, not something she showed.
