# REQ-5794

Unless no theme with an approved puzzle left meets this rule, the Director MUST NOT offer a theme again until at least 4 other themes have come since it last came.

The owner wants the themes to vary, and counting from the theme's last offer keeps it from coming back on consecutive days. When no theme meets the rule, the Director takes the theme offered longest ago, which I chose as the default because a small first bank would otherwise stop the branch.
