# REQ-5408

Every unanswerable problem MUST have the content of a complete problem with one given withheld: a model choice, step fields, hint rungs and plan cards built from that complete problem.

An unanswerable problem is a T1 to T4 word problem of a subtype `T1.insufficient` to `T4.insufficient`, which can't be answered because a needed given is missing. Without that content its phases would be empty, and an empty phase tells her the problem can't be solved.
