# REQ-7200

A riddle's target MUST be able to come from any of seven constructions, equal groups, the two meanings of division, a fraction of a number, decimals, percentages, ratios and multi-step expressions, as well as from the word-problem types of tiers T1 to T4.

Without them, fractions, percentages and ratios, where the studies RES-4260 read place the errors of meaning, have no composing measure. The addendum gives each construction an example target: `7 · 8`, `120 : 6`, `3/4 от 20` (3/4 of 20), `2,5 · 4`, `25 % от 80` and `80 − 20 % от 80`, a division of 30 in the ratio 3 : 2, and `(120 − 30) : 3` and `48 : 6 + 5`.
