# REQ-5012

Whenever the task window is closed, during an adventure included, the game MUST let the player reach the heroine's room, the Diary, the forge, the shop, the familiars, the puzzle branch «Петельки Смотрителя» (the Keeper's Loops) and «Свободное перо» (Free Pen).

A puzzle is therefore open at a rest stop during an adventure and after the day's finale. REQ-5004 keeps play to one new adventure a day, which the window after the finale used to do.
