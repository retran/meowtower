# REQ-5704

The game MUST keep at most three puzzles open at once, where a puzzle in the box doesn't count as open and a puzzle taken back out of the box does, so with three open she puts one away before she takes one out.

Few open puzzles never pile up into a list of unfinished work, which would turn optional play into pressure. The addendum lets her put a puzzle away and says nothing about taking it back; I chose as the default that she can take it back out at any time.
