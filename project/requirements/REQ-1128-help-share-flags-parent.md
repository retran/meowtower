# REQ-1128

When «Не знаю» (I don't know) and hints before the answer together exceed 30 % of an adventure's first attempts and exceed their mean share over up to 7 adventures before it by at least 10 percentage points, the report MUST show the parent a flag on that adventure.

In the player's first adventure, the 30 % threshold alone applies. Default chosen by the requirements step. The research says only "clearly exceed past days".
