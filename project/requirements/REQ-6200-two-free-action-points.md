# REQ-6200

Each adventure MUST offer at least 2 free-action points, at least one of which isn't the lead-in to a trial, where what the player sends is kept as a story fact that a later scene reads.

A free-action point is a place where she can type, dictate or send a starter and see it change the story, because a choice that changes nothing isn't a choice. The change travels only through the story, so a free action never changes a trial, its outcome or which task comes, and the measurement stays with the Director.
