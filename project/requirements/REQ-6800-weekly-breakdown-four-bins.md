# REQ-6800

The report's dynamics screen MUST split each node's first attempts of each week into four bins, «сама» (on her own), «хватило первой ступени» (rung 1 was enough), «с опорой» (with support) and «требует обучения» (needs teaching), and show the count in each bin.

A count such as "5 сама, 1 хватило первой ступени, 2 требует обучения" replaces "5 of 8", as the owner's example shows, and a count is exact at any size. The dynamics screen comes after the MVP, and report v1's nine screens don't change.
