# REQ-6202

When the player sends a free action that isn't an unchanged starter, a later scene of the same adventure or the next one MUST show her a consequence of it.

The addendum gives no window. The requirements step keeps the one the research chose, because a consequence that comes later than the next day's story can no longer be told apart from chance. The parent judges whether the scene shows the consequence.
