# REQ-6138

When an eye exercise takes the player's eyes off the screen, the familiar MUST tell her how long to do it in a measure she keeps herself, such as slow breaths, with no number, minutes or seconds in the line.

REQ-0302 bans numbers in the line of a timed event, so the measure carries no count; RES-4110's example of ten slow breaths would break that rule. The parent judges.
