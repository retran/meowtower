# REQ-6254

When a sequence of 3 to 5 words, counted by lemma so Russian inflections match, has appeared more than 3 times in the Master's text over the last 14 days, the game MUST tell the Master not to use it in the scenes that follow.

A language model left to itself repeats its favourite phrases, and the player reads every scene.
