# REQ-7136

Every presentation of a probe family MUST fall fewer than 14 days after the family's first presentation, whether the player plays on those days or not.

Checks at least 14 days apart count as two windows of her skill, since «Устойчиво» (stable) needs two checks at least 14 days apart (REQ-0944), so a family inside that window meets one state of her skill. Counting calendar days here shows the player no clock; it only bounds the family.
