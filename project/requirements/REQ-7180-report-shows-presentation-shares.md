# REQ-7180

The report MUST show, overall and for each node, each probe presentation's share of right answers without help, with «мало данных» (too little data) in place of the share in any cell that holds fewer than 12 observations.

12 is the research's judgement: at 12 observations and one half right, the 80 % interval spans about 33 % to 67 %, and a wider one is too loose to show the parent as a number. As a default, the planned cards of the Dutch-after-words presentation, opening or reopened, are its condition and not help; a card tap in the Dutch presentation (REQ-7138) and a hint rung on any letter are help.
