# REQ-7040

Each quadrant MUST show the check it suggests: for high at home and at school, confirm by retention once the retention check has a decision; for low on both, a lesson on the row's nodes and then their trajectory; for high at home and low at school, the Dutch probe on the row's nodes once the probe has a decision, the Sources track and asking the teacher about the test conditions; for low at home and high at school, the sandbox, a comparison of the goal's or the category's wording with the row's nodes, and recalibration of the home tasks.
