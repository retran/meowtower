# REQ-5100

When the player opens the hint ladder on a task with the thread button before answering, the opening MUST cost exactly 1 guiding thread.

The decision to ask for help carries the price, so the player isn't punished for needing a deeper rung, and a price above nothing keeps threads worth something and hints from being pressed without thought. A ladder the familiar offers free on a puzzle isn't opened with the thread button, and its own record covers it; a thread spent on a puzzle's ladder still comes off the same balance (RES-4070).
