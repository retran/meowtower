# REQ-6974

A transfer figure in the report MUST read «мало данных» (too little data) when it rests on fewer than 10 eligible observations.

At 10 observations the 80 % interval is still about 35 points wide. A higher floor would hide every domain figure for most of the first year, since a domain reaches 10 within about a school term; the owner can raise it once the counts are known.
