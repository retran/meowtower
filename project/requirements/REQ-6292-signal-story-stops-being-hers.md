# REQ-6292

When the player's own words per adventure, not counting starter text, fall by 30 % or more between the last 14 days of play and the 14 before, and the share of her sends that are an unchanged starter rises over the same windows, the report MUST raise the signal "the story is stopping being hers".

A fall in her own words alone could mean shorter scenes. Together with a rising share of unchanged starters, it means she is letting the Master write for her.
