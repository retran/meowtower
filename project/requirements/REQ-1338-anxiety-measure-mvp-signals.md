# REQ-1338

Until the game records erasures, long hesitation and the phrases «страшно» (scary) and «не хочу» (I don't want to), the anxiety limit MUST use only runs of 3 `alt` outcomes in a row and a share of rapid guesses in the last third of a session higher than in its first two thirds.

The run of 3 follows the anxiety signal in RES-0300. Default chosen by the requirements step. The research doesn't define "growing towards the end of a session", and thirds match the parts of a session the full report splits by.
