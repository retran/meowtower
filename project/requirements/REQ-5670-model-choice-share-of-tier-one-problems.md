# REQ-5670

Between 20% and 30% of the T1 word problems the player gets in any 30 days, or within one problem of that range, MUST open with a model choice.

RES-4060 keeps the model choice at one word problem in four, and a T1 problem never opens with a plan (REQ-5600), so T1 problems keep that share whole. The band, the window and the tolerance are the default chosen by the requirements step, as for compound problems.
