# REQ-6404

When a stretch node's probe leaves its state open, the Director MUST complete the node's full block within the 3 adventure days that follow the probe's day.

At 2 stretch tasks a day (REQ-1004) a block of 5 takes 3 adventure days, and a window of 7 calendar days fails whenever she plays on fewer than 3 of them. Adventure days keep the block within reach and read no clock.
