# REQ-7120

A Dutch probe text MUST enter the parent's review only after an offline language-check role of its own has checked its grammar and naturalness and passed it.

A stiff or wrong Dutch sentence makes the Dutch presentation harder for reasons neither the school's Dutch nor the maths causes, and that error moves the gap only towards a language cause.
