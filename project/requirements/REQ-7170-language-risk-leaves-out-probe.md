# REQ-7170

The language-risk limit MUST count the mistakes on tasks other than Dutch probe letters that hold a risk term whose explanation the player didn't open.

Without the count, a mistake caused by a word in Russian reads as a gap in the maths. A Dutch letter has risk terms in every sentence, so counting letters would fill the limit with the probe's own errors.
