# REQ-6414

Withdrawn by ADR-3440 and replaced by REQ-7284: the owner decided on 2026-11-15 that rewards, pets and the result of an action succeed or fail by how the player solves the tasks, and that she is old enough to take a failure.

Every first attempt MUST give 1 button, whatever its outcome, with a Volley counted as the 2 first attempts it replaces.

A Volley of 8 to 10 facts takes the place of the 2 mental arithmetic tasks, and a button for each of its facts would pay a fact run four or five times what those two tasks paid.

Imposed by the owner's instruction of 2026-09-28 to decide the conflicts the specification step found.
