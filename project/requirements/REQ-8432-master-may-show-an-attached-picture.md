# REQ-8432

The Master MUST have a tool that shows the player one picture already attached to a library entry or to the current encounter, and the game MUST refuse a picture that is attached to neither.

The Master decides when a picture helps, as a Dungeon Master decides when to hand a player a drawing. A picture from nowhere would skip the parent's approval of REQ-8426.
