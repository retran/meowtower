# REQ-5266

The riddle's field label, its three starter buttons, the paraphrase templates and the two confirmation buttons MUST each come from the per-language content files.

The starters are «У…было…» (…had…), «Купили…» (They bought…) and «Шли…» (They walked…); the buttons are «Да, так» (Yes, that's it) and «Нет, я имела в виду другое» (No, I meant something else).

A string hard-coded in a component has to be found and moved before English or Dutch can ship.
