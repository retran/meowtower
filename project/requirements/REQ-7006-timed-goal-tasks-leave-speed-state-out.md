# REQ-7006

When the owner records that the school learning system times its goal tasks, a node at «понимает, нужна скорость» (understands, needs speed) MUST count as neither high nor low on a goal row.

A timed school task and an untimed home cut would again read a speed gap as a knowledge gap. The goal row then follows the addendum's own cut, «бегло» (fluent) or above against «понимает» (understands) or below, and Cito rows keep the cut of REQ-7004 because the Cito tests stay untimed.

Written from RES-4240 on the owner's instruction of 2026-09-28 to process addendum 2.
