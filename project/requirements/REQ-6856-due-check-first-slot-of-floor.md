# REQ-6856

From game day 28 after the meeting its window counts from, late checks included, the Director MUST place a due retention check in the first slot of the node's domain floor, and when more checks are due on one floor, each later due check in the slot after the one before it.

A node's tasks come only on its domain's floor, and the first slot comes before any fatigue signal there. On a day with no floor of that domain the check waits, and the three-day window over domains brings the floor within 3 adventure days.
