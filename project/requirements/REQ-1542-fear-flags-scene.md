# REQ-1542

When the player writes that she is scared, or skips a creepy scene without finishing it twice in a row on one day, the game MUST flag the scene for the parent.

The research leaves open how many skips count as "several in a row"; two in a row on one day is the default. Default chosen by the requirements step.
