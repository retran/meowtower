# REQ-6946

A first encounter MUST be eligible only when it is a graded first attempt on a task that carries no new form and fills none of the slots REQ-6954 lists, isn't a rapid guess, doesn't carry the fatigue weight, and follows no lesson mark on its node in the 21 game days before it.

Each excluded case measures something other than transfer: a warm-up isn't graded, a new form such as the bridge's Dutch keywords belongs to its own stream, a retention check, a Dutch probe presentation or a live frame measures something else, a guess or a tired answer is noise, and a lesson turns the encounter into a taught one. The 21 game days match addendum 2's rule for retention.
