# REQ-6064

Report v1 MUST have nine screens: the summary, VWO readiness, the graph map, the node card, misconceptions, limits, science, the story book and «Работа с источниками» (Working with sources).

The dynamics screen, the "home and school" screen and the timeline wait until after the MVP: the dynamics screen needs Ascents and weeks of history, and the two school screens need snapshots, which the game takes in only after the MVP. The Sources track is in the MVP and its report stays apart from the graph (RES-4090), so it adds the ninth screen. The fact report and each Cito block's readiness (RES-4080), the section «Нестандартное мышление» (Non-standard thinking) on the VWO readiness screen (RES-4070) and the Interest section (RES-4120) sit inside these nine screens, and their own requirements say what they show. Default chosen by the requirements step: the design step places each section, and the full report after the MVP may add screens beyond the nine.
