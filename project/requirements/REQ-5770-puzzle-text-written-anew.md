# REQ-5770

A puzzle's statement, story, figure and solution MUST be written anew, and never copied or scanned from the source of its idea.

The sources are under copyright, and the free MCCME (Moscow Centre for Continuous Mathematical Education) PDFs allow only reading and printing, while Russian, Dutch and US law leave a puzzle's idea free to use. The parent judges it when approving the puzzle.
