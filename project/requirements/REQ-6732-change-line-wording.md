# REQ-6732

When a bar shows a change line, the line MUST read «возможно, выросло» (possibly rose) or «возможно, снизилось» (possibly fell) when it claims a change, and «без заметного изменения» (no clear change) otherwise.

The addendum asks that a reading be worded as something to check, and «возможно» marks the change as one the parent can check, not a verdict.
