# REQ-6048

The server MUST NOT send the wording of a school goal to any service outside the home.

Goal wording isn't one of the kinds of data REQ-5042 lets leave the Mac, and REQ-5042 already keeps the school's goal list on the Mac. Default chosen by the requirements step: a Parent Room opened on a family device on the home network may show goal wording, as it shows reports that REQ-5042 also keeps on the Mac.
