# REQ-1318

The holding-steps value MUST be the largest k at which the player's last 2 counted attempts on k-step word problems, both within the last 30 days, are right, and empty until some k has 2 counted attempts.

A counted attempt is an unassisted first attempt that is not a rapid guess and that the parent hasn't excluded, and a partial answer counts as not right.
