# REQ-6234

The adventure of the day MUST run in this order: «В прошлый раз…» (Last time…) with the daily quests once their system has opened, then a story choice between two routes, then 3 maths floors, or 4 when the forecast leaves time, then a finale that ends on a cliffhanger.

The route choice comes before the first floor, because a choice over goals ties to motivation. The choice of routes adds to the daily quests and never replaces them.
