# REQ-6232

The first scene after at least one game day on which the player didn't play MUST NOT mention the gap or her absence, say that anyone missed her or waited for her, or ask where she was.

A character who misses her is the parasocial pressure Radesky et al. found in apps for young children. The requirements step chose a gap of at least one game day with no play, because RES-4120 names none.
