# REQ-5710

Wherever a Russian text the player or the parent sees names the branch or one puzzle, it MUST call them «Петельки Смотрителя» (the Keeper's loops) and «головоломка» (puzzle).

«Узелок» already names a familiar in every text she sees, and «петелька» alone already means a knot in task text, so a single puzzle needs a word of its own. The parent's schedule and sandbox use the same two words; identifiers she never sees, such as the puzzle event types, keep their names. The English and Dutch names wait for those languages.
