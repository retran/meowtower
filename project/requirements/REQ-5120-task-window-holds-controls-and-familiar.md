# REQ-5120

The task window MUST hold only the task, the answer field or options, the keypad, «Не знаю» (I don't know), the guiding thread button and «Готово» (Done), with no sprite, effect or story text, except the texts and controls a task's own form adds: a shown hint rung with the familiar's portrait and framing line beside it, the short solution and a fluent fact's strategy hint after the answer, the estimate's four options and «Проверить нить» (Check the thread) with its check field and match signal, «Нельзя узнать» (Can't be known) with its four options on a T1 to T4 word problem, a riddle's text field with its starters, paraphrase line and two confirmation buttons, a grouping task's tappable numbers and the loops they draw, and a drawn source with its tappable regions.

When the guiding thread button shows is set by REQ-6250. The notes of RES-4020, RES-4030, RES-4040, RES-4050, RES-4090 and RES-4120 are folded in here, because one list of controls has to hold for every form.
