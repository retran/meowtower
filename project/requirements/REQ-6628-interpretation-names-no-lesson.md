# REQ-6628

An interpretation the report draws on its own MUST NOT propose a lesson, a date for one or an exercise.

REQ-2304 forbids a lesson plan, and a proposed lesson, a date to hold one or an exercise to set is part of one. A line can still name the window its figures come from.
