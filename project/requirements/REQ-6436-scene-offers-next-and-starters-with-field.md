# REQ-6436

Every scene MUST offer a «Дальше» (Next) button that moves the story on without her typing, and three starters wherever the free-text field is open.

A starter inserts its words into the free-text field and never sends (REQ-6210, REQ-6218), and the field opens only at the points REQ-5268 lists, so a scene without the field has nowhere to put a starter. The count of three comes from the approved scene design.

Imposed by the owner's instruction of 2026-09-28 to decide the conflicts the specification step found.
