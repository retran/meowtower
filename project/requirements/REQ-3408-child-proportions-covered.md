# REQ-3408

Every character MUST be drawn with child proportions and covered clothes: shoulders covered, hems at the knee or longer, no maid costume and no suggestive pose.

The style's reference comes from an adult franchise, and the game takes its mood and none of its presentation. The parent judges.
