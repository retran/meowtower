# REQ-5312

The estimate MUST offer four rounded values of different orders of magnitude as separate buttons, and never intervals on a number line.

On a number line the intervals follow the order of their values, so an outer correct interval holds its own tenth or tenfold value, and the correct option can't be spread evenly over the positions while the errors of order fall outside it. Decided on 2026-09-28 in the research.
