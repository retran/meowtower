# REQ-7130

The Dutch-after-words presentation of a probe family MUST come on a later game day than the family's Dutch presentation.

A word card read before the Dutch presentation teaches its words, and no counterbalancing can undo that, so the Dutch presentation would no longer be Dutch text without help.
