# REQ-5130

When a first attempt ends with a wrong answer or «Не знаю» (I don't know), or the player saw a rung 2 or rung 3 hint on it whatever its outcome, the game MUST give one second attempt on a parallel task as REQ-5166 defines it.

After a miss, the twin measures whether the review taught the step. A right answer after a rung 2 or rung 3 hint can mean the hint led the player to the answer without teaching her, and the twin checks which. A «Не знаю» after rung 3 meets both triggers and still gets one twin.
