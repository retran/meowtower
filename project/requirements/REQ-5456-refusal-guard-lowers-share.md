# REQ-5456

When the player has answered «Нельзя узнать» (can't be known) on 3 or more of her last 20 solvable T1 to T4 word problems, the Director MUST hold the share of unanswerable problems at half its usual value, halving it once and never again, until a later window of 20 solvable T1 to T4 word problems holds 2 or fewer such answers.

A lower share makes «Нельзя узнать» a worse guess, so she meets fewer problems where refusing pays while the pattern lasts. Halving only once keeps some unanswerable problems in play, so the skill is still observed.
