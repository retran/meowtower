# REQ-5148

When a node meets the rules for both «на пороге» (on the threshold) and «почти готово» (nearly ready), the report MUST show both marks, with «на пороге» first.

«На пороге» is the narrower claim and tells the parent what to do, and keeping «почти готово» leaves its rule unchanged.
