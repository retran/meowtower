# REQ-5122

When the task window shows a hint rung before the answer, including a rung restored on resume, it MUST show beside it the portrait of the familiar accompanying the player and one approved framing line for that familiar's kind and that rung, because the addendum makes the familiar the one who gives the help.

Any one of the approved variants meets this. A strategy hint shown after the answer isn't a rung before the answer and falls outside this rule. When no approved framing line exists for that kind and rung, the default I chose, which isn't in the research and which the owner approves with this record, is to show the rung with the portrait and no line, because the rung itself is what helps.
