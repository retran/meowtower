# REQ-6948

A first encounter of kind subtype MUST be eligible only when its node has at least one prerequisite and each prerequisite of its node is «бегло» (fluent) or higher by a tested result, a probe or a full block and never an inferred state.

Far transfer tests whether the player generalises from prerequisites she has mastered, so a node with none, such as a Sources track node, gives no far observation. During cold start her prerequisites are still untested, so its first encounters measure placement. Default chosen by the requirements step: the research doesn't say what a node without prerequisites gives, and the requirement reads it as giving no eligible far observation.
