# REQ-6734

When the template versions behind a bar differ between its two windows, the bar's comparison of its two windows MUST carry «контент изменён» (content changed).

REQ-1424 labels a node's comparison, and a bar compares a family of templates across two windows. The label sits on the comparison, so it shows also on the gap bar and on a bar whose change line REQ-6722 hides.
