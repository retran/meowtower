# REQ-6288

The report MUST hold an "Interest" section, built from the event log and the parent's daily mark, that shows whether the player came back by herself, her extensions, the length of her own text and its share of what she sends, the optional activities and the systems she used, and a figure for how often the Master repeats itself.

Whether she came back by herself comes from the parent's mark of REQ-6290, since no event can tell. The section gives the parent, and the acceptance of backlog items, a measure of her interest.
