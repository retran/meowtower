# REQ-5536

When the measured star yarn is above 60 in each of two weeks in a row, the owner MUST open a new record that resizes the forge recipes.

60 yarn is about a fifth above RES-2100's typical week of about 50. One high week can come from an unusual stretch of play, while two in a row above that line show that the short loop has moved the economy. The owner judges.
