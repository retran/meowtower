# REQ-6134

When an eye exercise takes the player's eyes off the screen, that is looking far out of the window or covering the eyes with the palms, the exercise MUST end only when she taps «Готово» (Done).

No signal the game sends reaches her while she isn't looking, so she ends the moment herself.
