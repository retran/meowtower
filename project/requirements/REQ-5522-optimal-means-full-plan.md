# REQ-5522

The engine MUST score a grouping `optimal` only when the submitted links equal one full optimal plan of the template, and every other non-empty admissible set of links `valid`.

In `38 + 47 + 62 + 53`, `38 + 62` linked alone is `valid`, because she still adds the other two numbers the long way. A mark on a number to round counts as a submitted link.
