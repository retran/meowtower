# REQ-5334

The time the player spends in the estimate step and in the check field MUST be left out of the answer time that the fluency test and the rapid-guess test read.

Counting it would read a player who checks her work as slow and label her «Понимает, нужна скорость» (understands, needs speed), which punishes the habit the check is meant to build.
