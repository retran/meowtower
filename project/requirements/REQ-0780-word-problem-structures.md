# REQ-0780

Every word problem MUST take its structure from the catalogue of structures: a chain, a fork followed by a comparison, «части и целое» (parts and whole), «на N больше / в N раз» (N more / N times as many), «цена · количество → сдача» (price times quantity to change), motion or work.
