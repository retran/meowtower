# REQ-5812

The player's screens MUST NOT show a Cito test's date, the name Cito or a word naming a test, such as «тест» (test) or «контрольная» (test paper).

The dates change only the order of practice, and a date or a test on her screen would turn practice into the drill for a test that Cito advises against. The two example words are a default chosen while writing this requirement, and the design step may add to them.
