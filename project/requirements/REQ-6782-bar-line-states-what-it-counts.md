# REQ-6782

Each bar MUST carry one line that states what its figure counts, such as «верно 42 из 50 голых примеров на освоенных узлах» (42 of 50 bare tasks on mastered nodes right).

A bar labelled with what it counts, and not with an ability, keeps the difficulty confound in view: the Director sets each family's difficulty, so two bars aren't on one scale.
