# REQ-5828

Over a 60-day simulation with the block-priority and school-goal terms in force, the Director MUST keep the flow corridor, the three-day window, the stretch cap of 2 tasks a day and the rule that no value term rises as the expected chance of success falls.

The new terms add value to nodes by block and by goal, and they must not push the Director into a queue that breaks the player's flow, starves the three-day review, exceeds the stretch cap or favours tasks she is likely to fail. The addendum's acceptance test 10 runs this simulation.
