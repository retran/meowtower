# REQ-0344

For 10 minutes after a rest stop ends, the «Привал» (Rest stop) button MUST stay inactive.

The research says "10 minutes after a rest stop" without saying whether they count from its start or its end, so they count from its end. The button shows no countdown for the wait, because the player's screens show no timer.

Default chosen by the requirements step.
