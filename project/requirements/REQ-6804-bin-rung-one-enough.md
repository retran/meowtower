# REQ-6804

The weekly breakdown MUST put a first attempt in «хватило первой ступени» (rung 1 was enough) when it ends `clean` with rung 1 as its deepest rung on a hint ladder of two or more rungs.

Rung 1 on a longer ladder is a nudge, and the owner wants a nudge told apart from real support. The owner named this bin «на пороге» (on the threshold), but an approved node mark already owns that name, and one word for two things misleads the parent.
