# REQ-6636

The language line MUST appear only when the 95 % Newcombe hybrid score interval of each of its two differences excludes zero with the Dutch share the lower one: Dutch below Russian, and Dutch below Dutch after the words.

A language gap shows in both: Dutch falls below Russian and recovers once the words are explained. The 95 % trigger cuts the chance of a line per contrast from about 20 % to about 5 %, while the 80 % interval stays the one the parent reads. Default chosen by the requirements step: both differences are required, because RES-4200's simulated rates, about 1.5 % for no gap and 88 % for both gaps, fit a rule that needs both, and a drop on one side alone has a cause other than language.
