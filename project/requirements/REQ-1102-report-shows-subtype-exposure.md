# REQ-1102

The report MUST show, for each subtype, how many times the player has been shown a task of that subtype, beside her accuracy before the review and after the review.

Daily play shows every solution, so part of the growth on a familiar subtype is familiarity with the format, and the count lets the parent weigh it.
