# REQ-5606

Between 20% and 30% of the compound word problems (tiers T2 to T4) the player gets in any 30 days, or within one problem of that range, MUST open with a plan.

The addendum asks for about 25% and names no window. Default chosen by the requirements step: the band and the 30-day window match the step-input share of REQ-0703, so both shares are checked the same way, and the tolerance of one problem lets a window with few problems meet the band.
