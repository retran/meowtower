# REQ-2338

The VWO readiness ladder MUST give the step «1S покрыт без запаса» (1S covered with no margin) when at least 90 % of 1F nodes and at least 90 % of 1S nodes are verified at «понимает» (understands) or above and no 1F node is in «не освоен» (not mastered).
