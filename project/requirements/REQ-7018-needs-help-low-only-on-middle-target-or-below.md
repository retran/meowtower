# REQ-7018

The school side of a goal row MUST count the status `needs_help` as low when the goal's target level is 3 or below, and place the goal in no quadrant when that level is above 3.

A missed high target can leave the player well above the national middle, and level 3 is the national middle band, the 40th to 59th percentile.
