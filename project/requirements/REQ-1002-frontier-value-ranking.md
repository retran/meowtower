# REQ-1002

For a frontier slot, the Director MUST choose the candidate node of highest value, where value rises with the node's uncertainty, the time since it was last seen, its place on the frontier, a due lesson recheck, an open escalation, a due review and a fresh lesson mark, and falls with its shows in the last 3 days.
