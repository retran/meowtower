# REQ-8440

Every request to the scenario writer, the arc writer and the Master MUST carry, as facts with no numbers, the items she wears and their perks, her team and its stages, the keys and materials she holds, the bestiary's gaps by element and her nearest goal.

A story that knows what she holds can aim at it, such as a training arc for a pet close to its next stage.
