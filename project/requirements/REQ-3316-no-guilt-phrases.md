# REQ-3316

Text the heroine sees MUST NOT contain a phrase of guilt or attachment, such as «я скучал, почему ты не приходила» (I missed you, why didn't you come) or «Ты нас подвела» (You let us down).

Such phrases have no fixed wording, so a word list can't catch them and the safety check measures them on a test set.
