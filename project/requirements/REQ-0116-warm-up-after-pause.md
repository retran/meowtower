# REQ-0116

When a pause has lasted longer than 5 minutes, the next new task MUST be an unscored warm-up, while a task left open stays first.

A warm-up after every short pause, such as an eye exercise, would cost a task slot each time and break the room's flow; 5 minutes is also when an open task goes idle and when the eye counter resets.

Decided by research on the owner's instruction of 2026-09-27.
