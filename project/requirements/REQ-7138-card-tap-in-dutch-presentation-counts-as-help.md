# REQ-7138

When the player opens a word card during a family's Dutch presentation, the attempt MUST stay in the Dutch presentation's count as an attempt that isn't unassisted.

This departs from the owner's addendum, which moves such an attempt to the after-words presentation, because moving it leaves the Dutch presentation with only the texts she read well and mixes her own taps with the planned cards in the after-words presentation. A tap is help she asked for, like a hint rung.
