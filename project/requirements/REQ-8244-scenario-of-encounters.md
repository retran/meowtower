# REQ-8244

Each adventure's scenario MUST be prepared before she plays it, as a graph of 10 to 20 encounters.

The owner decided on 2026-11-01 that a scenario is written as encounters. An encounter states a place, a situation, a goal, its people and their aims, what presses on her and how many checks it holds.
