# REQ-7400

The language line MUST appear only when the 95 % Newcombe hybrid score interval of at least one of its two differences excludes zero with the Dutch share the lower one: Dutch below Russian, or Dutch below Dutch after the words.

At 20 observations a presentation, a rule that needs both differences finds a 45-point language gap on 75.5 % of seeds, where the maths line finds a maths gap on 96.1 %, so the report would show a weak side in language less clearly than one in maths. This rule finds it on 94.6 % and gives the rates check 5's bars were set on. The 95 % trigger cuts the chance of a line per contrast from about 20 % to about 5 %, while the 80 % interval stays the one the parent reads (RES-4200). The rule costs a false language line on 4.05 % of seeds for a player whose gap is maths alone, against 0.37 % under the rule that needs both (RES-4280).
