# REQ-5614

A plan MUST show exactly 1 decoy card on a T2 or T4 problem, and 1 or 2 on a T3 problem.

T2 has two steps, T3 three and T4 four (RES-0800), so with one card per step this gives every tier the addendum's 3 to 5 cards, counting the decoys. Read as needed cards alone, 3 to 5 would exclude every two-step problem.
