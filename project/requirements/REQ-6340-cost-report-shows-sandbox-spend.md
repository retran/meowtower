# REQ-6340

The cost report MUST show the sandbox's spend on a line of its own, against the sandbox's monthly cap.

The parent then sees what the sandbox costs apart from play and can tell which limit is close. The sandbox's spend includes the command-line sandbox's calls, since both spend under the same cap.
