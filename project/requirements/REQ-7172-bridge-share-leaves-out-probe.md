# REQ-7172

While the parent has the Dutch bridge on, its keywords MUST appear in 15 % to 25 % of the T1 to T4 and Sources tasks, other than Dutch probe letters, shown to the player on nodes at «Понимает» (understands) or above, over any 14 game days on which she plays.

The band and the 14 days keep the requirements step's default. A Dutch letter is Dutch throughout, so counting it would push the measured share out of its band.
