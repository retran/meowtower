# REQ-5708

Withdrawn by ADR-3220 and replaced by REQ-8018: the game accumulates no experience.

When the player leaves a puzzle unsolved or puts it away, the game MUST NOT take away or withhold any experience, star yarn, star-steel shards, buttons, guiding threads, quest progress, streak or title she has or would otherwise get.

The owner wants the branch played without pressure, and a loss for an unsolved puzzle is pressure.

Written from RES-4070 on the owner's instruction of 2026-09-28.

Imposed by the owner's addendum 1 of 2026-09-28.
