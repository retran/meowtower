# REQ-7000

After the MVP, the "home and school" screen MUST place each goal with a confirmed snapshot link, and each Cito category the parent entered, in one of four quadrants, high or low at home crossed with high or low at school, or in no quadrant.

A row goes in no quadrant when either side supports no cut, because a wrong cell costs the parent more than an empty one.

Written from RES-4240 on the owner's instruction of 2026-09-28 to process addendum 2.
Imposed by the owner's addendum 2 of 2026-09-28.
