# REQ-5890

The report MUST show, each week and for each Cito block, the share of its nodes and subtypes that are fluent or stable and, for blocks 1 and 2, the share of their facts that are automatic, beside whether the block counts as ready for the nearest horizon.

The shares are the two numbers the ready rule tests, so the parent sees how far each block is from ready and not only whether it is.
