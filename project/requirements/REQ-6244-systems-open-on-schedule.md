# REQ-6244

Unless the parent has opened every system at once, the game MUST open new systems by days of play on this schedule: levels and the chest on day 1; guiding threads, the hint ladder and the volley on day 2; daily quests and the first knot on day 3; the Diary and the bestiary on day 4; a second familiar on day 5; the forge on day 6; the shop, the free pen and the Tower's dictionary on day 8; evolution and the Underside when their events come.

Opening one new thing a day gives her something new to find on each of the first days and keeps any one day from burying her in systems. Task forms aren't systems, so the schedule holds none back, and counting days of play means a missed calendar day delays nothing. The addendum gives the forge "days 6 and 7" and the shop, free pen and dictionary "week 2"; the requirements step chose the first day of each, day 6 and day 8.
