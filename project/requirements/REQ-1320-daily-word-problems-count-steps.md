# REQ-1320

Every unassisted first attempt on a k-step word problem in daily play, from a Guardian or from a room, MUST count towards step k of the holding-steps limit unless it is a rapid guess or the parent excluded it.

The step ladder then fills during the MVP without a ladder block of its own.
