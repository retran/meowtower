# REQ-7064

The screen MUST place a Cito row in no quadrant while its category was typed or its signal is other, until the parent maps the category to nodes or the signal to one of the listed signals.

Default chosen by the requirements step: the parent maps the signal as well as the category, because a signal in the printout's own words has no direction the screen can read.
