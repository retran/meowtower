# REQ-5634

When a plan has several faults, the engine MUST give it the first label that applies in the order `used_distractor`, `missing_step`, `extra_step`, `wrong_order`.

A wrong structure says the most about how she models the problem, a missing step the next most, a harmless extra step less, and order alone least.
