# REQ-0350

When the game detects an anxiety signal, the next task MUST be an easy one, followed by a campfire scene or a funny scene.

An anxiety signal is a run of 3 `alt` outcomes, a share of fast guesses rising towards the end of the session, frequent erasing with long hesitation, or «мне страшно» (I'm scared) or «не хочу» (I don't want to) in free text.
