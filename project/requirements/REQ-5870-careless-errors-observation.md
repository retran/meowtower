# REQ-5870

When the share of wrong first attempts on fluent, stable or automatic material, over all error classes together, is above 10 % in a week, the report MUST raise the observation «небрежные ошибки на знакомом» (careless errors on familiar material).

The owner set 10 %. Material she has mastered should rarely go wrong, and a slip on an easy item costs more on Cito's scale than a miss on a hard one. Counting all classes together is how the addendum states the rule, because careless slips spread across classes.
