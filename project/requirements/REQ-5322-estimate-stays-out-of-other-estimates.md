# REQ-5322

During the MVP, an estimate MUST NOT feed the "on her own" estimate, the fluency estimate or the estimate of node N4 (rounding and estimating a result).

N4 measures estimating on tasks of its own, and whether the estimates on other nodes' tasks should feed it waits until a refit of the model shows they improve prediction.
