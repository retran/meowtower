# REQ-6958

A transfer observation MUST be the first attempt alone, sorted into the categories of the weekly breakdown by depth of help.

A second attempt follows the feedback of the first, so only the first shows whether she transferred. The categories are «сама» (on her own), «хватило первой ступени» (rung 1 was enough), «с опорой» (with support, rungs 2 to 3) and «требует обучения» (needs teaching). Default chosen by the requirements step: RES-4230 names the rung 1 category «на пороге», and the category takes the name «хватило первой ступени» that RES-4220 and RES-4200 decided, since the node mark already owns «на пороге».
