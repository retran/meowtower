# REQ-8230

The Master MUST tell her own real-life state from play inside the story, such as a heroine's fear or a villain's threat, and MUST name a real-life signal in her text in its own reply, as `none`, `everyday`, `scared` or `serious`, and a level above `none` MUST notify the parent without pausing the game.

The owner decided on 2026-11-01 that the Master does this in its ordinary work, with no separate model call and no local triggers.
