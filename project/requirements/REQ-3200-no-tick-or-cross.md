# REQ-3200

Withdrawn by ADR-3440 and replaced by REQ-7294: the owner decided on 2026-11-15 that rewards, pets and the result of an action succeed or fail by how the player solves the tasks, and that she is old enough to take a failure.

The player's screens MUST NOT show a tick or a cross to mark an answer or a result.

A tick and a cross are the school's verdict marks, and the game names outcomes as events in the world.
