# REQ-7060

The Cito result form MUST let the parent choose a category entry's category from the four Leerling in beeld domains, «Getallen», «Verhoudingen», «Meten en meetkunde» and «Verbanden», and the four LOVS standard categories, «getallen», «optellen & aftrekken», «vermenigvuldigen & delen» and «meten, tijd en geld», each labelled in the list with its system, or type it as the printout writes it.

Whether Leerling in beeld keeps the LOVS form of the analysis is unconfirmed, and a closed list would refuse the one copy of the value. The domain «Getallen» and the LOVS category «getallen» follow different mappings (REQ-7066, REQ-7068), so the list names the system beside each.
