# REQ-6216

At least one of each adventure's free-action points MUST count as the player's own action only text she typed, dictated or edited, and never a starter she sent unchanged.

An unchanged starter still counts at the other points, because she chose it, but the Master's words alone must not meet the floor of REQ-6200. How much she actually writes is left to the signal of REQ-6292.
