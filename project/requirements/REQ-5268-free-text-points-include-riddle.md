# REQ-5268

The free-text field MUST open only on entering a floor, before a Guardian, at the campfire on a rest stop, at the session finale, on meeting a new creature, at a riddle, and in «Свободное перо» (Free Pen) once it has opened, unless the parent switched it off.

The riddle's field is the story's free-text field, so it shows the note REQ-1632 requires, «Эту историю могут читать мама и папа» (Mum and Dad can read this story), because the parent reads her riddles. Default chosen by the requirements step: one field, not a second one with a copied note.
