# REQ-7044

Every row on the "home and school" screen MUST carry the line that the home side and the school side measure different things.

The parent judges. The home state measures her own counted answers against fixed rules, the status measures her against a personal target, and a Cito category measures a domain against her own overall score, so a disagreement doesn't read as one side being wrong.
