# REQ-6434

Once the free pen has opened, and unless the parent has switched it off, the game MUST let the player open the free pen at any moment the task window isn't open, during an adventure included, while the Master can still reply to her.

With the gateway off or the day's adventure budget spent, the pen has no reply to give, and REQ-6228 closes it through a story scene when the budget runs out. Outside those cases the pen stays there for her to write when she wants to.
