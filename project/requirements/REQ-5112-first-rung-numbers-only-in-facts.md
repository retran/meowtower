# REQ-5112

The first hint rung MUST contain no numbers unless it is the strategy rung of a basic fact.

A strategy through a known fact can't be said without its numbers. Those numbers come from the engine, and the rule that rungs 1 and 2 never hold the answer still applies to the strategy rung.
