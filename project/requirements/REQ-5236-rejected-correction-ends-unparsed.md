# REQ-5236

When the player rejects the paraphrase of her corrected text, or her corrected text fails to parse, the riddle MUST end as `unparsed`, with base experience and no observation.

She gets one correction after «Нет, я имела в виду другое» (No, I meant something else), and a parse error must never become her error.
