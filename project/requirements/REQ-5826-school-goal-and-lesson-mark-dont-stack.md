# REQ-5826

When a confirmed school goal and a fresh lesson mark reach the same node, the node's value MUST include the larger of the school-goal term and the lesson-mark term, never both.

Stacking would give the node 2.5 of extra value and crowd out the three-day window's reserve, and the Director already takes the larger of the recheck and lesson-mark terms. Decided by research on the owner's instruction of 2026-09-28.
