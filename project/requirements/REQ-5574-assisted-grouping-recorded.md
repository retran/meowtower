# REQ-5574

When the player buys a hint rung on a grouping task or answers its second attempt, the event log MUST record the grouping as assisted.

A rung that names the convenient pair hands her the grouping, so a grouping after it shows nothing about what she noticed. ADR-0060 sends a second attempt only to the "with help" estimate, because it follows the short solution.
