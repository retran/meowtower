# REQ-5402

«Нельзя узнать» (can't be known) MUST sit in the task window's action row, with another control or a gap of at least one button's width between it and «Не знаю» (I don't know), and never on the keypad or next to a digit key.

Both buttons end a first attempt, so a slip from one to the other records an answer she didn't give.
