# REQ-7042

Each of the two disagreeing quadrants MUST name its possible causes on both the home side and the school side: for high at home and low at school, a home state resting on one block of 5 answers on the home side, and the format, the language, anxiety and the test conditions on the school side; for low at home and high at school, home tasks that are harder or badly calibrated and a home state resting on one block of 5 answers on the home side, and a school that tests the goal in a narrow format on the school side.

The parent judges. A cause named on one side only reads as a verdict on the player or on one measure.
