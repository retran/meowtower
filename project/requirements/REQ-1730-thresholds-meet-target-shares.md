# REQ-1730

Before release, the outcome thresholds MUST produce, in a simulation on mixed-knowledge profiles after a cold start, the `success` branch in 55-75 % of rooms, the cunning bypass in at most 25 % of floor-days, triumph in 15-35 % of floor-days and the chapter finale's triumph variant in about half of chapters.
