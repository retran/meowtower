# REQ-5584

The report MUST describe the «видит удобные приёмы» figure to the parent as what the player marks when the task invites her to link numbers.

Hickendorff (2018) found that an instruction to look for a shortcut raised shortcut use in addition and multiplication, and Hickendorff (2022) found that prompted children knew more strategies than they used on their own, so the figure measures noticing on invitation, not unprompted use. The parent judges the wording.
