# REQ-1130

When «Не знаю» (I don't know) and hints before the answer together exceed 30 % of an adventure's first attempts and exceed their mean share over up to 7 adventures before it by at least 10 percentage points, the Director MUST raise the share of review tasks in the next adventure.

In the player's first adventure, the 30 % threshold alone applies. Default chosen by the requirements step. The research names neither the comparison with past days nor when the raised share applies.
