# REQ-1900

Withdrawn by ADR-3440 and replaced by REQ-1958: the owner decided on 2026-11-15 that rewards, pets and the result of an action succeed or fail by how the player solves the tasks, and that she is old enough to take a failure.

When the story reaches a planned friendship with a roster familiar, the friendship MUST succeed whatever the player answered in the tasks.
