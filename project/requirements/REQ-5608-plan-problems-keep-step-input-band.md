# REQ-5608

Between 40% and 60% of the compound word problems that open with a plan in any 30 days, or within one problem of that range, MUST take step-by-step input, and the rest the final answer only.

Whether a problem gets a plan has to be independent of its input form, and this band makes that checkable: plan problems split between the two input forms as all compound problems do. The tolerance of one problem lets a window with few plan problems meet the band. Default chosen by the requirements step.
