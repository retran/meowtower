# REQ-8010

Withdrawn by ADR-3220 and replaced by REQ-8390 and REQ-8392: the player sees abilities by domain in game terms, and nodes stay in the Parent Room.

The game MUST show the player each node as an ability with its level as a number and her progress to the next level.

The owner decided on 2026-10-31 that the game is an RPG and the player sees her ability levels and rank. Showing the distance to the next level also follows the evidence on progress near a goal.
