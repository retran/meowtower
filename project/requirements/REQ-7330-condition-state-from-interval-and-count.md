# REQ-7330

The report MUST give a condition the state «выполняется» (met) when every measure in it has at least 20 observations in the judging window and the 80 % interval of its measure, Wilson's for one share and Newcombe's hybrid score interval for a difference, lies wholly on the condition's side of its number, «не выполняется» (not met) when the counts suffice and the interval lies wholly on the other side, and «открыто» (open) otherwise.

Twenty is the minimum per cell Simmons and colleagues set; their cells hold separate people and hers are repeated attempts by one child, so the transfer is research's judgement. The 80 % interval is the one the report draws (REQ-6616, REQ-6712), so the rule and the bar the parent reads agree.
