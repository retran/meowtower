# REQ-6432

When an eye exercise takes the player's eyes off the screen, that is looking far out of the window or covering the eyes with the palms, the exercise MUST end only when she taps «Готово» (Done) or the «Пропустить» (Skip) button the parent switched on.

No signal the game sends reaches her while she isn't looking, so she ends the moment herself, and an idle pause leaves the exercise open for her return.

Imposed by the owner's instruction of 2026-09-28 to decide the conflicts the specification step found.
