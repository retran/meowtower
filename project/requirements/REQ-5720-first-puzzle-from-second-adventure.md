# REQ-5720

The game MUST offer the first puzzle no earlier than the finale of the player's second adventure.

The addendum brings the first puzzles from the second or third adventure and never from «Пробуждение» (the Awakening), and a new puzzle comes only after a finale; I chose the second adventure as the default earliest point.
