# REQ-6424

A Volley MUST hold 3 facts that aren't yet automatic, or every such fact when fewer than 3 exist, and more than 3 only when too few automatic facts exist to fill its other places.

In the first weeks of play fewer facts are automatic than a Volley has places, so its other places can't all hold automatic facts.

Imposed by the owner's instruction of 2026-09-28 to decide the conflicts the specification step found.
