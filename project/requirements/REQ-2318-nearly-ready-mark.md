# REQ-2318

The report MUST mark a node «почти готово» (nearly ready) when its state is below «бегло» (fluent) and its «с помощью» (with help) estimate is at least 0.7 over at least 3 assisted attempts within the last 30 days.

The 0.7 is the bottom of the flow corridor, and 3 attempts keep one lucky hint from marking a node.
