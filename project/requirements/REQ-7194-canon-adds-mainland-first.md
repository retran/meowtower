# REQ-7194

A canon record MUST add the Mainland and its letters, and say why some letters arrive as bare sums or in Russian, before any letter scene is written.

The canon names no land beyond Misty Ford, the forest and the Tower, and changes only through its own records.

Written from RES-4250 on the owner's instruction of 2026-09-28 to process addendum 2.

## Open review findings

1. Round 2, a preference: move the ordering "before any letter scene is written" into the epic. I rejected it, because RES-4250 conclusion 20 states the order as the obligation, and a canon record written after the scenes would let the scenes set the canon.
