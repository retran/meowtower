# REQ-5610

Between 20% and 30% of the compound word problems the player gets in any 30 days, or within one problem of that range, MUST open with a model choice.

The model choice keeps its share of one word problem in four and is drawn from the problems without a plan, so compound problems split about 25% plan, 25% model choice and 50% neither. T1 problems keep the same share of model choices by REQ-5670. The band, the window and the tolerance are the default chosen by the requirements step, as for the plan's share.
