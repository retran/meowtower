# REQ-6884

The report MUST list per node «проверка запланирована» (check planned) with its due window, «удержание подтверждено» (retention confirmed) and «удержание не подтвердилось» (retention not confirmed) with the date of the observation that decided them, each with its count of right observations out of the total, and every retention observation of the node with its date and result, those without a check, on exempt nodes and after a confirmed series included.
