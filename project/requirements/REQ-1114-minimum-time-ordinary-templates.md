# REQ-1114

The minimum time of a template outside the small spaces MUST be max(1500 ms, min(0.15 x the template's fluency threshold, 10,000 ms)) plus the motor correction.

The rule follows the normative-threshold method: 0.15 of the fluency threshold is about 10 % of the player's expected time, capped at 10 s, and 1500 ms leaves time to read the task.
