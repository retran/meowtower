# REQ-7198

When a probe family's 14 days end before all its presentations have been shown, the report MUST count the family as closed with the presentations it has.

A family held open past its window would compare two states of her skill (REQ-7136). As a default, a family still inside its 14 days feeds the report with the presentations shown so far.
