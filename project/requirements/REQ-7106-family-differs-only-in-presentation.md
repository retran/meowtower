# REQ-7106

The presentations of one probe family MUST differ only in their presentation: every text presentation tells the same context, and each presentation draws its numbers from the family's template under a seed of its own with the same difficulty parameters.

Any other difference between two presentations, a harder number, another subtype or another story, enters the gap beside the language, and the report would read it as a language or format effect. As a default the research left open, the Dutch-after-words presentation reuses the Dutch presentation's frame with new numbers, as the owner's addendum describes it, and the Russian presentation tells the same context in Russian.
