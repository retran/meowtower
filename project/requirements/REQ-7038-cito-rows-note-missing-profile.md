# REQ-7038

Whenever a Cito result is recorded, the Cito section of the screen MUST show, whether or not it holds a row, that Cito builds no category profile for the top and the bottom 10 % of pupils.

A pupil at I+ or at V- gets no category reading at all, and the parent should know that the missing rows are Cito's rule and not a gap in her entry.
