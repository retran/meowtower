# REQ-6924

When a subtype has templates in both formats, the game MUST show only its bare templates from the subtype's first show until its node is «бегло» (fluent) or higher by a tested result, a probe or a full block and never an inferred state, or until 14 game days have passed since that first show, whichever comes first.

The addendum asks the Director to keep first encounters in reserve for the first 2 weeks. The requirements read the 2 weeks per subtype, as the research decided, so a subtype first met in month three is held too. Holding the context format keeps the node's bare share at or above half, so REQ-5864 still holds, where holding bare back could break it.
