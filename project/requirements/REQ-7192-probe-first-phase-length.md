# REQ-7192

The Dutch probe MUST show 3 to 5 probe tasks on each game day until the Russian, Dutch and Dutch-after-words presentations of the overall table each hold 20 observations or the player has played on 28 game days of the probe, whichever comes first, and 1 to 2 a game day after that.

The owner's addendum asks for about 4 a day; I read that as 3 to 5. Those three presentations carry the two named gaps; the bare and source presentations appear in fewer families and would hold the phase open for no gain. At 20 observations the probe shows a 28-point gap clearly and a 20-point gap only just. Counting game days with play keeps the rule free of any clock.
