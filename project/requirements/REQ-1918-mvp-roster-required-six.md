# REQ-1918

The MVP roster MUST contain six required familiars: «Пуговка» (Pugovka) and «Шуршик» (Shurshik, Rustler) for Spark, «Винтик» (Vintik) and «Бубенец» (Bubenets, Sleigh Bell) for Stride, and «Безешка» (Bezeshka) and «Корица» (Koritsa, Cinnamon) for Crumb.
