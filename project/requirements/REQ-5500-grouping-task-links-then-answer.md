# REQ-5500

A grouping task MUST show an expression with a convenient grouping, let the player link two numbers with taps or mark one number to round, and then take her answer.

An answer alone can't show whether she calculated the long way or used a shortcut, and a link she draws is the one trace of method the game can collect without written work, speech or an eye tracker. The examples the addendum gives are `25 * 37 * 4`, `38 + 47 + 62 + 53`, `125 * 8 * 13`, `998 + 347` and `99 * 6`; a number marked to round, such as `998`, stands for `1000 - 2`.
