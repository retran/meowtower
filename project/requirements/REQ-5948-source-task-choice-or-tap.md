# REQ-5948

Every task MUST take its answer as free input, except tasks on recognising shapes, nets, symmetry, «объясни, почему» (explain why), natural science and problem models, which take a choice of options, and Sources track tasks, which may also take a choice of at least 4 options or one tap on a region of the source.

A typed answer can't be guessed, so a choice of options appears only where typing the answer is awkward. In a track task the reading step is what is measured, and pointing at the right cell is the answer to a find question.
