# REQ-1824

Withdrawn by ADR-3270 and replaced by REQ-8230: a serious signal notifies the parent and doesn't pause the game.

When the player's text carries a serious real-life signal, such as danger, someone hurting her, a request to keep a secret from her parents, thoughts of self-harm or an unknown adult online, the game MUST pause with a «Вернуться в историю» (Back to the story) button.
