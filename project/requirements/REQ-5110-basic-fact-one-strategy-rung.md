# REQ-5110

The hint ladder of a basic fact that offers one before the answer MUST have exactly one rung, which gives a strategy through a known fact, such as «7 · 8 — это 7 · 7 и ещё 7» (7 x 8 is 7 x 7 and 7 more).

A basic fact has one real step (REQ-5106), and a strategy through a known fact teaches the step without giving the answer.
