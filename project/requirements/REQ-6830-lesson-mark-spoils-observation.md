# REQ-6830

An attempt MUST NOT count as a retention observation when the node had a lesson mark or a «тренировали факты» (we practised facts) mark since its latest meeting before that attempt's task was shown.

A right answer after the parent's lesson measures the lesson, not retention. The facts mark is the parent's lesson mark for facts.
