# REQ-5324

When the exact answer differs from the correct result by a factor of 10 to a whole power other than 0 and the estimate is right, the engine MUST label the attempt `magnitude`.

The label shows the parent that the player feels the size of the result but slipped on places or the decimal comma in the calculation.
