# REQ-5434

About 10 % of the T1 to T3 word problems the player gets on nodes whose ordinary subtype is «понимает» (understands) or above MUST be of a surplus subtype, `T1.surplus` to `T3.surplus`, carrying one number the answer doesn't need.

I chose to measure the share over the problems the Director draws, with no time window, because at about one word problem a day a monthly window holds too few problems to show a share. Below «понимает» the Director may not choose a surplus subtype at all, so those nodes stay out of the share.
