# REQ-5798

The section «Нестандартное мышление» (non-standard thinking) MUST show the puzzles solved in each theme, the mean hint rung, the puzzles the player came back to by herself, the puzzles solved without a hint on the second or third game day after they were offered, and the puzzles offered, opened and shelved.

The counts of puzzles offered, opened and shelved are what the owner's rule reads when it grows the branch by her interest. I chose as the default that she came back to a puzzle by herself when she opened it on a later game day than it was offered.
