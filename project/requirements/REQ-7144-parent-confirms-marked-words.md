# REQ-7144

The parent's approval of a Dutch probe text MUST cover the words the text marks as likely unfamiliar and the card text of each marked word, so the parent can add or remove a mark or change a card before approving.

Most marked words lie beyond the bridge's 30 to 50 approved keywords, so their cards are new text, and the parent knows which Dutch words the player meets at school. Each card's text also passes the forbidden-list check every line she sees passes (REQ-3326).
