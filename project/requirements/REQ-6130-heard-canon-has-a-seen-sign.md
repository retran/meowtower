# REQ-6130

Every creature, place or clue that the canon defines by what the heroine hears MUST also have a sign the player sees, starting with Whisperkin, the Music Box and the humming lamps and carousel of the dreamcore floors.

Story text can still describe a whisper, because she reads it; what must not exist is a clue she can only hear. The owner judges, since the canon is the owner's.
