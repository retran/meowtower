# REQ-5712

The name of the branch or of any puzzle MUST NOT contain «узелок», Irma's name or any word for the Keeper's familiar.

«Узелок» would read as "the Keeper's familiars", and the campaign year withholds both Irma and the Keeper's familiar until its later checkpoints.
