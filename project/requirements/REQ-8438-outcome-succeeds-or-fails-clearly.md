# REQ-8438

A clean answer MUST make the action succeed, a partial one MUST make it succeed at a cost the story names, and a wrong answer or «Не знаю» (I don't know) MUST make it fail, with the story going on by another way.

The player is old enough to take a failure, and an action that plainly fails gives the next check its weight; the story never ends on an answer.
