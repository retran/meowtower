# REQ-6410

A hint rung MUST NOT give the result of its task's last calculation.

With one rung per real step, a two-step task has no third rung, so the rule that the ladder stops before the answer has to bind every rung.

Imposed by the owner's instruction of 2026-09-28 to decide the conflicts the specification step found.
