# REQ-5006

When a new game day begins, the story MUST present the change of day as «Башня перевязалась за ночь» (The Tower re-knitted itself overnight).

She meets the new day as the Tower changing, never as a deadline, and REQ-5002 keeps the hour itself hidden. The parent judges.
