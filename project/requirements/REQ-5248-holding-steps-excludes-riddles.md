# REQ-5248

Every unassisted first attempt on a k-step word problem in daily play, from a Guardian or from a room, MUST count towards step k of the holding-steps limit unless it is a rapid guess, the parent excluded it, it is a riddle the player composed, or it is a problem with a missing number.

The step ladder then fills during the MVP without a ladder block of its own, and a problem with a missing number measures noticing, not holding steps (RES-4040), while a riddle measures composing, which RES-4020 found no study validating as a measure of one child.
