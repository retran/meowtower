# REQ-0142

The Parent Room MUST hold a memo on how to talk with the child about the game: don't discuss node estimates with her, share her joy at triumphs while praising effort and courage and never «ум» (cleverness), don't question her about «хитрые обходы» (clever detours) or turn them into reproach, and don't use the game as a reward or a punishment.
