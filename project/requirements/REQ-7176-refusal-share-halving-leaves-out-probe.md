# REQ-7176

When the player has answered «Нельзя узнать» (can't be known) on 3 or more of her last 20 solvable T1 to T4 word problems, not counting Dutch probe letters, the Director MUST hold the share of unanswerable problems at half its usual value, halving it once and never again, until a later window of 20 such problems holds 2 or fewer such answers.

A lower share makes «Нельзя узнать» a worse guess, so she meets fewer problems where refusing pays while the pattern lasts. Halving only once keeps some unanswerable problems in play, so the skill is still observed. A letter offers no «Нельзя узнать», so counting it would dilute both windows.

Written from RES-4250 on the owner's instruction of 2026-09-28 to process addendum 2.
