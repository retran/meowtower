# REQ-7148

Each maths floor MUST run in this order: an entry scene, an unscored warm-up, either 2 mental arithmetic tasks or one Volley, the Sources track's tasks on a floor that carries them, at most 2 Dutch probe letters on a floor that carries them, 1 or 2 rooms of trials, sometimes a Guardian, then the floor chest.

The letters come as a fixed part of the floor and take no room slot, as the Sources track's tasks do, so the Director's room slots and the flow corridor stay as they are, and a limit of 2 keeps a floor's added length small. I placed the letters beside the Sources track's tasks because both are fixed parts of the same kind, before the rooms; the owner can move them. Ten facts at the 3 s threshold take about half a minute of answer time, so the Volley fits the budget of the 2 mental arithmetic tasks it replaces.
