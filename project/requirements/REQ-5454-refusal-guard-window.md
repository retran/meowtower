# REQ-5454

When the player has answered «Нельзя узнать» (can't be known) on 3 or more of her last 20 solvable T1 to T4 word problems, the report MUST show the observation «склонна отказываться от задачи» (tends to refuse the problem).

The guard applies only once 20 such problems exist. At 7 to 9 word problems a week, one slip is 11 % to 15 % of a week and would cross the owner's 10 % in a weekly window; in a window of 20 one slip is 5 %, and 3 is the first count above 10 %.
