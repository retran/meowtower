# REQ-1902

A familiar's friendship MUST grow by the same amount whether the player's answers are right, wrong or «Не знаю» (I don't know).

Friendship grows from the scenes, rest stops and battles she shares with the familiar, so a collection that paid for correct answers would punish mistakes.
