# REQ-3112

Every text on screen MUST reach a contrast of at least 4.5:1 against the ground or fill behind it, in every theme, preset palette and custom palette.

The player can paint the colour roles herself, so readability has to hold for any colour she picks and after every theme switch, and the game adjusts her colours where they fall short.
