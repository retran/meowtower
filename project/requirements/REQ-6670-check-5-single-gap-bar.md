# REQ-6670

Build check 5 MUST pass only when each single-gap student gets its own line on at least 15 of 20 seeds and the other line on at most 4 of 20.

At the simulated rates, about 95 % and 96 % for the own lines and at most about 4 % for the wrong one, a working report passes this bar on at least about 97 % of builds.
