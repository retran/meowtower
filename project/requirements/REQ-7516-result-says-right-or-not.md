# REQ-7516

The result of an answer MUST say in plain words whether the answer was right, was not right, or was left unanswered.

A child who read «Узел ослаблен» could not tell whether the task was solved. The words stay inside REQ-2414, which forbids «верно» and «неверно», and they replace the four outcome labels of REQ-3310.
