# REQ-2002

Withdrawn by ADR-3220 and replaced by REQ-8022: the owner decided that ability levels and rank follow the graph and may fall.

The game MUST NOT reduce the player's experience, level, rank or any other progress she has earned after an answer, a mistake or a missed day.
