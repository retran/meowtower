# REQ-6638

When the 95 % interval of a language or maths contrast still holds zero or its threshold, the report MUST show «пока не ясно» (not clear yet) in its place.

A missing line would read as "no gap", and «пока не ясно» says the data can't tell yet. A maths share whose 95 % interval lies wholly above 0.8 draws no line and doesn't read «пока не ясно», because the data does tell: no maths gap.
