# REQ-5132

When a first attempt ends with a partial answer («почти», almost) and the player saw no hint rung beyond rung 1, the game MUST NOT give a second attempt.

A second attempt after a nearly right answer tells the knowledge model little about what the player does alone and makes the room longer; a rung 2 or rung 3 hint changes that, because the twin then checks the hint.
