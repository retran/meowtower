# REQ-6354

A template or a puzzle disabled or restored from the sandbox MUST stay as the parent left it for the player after the server restarts and after derived data are rebuilt from the log.

A disabling that lapses puts the item back in front of her, and a restore that lapses hides it again, with nothing to tell the parent.
