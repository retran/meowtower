# REQ-8450

Every check in the game, in a scene, a fight, a catch or a Guardian problem, MUST be one or more maths tasks the Director chooses, and its outcome MUST come from the engine's verdicts on them.

The owner decided on 2026-11-15 that any check is a maths task, or several by the difficulty of the obstacle.
