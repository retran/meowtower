# REQ-5406

An unanswerable problem MUST show the same phases and fields as a solvable problem of its tier and answer form, the model choice or plan and the step fields included.

An unanswerable problem is a T1 to T4 word problem of a subtype `T1.insufficient` to `T4.insufficient`, which can't be answered because a needed given is missing. A phase or field present on one kind and missing on the other tells her the kind before she answers. The check draws 1,000 seeds and compares an unanswerable and a solvable problem of each tier and form.
