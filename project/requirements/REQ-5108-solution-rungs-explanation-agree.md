# REQ-5108

A task's short solution, each of its hint rungs and its per-trap template explanations MUST use the same steps and the same numbers.

A rung whose steps or numbers differ from the solution she reads afterwards leads her wrong and makes the solution look like a different method. The wording drops the approved "three hint rungs", because a ladder may now be shorter.
