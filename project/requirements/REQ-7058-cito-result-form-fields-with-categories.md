# REQ-7058

The Parent Room MUST hold a form for a Cito result with these fields: the test moment, the test taken and its level, the vaardigheidsscore, the functioneringsniveau with "<" and ">" allowed, the referentieniveau, the level on the I to V or the A to E scale, the subject, an optional split between bare and context items, an optional expected test advice, an optional list of category entries, each with the category, the signal and the deviation in per cent where the printout shows it, and a note.

The teacher's printout is the only copy of the category analysis, and the M7 result of January 2027 is entered before the report that reads it exists, so the form takes it in the MVP. Each other field exists in Cito's reports, a school may report A to E in place of I to V, and a functioning level may read only "<" or ">".
