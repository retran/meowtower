# REQ-6252

Every chest MUST offer three rewards from three different categories, drawn from cosmetics; star-steel shards and star yarn, which form one category; buttons; and, once the Diary has opened, Diary pages.

A Diary page before the Diary opens would show a system the schedule hasn't opened yet. Until then only three categories exist, so every chest offers one reward from each.
