# REQ-2330

The VWO readiness block MUST show three measures: 1F coverage, the share of verified 1F nodes at «понимает» (understands) or above; the 1S margin, the share of 1S nodes in «бегло» (fluent) or «устойчиво» (stable); and the ceiling, the number of stretch nodes mastered.
