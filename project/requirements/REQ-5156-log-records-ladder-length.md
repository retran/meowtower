# REQ-5156

When the player submits an attempt, the event log MUST record the number of rungs the task's hint ladder has, beside the deepest rung she saw.

A rung 1 on a one-rung basic-fact ladder is its deepest hint, and the log has to tell that apart from rung 1 of three.
