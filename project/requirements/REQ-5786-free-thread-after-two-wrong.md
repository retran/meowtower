# REQ-5786

When the player gives a puzzle its second wrong answer, the familiar MUST offer her one free guiding thread for that puzzle's hint ladder, once for each puzzle.

After two wrong answers she is likely stuck, and a free thread gets her moving without spending her stock. The addendum doesn't say where the thread can be spent; I chose that puzzle's hint ladder as the default, so the thread gives no advantage in the adventure.
