# REQ-5034

The framing of hint rungs in the familiar's voice and the retelling of puzzles MUST spend from the offline key, under an offline role and never under the planner's play role.

Both are made ahead of play: the parent approves each framing and each puzzle before she sees it (REQ-5074), so they are offline content, which spends from the offline key (REQ-2728). A retold puzzle also carries numbers and names an idea such as parity, which a request to the planner may not carry.
