# REQ-6042

Where the school's value and the home state for a goal differ, the "home and school" report MUST word the difference as a difference between two measures, not as a fault in the player or in either measure.

The parent judges the wording. A goal can be "target reached" at school and not mastered at home with neither being wrong.
