# REQ-5846

A Volley MUST hold 3 facts that aren't yet automatic, or every such fact when fewer than 3 exist, and fill its other places with automatic facts.

The owner set about 1 in 3, which is 3 facts in a Volley of 8, 9 or 10, and that exact count is a default chosen while writing this requirement. In my reasoning, not a source: mostly known facts keep the on-target result in reach, while each Volley still trains the weak facts.
