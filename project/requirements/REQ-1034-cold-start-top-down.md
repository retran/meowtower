# REQ-1034

During cold start, which lasts from the first adventure until fewer than half of the 1F and 1S nodes remain unchecked or the 10th adventure ends, whichever comes first, the Director MUST probe each domain's prerequisite chain from a typical node of group 7-8 downwards, going lower only after a probe escalates.
