# REQ-8378

A campaign MUST open from a bible: logline, format, setting as a generator of problems, the series engine, the cast's dynamics, a pilot synopsis and a roadmap of its seasons.

The engine answers what the heroine does in an ordinary episode in the middle of a season, so generation never runs out of stories.
