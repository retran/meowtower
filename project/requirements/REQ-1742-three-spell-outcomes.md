# REQ-1742

A single spell MUST have exactly three outcomes: `clean` for a correct verdict, `partial` («почти», almost) for a partial verdict, and `alt` («ослаблен», loosened) for a wrong answer or «Не знаю» (I don't know).

Scoring in ordered categories keeps more information than right or wrong (Masters, 1982).
