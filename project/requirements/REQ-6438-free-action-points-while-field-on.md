# REQ-6438

Each adventure played while the free-text field is on MUST offer at least 2 free-action points, at least one of which isn't the lead-in to a trial, where what the player sends is kept as a story fact that a later scene reads.

A free-action point is a place where she can type, dictate or send a starter and see it change the story. With the field off she can do none of these, because starters need the field and ready-made options that send on a tap stay reserved for plot choices (REQ-6218).
