# REQ-5516

A grouping template's technique MUST be one of four: the commutative and associative laws, rounding with a correction, the distributive law, or convenient pairs such as 25 and 4, 125 and 8, and 50 and 2.

The addendum lists the commutative and associative laws together, so they count as one technique. The distributive law stays on the list to match the addendum, but no template can declare it, because REQ-5518 files every rounded product under rounding with a correction.
