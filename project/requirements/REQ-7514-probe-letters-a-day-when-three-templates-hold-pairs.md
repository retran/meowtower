# REQ-7514

On each game day on which at least 3 eligible templates hold an approved pair, the Dutch probe MUST show 3 to 5 probe tasks until the Russian, Dutch and Dutch-after-words presentations of the overall table each hold 20 observations or the player has played on 28 game days of the probe, whichever comes first, and 1 to 2 a game day after that.

The owner's addendum asks for about 4 a day, read as 3 to 5. No two open families share a template, so with fewer than 3 templates holding a pair no plan can show 3 letters, and ADR-0430 counts such a day as `probe_day_short` for the parent. At 20 observations the probe shows a 28-point gap clearly and a 20-point gap only just. Counting game days with play keeps the rule free of any clock.

Imposed by the owner's instruction of 2026-09-29 to settle the open findings.
