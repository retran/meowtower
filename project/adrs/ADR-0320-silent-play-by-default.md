# 0320. The game is silent until the parent turns a channel on, every sound goes through one registry that the build checks for a picture, an eyes-off eye exercise ends at her tap, and a prepared line from a checked bank answers her free text first

## Decision

The game plays no sound by default. Sound can only come from one client module that plays named sound events, and the build fails when an event has no visual equivalent. The eye exercises that take her eyes off the screen end when she taps «Готово» (Done). When she sends free text, a prepared reaction line from a bank checked before play shows first, and the Master's reply follows under ADR-0110's unchanged checks. The owner's addendum 1 of 2026-09-28 imposes the silence, the pictures and the 2-second first line; RES-4110 records the reasoning and decided the reaction line and the self-paced end. ADR-0210 owns the cross-cutting parts, the game day, what leaves the Mac, the event register and the stage order, and this decision builds on it.

The decision has seven parts, each a rule a program or a named person checks.

1. The parent owns sound. The Parent Room's settings panel (ADR-0180) holds two channels, music and effects, each with a switch and a volume of 0 to 5 steps (REQ-6104). A new install and a reset set both channels to off and volume 0 (REQ-6102), so the game plays nothing until the parent turns a channel on (REQ-6100). A change goes through `PUT /api/parent/settings` and is logged as `settings_changed` with the key `sound.music` or `sound.effects` and the value `{ on, volume }` (REQ-6110). When the parent turns a channel on while its volume is 0, the panel moves the volume to step 1 and its confirmation says so, «Эффекты включены, громкость 1 из 5» (Effects on, volume 1 of 5), because a switch that is on and plays nothing would look broken; I chose this. Turning a channel off keeps its volume, so turning it on again restores it. The player's settings screen holds no sound control (REQ-6108), and the schema of `looks_set` has no sound field, so a request that tries to set one gets a 400 response.

2. Silence leaves no trace. The client module `src/client/audio/` is the only code that may create an `AudioContext` or `webkitAudioContext`, construct an `Audio` element or call `play()` on a media element; an ESLint rule rejects those three anywhere else and rejects any use of `navigator.audioSession`. While both channels are off, the module creates no audio context, plays no media element and requests no sound file (REQ-6112). Sound files are served under `/sound/`, and the service worker of ADR-0010 leaves that path out of its cache while both channels are off, because a cache that fetched them at install would break the rule without the module ever playing anything. When both channels go off, the client deletes every `/sound/` entry from the service worker's cache, so a file cached while a channel was on can't play from the cache later. Once the parent turns a channel on, the module creates or resumes its audio context only inside the handler of her next tap, and a sound event that fires while the context isn't running is dropped, never queued (REQ-6114). I chose dropping, because a sound queued until the next tap would play at a moment that has nothing to do with it, and the picture already carried the event. The module never sets the audio session type, so Safari's default session keeps the iPad's silent mode in force (REQ-6116). When the parent changes a channel, the server pushes a `settings` message on her SSE stream of ADR-0030 in the same step that logs `settings_changed`, and the client stops a channel turned off at once and closes the audio context when both are off. I chose the push, because a stream with no other traffic would otherwise leave floor music playing after the parent turned it off.

3. The volume has a ceiling. Every sound plays through ADR-0150's one gain node, which now has one gain per channel under it. Step 5 of either channel is a gain of -6 dB and each step below it takes another 6 dB off, so step 1 is -30 dB (REQ-6106). The -6 dB ceiling is a budget I chose so that music and effects at full volume together still peak at or below the -3 dBTP true peak each file is normalised to (ADR-0150, part 7): two files at -3 dBTP summed at unity gain can reach +3 dB, and 6 dB less on each keeps the sum at -3. The ceiling is a constant in `src/client/audio/levels.ts`, `verify/baselines.json` holds the same value, and a group 2 test fails when a step maps above it. The row stands in the Baselines table of ADR-0190.

4. Every sound names its event, and every event has a picture. `src/shared/sound-events.ts` is a typed registry: each sound event names its channel, its optional file and its visual equivalent, a `VisualCue` that points to the scene, card or component state that shows it (REQ-6118). `playSound(event)` is the module's only public call, so no code plays a file by path. The `visual` field is required by the type, so `tsc` fails the build on an event with none. A group 1 check fails it too when a `VisualCue` names no existing scene, card or component (REQ-6120). Each `VisualCue` also lists its carriers from `shape`, `word` and `motion`, and the check fails a cue whose list is empty, so no cue rests on colour alone (REQ-6122); the parent judges at stage acceptance whether the carriers read. The registry for the MVP holds these events, each with the picture the addendum asks for, a scene and a card:

   | Sound event | Channel | Visual equivalent |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | `level_up` | effects | the ceremony of light and motion with its card (REQ-6126) |
   | `chest_opened` | effects | the chest's opening scene and the card of what it held |
   | `familiar_evolved`, `familiar_hatched` | effects | the evolution or hatching scene and the familiar's card |
   | `eye_exercise_opens`, `eye_exercise_closes` | effects | the familiar at the window with the exercise's card, and the card closing |
   | `rest_stop_offered`, `soft_stop` | effects | the scene with the System line and its card, as ADR-0090 draws it |
   | `spell_cast` | effects | the spell's animation, including a trick's (REQ-6128) |
   | `floor_music`, `dreamcore_music` | music | the floor's background, or the dreamcore scene layer of ADR-0110 |

   No sound event may fire while the task window is open: each event declares its context, the check fails an event whose context is the task window, and `playSound` drops every effects event that fires while the window is open, so no task, template, hint, puzzle or answer form can give information by sound (REQ-6124). Floor music keeps playing under an open task window, because it starts at a floor's entry and changes only with the floor or the dreamcore layer, never with a task, so it carries nothing about the task. ADR-0040's template interface has no sound field, and the lint rule keeps `src/server/tasks/` and the task window's components from importing the audio module. Every event's picture must convey the same information alone (REQ-6164), and the parent judges each one with both channels off at the stage acceptance of the stage that brings it. No sound event marks the moment «Готово» turns active in an eye exercise, because a cue that exists only with sound on would make the exercise end differently by setting.

5. The rewards and the canon show what they used to sound. A level-up is a ceremony of light and motion that is complete with both channels off, and `level_up` plays with it only when effects are on (REQ-6126). A trick's catalogue entry must name an animation preset that differs from its base spell's, and may name a sound; ADR-0140's content check fails a trick whose preset equals the base spell's, and the schema still has no field any other rule reads (REQ-6128). Every creature, place or clue the canon defines by what the heroine hears carries a `seenSign` in the canon data, and a content check fails the build when an entry tagged `heard` has none (REQ-6130). For the four the research names, the canon amendment carries the signs the owner writes; until then these defaults, which I chose, stand in the data: Whisperkin leaves her name in drifting paper dust round the corner; the Music Box sends crooked drawn notes that float closer; the humming lamps and the carousel glow in slow rings that widen and fade. Each ring or drift has a period of at least 334 ms, so ADR-0150's flash check holds. Story text may still describe a whisper, because she reads it.

6. An eyes-off eye exercise ends by her tap. Blinking and tracing a figure eight keep her eyes on the screen, so they keep ADR-0090's 35 seconds and end by themselves, with the card closing as their visual end (REQ-6132). Looking far out of the window and covering the eyes with the palms end only when she taps «Готово» (REQ-6134). The button stays inactive for the first 30 seconds with no countdown and then turns active as an instant change of state (REQ-6136). The familiar's line tells her the length in a measure she keeps herself, with no number, such as «Закрой глаза ладошками и дыши медленно-медленно, как спящий кот» (Cover your eyes with your palms and breathe slowly, slowly, like a sleeping cat), and ADR-0160's check refuses a digit or a time word in that pool as it does for every timed event's line (REQ-6138). The Parent Room's «Пропустить» (Skip) stays available from the first second when the parent has switched it on (ADR-0090). The server logs `eye_exercise_ended` with `kind`, `endedBy` (`timer`, `done`, `skip` or `pause`) and `activeMs`, the time from the exercise's start to its end. ADR-0090's eye count and the day's active time read the exercise's length from that event, because the length is no longer fixed. During an eyes-off exercise the client starts ADR-0030's 90-second idle clock only when «Готово» turns active, so an exercise she never ends pauses the adventure 120 seconds after it began. I chose this reading of REQ-2408 and REQ-2412 together: during the first 30 seconds she is doing the exercise, not doing nothing. That pause logs `eye_exercise_ended` with `endedBy: pause`, and a resume continues after the exercise.

7. Her free text gets a first line at once, and nothing unchecked shows. After she sends free text, the server runs ADR-0110's local triggers on her raw text. If neither a serious nor a narrator trigger fires, the server takes the next line from the `reaction` category of `content/lines.ru.json`, logs `reaction_line_shown` with `lineId`, `adventureId` and the id of the `free_text` event, and returns the line in its reply to the free-text request. The client shows the line's first character on receipt (REQ-6150). The reaction line doesn't wait for the judge or the Master, and the Master's reply still shows only after all of it passes ADR-0110's checks, within the 6-second budget. A serious or narrator trigger takes ADR-0110's fixed path with no reaction line, so the fixed line never follows a chatty one. REQ-6150 imposes 2 seconds at the 95th percentile from her tap to the first character; I chose a target of 1 second, stricter, because the path is a local request with no model call like ADR-0030's 300 ms answer reply, and the other second is slack for home Wi-Fi.

   A reaction line enters the bank only through ADR-0110's `line_approved` event. At approval the server runs the shared check module of ADR-0120 over it in full, schema, length, speaker, numerals, the forbidden-word list of ADR-0160 and the safety and creepiness checks by `JUDGE_MODEL` on the offline key, and stores the result against a hash of the line's text (REQ-6152). A group 1 content check reruns the checks that need no model on every reaction line, and fails a line whose stored judge result is missing or belongs to another hash. Every reaction line carries creepiness levels 0 to 2, so one bank serves every scene, the cosy ones included. A reaction line doesn't judge what she wrote, doesn't promise an answer and doesn't mention waiting or time, because it shows before anything has read her text and must read well after a sad, a funny or a strange one. The approval screen shows that rule as a checklist item beside each reaction candidate, and the parent judges it when approving each line. The dialogue book shows each reaction line with its scene, and the parent can flag it as a failure, which logs `reaction_line_flagged` with `lineId` and takes the line out of the cycle.

   The `reaction` category plays as the shuffled cycle ADR-0110 gives every pool category, and the cycle runs across sessions and adventures. When a cycle ends, the lines already shown in the current session or the current adventure go to the end of the new shuffle, so no line shows again in an adventure while the bank holds one not yet shown there (REQ-6154), and none shows twice in a session (REQ-1552). The stage 0.3 gate requires at least 60 approved unflagged lines, and a group 1 content test fails the build below that at the gate. After stage 0.3, flags can take the bank below 60; the check then reports rather than stops, the server keeps cycling the lines it has, and the Parent Room shows one `reaction_bank_low` notice until approvals bring the bank back to 60. I chose 60 because a heavy session reaches about 15 free-text points: at most 4 floor entries, 4 Guardians, 3 camps, 1 session end and a few new creatures. So while the bank holds 60 lines, a heavy session never needs to repeat a line, and a line comes back about once every four such sessions.

   While she waits for the Master, the familiar plays one of its waiting animations in the scene column, chosen as a shuffled cycle per familiar, from the moment she sends until the Master's reply begins to show (REQ-6146). The free-text field opens only after she has a familiar, because Session 0 gives her the starter before its first free text (ADR-0140). Where the scene doesn't draw the familiar, the client draws it for the wait. The waiting animations are funny actions, such as chasing its tail or juggling a thread, with no filling, counting or circling shape, because those shapes read as a spinner or a progress bar and tell her time is passing, and no spinner, progress bar, `role="progressbar"`, hourglass or «Система обрабатывает…» (The System is processing...) line shows (REQ-6148). Voice input stays beside the field as REQ-3234 asks, and every story choice stays possible by the three suggestions, «Дальше» (Next) or typing (REQ-6140).

   Every tap changes the tapped control's look within 100 ms (REQ-6142). The pressed look is a CSS `:active` state, so it never waits for script, and the client registers one passive `touchstart` listener on the document, because Safari on iOS applies `:active` to a touched element only when a `touchstart` listener exists. Inside the task window the pressed look is an instant change of state with no transition (REQ-6144). `src/ui/ds/corrections.css` sets `transition: none` on `.tw-btn` inside the task window, and `divergences.json` lists the departure with RES-4110 as its finding, because the owner's `bundle.css` gives `.tw-btn` a 90 ms press transition that ADR-0150's no-animation test would count.

The silent acceptance test is a group 4 test of ADR-0190 (REQ-6156). It plays the Awakening and one adventure of the day on WebKit at the iPad viewport with default settings, the gateway in `replay` mode, and only taps. An init script counts every `AudioContext` and `webkitAudioContext` construction, every `Audio` construction and every `HTMLMediaElement.prototype.play` call, and the network log counts requests under `/sound/` and responses with an `audio/` type. The test passes only when both flows reach their end and all the counts are zero, because Playwright mutes its browsers by default and a muted run alone proves nothing. The stage 0 spike's iPad checklist adds three rows: a new install plays nothing (REQ-6158), each parent switch makes its own channel sound (REQ-6160), and with silent mode on neither channel sounds (REQ-6162).

Once this is accepted and built, the game plays every flow with no sound, proves it by counting, and gives the parent two channels that start at the next tap. Every event it shows carries a picture the build checks, the eyes-off exercises end at her tap, and her free text gets a line within a second. The game works with no sound file at all, because each registry entry's file is optional and a missing file plays nothing, so a build with no sound files still passes every check. What doesn't work yet: before stage 0.3, and until the bank first holds 60 approved lines, the server sends no reaction line and free text waits for the Master as ADR-0110 describes; the canon signs are my defaults until the owner's canon amendment replaces them; and the parent's judgement of each picture waits for the stage acceptance that brings its event.

## Why

The owner's addendum 1 of 2026-09-28 says the player can't use sound and the game must work fully without it, with sound off by default and turned on only by the parent (RES-4110). Sound on the first touch (RES-2500) and a switch on her own screen (ADR-0150, part 3) contradict that, so the switch moves behind the Parent Room's PIN, and the change is a parent's `settings_changed` (RES-4110, finding on the four records).

A rule a program checks lasts where a paragraph drifts, which is why every "has a picture" rule here is a type or a check (design rule D2). A registry with a required `visual` field makes a sound without a picture a compile error. The ban on audio calls outside one module makes the registry the only way to play a sound, so the check covers every sound.

Counting audio acts is the only proof of silence a test can give, because Playwright passes `--mute-audio` to Chromium by default and a muted run passes even if the game plays on every tap (RES-4110, Playwright's `BrowserType` page). Starting audio only inside a tap follows the Web Audio draft, which lets Safari refuse to start a context without sticky activation. Leaving the session type alone keeps silent mode in force, since `playback` overrides the ringer switch (RES-4110, WebKit bug 237322). iPadOS Safari has no Vibration API, so the screen is the only other channel (RES-4110).

The eye exercise ends at her tap, because a picture can't reach closed eyes, and RES-0300 already accepts a button that turns active without showing time (RES-4110, decided on 2026-09-28). The familiar's measure carries no number, because REQ-0302 bans numbers in a timed event's line (REQ-6138).

The prepared reaction line meets 2 seconds with no model cost and keeps the rule that nothing unchecked shows, because a child can't unread a line (RES-4110, decided on 2026-09-28; ADR-0110 rejected streaming for the same reason). The server picks the line after its local triggers run, so the serious path never opens with a chatty line. The server also writes the line's event in the same step, so the no-repeat rule rests on the log (ADR-0020).

The pressed look must be instant inside the task window, because RES-3500 lets nothing animate there and MDN counts a CSS transition in `getAnimations()` (RES-4110). WCAG 2.2's criteria 1.1.1, 1.3.3 and 1.4.1 describe the picture rule and the ban on colour alone (RES-4110).

The strongest objection is that the reaction line is a fake answer. She writes something that matters to her, and the first line she reads wasn't written for it. With 60 lines and about 5 to 15 free-text points a day, she meets each line about once a week, and a child who reads daily learns the set within a month. Once she knows the set, the first line tells her the game didn't read her, which is the canned feeling the addendum's risk 4 warns about. The line meets the letter of a budget that was aimed at the Master's own text. I keep it, because the owner approved it in RES-4110 and every other option in the table either shows text before the model-based checks pass or misses 2 seconds. The bank grows by the parent's approvals, and a reversal condition below watches for the canned feeling in the parent's flags.

## Alternatives

| Option | Better at | Why it lost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Do nothing: sound on the first touch, her switch, the 6-second wait and the 35-second exercise | no work and no new failure state | breaks the addendum on the default, the owner of the switch, the pictures and the 2-second first line; an eyes-off exercise ends while her eyes are closed |
| A global `muted` flag, default true, with pictures added screen by screen and checked by the parent | the least code: one flag and a review | nothing stops a later sound without a picture; a muted Playwright run proves nothing, so silence rests on reading the code |
| No audio code at all in the MVP | silence is certain, and the test is trivial | the addendum gives the parent music and effects to turn on (REQ-6104), and the spike must show both work on a real iPad (REQ-6160) |
| A registry, the self-paced end and the reaction line, as decided | every sound has a checked picture, silence is counted, and every approved check still holds | costs a module, a registry, two checks, 60 approved lines and a parent's judgement per picture |
| The Master's reply streamed line by line, each line checked before it shows | the text she reads is the Master's own | the checks that need the whole reply run after the first lines show; a later failing line leaves a scene with no ending; no figure says the first checked line comes within 2 seconds (RES-4110) |

| Drop the two eyes-off exercises | every end is visible | looking far away is the main point of an eye break (RES-4110), and REQ-0308 keeps all four |

## What it costs

The builder pays one audio module, the registry and its checks, the lint rules, the corrections rule, the service worker exclusion and the acceptance test: by my estimate about 400 lines and two days, written once. Each new sound later costs a registry entry and a picture, and the build refuses the sound until the picture exists.

The parent pays about 30 minutes, by my estimate, to approve the first 60 reaction lines, which join ADR-0110's candidate queue with its 100-pending ceiling and 30-day expiry, so no new pile forms. Those approvals gate stage 0.3, because the gate requires the 60 lines. The parent judges each picture, the colour carriers, the waiting animations and the familiar's measure at stage acceptance, about 15 minutes a stage, never in real time. Turning sound on is optional and done once. The owner writes the canon signs once. If nobody attends for a month, the game plays silent, the defaults stand in the canon data, and the reaction bank stays at the size it has, so nothing is lost and nothing waits.

The interruption budget: this decision sends the parent one notice, `reaction_bank_low`, once per time the bank falls below 60 lines after stage 0.3, and interrupts the player with nothing new. The eye exercise already interrupts her three times an hour (ADR-0090). What changes is that an eyes-off exercise now waits for her tap: it used to end at 35 seconds and now lasts at least 30 and at most 120, when the idle pause ends it, so it can run 85 seconds longer than before.

The player pays the canned first line, as the objection says, and at least 30 seconds of an eyes-off exercise where she might tap «Готово» without resting, which the scene can't detect (RES-4110).

Ceilings on what accumulates: the reaction cycle is bounded by the bank; the waiting-animation cycle by the familiar's set; the `eye_exercise_ended` and `reaction_line_shown` events join the `events` table under ADR-0020's 1 GB notice, at about 25 events a day. The volume is bounded by the -6 dB ceiling. Nothing here needs a drain.

The security boundary protects two things, ordered by the likelihood of damage:

1. The player from a sudden loud sound. The per-channel ceiling, the file normalisation and the ramp of ADR-0150 defend it.
2. The parent's choice from the player. The switch sits behind the PIN (ADR-0180), her routes' schemas have no sound field, and `looks_set` refuses one.

Failure states, each with its next step and one audience:

| State | When | Next step | Audience |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `audio_blocked` | a channel is on, and Safari refuses to start or resume the context in a tap | the event is dropped, the picture shows, and the next tap tries again; the client reports once a session | developer |
| `sound_file_missing` | a registry file fails to load | the event plays nothing and its picture shows; the client reports once per file a session | developer |
| `visual_equivalent_missing` | a sound event has no `VisualCue`, a cue names nothing, lists no carrier, or fires in the task window | the build stops and names the event | developer |
| `reaction_bank_short` | at the stage 0.3 gate, fewer than 60 approved unflagged reaction lines, or at any time a line's stored judge result doesn't match its text | the build stops and names the count or the line | developer |
| `reaction_bank_low` | after stage 0.3, flags take the bank below 60 lines | one Parent Room notice until approvals restore 60; the server keeps cycling the lines it has | parent |
| `reaction_late` | no reaction line reaches the client within 2 seconds of her tap | the familiar's waiting animation goes on, and the Master's reply follows as ADR-0110 describes; the client sends its measured time from tap to first character in a client report after each free text, and the p95 of those reports shows the miss | developer |
| `eye_exercise_abandoned` | she never taps «Готово» | ADR-0030's idle pause at 120 seconds; the exercise ends as `pause`, and her next tap resumes after it | player |

When the server is unreachable, the free-text request can't leave, and ADR-0030's waiting scene shows. That case is deliberately the same as `server_unreachable`, because to her it is the same wait. A resent free-text request with the same `clientSeq` gets the same reaction line back and logs nothing new (ADR-0030). The parent changing sound on a phone while she plays reaches her client with the next SSE message or packet, so a channel turned off can play at most until then.

## What would reverse it

- If the stage 0 spike shows Safari on the iPad playing Web Audio with silent mode on and the session at its default type, REQ-6116 can't be met by leaving the session alone. The owner then chooses between removing sound from the MVP and accepting the gap.
- If `verify --live` at stage 0.3 shows the Master's checked reply at a p95 of 2 seconds or less, the reaction line has no job left, and it is removed so the first line she reads is the Master's own.
- If `reaction_line_flagged` fires in more than 1 in 10 adventures of the first month, the canned feeling is real. The bank then grows, or line-by-line checking is weighed again with a measured time to the first checked line.
- If `eye_exercise_ended` shows `done` within 2 seconds of the button turning active in more than half of the eyes-off exercises over two weeks, she watches the button instead of resting, and the end of the exercise is reopened with the owner.
- If the spike's iPad shows a pressed look later than 100 ms after a tap, the pressed look moves from `:active` to a class set in a `pointerdown` handler.

The premortem, written as though it had happened: at stage 0.3 the silent test was green, and on the second evening the parent heard the game chime on the iPad with both channels off. The service worker had cached `/sound/` at install, and a stale client build from before the channel check replayed a cached file. The test counted requests and saw none, because the cached file never touched the network. The rules that keep `/sound/` out of the cache and clear it when both channels go off are in this decision because of that, and the acceptance test now also runs after a channel was on and turned off. A second failure came from the reaction line: in the first week the player wrote that her familiar was sad, and the line «Ого, вот это да!» (Wow, look at that!) showed first. The parent flagged it, and the rule that a reaction line never judges what she wrote reached the approval screen as a checklist item beside each candidate.

## Consequences

- ADR-0210's event register gains three events this decision owns: `eye_exercise_ended` with `kind`, `endedBy` and `activeMs`, `reaction_line_shown` with `lineId`, `adventureId` and the id of its `free_text` event, and `reaction_line_flagged` with `lineId`.
- ADR-0180's placed panels change: the approval screen for reaction candidates shows the no-judging rule as a checklist item, the dialogue book gains the flag on a reaction line, and the notice list gains `reaction_bank_low`.
- `src/client/audio/`, `src/client/audio/levels.ts`, `src/shared/sound-events.ts` and the `VisualCue` type are created; the lint step gains the audio-call rule, the `audioSession` rule and the import rule for the task code.
- Group 1 gains the registry check and the reaction bank check; group 2 the ceiling test; group 4 the silent acceptance test and the tests under "How I will know".
- `content/lines.ru.json` gains the `reaction` category and the eyes-off exercise lines; `content/familiars.yaml` gains each familiar's waiting animations; the canon data gains `heard` tags and `seenSign`.
- `docs/ipad-checklist.md` gains the three stage 0 rows.
- The owner's design files keep sound in the player's settings on screen `31-Settings` and the 90 ms press on `.tw-btn`. The port follows this decision, the drift check reports the difference once, and the owner can carry the change into `design/` by hand.

## Amends

- ADR-0020: `looks_set`, "she changed her theme, palette, text size or sound", becomes "she changed her theme, palette or text size", and a sound change is the parent's `settings_changed`.
- ADR-0090: "It lasts 35 seconds, which I chose inside REQ-0306's 30 to 40, and ends by itself" becomes "Blinking and the figure eight last 35 seconds and end by themselves; looking far away and palming end at her tap on «Готово», active from 30 seconds (ADR-0320)".
- ADR-0090: the forecast's "3 of 35 seconds in an hour" becomes "3 at the median of her logged exercise lengths, or 35 seconds before any are logged".
- ADR-0090: the eye count and the day's active time take each eye exercise's length from `eye_exercise_ended`.
- ADR-0110, part 4: "After free text, the wait has a p95 of at most 6 seconds" becomes "After free text, a reaction line shows first within ADR-0320's budget, and the Master's checked reply follows with a p95 of at most 6 seconds".

## How I will know it was realised

1. The silent acceptance test of group 4 passes on WebKit with default settings, with the Awakening and one adventure reaching their end and every count at zero, and fails when a test build constructs an `Audio` element on a tap outside the audio module's channel gate. The test runs once more after the parent turns effects on, plays a sound, and turns effects off again, and finds every count at zero and no `/sound/` entry in the service worker's cache.
2. The iPad checklist at stage 0 records a new install playing nothing, each switch sounding its own channel, and silent mode silencing both.
3. Adding a registry entry with no `visual`, with an empty carrier list, or with the task window as its context turns group 1 red and names the event; `grep -rn "webkitAudioContext\|new AudioContext\|new Audio(\|\.play()" src` finds hits only in `src/client/audio/`.
4. A test sends `looks_set` with a sound field and gets 400, and a Playwright test of her settings screen finds no sound control; a Parent Room test turns effects on at volume 0 and finds one `settings_changed` event with volume 1 and the confirmation naming it. With music playing on her client, turning music off in the Parent Room stops it within 1 second, a budget I chose for a push on the home network.
5. The ceiling test maps every step of both channels to a gain at or below -6 dB.
6. A Playwright test with the clock mocked finds «Готово» inactive at 29 seconds and active at 30 in both eyes-off exercises, no digit on the screen, the exercise open at 60 seconds, and an `eye_exercise_ended` with `done` after the tap; with no tap, the adventure pauses at 120 seconds.
7. A test sends 200 free texts over 20 simulated adventures and finds each reaction line shown before the Master's reply, no line repeated in an adventure while an unshown one remained, none repeated in a session, and no reaction line before a serious trigger's fixed line. Its p95 from tap to first character is at most 1 second on the iPad viewport.
8. A Playwright test with effects and music on opens the task window after a level-up is due and finds no effects play call while the window is open and the floor music unchanged.
9. A Playwright test taps every control kind and finds its style changed within 100 ms, and after a tap on each action button of the task window finds no `getAnimations()` result.
10. At stage acceptance the parent signs off, with both channels off, the level-up ceremony, the chest, hatching, evolution, the eye exercise, the rest stop and the soft stop, the waiting animations and the eyes-off lines; the owner signs off the canon signs.

## What this does not settle

- Which sound files the game uses, and whether the MVP ships any. This decision caps them, names their events and makes them optional.
- The art of each picture, the ceremony and the waiting animations: ADR-0170's style and the owner's design.
- The canon's own wording of the four signs; the owner writes it, and my defaults hold only until then.
- The game day, what leaves the Mac, the event register and the stage order: ADR-0210.
- The texts of the reaction lines and the eyes-off lines; the parent approves them through ADR-0110's pool.
- Dictation's own behaviour, which stays as ADR-0150, part 7, sets it.
- Whether she actually rests during an eyes-off exercise. Nothing on the screen can tell, and the reversal condition on quick taps is the only watch.
