# 0370. Every gap the decisions and specifications still leave open settles by ADR-0360's defaults, rejected findings stay as records, and three requirements get replacements

## Decision

On 2026-09-28 the owner asked for the remaining issues in the decisions and specifications to be fixed, after telling research to decide conflicts itself. The approved decisions and the live specifications hold 153 findings under `## Open findings` and `## Open review findings`. I read each against the records it names: 84 are findings their author rejected for a valid reason, most of them requests for reasons in a specification, which spec rule S8 forbids, and they stay in their files as records. Three are already settled, by ADR-0360 entry 62, by the current text of ADR-0210 and by SPC-0310's own failure table. I settle the other 66 in the entries below, one entry per finding. Each entry names the section it closes, the records it touches, the rule that now holds and its reason.

I applied ADR-0360's four defaults in its order. A rule that keeps her measurement fair, her data on the Mac and her screens free of clocks wins. The decision that owns a capability wins a detail of it. An approved requirement wins over a decision unless it can't hold, and then a replacement, REQ-6500 to REQ-6504, carries the rule. A pure gap gets the smallest rule a test can check.

1. SPC-0010, Open review findings, item 2 (ADR-0010). `storage_ceiling` counts `data/` and the live database with its write-ahead log, which `meowtower` reads as the size of `meowtower.sqlite` and `meowtower.sqlite-wal` in `/var/lib/meowtower`. The volume sits on the same Mac disk and grows with every event, so a ceiling that leaves it out lets the disk fill unannounced.
2. SPC-0020, Open review findings, item 1 (REQ-2224; ADR-0020). Every entry in the projection registry declares its class, `game` or `knowledge`, and the import check of REQ-2224 fails a `game` projection that imports the model, threshold or graph modules. `grouping_stream`, the composing stream, the `plan` stream, `check_week` and `estimate_stream` are `knowledge` projections, because they hold scores the parent's figures read; `phrase_counts`, `cito_rules` and both word-problem counters of entry 59 are `game` projections, because they read no model, threshold or graph version. A projection with no class gives the check nothing to test.
3. SPC-0020, Open review findings, item 2 (REQ-2210; ADR-0020, ADR-0310). When a blob file exists with no `blobs` row, `meowtower` hashes the file: if the hash matches the file's name, it inserts the missing row and goes on with the write, and if not, it refuses the write with `blob_changed`. At start-up it does the same for every file in `data/blobs/` that has no row. The file's name is its hash, so the hash proves the content and a crash between the sync and the insert loses nothing.

The server in its container can't read the owner of a process on the Mac, and the job already runs there each minute, so a judge restarted under another account loses its route within about a minute.
65. SPC-0350, Open review findings, item 1 (REQ-1688; ADR-0100, ADR-0350). Agreement is the share, in percent, of the test set's items on which the judge's label equals the reference model's, and a judge matches the reference, as REQ-1688 asks, when its agreement is no more than one percentage point below the reference model's agreement with itself over two runs of the same test set, and it also gives the gravest label on every item the reference gives it: `serious` on `signal`, and a failing answer on `safety`. The bake-off sends the reference's two runs through the gateway with the settings play uses for that model, as two separate requests per item with no response cache, so its self-agreement shows how much its labels vary in play. The margin is ADR-0350's tie, where judges within one percentage point of agreement are equal because a smaller gap on a few hundred items is below the sampling error, and it keeps the bar reachable when the reference answers every item the same way twice. SPC-0100 states the measure, and ADR-0350's tie reads it. The reference's own repeat is the closest match any model can show, so the bar asks nothing the reference itself can't do and needs no number I would have to invent. A judge that misses a serious case the reference catches fails however well it agrees elsewhere, because that miss is the harm the check exists to catch.
66. SPC-0350, Open review findings, item 2 (REQ-2648; ADR-0100). SPC-0100's provider facts become a table, `content/providers.json`, with each provider's company, country and retention, and the start-up check refuses to start with `model_config_invalid` when a provider on either tier list has no row. The Parent Room page reads the country from the same row the start-up check reads.

Once this record and its three requirements are approved, every specification can drop its remaining open findings and state one rule for each, and the rejected findings stay where they are as records. What doesn't work yet: the specifications still carry the old findings until the spec step rewrites them, and entries 23 and 65 need the bake-off's test sets for `signal` and `safety` to gain `scared` items and graded serious items before a judge can pass.

## Why

A finding a specification can't settle blocks the epic step for its part, because an epic built on an open rule builds one side of it. The owner asked for the remaining issues to be fixed, and ADR-0360 set the defaults for doing so; I kept them so both records read the same way.

Most of what remains isn't open. Spec rule S8 keeps reasons out of a specification, and 41 of the 84 rejections refuse a request to add them; the other 43 refuse a change that an approved decision, an approved requirement or the record template already rules out, such as moving an event's payload out of the decision that owns it (ADR-0020). Deleting those records would lose the author's reason, and the approver reads them, so they stay.

The 66 settlements are of three kinds. Most are gaps: a failure state with no status, an event with no route, an upcaster with no value. There I chose the smallest rule a test can check, so a later decision can replace it without undoing much. Some are readings of a decision that contradicted itself, such as entry 18, where the text counts six kinds and lists seven; there I followed the list. Three touch approved requirements that can't hold as written (entries 33, 34 and 61), and each gets a replacement the owner approves on its own.

The strongest objection is that several entries invent game behaviour no research recorded: a special reward granted once per floor (entry 43), a `scared` label for the judge (entry 23), a gravest-label rule for the judge (entry 65) and a single reference for a heroine sheet (entry 48). A wrong invention here reaches her screens. I kept each one small and gave each of the four a reversal condition below, as I did for entry 14, whose bands change a figure on the parent's map, and each entry can be rejected alone, which reopens only its finding. To reject one before approving, the owner deletes the entry's text and its lines in Amends and Consequences, lists it under What this does not settle, and leaves its number empty so the other numbers still hold.

## What it costs

The owner pays one reading of 66 entries and three requirements, about an hour and a half by my estimate, and can reject any one alone.

The player pays in five places. When the `scared` label fires on a story that holds no fear, the fear path starts and her scene gets a kind resolution it didn't need, and the level falls (entry 23). A creepy scene she skips counts towards the fear path only at level 1 or 2 (entry 24), which is the case the path exists for. The parent pays a smaller review queue in one place: an edited frame waits for a `frames:generate` run before it can be accepted (entry 35).

The developer pays a class on every projection, a blob recovery at start-up, three upcaster values, a composite threshold version, an idempotent `next`, a repeat path for explanations, five statuses, a `scared` label with its test items, a content test over every text, a minimum level on library scenes, a verify stage for template explanations, three new frame events, a snapshot read in verify, an event queue on the client, a per-surface palette fallback, a dictation line, a token test, two Parent Room routes, a first stage on each check, a spike sound page, a draft route, a projection for the T1 counter and a provider table.

The premortem, written as though it had happened: at the stage 0 bake-off no local judge matched the reference on `signal`, because the reference labelled two runs of everyday worries almost alike and every candidate split them differently, and every check stayed hosted. In the same month the `scared` label started the fear path on a story she wrote about a brave mouse who «не боится», and the scene resolved kindly when nothing needed resolving. The reversal conditions below watch both.

## What would reverse it

- If no local candidate matches the reference on any check at the stage 0 bake-off while two candidates pass the gravest-label rule, entry 65's bar reopens, because it then keeps her text off the Mac for disagreements on labels that do no harm.
- If, over a term of stage 0.3 play, more than 3 nodes shown with high confidence are found a state lower at their next full block, entry 14's bands reopen, because high confidence then promises the parent more than the evidence holds.
- If the `scared` label starts the fear path on more than 2 game days in 14 while the parent, reading the dialogue book, finds no fear in the text, entry 23 reopens, because the label then fires on words about fear rather than on fear.
- If more than half of a heroine sheet's variants fail the style check twice in a row, entry 48 reopens, because one reference then doesn't hold the style.
- If, at the stage 0.3 review, the parent reports that the player never equipped or used any floor's special reward she received, or asks for one on each `triumph`, entry 43 reopens, because the reward then doesn't reward her or rewards her too rarely.
- If any replacement requirement is rejected, its entry reopens and the requirement it would have superseded stands.
