# 0360. Each conflict the specifications found settles for the approved requirement and a fair measurement, the addendum decision that owns a detail wins it, and twenty requirements that can't hold as written get replacements

## Decision

On 2026-09-28 the owner told research to decide every conflict the spec step found. The specifications SPC-0010 to SPC-0350 left 101 open findings; I settle all of them in the 95 entries below, one entry per distinct conflict, because five findings appear in two specifications each and entry 16 settles two findings of SPC-0070 together. Each entry names the specification section it closes, the records it settles, the rule that now holds and its reason. I applied four defaults in this order. A rule that keeps her measurement fair, her data on the Mac and her screens free of clocks wins. A later addendum decision, ADR-0210 to ADR-0350, wins over an older one unless it breaks an approved requirement, and where ADR-0210 and a sibling disagree on a detail the sibling owns, the sibling wins. An approved requirement wins over a decision unless it can't be met, and then a replacement requirement, REQ-6400 to REQ-6438, carries the rule. A pure gap gets the smallest rule a test can check.

1. SPC-0010, Open findings, item 1 (REQ-2512; ADR-0010, ADR-0350). A local judge is a server process, so it runs under a launchd daemon as a dedicated standard macOS account without administrator rights, created once by `./meowtower setup`, and the server routes no check to a judge whose process runs as root or as a member of `admin`. The owner's own account is often an administrator, which REQ-2512 doesn't count as unprivileged.
2. SPC-0010, Open findings, item 2 (REQ-2510; ADR-0010). `./meowtower up` refuses to start with `gateway_unverified` when the hardware address of the recorded or the current gateway is unknown, and a host launchd job compares the gateway every 60 seconds while the stack runs and stops the containers with `wrong_network` on a difference. A Mac that changes network while serving, or meets another router at `192.168.1.1`, would otherwise serve the game outside the home.
3. SPC-0010, Open findings, item 3 (ADR-0010, ADR-0190). The server stops mounting `POST /api/stage0/write` and `GET /api/stage0/write/:id` from stage 0.1 on, and a route test finds both answer 404; the version 0 events they wrote stay readable. A write route that bypasses the play API is a way to put events in her log that no play rule checked.

What doesn't work yet: the specifications still carry the old findings until the spec step rewrites them, and entries 1 and 2 need a setup step on the Mac that the owner runs once.

## Why

The specifications couldn't choose, because a specification states what the system does and the method gives choices between decisions to the design step. Left open, 101 findings would block the epic step for every part of the game, since an epic built on a contested rule builds one side of it.

The order of the defaults comes from what the game is for. It checks a child's maths for her parent, so a rule that biases a figure the parent reads, sends her data off the Mac or shows her a clock costs more than any other kind of mistake (the vision and RES-4000). The addendum decisions are the owner's newest instructions, so they win over the older ones, but only inside the approved requirements, which the owner approved one by one. Where ADR-0210 and a sibling disagree, the sibling owns the capability and wrote its rule with the detail in view, while ADR-0210 wrote the shared rules for all of them at once.

I wrote a replacement requirement where the approved text can't be met: where two approved requirements contradict each other (entries 13, 19, 40, 43, 78, 81, 82, 83), where arithmetic rules it out (entries 18, 21, 57, 62) or where it would bias a figure (entries 17, 72). In four entries the approved text can be met, and I still let a decision's reason outweigh it, which runs against my third default, so the owner decides each of them on its own: entry 31, because REQ-2730's limit, met literally on a key that also pays the sandbox, no longer caps the run it exists to cap; entry 34, because a button for each Volley fact breaks ADR-0140's economy; entry 47, because ADR-0230 itself sent REQ-5218 back to the requirements step; and entry 63, because an interval that grows without end leaves a known fact unseen before the horizon. Everywhere else I bent the decision to the requirement, because an approved requirement is the owner's word and a decision is mine.

The strongest objection is that I decided about a hundred questions in one record, alone, with only the records to go on, and the owner will approve them in one reading. A wrong call in one entry then rides along with ninety good ones. I kept each entry to one rule and one reason, so a single entry can be rejected without the rest, and every replacement requirement is a file of its own that the owner approves or rejects separately.

## Alternatives

| Option | Better at | Why it lost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Do nothing: leave every finding to the owner | The owner decides each rule personally | The owner asked research to decide them, and until someone does, no epic can start on any part the findings touch |
| The newest decision always wins | One rule, no judgement per entry | ADR-0280 and ADR-0330 would close puzzles and the pen for the rest of a day, which REQ-5012 forbids and which is the daily maximum the owner removed |
| The approved requirement always wins, with no replacements | The approved set never changes | Requirements that contradict each other, such as REQ-6134 and REQ-0316, leave no rule to follow, and REQ-0822 fails whenever she plays fewer than 3 days in 7 |
| Each specification settles its own findings | The rule sits where it is used | A specification can't choose between decisions, and five conflicts span two specifications that would each choose alone |
| One record per specification or per area | A wrong call in one area can be rejected without reading the rest | Entries can already be rejected one at a time, each replacement requirement is its own file, and one record states one set of defaults applied the same way to every conflict |

## What it costs

The owner pays one reading of 95 entries and twenty requirements, about two hours by my estimate, and can reject any one of them alone.

The player pays in a few places. Word-problem rungs name quantities where they printed numbers (entry 8), so a rung helps her less. Raised mode adds only one review task for every 10 in-corridor slots (entry 16), because the flow band allows no more. A puzzle or the pen after the soft stop brings the soft stop again at its first boundary (entries 23 and 81). A grouping rung no longer shows the shortcut (entry 52).

The parent pays a smaller figure in two places: the bridge share and the reading-gap line count fewer tasks (entries 40 and 72). The ordinary review share falls from about 35 % to about 30 % of in-corridor slots (entry 16), which leaves more of the room slots to the frontier.

The developer pays a launchd daemon and a standard account for the judge, a network watch job, a `bakeoff` mode, a save route, a plan route and a `plan_draft` event, a queue that keeps every grouping set, a code-drawn spell, an opaque System panel, 20 neutral reaction lines and an interlude rule for short plans. The owner pays a setup step with administrator rights once, and a limit change on the offline key before and after every offline run (entry 31). The player pays slower stretch progress, because only one stretch block is open at a time (entry 18), and an adventure that doesn't fit before the soft stop carries over to the next game day (entry 15). and a Mac that can't read its gateway's hardware address doesn't start the game.

The premortem, written as though it had happened: at the stage 0.3 review the parent found the hint rungs on word problems vague, because a rung that says «возьми сколько конфет в первой коробке» reads worse than one with the number, and she had stopped buying them. In the same month the network watch stopped the game twice when the router rebooted and its hardware address answered late. The reversal conditions below watch both.

## What would reverse it

- If, over any 30 days of stage 0.3 play, she opens the hint ladder on T1 to T4 word problems at less than half the rate at which she opens it on arithmetic tasks she answers wrong, or the parent finds the word-problem rungs unhelpful at the stage 0.3 review, the rung rule for word problems reopens, because she has stopped finding those rungs useful.

- If raised mode shows no fall in the rapid-guess share over the next session in more than half its sessions at stage 0.3, the owner reopens REQ-1010 for a raised-mode exception, because entry 16 has then used all the room the band allows.
- If a puzzle or pen session opened after the soft stop plays past 20 minutes on more than 3 game days in 14, entries 23 and 81 reopen, because the repeated soft stop isn't ending the day.
- If the owner pays for a Volley's facts at one button each in a test economy and the weekly button income then stays within ADR-0140's planned week, entry 34 reopens, because its reason is gone.
- If, at stage 0.3, facts at the 14-day interval miss more often on return than facts on shorter intervals, entry 63's cap reopens, because the cap then lets known facts decay.
- If an offline run stops at the key's limit before its own budget is spent in more than one run in five, entry 31 reopens, because the headroom rule then fails the run it exists to cap.
