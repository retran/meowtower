# 0330. The Director offers two routes, opens one new system a day and plans a scene without tasks and a known character into every adventure, the Master writes starters that only insert and answers her own words in a later scene, and the report measures her interest, with the 60 % task-window share reported and never enforced

## Decision

The game keeps the story the player's own through rules that live on the Director's side of ADR-0110's split, where code decides and a program can check them, and through the Master's text, which the Director's orders constrain. The measurement stays where ADR-0070 and ADR-0080 put it: nothing in this decision changes a trial, its outcome, which task comes or how many graded slots a day plans. The report gets an "Interest" section, so the parent and the backlog's acceptance can see whether the rules work for her. This decision builds on ADR-0210, which owns the unseen game day at 04:00, the screens without tasks that open whenever the task window is closed, the money rules for the play key and the owner's decision that daily quests and daily rewards come every game day. It cites those rules and doesn't restate them.

1. The route choice. `planDay` (ADR-0070) returns two routes, A and B, for every new adventure (REQ-6234). Route A is the route `planDay` builds today. Route B keeps every floor the window forces: each domain overdue for 2 adventure days, and each domain that would miss the three-day window without a floor today (REQ-6236). Among the other places, B swaps A's free floor of lowest total value for the domain of highest total value not on A, when the swap keeps the window for the next two days. When no swap keeps it, B holds A's floors in another order and gets another opening beat (REQ-6238). Both routes get the same number of floors and the same planned number of graded slots, the smaller of their two forecasts, because topping up the shorter one with a room could push the forecast past 60 minutes. The choice plays as a story scene straight after «В прошлый раз…» (Last time…) and its daily quests, once quests have opened. The Master writes one teaser for each route from the route's floor keys and opening beat, and when its reply fails twice or the gateway is off, each door shows the library teaser of its first floor, written by hand for every floor. The two doors appear in an order drawn from the adventure seed, and «Дальше» (Next) takes route A (REQ-6240). I chose the drawn display order, because a route A that always stood on the left would teach her that the left door holds the maths the Director wants. The choice is a plot choice, so its options send on a tap (REQ-6218). The log records `route_offered` and `route_chosen`, a resumed adventure keeps the chosen route, and only the chosen route's floors count in the three-day window.
2. The schedule of new systems. A projection, `systems_open`, reads `system_unlocked` events. A day of play is a game day whose log holds at least one `scene_shown` of an adventure, with Session 0 excluded, so a missed calendar day delays nothing (REQ-6244). When the day's first adventure scene starts, which is the scene «В прошлый раз…» and the moment that game day becomes a day of play, the server appends `system_unlocked` for every system that REQ-6244 opens on that day and that isn't open yet, and that scene introduces the new system in one System line. So a system never opens in the middle of an adventure, and a day she spends only in her room or the pen opens nothing. The order is REQ-6244's: levels and the chest; guiding threads, the hint ladder and the volley; daily quests and the first knot; the Diary and the bestiary; a second familiar; the forge on day 6; the shop, «Свободное перо» (Free Pen) and the Tower's dictionary on day 8. Evolution opens when a familiar first reaches its evolution threshold, and the Underside at the Director's first slip (ADR-0110 part 7). Session 0 appends the day-1 unlocks, the levels and the chest, because its fourth step is the first chest; I chose this, because Session 0 comes before the first day of play (REQ-6243) and can't show a chest the schedule keeps closed. Task forms aren't systems, so no form waits. Every route of a closed system answers 404 with `system_closed`, and the client draws no entry to it. The Parent Room's setting "open every system" appends `system_unlocked` with `source: "parent"` for every system still closed (REQ-6246). Switching the setting off again closes nothing, because a closed shop would strand the items and buttons she already holds; I chose this.
3. Threads before they open. Until guiding threads open on day 2, every attempt is unassisted: the task window draws no thread button (REQ-6250), offers no hint rung, detailed explanation or backpack pocket, and still shows the free short solution after `partial` and `alt` (REQ-6245). No source grants a thread before threads open, and nothing is back-filled, because a stock that built up before she knows what threads do would arrive as a pile she has no use for. On the day threads open, ADR-0080's morning grant comes with the unlock at the start of the adventure, even when the day's first contact came earlier, so day 2 already gives the 3 morning threads (REQ-6248). Session 0's training step trains the answer field and «Не знаю» (I don't know), and never threads, hints or explanations (REQ-6243).
4. Starters. Every scene offers three starters of 3 to 5 words each, the third strange and funny, and «Дальше», which moves the story on while the field is empty (REQ-6208, REQ-6214). A tap on a starter inserts its words at the cursor, with a space before them when the field holds text, and never sends (REQ-6210). Keys 1 to 3 insert the matching starter only while the field is empty, so digits stay typable in her own text (REQ-6212). Each insertion appends `starter_inserted`. The Master's reply carries `starters`, a tuple of three strings, where ADR-0110's reply carried options, and each library scene carries its own three. The reply schema admits sending `choices` only on the order kinds `route_choice` and `name_suggest`, so a program enforces REQ-6218; adding a kind means changing this decision. The field opens at the points REQ-5268 lists, which ADR-0230 owns.
5. Free-action points and their consequences. The day plan marks as free-action points every floor entry, the campfire when a rest stop comes, and the finale's `session_end`. A floor entry isn't the lead-in to a trial, so a plan of 3 floors carries at least 4 points, at least 3 of them not tied to a trial, well above REQ-6200's 2 and 1 (REQ-6200). When she sends, the server writes `free_text` at payload version 2, which adds `origin`, one of `own`, `starter_edited` or `starter_unchanged`, and `ownWords`, the number of words she sent minus the words of an inserted starter. The server compares the normalised text with the scene's starters, so the client can't claim the origin. The finale's `session_end` is the adventure's own-words point: there, an unchanged starter still moves the story, as any send does, but doesn't count as her action (REQ-6216). I chose the finale, because every adventure reaches it, the resumed ones on a later day included, and it isn't tied to a trial. A send whose origin is `own` or `starter_edited` becomes a story fact of kind `player_action`, which the service stores itself, so a Master failure can't lose it. The planner's order lists the open `player_action` facts, at most 10, the newest first, and a fact older than 3 adventures closes unechoed, because a consequence that late can't be told apart from chance (REQ-6202); and its reply must name in some beat's `echoes` each fact older than the current adventure. A plan that leaves such a fact unechoed fails the planner's checks, takes ADR-0110's retry and then its fallback, and the service logs `consequence_missed` (REQ-6202). Scene orders carry their beat's `echoes`, and `scene_shown` logs them, so the parent's judgement has the trail. The change travels only through `remember`, `relation`, `running_joke`, `title` and the planner's beats, and the Director's event schemas still have no field the Master could use to change a trial (ADR-0110 part 1).
6. The scene without tasks and the known character. Every adventure plan holds one `interlude` order between the second floor's chest and the third floor's entry, which is neither a lead-in nor a transition (REQ-6204). Its budget is 90 seconds of the 10 minutes of story ADR-0090 allows, a figure I chose, so the time plan and the floor of 28 graded attempts stay as they are. The interlude's order names one character from the canon's recurring cast whom she has met, the one she met least recently by `scene_shown` speakers (REQ-6266). The cast is the canon's sections tagged `cast: recurring`, without her familiars and the System, because those two appear in every adventure and would meet the rule by default. Session 0's first scene introduces one member of the cast, so the rule holds from the first adventure; that is a canon obligation on the owner. The library holds at least 20 hand-written interludes, dealt like the decks of part 8, so a Master failure still plays a scene without tasks.
7. The task-window share. The report computes, for each adventure, the active time during which a task window was open, from ADR-0080's `open` state to its `closed` state, over the adventure's active time (ADR-0090), and shows it against the 60 % target (REQ-6206). The share gates nothing: the plan never reads it. The owner's research decided on 2026-09-28 that the 60 % is a reported target, because a cap would push graded attempts below 28 on the first slow days.
8. Freshness. A projection, `phrase_counts`, counts each sequence of 3 to 5 lemmas in the Master's scene text over the last 14 days. The lemmas come from `content/lemmas.ru.json`, a form-to-lemma table a tool script builds once from the OpenCorpora dictionary, and an unknown word counts as its normalised form. A sequence seen more than 3 times, the addendum's figure, which lets a phrase recur about once in 4 or 5 days of play before it counts as a habit, joins the do-not-use list that each later order carries (REQ-6254). The order carries at most 30 entries, the most frequent first; I chose 30 to keep the list under about 300 tokens of the 3,000 ADR-0110 budgets for the dynamic part. A sequence holding a whitelisted lemma sequence never joins it: the running jokes in story memory, the canon's sections tagged `catchphrase` and her current names (REQ-6264). CAN-0010's rule of once a session for each running joke stays as it is. After a drafted scene passes ADR-0110's checks, the service looks for do-not-use sequences in it; a scene that holds one gets one regeneration, and a reply to her free text gets none, so its 6-second wait stands (REQ-6256). A regenerated scene that still repeats shows anyway, because a repeat is a matter of taste and the library scene it would fall back to repeats more. Openings, finales and character temperaments come from three decks in `content/decks.ru.json`, each card an instruction to the Master, dealt without replacement by the adventure seed and reshuffled when spent (REQ-6258). The service scores each opening's similarity to the last 14 openings as the largest Jaccard overlap of their lemma pairs, on the Mac and with no model call (REQ-6260). The Interest section shows the week's median score beside the repetitiveness figure, and nothing gates on it. It appends `text_freshness_scored` for every Master scene. Each week the server draws 5 Master scenes of the last 7 days by the week's seed into the parent's review queue of ADR-0110 part 8 (REQ-6262); 5 is the addendum's figure, about one scene a day of play, which the parent reads in a few minutes.
9. Refusals. The Master refuses a story action only on the safety path of ADR-0110 part 6 or on an item of its checklist (REQ-6278), and its reply schema has no refusal field of its own. The prompt carries the canon's «Да, и…» (Yes, and…) rule, and the judge's checklist gains the item "refuses or blocks her action without a safety reason", so a reply that refuses on other grounds fails and takes the retry. A refusal's reply must carry a turn in the story in the same reply (REQ-6279). A lead-in's order carries `trialFixed: true`, and the prompt asks for a "yes, and" redirection when her action would cancel the trial (REQ-6280). The Director can't drop a trial for a story reason, because no effect in ADR-0110's closed set touches one. The name refusal stays, because a name shows on every later screen: its line now names the limit the name broke, length or a word the Tower doesn't take, and keeps her text in the field beside the suggestions, so she can shorten or change it (REQ-6282). The words come from the string file of ADR-0160, and a limit is named without its number, because a System line carries no digits.
10. The first scene after a gap. A scene order has no field for a date, a gap or a count of days, so the Master can't know she was away. Every line of the first scene of every adventure, whether from the Master, the pool or the library, passes a return-phrase check against `content/return-phrases.ru.json`, a list of phrases that miss her, wait for her or ask where she was, with every form written out as in ADR-0160 (REQ-6232, REQ-6233). I apply the check to every adventure's first scene, which covers each scene after a gap and needs no rule to find the gap. A Master reply that fails takes ADR-0110's retry and library path, and a pool or library line that fails fails the build. The owner writes the list and the parent approves it at the stage 0.3 acceptance; the family adds a phrase through a commit the parent approves. I chose a content file, because the owner is the parent, and a Parent Room editor would be a screen built for a list that changes a few times a year. The canon gains the rule that no character misses her, waits for her or asks about her absence, and the garland of ADR-0140 stays as it is, because it never counts days.
11. Screens she owns. She can reread any past chapter and rewatch any past scene from the story log, which the server builds from `scene_shown` with no model call (REQ-6268). She marks a scene as a favourite there, and favourites show in the story log and in the Parent Room's story book (REQ-6270). Hidden details are canon sections tagged `hidden_detail` with their checkpoint, and the prompt builder's checkpoint filter of ADR-0110 part 3 already keeps a later one out (REQ-6272). A scene order may carry one detail id, a limit I chose so a scene never turns into a search, which the client draws as a spot she can tap. No progression rule, quest template or Director event reads a detail, so the story never waits on one (REQ-6274). Rituals are entries in `content/rituals.ru.json`, each with an `on` field from the enum `floor_first_scene`, `chest_opened`, `finale` and `familiar_evolved`, and the schema has no field for a time or a date (REQ-6276). A "Show" card is made from a scene or from a moment in her room, and it reaches only the Parent Room's card panel behind the PIN (REQ-6284). The player's routes can create a card and never list or read one.
12. The free pen. «Свободное перо» opens on day 8 of the schedule, from the heroine's room and from a button on the scene screen, whenever the task window is closed and the parent's setting is on (REQ-6220, REQ-6224). The setting starts on, because REQ-6224 chose that default so the pen opens on its day in the schedule, and the parent can switch it off at any time. Each turn runs ADR-0110 part 6 and the reply checks of part 5, and the field shows the parent note (REQ-6221). Each turn is a `StoryRequest` of kind `free_pen` to ADR-0210's `FREE_PEN_MODEL` role. It carries story memory and no task, score or Director event, and the service writes no knowledge-model event for it (REQ-6222). A pen reply may apply only `remember` with scope `pen`, which the adventure's planner doesn't read. I chose this, because the planner's 200 facts would otherwise fill with pen facts and push out the adventure's own. Pen time counts in the day's active time and the eye count (REQ-6226). When the day's active time passes the soft-stop point, or the adventure bucket can't pay for the next turn, the pen closes the book through a library scene and offers no extension (REQ-6228). It stays closed for the rest of the game day, unless an «Ещё один ряд» (One more row) in the adventure moves the soft-stop point on, because a pen that reopened at once would make the close a speed bump. The pen spends only what the bucket of the current adventure left, as ADR-0210 defines it (REQ-6230). The day's plan creates that adventure at the first contact of the game day, so a pen opened before the first floor spends from it too. While the adventure is unfinished, the pen may spend only what exceeds the forecast cost of its remaining scenes. I chose this reservation, which is stricter than REQ-6230, because writing in the pen would otherwise turn the rest of the adventure into library scenes. With the gateway off, the pen shows a library line and stays closed.
13. The Parent Room. The report's summary screen, one of report v1's nine (REQ-6064), gains the "Interest" section, because the summary is the screen the parent reads first. The section shows, for the last 14 days of play and for each adventure in them:
    - whether she came back by herself, from the parent's mark;
    - extensions;
    - her own words per adventure, the share of her sends that are her own or edited, and the share of free-action points she passed with «Дальше» and no send;
    - the optional activities she used: the free pen, rereading, favourites, hidden details, cards and puzzles;
    - the systems she used;
    - the repetitiveness figure, the share of Master scenes whose draft held a do-not-use sequence, with the week's median opening similarity;
    - the task-window share against 60 % (REQ-6288, REQ-6206).

    The Parent Room asks, for each day of play in the last 7 days that has no mark, one question with two answers, which appends `parent_day_marked` (REQ-6290). The signal "the story is stopping being hers" rises when her own words per adventure, starter text excluded, fall by 30 % or more between the last 14 days of play and the 14 before, and the share of unchanged starters rises over the same windows (REQ-6292). Before 28 days of play the section shows «мало данных» (too little data) in place of the signal. Each week of the section shows whether the signal rose in it, and a backlog item's acceptance counts only a week without it (REQ-6294). The memo gains a checklist for the first week and keeps the rule against tying play to rewards or punishments outside the game (REQ-6242). The story book screen gains a PDF export through the browser's print and a print stylesheet, with no PDF library on the server, in the MVP (REQ-6286). The Parent Room's settings gain the free-pen switch and "open every system".
14. The acceptance run. ADR-0190's simulation group gains a 60-day run, the owner's addendum's test 17 (REQ-6296), which is point 1 of the checks under How I will know.

`pen_closed_soft_stop` and `pen_closed_budget` look the same to her on purpose, because a closing that named money or time would give her a clock or a price.

Once this decision is accepted and built on ADR-0010 to ADR-0200 and ADR-0210, an adventure offers two routes and takes the one she picks, systems open by the schedule, starters insert, every adventure holds a scene without tasks and a known character, her own words come back in a later scene, the Master's phrases are counted and steered, and she can reread, favourite, find details, make cards and write in the free pen. The parent reads the Interest section, marks days, reviews five scenes a week and prints the story book. With the gateway off, every part still works on the library, except the free pen, which stays closed. What doesn't work yet: the signal needs 28 days of play before it can rise, and it can't see a child who stops sending at all, because REQ-6292 also needs the share of unchanged starters to rise; the share of points passed with no send covers that case, and REQ-6292 goes back to the owner with this gap. The interludes, decks, hidden details, rituals, return phrases, the checklist and the canon's cast tags are content the owner writes, and until each exists its part plays nothing; an adventure whose Master can't write the interlude then plays without one, as `interlude_missing` records. ADR-0210 is itself a draft, and this decision can't be accepted before it.

## Why

RES-4120 found that section 12 of the owner's addendum 1 of 2026-09-28 fits ADR-0110's split: story choices, scenes without tasks, a recurring cast, running jokes, rereading, hidden details and rituals all live where the Director decides every trial and the Master writes around it. So I put each rule where a program can hold it every time, as the design step asks. The Director owns the route, the interlude, the known character and the schedule, because code decides who appears and what opens. The service owns the story facts, the origin of a send and the freshness counts, because a model can't be trusted to keep its own record. The schemas own the plot-choice rule, because a field that doesn't exist can't be misused. The ban on refusals can't rest on a schema, since a refusal can be written as prose, so it rests on the judge's checklist item and on the parent's reading in check 11.

The evidence points the same way, with limits RES-4120 names. Felt autonomy in play predicts enjoyment and wanting to play again (Ryan, Rigby and Przybylski 2006, adult samples), and choice and personalisation raised motivation in an arithmetic activity for children of 9 to 11 (Cordova and Lepper 1996, abstract only). A choice of routes is a choice over goals, which is why it comes before the first floor. Starters that insert keep the words she sends hers, while «Дальше» keeps the approved promise that she never has to type. Expected rewards tied to an activity undermine children's later free choice of it (Deci, Koestner and Ryan 1999), which is why the memo keeps its rule. Characters who miss the child are the parasocial pressure Radesky et al. (2022) found in apps for young children, which is why the order can't even tell the Master she was away.

Route B is built from A by one swap, because RES-4120 found the three-day window leaves about one free floor in three days: 9 places for 8 domains. Two independent runs of `planDay` would mostly return the same floors anyway, and a swap is easier to check against the window.

The 60 % stays a reported target by the owner's research decision of 2026-09-28. The day plan fills about 46 of 60 minutes with the task window, and at the 75-second default of ADR-0090 the 28 graded attempts alone take 35 of the 36 minutes a cap would allow (RES-4120). The interlude takes its 90 seconds out of the existing story cap for the same reason.

The free pen spends what the adventure bucket left, because the approved daily caps already take $55.80 of the $60 month and the parse bucket adds $0.1 a day (RES-4120, ADR-0210). The reservation for the unfinished adventure follows from the same bucket: the adventure and the pen share it, and the adventure was there first.

Freshness is counted on the Mac with a lemma table, because Holtzman et al. (2019) found likelihood decoding bland and repetitive, and ADR-0110 already matches words by their written-out forms without a morphology library. A table built once keeps that property for arbitrary words. The regeneration skips free-text replies, because REQ-1622's 6 seconds at the 95th percentile leave no room for a second call.

Route B differs from A only in floor order and opening beat on about two days in three, by RES-4120's count of one free floor in three days, so on most days the choice is of order and story, and verify counts `differsIn: "order"` from `route_offered`.

The strongest objection is that none of this changes the share of the hour she spends on maths. The task window still takes about three quarters of the adventure, the interlude takes story time from lead-ins, and the report only shows the share. The addendum's risk 1, maths as an entry fee, stays where the approved design left it, and the decision adds a way to see it. I accept it, because a cap breaks the owner's floor of 28 graded attempts on the first slow days, and the Interest section will show whether the share matters for her. The first reversal condition watches for the case where it does.

A second objection is that the schedule keeps the shop from her for a week, and the shop is the one place buttons have a use. She collects buttons from day 1 and can spend none until day 8. I accept it, because the addendum's order is the owner's instruction and the parent can open everything at once.

## Alternatives

| Option | Better at | Why it lost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Do nothing: the approved design | No day streaks, experience for effort, "yes, and" in the canon, a strange third option and free text at five points already; it adds no content and no cost | The options are the Master's words, not hers; nothing measures whether the Master repeats itself or whether she still writes; the backlog's acceptance rule has no measure; and the owner's addendum requires starters, routes, the schedule and the rest of section 12 |
| Measure first: build only the Interest section and add the addendum's rules when the signal rises | Spends effort only on risks that turn out real for her | The signal needs 28 days of play, so a month of a stale game passes before it fires; and the signal can't show what the free pen, rereading or starters would do before they exist |

| Two routes from two independent runs of `planDay` with different seeds | Each route is the Director's best plan by its own lights | The window leaves about one free floor, so the runs mostly agree, and a check that both keep the window is harder than a check on one swap |
| This decision: every rule of section 12, the Director owns the routes and the schedule, the 60 % reported | Keeps the measurement and the floor of 28, and gives the owner a measured share from the first week and a signal from day 28 of play | It leaves risk 1 as it is and asks for the most hand-written content |

## What it costs

The owner writes content before the parts play:

- three starters for every library scene;
- at least 20 interludes;
- the three decks;
- the return-phrase list;
- the rituals, the hidden details and the canon's `cast`, `catchphrase` and `hidden_detail` tags;
- one member of the cast in Session 0's first scene;
- the memo's checklist.

A tool script builds the lemma table once, and the owner commits it.

The player pays two taps where a sending option took one, since a starter now needs a send. She waits a week for the shop and eight days for the free pen.

The play key pays for the route teasers and the interlude inside the adventure's existing story, one regeneration for each repeating scene that isn't a free-text reply, and the pen's turns from what the adventure bucket left. No new bucket exists. By my estimate at RES-2700's prices, the two teasers and the interlude cost about 3 scene calls with their checks, about $0.05 an adventure, and regeneration at the 20 % of the fourth reversal condition adds about 5 calls, about $0.08. The price is paid inside the $1.5 bucket: on an adventure that reaches the cap, later scenes fall back to the library sooner.

The parent's interruption budget: this decision sends the parent no notification. The Parent Room holds one optional question for each day of play, five sample scenes a week, the cards she makes and one line while the signal is raised. The parent is never needed in real time. Two weeks without the parent lose no data and leave no queue that can't be recovered. The days go unmarked, and the "came back" row shows no mark for them. The sample queue drains itself: it holds at most 10 unread scenes, and a new week's draw drops the oldest, which stay in the dialogue book. The day-mark question drops a day older than 7 days. The cards wait, and at 50 unviewed the card panel says so once.

Ceilings on what accumulates:

- the do-not-use list is at most 30 entries in an order;
- `phrase_counts` drains past 14 days by itself;
- the sample queue holds 10 and drains;
- unviewed cards report once at 50;
- pen facts are at most 50 in a pen order, the least recently used dropped, a figure I chose on the pattern of ADR-0110's 200.

The log never drains, by ADR-0020's design.

The security boundary protects her text and the measurement. The threats, most likely first:

1. A card or the story book leaves the family. Cards reach only the Parent Room behind the PIN, and the player's routes can't list or read a card. The PDF is printed by the parent from that screen, and it holds nothing the story book doesn't already show on that device.
2. The client claims a send was her own text. The server computes `origin` from the scene's starters, so a forged field is never read.
3. A story choice moves the measurement. The chosen route's floors pass the same window check, and the Director's event schemas still carry no field the Master or a route teaser could use to change a trial.
4. The player opens a closed system through its route. The server answers 404, so only the parent's setting opens it.

## What would reverse it

- Over the first 8 weeks, the median task-window share of the weeks in which the signal rose is at least 5 points above that of the other weeks, the planned share being about 77 % in both. The owner then reopens the 10-minute story cap, because the share then matters for her.
- Unchanged starters make up more than 80 % of her sends over the first 28 days of play. Insert-only starters then fail to make the words hers, and REQ-6210 goes back to the owner.
- She picks the door in the same display position in more than 90 % of 14 route choices. The choice then isn't one, and the teasers or the choice itself are reopened.
- Regeneration fires on more than 20 % of scenes that aren't free-text replies over a week. The threshold of 3 uses in 14 days then fights the canon's voice, and the list's rule is reopened.
- On 3 or more of her first 8 days of play, she opens the system that opened that day on none of the next 7 days of play. The schedule then opens systems she doesn't use, and it goes back to the owner.
- She passes more than 80 % of free-action points with «Дальше» and no send over 14 days of play. The field then isn't hers, whatever the signal says, because a child who sends nothing moves neither of the signal's figures; the Interest section shows this share beside her own words.
- More than 90 % of 14 route choices are `via: "next"`. She then isn't choosing routes, and the teasers or the choice are reopened.
- The pen closes on `pen_closed_budget` on more than half of the days she writes in it. The reservation then leaves the pen too little, and the owner chooses between the pen and the adventure's spend.

## How I will know it was realised

1. The 60-day simulation of ADR-0190's group 3 shows, in every adventure: at least 2 free-action points, one not tied to a trial, and at least 4 and 3 on a completed 3-floor adventure; one interlude; and a known character in a scene order. It shows systems opening on the days REQ-6244 gives, a starter that only inserts, no task, score or knowledge-model event in the free pen, and first scenes free of the listed return phrases. It shows the signal rising on the synthetic profile whose own words fall 40 % while its unchanged starters rise, and staying down on the others. And no whitelisted sequence joins a do-not-use list (REQ-6296).
2. A route test over 90 simulated days finds both routes of every adventure keeping the three-day window and the overdue rule, with equal planned graded slots. They differ in a floor on every day a swap keeps the window for the next two days, and «Дальше» takes route A.
3. A replay test finds the display order of the doors varying with the seed, and route A on each side on between 40 % and 60 % of 100 days.
4. A packet test before day 2 of play finds no thread count, hint route or explanation offer, and finds the short solution after `partial` and `alt`. A ledger test finds no `thread_granted` before `system_unlocked` for threads, and 3 threads on day 2.
5. A component test presses 1, 2 and 3 on an empty field and on a field with text, and taps each chip. Each insertion leaves the field unsent, and a key on a field with text types the digit.
6. An origin test sends each starter unchanged, edited and replaced, and finds `origin` and `ownWords` computed on the server, whatever the client sends.
7. A planner test gives a plan that leaves a `player_action` fact from the previous adventure unechoed. The service rejects it, retries and logs `consequence_missed`.
8. A static check fails the build when a reply schema admits `choices` outside `route_choice` and `name_suggest`, when a ritual entry has a time or date field, or when a route of the story log imports the gateway.
9. A freshness test feeds 14 days of scenes that repeat a sequence 4 times. The sequence appears in the next order's list, a running joke and her names never do, a drafted scene that repeats it is regenerated once, and a free-text reply isn't.
10. A pen test runs the pen across the soft-stop point and across an empty bucket, and each closes through a library scene with no extension. The pen stays closed for the game day, and it can't spend below the reservation for an unfinished adventure.
11. The parent reads a week of the dialogue book with its sample scenes at the stage 0.3 acceptance. The parent finds her own words answered in a later scene, one strange starter in each scene, refusals only for safety and each with a way on, and no line that misses her. The parent also judges the memo's checklist (REQ-6202, REQ-6214, REQ-6218, REQ-6242, REQ-6278, REQ-6279, REQ-6280).
12. A content test runs the do-not-use rule over every line of the canon's recurring cast and familiars as though each line were used 4 times in 14 days, and fails on any line it would strike that the canon hasn't tagged `catchphrase`, so an untagged greeting is found before play.

The premortem, written as though it had already happened. Six weeks in, the parent found she had stopped writing, and the signal had never risen. The starters had taken two taps where the old options took one, so she had learned to press «Дальше» at every point. Her own words per adventure fell to nothing, but she sent nothing either, so the share of unchanged starters never rose and REQ-6292's signal, which needs both, stayed down.
